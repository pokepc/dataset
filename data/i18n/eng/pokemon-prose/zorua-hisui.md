## Tricky Fox Pokémon

Hisuian Zorua is a timid, cowardly Pokémon that protects itself by hiding its true identity and
taking on the forms of other people or Pokémon. It often becomes the likeness of an opponent or a
silent child, using the disguise to surprise others, search for food, or slip away from danger.

In Hisui, this Zorua is said to be a returned soul fueled by resentment. The energy gathered atop
its head can take the shape of foes, letting it vent its lingering malice through transformation.
