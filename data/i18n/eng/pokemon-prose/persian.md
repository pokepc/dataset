## Classy Cat Pokémon

Persian is a proud, haughty Pokémon admired for its elegant fur, graceful walk, and the gem that
gleams on its forehead. It moves with quiet agility, and its lithe muscles let it strike in an
instant, but its refined appearance hides a vicious temper.

It is difficult to keep as a pet because of its fickle meanness and quick claws. Persian may scratch
or bite for little reason, especially when annoyed or challenged, and it is known to attack anything
that meets its gaze.
