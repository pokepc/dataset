## Hairy Bug Pokémon

Weedle is often found in forests, grasslands, and thick patches of grass or bushes, where it feeds
on leaves and often hides beneath the foliage it eats. It has an extremely keen sense of smell,
using its large red proboscis to tell preferred leaves from ones it dislikes.

This small Pokémon is known for the sharp, toxic barb on top of its head, which can inject a
powerful poison and serves as its main defense against attackers. Its bright body color is said to
warn enemies away.
