## Brutal Pokémon

Hydreigon is a brutal three-headed Pokémon that travels the skies on six wings and attacks anything
that moves. Its heads on its arms have no brains, but all three work together to bite and destroy
whatever stands in its way.

Stories tell of villages ruined by Hydreigon, and it is feared for devouring everything in its path.
The three heads take turns sinking their teeth into an opponent, pressing the assault until its
target is defeated.
