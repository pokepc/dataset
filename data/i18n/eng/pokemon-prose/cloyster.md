## Bivalve Pokémon

Cloyster protects itself by clamping its hard shell shut, and once it does, even great strength
cannot pry it open. Its shell is so hard that it is said to resist even a bomb, and its innards have
never been seen.

When threatened, it fires its horns and shell spikes in quick volleys. In seas with strong tidal
currents, it grows large, sharp spikes, and if part of its shell is damaged, that area swells into
new spikes. It can also swim by swallowing water and jetting it out behind it.
