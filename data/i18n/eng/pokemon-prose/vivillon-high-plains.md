## Scale Pokémon

Vivillon with many different patterns are found all over the world, and each pattern reflects the
climate and topography of its habitat. The High Plains Pattern is one of these forms, and its wings
scatter colorful scales.

Forms only differ in appearance, and Vivillon’s pattern is fixed by the region tied to its original
trainer’s 3DS. It cannot be changed, and trading a Scatterbug, Spewpa, or egg does not alter the
pattern it will have when it evolves.
