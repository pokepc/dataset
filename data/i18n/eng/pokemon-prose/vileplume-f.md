## Flower Pokémon

Vileplume has the world’s largest petals, and the bigger they grow, the more toxic pollen they
contain. Its heavy head is hard to hold up, but as it walks or flaps its broad petals, it shakes
loose clouds of poisonous dust, often turning the air yellow.

Its pollen is intensely allergenic and can even cause paralysis, so approaching it is unwise. The
bloom’s loud flapping and sudden burst into flower help scatter its spores, which it may use to lure
prey before immobilizing them.
