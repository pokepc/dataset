## Alpha Pokémon

In mythology, Arceus is said to have existed before the universe and to have emerged from an egg
when nothing else did. Legends tell that it then shaped the world and all that exists within it,
even with its thousand arms.

In Hisuian lore, it is described as a heavenly source of light whose radiance guides and protects
all Pokémon. As the Grass Type form, it changes type to match a held Plate through Multitype.
