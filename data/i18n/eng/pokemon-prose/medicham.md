## Meditate Pokémon

Through meditation and harsh yoga training, Medicham sharpens its sixth sense and heightens the
energy within its body. It can sense thoughts, read an opponent’s aura, and even predict a foe’s
next move with psychokinetic power.

Its movements are elegant and dance-like, letting it avoid attacks and counter in the same motion.
By training its mind through long periods without food, it is said to conceal itself by blending
into fields and mountains.
