## Old Shrimp Pokémon

Anorith was restored from a prehistoric fossil and lived in the ocean about 100,000,000 years ago.
This ancient Pokémon is thought to be an ancestor of modern bug Pokémon, and its eight side wings
were once feet adapted for life in the sea.

It swims by undulating those eight wings, moving through the water like oars. Anorith also hunts
with its claws, using them to grip prey firmly or catch hidden prey among rocks on the seafloor.
