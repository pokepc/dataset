## Flame Pokémon

Mega Charizard Y soars high on powerful wings, flying in search of strong opponents. It breathes
intensely hot flames that can melt almost anything, and its heat is said to grow even fiercer after
harsh battles.

When it unleashes a superhot blast, the flame at the tip of its tail burns more intensely, sometimes
shifting to a pale blue when it becomes furious. Its fire can melt boulders and may accidentally
start forest fires.
