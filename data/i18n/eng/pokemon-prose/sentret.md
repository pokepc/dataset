## Scout Pokémon

Sentret is a very nervous, cautious Pokémon that stands up on its tail to scan a wide area and keep
watch over its surroundings. From this lookout, it can spot danger from far away and warn the others
with loud cries, screeches, and tail slaps on the ground.

It takes turns standing guard when the group sleeps, and a sentry wakes the rest at the first sign
of trouble. If Sentret becomes separated from its pack, fear keeps it from sleeping at all.
