## Magical Pokémon

Mismagius appears as if from nowhere, muttering incantations and placing curses that can torment
others with headaches, hallucinations, or terrifying visions. Its cry sounds like a spell, and it
often shows up where it is least expected.

Though feared for the misfortune it can bring, Mismagius does not always use its power to harm. Some
of its chants are said to bring happiness, ward off disaster, or even help people, and on rare
occasions it may cast spells that make others fall in love.
