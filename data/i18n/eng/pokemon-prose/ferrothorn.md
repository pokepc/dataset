## Thorn Pod Pokémon

Ferrothorn fights by swinging its three spiky feelers and firing steel spikes, strikes so forceful
they can reduce boulders to rubble. Its spikes are harder than steel, and it crawls across rock
walls by stabbing them into stone.

It often attaches itself to cave ceilings to fire spikes at targets passing below. It also scrapes
its spikes across rocks, then uses the tips of its feelers to absorb nutrients from the stone.
