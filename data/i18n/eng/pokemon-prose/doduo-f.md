## Twin Bird Pokémon

Doduo is a two-headed bird that cannot fly well, so it depends on its strong legs to run at high
speed across grassy plains. It races along with powerful strides, keeping itself stable by
alternately raising and lowering its heads, and its feet leave deep, giant footprints behind.
