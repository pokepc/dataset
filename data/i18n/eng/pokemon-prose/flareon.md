## Flame Pokémon

Flareon stores heat in a flame sac or chamber inside its body, where inhaled air is heated and then
expelled as fire that can exceed 3,000 degrees Fahrenheit. When it is agitated or preparing to
attack, its temperature can climb to around 1,650 to 1,700 degrees Fahrenheit.
