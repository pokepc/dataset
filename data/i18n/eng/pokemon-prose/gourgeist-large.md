## Pumpkin Pokémon

On nights of the new moon, Gourgeist wanders through town streets, singing in eerie voices that are
said to curse anyone who hears them. In the deep darkness, its cries are thought to be the wails of
suffering spirits, and it is said to come knocking at doors, carrying those who answer away to the
afterlife.

It ensnares prey with its hairlike arms and sings joyfully while watching them suffer. This Large
Size form has fixed size and stats, unlike other Gourgeist forms.
