## Mouse Pokémon

Sandslash’s body is covered in tough spines made from hardened hide, and its claws are sharp enough
to slash foes or climb trees. The spikes and claws can break off, but they grow back quickly, and
the old spikes are replaced each year.

When threatened, Sandslash curls into a spiny ball and rolls to attack or escape, using its back
spines to jab enemies and sometimes leaping in to rake them with its claws. It can also kick up a
blinding dust storm while rushing about, and it may curl up to avoid the heat.
