## Water Weed Pokémon

Lotad lives in ponds and lakes, floating on the water’s surface like an aquatic plant. It is known
to search for clean water, and if it goes too long without drinking, the broad leaf on its head
wilts and it grows weak.

Its leaf became too large and heavy for land life, so Lotad came to live in the water. On rare
occasions, it travels on land in search of clean water, and it can even ferry small Pokémon across
ponds on its leaf.
