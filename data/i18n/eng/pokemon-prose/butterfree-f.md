## Butterfly Pokémon

Butterfree flits from flower to flower, drawn to nectar and honey. It can find even faint flower
patches, search out blooms far from its nest, and carry honey back by rubbing it onto the hairs on
its legs. Its wings are coated with water-repellent powder, letting it fly in rain and heavy
weather.

When threatened, it beats its wings rapidly to scatter highly toxic dust or poisonous scales for
defense. Its large eyes are made up of many tiny eyes, and it is known to drive off bird Pokémon
that go after Cater
