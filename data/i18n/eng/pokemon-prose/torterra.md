## Continent Pokémon

Torterra is a remarkable, large-bodied Pokémon with unmatched strength that roams the wilderness in
search of clean water. Its broad, unmoving back can support small Pokémon, which sometimes gather
there to build nests, and some are even born on it and remain there for life.

When groups of Torterra migrate for water, they can be mistaken for moving forests. In ancient
times, people imagined that a gigantic Torterra lived beneath the ground.
