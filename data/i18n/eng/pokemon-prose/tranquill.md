## Wild Pigeon Pokémon

Tranquill has an acute memory that lets it always find its way back to its nest and Trainer, no
matter how far it travels. It can fly at a moderate speed, and delivery workers often choose it as a
partner because of this dependable navigation.

Many people also believe that deep in the forests where Tranquill live lies a peaceful place free of
war. This calm image adds to its reputation as a bright Pokémon that stays loyal and never becomes
separated from its Trainer.
