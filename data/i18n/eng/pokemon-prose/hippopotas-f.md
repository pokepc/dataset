## Hippo Pokémon

Hippopotas lives in arid places and is active during the day, spending cold desert nights burrowed
snugly in the sand. Though large and languid, it is hard to notice when it lurks beneath the soil,
and it often travels easily through desert sands with its nostrils shut tight.

It protects itself by enshrouding its body with sand to ward off germs and dislikes getting wet.
Instead of perspiration, it releases grainy sand from its body, and when agitated or excited it
expels sand from its nostrils.
