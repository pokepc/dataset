## Ninja Pokémon

Greninja creates throwing stars from compressed water and spins them into deadly, high-speed attacks
that can split metal. It strikes with blades of sharpened water, using the force and precision of
its throws to overwhelm opponents.

With a ninja’s grace, it appears and vanishes in an instant. It uses swift, elusive movements to toy
with its enemies before cutting them down with its watery stars.
