## Posy Picker Pokémon

Comfey uses the sticky vines on its head to pick flowers and fasten them to its body with fluids it
produces. Once attached, the flowers are kept fresh and give off a pleasant aroma, and each Comfey’s
choice of blooms creates a unique scent.

It feels uneasy when it has no flowers. The flowers on its vine are said to have a calming, even
healing effect, and baths made with them are popular for their relaxing properties.
