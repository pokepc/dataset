## Spikes Pokémon

Rhyhorn is strong but not very bright, with a thick hide, massive bones, and a tiny brain. Its
tackles can knock heavy objects flying and even shatter buildings or a mountain, but it is too
slow-witted to help people work.

It is awkward at turning because of its four short legs, so it charges in a straight line and keeps
going until it falls asleep or hits something. While rushing, it may even forget why it started,
though it sometimes seems to remember after demolishing an obstacle.
