## Sun Pokémon

Espeon is extremely loyal to Trainers it deems worthy, and it is said to have awakened its
precognitive abilities to protect them from harm. Its fine, velvet-like fur is so sensitive that it
can sense tiny shifts in air currents, allowing it to predict the weather and an opponent’s next
move.
