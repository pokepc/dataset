## Torch Pokémon

Larvesta is said to have been born from the sun, and people long ago believed it fell from it and
even had a nest there. Its body stays warm all over, and it protects itself with flame.

It makes its home at the bases of volcanoes. Using the five horns on its head, it sprays fire to
intimidate predators, drive off enemies, and scare prey, with flames that can become extremely hot.

When it evolves, its whole body is engulfed in flames and it is enclosed in a cocoon of fire.
