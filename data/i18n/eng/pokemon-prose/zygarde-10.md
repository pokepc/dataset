## Order Pokémon

When the Kalos region’s ecosystem falls into disarray, Zygarde appears to restore balance, emerging
from the cave where it lives to keep watch over those who would damage it. It is thought to monitor
the ecosystem closely, and rumors say it holds even greater power in reserve.

This form is formed when about half of its cells have gathered. When it faces those that threaten
the ecosystem, it can become fiercely merciless, and some say it can change into an even stronger
form in battle.
