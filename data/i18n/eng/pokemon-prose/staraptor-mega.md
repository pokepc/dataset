## Predator Pokémon

Mega Staraptor has a savage nature and boldly challenges foes far larger than itself. It is so
relentless that it keeps attacking even when injured, and it lives alone after leaving the flock.

Its wings and legs are powerfully muscled, giving it sturdy flight and the strength to carry off
even large, burly targets. It can fly while gripping a small Pokémon, and it is also known to fuss
over the shape of its comb.
