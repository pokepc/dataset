## Iron Armor Pokémon

Aron lives deep in mountains and builds its steel body by feeding on iron ore it digs up there. Its
hunger can also drive it to chew on railroad tracks, bridges, and even cars, sometimes causing
serious damage in the process.

Clad in steel armor, Aron can make an all-out charge that smashes heavy vehicles, then eat the
wreckage. When it evolves, the old armor peels off and is replaced, and people in ancient times
collected the shed metal for practical use.
