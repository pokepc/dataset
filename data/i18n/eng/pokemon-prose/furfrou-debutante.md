## Poodle Pokémon

Trimming Furfrou’s fluffy fur makes it look more elegant and also lets it move more swiftly. Its fur
keeps growing if left alone, but it will only allow someone it trusts to trim it.

In Kalos, these Pokémon were once guardians of the king, and aristocrats competed to create the most
exquisite styles. This trim is only a change in appearance and can be altered at any time; it
returns to Natural Form after five days unless trimmed again or stored in the PC.
