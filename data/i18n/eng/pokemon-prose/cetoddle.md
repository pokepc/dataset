## Terra Whale Pokémon

Cetoddle left the ocean and began living on land long ago, and it appears to be closely related to
Wailmer. It is a species that has adapted to life away from the sea while retaining a strong link to
its watery origins.

It lives in frigid regions in small pods of around five individuals. Cetoddle is drawn to the
minerals found in snow and ice, which it seems to enjoy seeking out in its cold habitat.
