## Ball Pokémon

Electrode stores electricity under great pressure and is highly attracted to it, often gathering
near power plants or in charged air to feed. The more energy it takes in, the faster it moves, but
it also becomes more unstable.

It is notorious for exploding in response to even slight shocks or other small stimuli, earning a
fearful reputation. When packed with stored electricity, it can drift on the wind, and its body
surface is said to resemble an Apricorn, releasing a blast of power when irritated.
