## Constraint Pokémon

Mega Meowstic keeps the eyelike patterns inside its ears tightly hidden, since they can unleash
immense psychic power when exposed. In danger, it raises its ears and releases that force with
enough strength to grind a 10-ton truck into dust.

Its defensive instinct is especially strong, and it can draw out its full power when protecting
itself or a partner. Male and female Meowstic differ in moves and hidden abilities, and those
differences are treated as practical forms.
