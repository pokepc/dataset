## Vibration Pokémon

Palpitoad lives both in water and on land, using its long, sticky tongue to catch prey and
immobilize opponents. It can also weaken prey with powerful sound waves before entangling them.

By vibrating the bumps on its head, it creates waves in water and earthquake-like tremors on land.
Some Palpitoad have cries that are especially pleasing to hear, and those with larger lumps can
produce a wider range of sounds.
