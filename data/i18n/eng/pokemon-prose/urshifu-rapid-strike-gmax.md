## Wushu Pokémon

Urshifu in its Gigantamax form is a fierce believer in finishing battles with a single crushing
strike. It closes in on opponents with sudden leaps, then delivers a devastating blow with a
hardened fist.

This Pokémon is said to live in the mountains of a distant region, where it races across sheer
cliffs as part of its training. Through this relentless practice, it strengthens its legs and
sharpens its techniques.
