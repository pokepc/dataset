## Fish Pokémon

Feebas is a shabby, ragged Pokémon with tattered fins that draw little attention from people,
researchers, or predators. It is widely regarded as ugly and unpopular, and its slow, dimwitted
nature makes it an easy catch.

Despite its poor appearance, Feebas has remarkable vitality. It can live in the sea, rivers, ponds,
and even polluted or dirty water, surviving on very little water and adapting to harsh conditions.
