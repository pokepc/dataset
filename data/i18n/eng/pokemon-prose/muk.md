## Sludge Pokémon

Muk is thickly covered in filthy sludge, and its body leaks a foul toxic fluid. Even a single drop
can turn water stagnant, wilt plants, or leave the ground barren for years.

Its stench is so overpowering that it can make people faint, yet Muk no longer has a sense of smell.
It often gathers where sludge and litter accumulate, especially in warm, humid places, and its
numbers have declined as cleaner environments leave it little food or habitat.
