## Poison Moth Pokémon

Dustox is a nocturnal Pokémon drawn to light, often gathering around streetlamps, city lights, and
bonfires on dark nights. Swarms may descend on settled areas, where they feed on the leaves of
roadside trees and boulevards.

It searches for food by using its antennae like radar. When disturbed or attacked, it flutters its
wings to scatter a fine dust or powder that is highly toxic, spreading it widely with every flap.
