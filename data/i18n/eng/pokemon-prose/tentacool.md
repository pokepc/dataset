## Jellyfish Pokémon

Tentacool drifts aimlessly across the surface of shallow seas, where its watery body blends into the
water and makes it hard to spot. It searches for prey with long, toxic tentacles, and anyone who
brushes against them may be stung or poisoned.

Its body is almost entirely water, so it can dry out and become shriveled on a beach when the tide
goes out. Many are still alive, however, and can revive if returned to the sea.
