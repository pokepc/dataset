## Color Swap Pokémon

Kecleon can freely change its body color to blend in with its surroundings, though the zigzag
pattern on its belly never changes. It also shifts color with its mood, and if it is startled or
ignored for too long, it may revert to its original colors or even pout and stay hidden.

By matching nearby shading, it can sneak up on prey unnoticed before striking with its long,
stretchy tongue to ensnare the target. Its color can also reflect its condition, with darker shades
indicating better health.
