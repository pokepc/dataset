## Patient Pokémon

Wobbuffet lives quietly in darkness, often in caves, to keep its pitch-black tail hidden. It hates
light and sudden shocks, and it is never first to attack.

It endures blows rather than striking on its own, but if attacked or if its tail is hit, it inflates
its body and lashes back fiercely. In that moment, it may even try to drag its foe down with it.

When several Wobbuffet meet, they become competitive and test one another’s endurance, sometimes
even seeing which can go longest without food.
