## Mitosis Pokémon

Duosion's brain has split into two, and the two halves rarely think alike, making its actions
utterly unpredictable. At times it may try to do two different things at once because each half is
following a separate thought.

When both halves of its brain manage to agree, Duosion's psychic power is maximized. In that state,
its power is said to extend over a range of more than half a mile.
