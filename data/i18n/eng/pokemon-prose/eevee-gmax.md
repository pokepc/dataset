## Evolution Pokémon

Eevee has an unstable genetic makeup, and its irregular DNA is easily influenced by its
surroundings. Because of this, it can change form quickly in response to environmental shifts and
other stimuli, even taking on different evolutionary forms to suit harsh conditions.

Its body can alter its composition to match its surroundings, and its genes conceal many possible
evolutions. Rare and mysterious, Eevee is known for the remarkable variety of forms it can become.
