## Water Fish Pokémon

Quagsire is a carefree, easygoing Pokémon with a sluggish manner and a body that is always slimy. As
it swims, it often bumps into boat hulls, boulders, or the river bottom, yet it seems not to mind at
all.

It hunts by lying still in water with its mouth wide open, waiting for prey to blunder in. Because
it moves so little, it does not get very hungry.
