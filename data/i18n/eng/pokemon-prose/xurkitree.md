## Glowing Pokémon

Xurkitree is one of the mysterious Ultra Beasts, and witnesses say astonishing electric shocks pour
from its entire body. It first appeared through an Ultra Wormhole and even raided a power plant,
leading people to believe it feeds on electricity.

Though it is dangerous in this world and utterly alien to it, Xurkitree is said to be a common
organism in its native world. Like some other Ultra Beasts, it can stand motionless for long
periods, with its arms and legs planted into the ground like a tree.
