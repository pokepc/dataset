## Mole Pokémon

Diglett lives mostly underground, usually about a yard below the surface, where it feeds on plant
roots and moves through tunnels at a shallow depth. Its burrowing leaves raised earth behind, making
it easy to spot, and it may occasionally pop up in caves or above ground.

It prefers dark places and avoids bright sunlight, which makes its thin skin weak. Farmers value
Diglett because its digging tills the soil and can even enrich it, though it may also become a pest
by chewing on roots.
