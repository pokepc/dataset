## Bagworm Pokémon

Burmy shelters its small, vulnerable body with a cloak it always seems to make, even when no
suitable materials are nearby. In its Trash Cloak, it adapts its covering to the surroundings, and
if the cloak is damaged in battle, it quickly repairs it with whatever is at hand.

The cloak helps protect it from cold winds, and it may become thinner in hot weather. This form is
only an appearance, though it changes to match the terrain after battle and becomes permanent when
Burmy evolves.
