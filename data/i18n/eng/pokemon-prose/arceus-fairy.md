## Alpha Pokémon

In mythology, Arceus is said to have been born before the universe existed, emerging from an egg in
a place where nothing was and then shaping the world. Legends also tell of it as the force that
created all things, even the Pokémon said to have shaped the universe with its 1,000 arms.

In Hisui, it is described as a heavenly source of light that pours across the land, guiding and
protecting all Pokémon. As a Fairy Type form, it changes type with a held Plate through Multitype.
