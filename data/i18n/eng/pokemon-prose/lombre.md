## Jolly Pokémon

Lombre lives at sunny water’s edges, where it sleeps by day on a bed of water grass and becomes
active after dusk. Nocturnal and mischievous, it likes to startle people in the evening and tug on
anglers’ fishing lines from beneath the surface.

Its body is covered in a slippery, slimy film, and its hands feel unpleasant to the touch. It feeds
on aquatic moss growing on riverbeds, and it is often mistaken for a human child.
