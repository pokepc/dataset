## Bronze Bell Pokémon

In ancient times, Bronzong was revered as a bringer of rain and plentiful harvests, and people
believed that asking it for rain would help crops grow. Some also thought its bell-like cry could
open a passage to another world and summon rain clouds.

It was found buried underground after a very long sleep, and its body patterns have led some
scientists to suspect it came from outside the Galar region. When angered, it gives off a warning
cry that rings like a bell.
