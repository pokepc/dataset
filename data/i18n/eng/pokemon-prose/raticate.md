## Mouse Pokémon

Raticate uses its whiskers to keep its balance, and it may slow down if they are cut off. Its
steadily growing fangs must be worn down by gnawing on hard things such as rocks, logs, and even
walls, and it can chew through thick concrete with ease.

Its hind feet are webbed, making it a strong swimmer that can cross rivers and search wide areas for
food. Despite its ordinary appearance, it is quick to bite and can be surprisingly violent if
provoked.
