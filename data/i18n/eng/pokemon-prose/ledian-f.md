## Five Star Pokémon

Ledian is said to use starlight as its energy, and in clear, star-filled places it can be found in
great numbers. At night, it flutters through the sky and scatters a glowing, sparkly powder, and
some believe the dust brings good fortune if it touches you.

The patterns on its back are tied to the night sky, growing larger when more stars appear and
smaller when fewer are visible. It also loves berries, and when daytime warmth arrives, it curls up
in the grass or inside a big leaf to sleep.
