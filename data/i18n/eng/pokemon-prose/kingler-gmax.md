## Pincer Pokémon

Kingler’s enormous claw grows hard as steel and has tremendous crushing strength, but its size makes
it heavy and difficult to aim or move. If it lifts the claw too quickly, it can lose its balance and
stagger.

It uses the oversized pincer to wave at others and communicate, but the burden quickly tires it.
Outside of battle, the huge claw often just gets in the way.
