## Single Bloom Pokémon

Floette flutters through flower fields, tending blossoms that are beginning to wilt and giving them
its own power. It is especially delighted by a well-kept flower bed in bloom, where it appears to
celebrate with an elegant dance.

It can draw out the hidden power of flowers to battle, raising them as weapons; the more beautiful
the blossom, the greater the power it holds. Floette is fiercely protective of flower beds and never
forgives those who damage them.
