## Single Bloom Pokémon

Flabébé draws out and controls the hidden power of flowers, and the bloom it carries is likely part
of its own body. The pollen it gathers forms a crown with hidden healing properties.

When Flabébé finds a flower it likes, it settles on it for life and floats along in the wind with a
calm heart. It keeps searching until it finds a flower with the color and shape it wants, and its
flower form is fixed once encountered.
