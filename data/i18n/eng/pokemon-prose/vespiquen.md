## Beehive Pokémon

Vespiquen keeps its colony in the chambers of its body, where it raises grubs on honey gathered by
Combee. It can produce different pheromones to direct these grubs and its attendants, and those that
give off stronger scents tend to be followed by larger swarms.

In battle, it commands its grubs with skill, and they will defend it fiercely even at great risk. It
also orders its subjects to maintain its hive and drives away intruders that sneak into its nest,
sometimes using them as food.
