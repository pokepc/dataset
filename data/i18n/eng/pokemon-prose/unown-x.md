## Symbol Pokémon

Unown’s flat, thin body is usually found stuck to walls, and its shape seems to carry meaning. Its
many forms resemble ancient writing or hieroglyphs, leaving it a mystery whether the symbols
inspired Unown or the other way around.

It is also thought that different forms may have different abilities, and some researchers believe
Unown communicate telepathically. When several are together, an odd power is said to emerge, but
when one is alone, nothing happens.
