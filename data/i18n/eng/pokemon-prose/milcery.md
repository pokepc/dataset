## Cream Pokémon

Milcery is said to be born from sweet-smelling particles floating in the air, and its body is made
of cream. It has a delicate, confection-like presence that makes it seem almost like a living
dessert.

Stories say that any patisserie visited by Milcery is sure to enjoy success and good fortune. Its
appearance is closely tied to sweetness, and its presence is regarded as a lucky sign.
