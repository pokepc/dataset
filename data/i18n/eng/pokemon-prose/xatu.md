## Mystic Pokémon

Xatu is an odd Pokémon that is said to see both the past and the future at once. Some believe its
right eye watches what is yet to come while its left eye looks into what has already passed, and
others revere it for its mystical foresight.

It often stands rooted and silent, staring at the sun and following its movement through the day.
After meditating at sunrise, it may remain motionless for hours, as if unwilling or unable to change
what it has foreseen.
