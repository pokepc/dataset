## Rock Head Pokémon

Bagon dreams of soaring through the sky, and it practices every day by leaping off cliffs in hopes
that it will one day fly. Its frustration at not being able to fly can drive it to angrily headbutt
rocks and anything nearby, shattering boulders and stones alike.

Though small, Bagon is powerfully built, with well-developed neck muscles and a head as hard as
tempered steel or stone. It usually lives alone, and its hard head can cleave a boulder in a single
strike.
