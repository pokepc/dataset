## Candle Pokémon

Litwick’s flame is usually out, but it begins to glow when it absorbs life force from people or
Pokémon, using that energy as fuel. The brighter its flame burns, the younger the life it has taken
in, and the more eerie its light becomes.

It often pretends to be a guide and lures travelers along before draining their energy. The flame on
its head also keeps its body slightly warm, and it is said to lead lost children toward the spirit
world.
