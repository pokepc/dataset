## Weather Pokémon

Castform’s appearance changes with the weather, as temperature and humidity alter its sensitive
cells. It can take the forms of the sun, rain clouds, or snow clouds, borrowing nature’s power to
protect its tiny body.

Its form shifts along with the weather, and its feelings seem to change with it. When conditions
grow rough, Castform’s disposition does too. Outside of battle, it remains in its normal form
regardless of the weather.
