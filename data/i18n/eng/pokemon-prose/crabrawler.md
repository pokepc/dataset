## Boxing Pokémon

Crabrawler uses its hard pincers for both offense and defense, guarding its weak spots while it
waits for an opening to strike. It fights with rapid punches, and battles between two Crabrawler can
look like boxing matches.

It punches so often that its pincers may come off from overuse, but they grow back quickly. When it
loses, it foams at the mouth and faints. It has even been known to mistake Exeggutor for a coconut
tree and climb it, only to be shaken off and stomped.
