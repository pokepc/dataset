## Dragon Pokémon

Dragonair is a mystical Pokémon that exudes a gentle aura, and when its body glows or becomes
shrouded in that aura, the weather can change instantly. Its crystalline orbs are said to hold great
power, letting it alter conditions around it by releasing energy from the crystals on its neck and
tail.
