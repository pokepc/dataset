## Despot Pokémon

With its enormous jaws, Tyrantrum could shred thick metal and even demolish a car with a single
bite. In the ancient world, nothing could stop it, and it is said to have ruled like a king.

This Pokémon comes from about 100 million years ago. It has a vicious yet magnificent presence, and
while it has been restored only partially, there is room to believe its body was once covered in a
feather-like coat.
