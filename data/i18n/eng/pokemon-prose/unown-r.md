## Symbol Pokémon

Unown (R) is one of many strangely shaped forms of a Pokémon that resembles ancient writing and
hieroglyphs. Its flat, thin body is often found stuck to walls, and its shape seems to carry some
kind of meaning, though researchers still do not know whether writing or Unown came first.

These forms are thought to communicate telepathically when together. When alone, little happens, but
if two or more are present, an odd power is said to emerge. Forms only change its appearance, and
each one appears randomly.
