## Sharp Claw Pokémon

Sneasel is a cunning, savage Pokémon that lurks in darkness to ambush prey. Its paws hide sharp,
hooked claws, which it can suddenly extend to startle foes, and it uses them to scale trees and
cliffs with ease.

It seeks out unguarded nests and steals eggs, sometimes working in pairs to lure parents away before
raiding them. It also feeds on eggs and can attack vulnerable spots on prey with its claws, making
it a relentless and dangerous hunter.
