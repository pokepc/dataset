## Cotton Bird Pokémon

Swablu has light, fluffy wings that look like cottony clouds, and it takes diligent care of them. It
constantly grooms itself, and if its wings become dirty, it seeks out springwater or a stream to
wash them clean.

It is very fond of cleanliness and will wipe and polish dirty things with its wings. Swablu is not
frightened of people, and it often lands softly on their heads, sitting there like a cotton-fluff
hat.
