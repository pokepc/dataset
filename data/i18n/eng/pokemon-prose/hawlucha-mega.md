## Wrestling Pokémon

Although Mega Hawlucha is small, its refined fighting skill lets it keep pace with powerful
bruisers. It uses its wings to control its position in the air, striking from above and leaping
nimbly to exploit blind spots.

In battle, it overwhelms opponents with quick moves and superb submission holds, wearing them down
before ending the fight with a flashy finishing move. It always strikes a pose first, though that
extra showboating can sometimes leave it open to a counterattack.
