## Armor Pokémon

Donphan has sharp, hard tusks and a rugged hide so tough that ordinary attacks barely leave a mark.
Its tusks grow slowly, and the longer and larger they are, the higher its rank in the herd.

It is known for curling into a ball and charging at high speed, a rolling attack that is hard to
stop once it begins. With its massive strength, Donphan can even knock down or destroy a house, and
it helps clear rock and mud slides from mountain trails.
