## Clay Doll Pokémon

Baltoy moves by spinning around on a single foot, and some have even been seen spinning on their
heads. It was discovered in ancient ruins, where images of similar Pokémon were found on old walls
and cave paintings from primitive times.

When Baltoy finds others of its kind, it gathers with them and cries out noisily in unison. Even
while sleeping, it cleverly balances itself on one foot, and large groups of them can sometimes be
found in old graveyards.
