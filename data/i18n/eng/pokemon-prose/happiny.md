## Playhouse Pokémon

Happiny carefully carries a round, white stone in its belly pouch, treating it like an egg in
imitation of Chansey. Too small to lay eggs yet, it searches for round white stones and treasures
them dearly, sometimes giving one to a friend it gets along with well.

It is usually well behaved, but if its precious stone is taken away or not returned, it will cry and
throw a tantrum. Happiny also likes children and may play house for fun, though it can become
overloaded when it gathers too many stones.
