## Fairy Pokémon

Although Snubbull looks frightening, it is kind, affectionate, and playful. It grows close to others
easily, and its affectionate nature makes it popular, especially with women, who often find it cute
and enjoy frolicking with it.

Snubbull is actually timid and cowardly, so it growls and bares its fangs to seem threatening. Its
scary face can send smaller Pokémon fleeing, though it appears a little sad when that happens.
Despite its bold look, it is laid-back and may sleep much of the day.
