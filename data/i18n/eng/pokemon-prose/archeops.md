## First Bird Pokémon

Archeops is an intelligent ancient Pokémon that often hunted in flocks, cooperating to corner prey
before one moved in for the catch. Though capable of flight, it is much better at running and needs
a long runway to take off, building up to about 25 mph before it can launch itself into the air.

Its wing muscles are still weak, so flight comes less naturally than sprinting. Even so, it was
apparently better at hunting on the ground, and its delicate plumage is difficult to restore without
expert care.
