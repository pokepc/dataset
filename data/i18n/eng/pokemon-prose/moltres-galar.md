## Flame Pokémon

Moltres is a legendary bird Pokémon whose flaming wings scatter embers and blaze with a dazzling red
glow. When it flaps, the sky can seem to turn red, and its fiery presence is said to be a thrilling
sight.

Stories say this Pokémon brings the coming of spring and even early warmth to wintry lands it
visits. Some also tell of its radiant, flame-cloaked wings lighting paths for travelers lost in the
mountains.
