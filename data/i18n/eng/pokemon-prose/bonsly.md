## Bonsai Pokémon

Bonsly lives in dry, rocky places and does not do well around water, where it stands out against the
sparse greenery. As its green spheres dry out, their shine becomes duller.

It keeps its body in balance by exuding moisture from its eyes, like sweating. This water can look
like tears and may seem to invite sympathy, but it is simply a way to stay healthy and manage excess
fluid.
