## Draft Horse Pokémon

Mudsdale remains calm and unmoving no matter the situation, mixing dirt with saliva to make a
special mud. It spits this mud to create a coating that helps resist wind and rain, and old houses
were sometimes lined with it for protection.

The mud that hardens around its legs becomes harder than stone, giving its heavy kicks tremendous
power. Mudsdale can trudge mountain roads for days without rest, carrying or dragging loads of over
10 tons, and can even reduce a truck to scrap with a single kick.
