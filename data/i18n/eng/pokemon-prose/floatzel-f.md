## Sea Weasel Pokémon

Floatzel floats with a well-developed flotation sac that keeps it from drowning, even in stormy
seas. The sac developed as it pursued aquatic prey, and it can even serve as a makeshift raft. One
might spot it near fishing hamlets or ports, where it is often seen in the water.

It is known to rescue drowning people, and with its sac inflated it can carry someone on its back
before deflating it to dive. At the same time, it may also snatch prey, making it a familiar sight
in busy coastal waters.
