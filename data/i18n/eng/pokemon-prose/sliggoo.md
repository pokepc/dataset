## Soft Tissue Pokémon

Sliggoo’s entire body is covered in a gooey mucus that can dissolve almost anything, and it uses
that caustic slime to drive off predators. Toothless and not very strong, it sprays mucus on prey
and then slurps up whatever has been melted away.
