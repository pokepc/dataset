## Copperderm Pokémon

Cufant digs up the ground with its trunk and has remarkable strength, easily carrying loads of over
five tons. Its copper body is especially suited to heavy labor, making it excellent at jobs that
demand serious power.

When rain falls, Cufant’s copper body tarnishes and turns a vivid green. This change gives it a
distinctive look, but its strength remains as dependable as ever.
