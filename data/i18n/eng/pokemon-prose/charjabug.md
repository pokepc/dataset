## Battery Pokémon

Charjabug stores electricity in an electric sac in its body, converting the leaves and humus it eats
into power. It often buries itself in fallen leaves and barely moves while it feeds, but if stepped
on it can give off a shock.

Its stout shell offers excellent protection, and it uses stored electricity against persistent
opponents. It can also strike enemies with jolts discharged from the tips of its jaws.
