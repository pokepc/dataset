## Renegade Pokémon

Giratina is said to live in a world on the reverse side of ours, where common knowledge is distorted
and strange. It has also been seen in an ancient cemetery, and one Hisuian verse describes it as the
deep shadow cast by a powerful light.

Banished for its violence, Giratina silently gazed upon the old world from the Distortion World. In
that realm, or when holding a Griseous Orb, it takes its Origin Forme.
