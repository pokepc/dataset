## Bagworm Pokémon

When Burmy evolves, the cloak it was wearing becomes part of Wormadam Sandy Cloak’s body, and it is
never shed. Its form is fixed by the materials present at evolution, so Wormadam’s appearance can
vary depending on where it evolved.

The cloak may become thicker if evolution happens on a cold day. Once the change is made, Burmy’s
current cloak determines Wormadam’s form and cannot be changed again.
