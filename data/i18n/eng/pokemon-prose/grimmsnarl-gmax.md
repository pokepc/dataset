## Bulk Up Pokémon

With its hair wrapped tightly around its body, this Pokémon greatly enhances its muscles and can
overpower even Machamp. Its dense fur acts like a second set of muscles, giving it tremendous
strength.

When the hairs unfurl, they latch onto opponents and hold them fast like tentacles. In battle, it
uses this hair both to ensnare foes and to crush them with overwhelming power.
