## Water Fish Pokémon

Wooper lives in cold water and often sleeps half-buried in mud at the bottom. When the weather
cools, it may leave the water or come ashore to search for food, sometimes along the shore in the
evening.

On land, it coats its body with a slimy, poisonous film. This toxic layer makes it dangerous to
touch and also helps keep its skin from drying out.
