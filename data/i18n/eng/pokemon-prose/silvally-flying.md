## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally awakens its hidden strength through a deep bond of trust with its Trainer. Once freed from
the heavy control mask it once wore, its speed increases greatly, and it can change its type at will
in battle.

This form represents its true power after evolving and being awakened by that bond. Its will is
strengthened by the trust it shares with its Trainer, allowing it to break away from its mask and
fully unleash its flexible nature.
