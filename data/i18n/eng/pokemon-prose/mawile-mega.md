## Deceiver Pokémon

Mega Mawile’s huge jaws are actually transformed steel horns attached to its head. It uses its
docile-looking, cute face to lull foes into complacency, then strikes when they least expect it with
its massive, relentless jaws.

It has two mouths, but the large jaws on the back of its head do the eating. Those jaws are strong
enough to chew through iron beams, making this Pokémon both cunning and terrifying.
