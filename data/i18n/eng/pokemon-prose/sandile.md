## Desert Croc Pokémon

Sandile lives buried beneath hot desert sands, where the sun-warmed ground helps keep its body
temperature from dropping. It moves under the surface with only its nose and eyes exposed, and a
dark membrane protects its eyes from the glare.

It can swim through sand to stay hidden and to avoid enemies, and its strong legs and lower body
push the sand aside as it goes. At night, when the desert turns cold, it burrows deep and sleeps
until sunrise.
