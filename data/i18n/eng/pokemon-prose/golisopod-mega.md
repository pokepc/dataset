## Hard Scale Pokémon

Mega Golisopod battles with six arms and giant claws that can extend and retract at will, cleaving
seawater—or even air—cleanly in two. Its shell is as hard as diamond, and it takes advantage of
every opening, finishing opponents off with the small claws on its front legs.

Though it is a fierce fighter that will do anything to win, it spends much of its time peacefully
meditating in caves deep beneath the sea. It lives in sunken ships or holes in the seabed, and is
sometimes accompanied by Wimpod.
