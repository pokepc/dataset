## Bagworm Pokémon

Pineco hangs motionless from tree branches, waiting for flying insect prey to come near. It does not
move around much on its own, and if disturbed while eating, it can suddenly drop to the ground and
explode.

It covers its body with layers of tree bark, using saliva that hardens in air to glue the bark on.
The extra weight does not bother it, and the shell makes Pineco look like a pinecone while helping
protect it from bird Pokémon that peck at it by mistake.
