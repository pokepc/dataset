## Emotion Pokémon

Kirlia is highly perceptive of its Trainer’s feelings, and positive emotions seem to strengthen it.
When its Trainer is happy, it overflows with energy and dances or spins joyfully, and it is said to
grow more beautiful in that atmosphere.

With its highly developed brain, Kirlia controls psychokinetic power that can distort the space
around it, making the air shimmer with mirage-like scenery. Its head horns help amplify this power,
and it is even said to glimpse the future and project visions of paradise.
