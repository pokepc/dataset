## Wish Pokémon

Jirachi is said to sleep for a thousand years at a time, waking for only seven days. When a pure
voice sings to it, it can be roused from its long slumber, and legends say it will grant wishes
written on notes attached to its head.

Stories also say Jirachi can make any wish come true. While it sleeps, a tough crystalline shell
protects its body, and if it senses danger, it may fight without fully awakening.
