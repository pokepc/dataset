## Guts Pokémon

Makuhita is a tenacious Pokémon that keeps getting back up and attacking no matter how many times it
is knocked down. Each time it rises, it stores more energy in its body, and its tireless spirit
never gives up hope.

It trains rigorously to toughen itself, slamming into thick trees or repeatedly striking their
trunks. It eats plenty, sleeps well, and follows a routine of training, resting, and training again
to build a sturdy body and dauntless spirit.
