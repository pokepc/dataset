## Long Tail Pokémon

Aipom lives high among tall trees, where it moves from branch to branch with ease. Its powerful tail
can grasp branches, hold it in the air, and keep it steady while it swings or sleeps in the
treetops.

The tail ends in a handlike appendage that it can use almost like a real hand, making it better at
plucking fruit and handling objects than its clumsy forepaws. Because it relies on its tail so much,
its actual hands have become awkward.
