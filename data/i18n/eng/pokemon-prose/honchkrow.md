## Big Boss Pokémon

Honchkrow becomes active at night, and a single deep cry can draw a huge flock of Murkrow to its
side so quickly that it seems to summon darkness itself. Known as the Summoner of Night, it rules
over its followers with a harsh, merciless nature.

It makes its Murkrow cronies bring it food while it idles away the time grooming itself in its nest.
If they fail or betray it, Honchkrow will hunt them down and punish them, though it usually leaves
most fighting to its goons and only strikes the final blow itself
