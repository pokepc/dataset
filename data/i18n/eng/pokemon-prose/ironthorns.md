## Paradox Pokémon

Iron Thorns bears a striking resemblance to a Pokémon once described in a dubious magazine as a
Tyranitar from a billion years in the future. Its silhouette and notable features also match those
recorded for an object called Iron Thorns in an expedition journal.

These references suggest a creature with an unusual, future-like design that echoes a familiar
ancient power while presenting it in a distinctly mechanical form.
