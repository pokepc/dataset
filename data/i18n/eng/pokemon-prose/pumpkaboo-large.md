## Pumpkin Pokémon

A spirit trapped within its pumpkin body inhabits Pumpkaboo, and it grows especially restless and
active after sunset. It is said to guide wandering spirits to where they belong, helping them move
on to the afterlife.

The light shining from the holes in its pumpkin can hypnotize and control those who see it. Its size
is fixed when it is encountered, and the Large Size form has different stats from other forms.
