## Dream Eater Pokémon

Munna floats through the air and drifts in late at night beside people’s pillows. It feeds on the
dreams of people and Pokémon, and when it eats a dream the sleeper may forget what it was about.

Munna is drawn to nightmares and eats them, but it also consumes pleasant dreams. The mist it
releases changes with what it has eaten: pink for good dreams and black for nightmares, while the
patterns on its body give off a faint glow.
