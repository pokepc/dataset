## Mach Pokémon

Garchomp folds its body and extends its wings to rush through the sky like a jet plane, flying at
sonic speed. Its fine scales reduce drag, and their sharp edges can injure anything that attacks it.

The protuberances on its head act as sensors, letting it detect distant prey. It is a ferocious
hunter that can pursue prey underground or in the air, and it is said to dive into flocks of bird
Pokémon and swallow them whole.
