## Cricket Pokémon

Kricketune crosses its knifelike arms in front of its chest when it cries, using them to help
produce sound. By letting its cry resonate in the hollow of its belly, it creates a captivating
melody that can be composed on the spot.

Its melodies vary greatly from individual to individual and can signal its emotions. Scientists
study these patterns, and one village even holds a contest around its amazingly varied cries.
