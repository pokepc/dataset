## Electric Pokémon

Electabuzz feeds on strong electrical energy and is often found near power plants, where it can eat
so much electricity that blackouts may follow. Its body constantly leaks power, and in darkness it
glows a whitish-blue. Coming too close can make your hair stand on end.
