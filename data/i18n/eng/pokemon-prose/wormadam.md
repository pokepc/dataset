## Bagworm Pokémon

When Burmy evolves, its cloak becomes part of Wormadam’s body and is never shed. Its form is
determined by the environment where it evolved, so the materials at hand are absorbed and can create
many variations.

A Wormadam that evolves on a cold day is said to have a thicker cloak. Once evolved, its current
cloak becomes its form and cannot be changed, and different forms can have different stats and
movepools.
