## Spray Pokémon

Panpour stores nutrient-rich water in the tuft on its head, and it uses its tail to spray that water
onto plants. The plants it waters grow large, showing the water's strong nourishing effect.

It does not do well in dry places, so it keeps itself damp by shooting water from its head tuft
through its tail. This constant moisture helps it stay healthy while it tends to plant life.
