## Megaton Pokémon

Golem has a boulder-like body with an extremely hard hide that can withstand even dynamite blasts
without a scratch, though it dislikes rain and humidity. It lives on mountains and is known to roll
downhill, sometimes in great numbers after earthquakes, leaving grooves that people use as
guideways.
