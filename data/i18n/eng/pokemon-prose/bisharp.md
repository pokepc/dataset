## Sword Blade Pokémon

Bisharp leads a retinue of Pawniard and hounds prey as a group, using its minions to wear targets
down before it moves in to deliver the finishing blow. It is pitiless and keeps a close eye on its
followers, battling to prove itself as boss and driving them away if it loses.

Even when it strikes, Bisharp shows no change in expression. If the blade on its head is chipped, it
is said to retire from leadership, and it can clash fiercely with Fraxure over sharpening stones.
