## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally's power is awakened by a deep bond of trust with its Trainer. Once that bond is strong
enough, it breaks free of its control mask and its true strength emerges, allowing it to change its
type at will.

In this form, it has evolved and is no longer weighed down by the mask, so its speed rises greatly.
The Trainer who helped awaken it also gave it the name it is now known by.
