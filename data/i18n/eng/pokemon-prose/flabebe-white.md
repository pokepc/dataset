## Single Bloom Pokémon

Flabébé draws out and controls the hidden power of flowers, and the bloom it holds is likely part of
its body. It wears a crown of pollen gathered from its flower, and the crown is said to have hidden
healing properties.

When it finds a flower with the color and shape it likes, it settles there for life and floats along
in the wind with a peaceful heart. A Flabébé will keep traveling until it finds such a flower, and
this White Flower form’s bloom is set when encountered and cannot be changed.
