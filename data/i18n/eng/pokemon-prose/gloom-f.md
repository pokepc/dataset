## Weed Pokémon

Gloom drips a sticky nectar or honey from its mouth that is mistaken for drool. Though it is sweet,
the fluid smells so awful that it can cling stubbornly and draw prey despite the repulsive odor.

Its flower pistils also give off a foul stench that can become even worse when it feels threatened.
The smell can make people faint or even lose their sense of direction, though a few oddly enjoy its
nose-bending stink.
