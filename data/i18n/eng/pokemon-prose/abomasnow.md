## Frost Tree Pokémon

Abomasnow lives quietly deep in perpetually snow-covered mountains, where it hides itself by
whipping up blizzards and blanketing wide areas in snow. Its massive body can shake loose whiteout
conditions, and its powerful arms are strong enough to split huge boulders.
