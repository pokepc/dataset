## Gnash Teeth Pokémon

Bruxish burrows beneath the sand and waits for prey while radiating psychic power from the
protuberance on its head. When it attacks, it uses psychokinesis to stun its target before crushing
it with its strong teeth.

Its teeth can grind prey to mush, and even Shellder’s shell is no match for them. Bruxish’s thick
skin also helps protect it from Mareanie’s spikes, which it can crunch up and eat.
