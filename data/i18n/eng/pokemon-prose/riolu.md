## Emanation Pokémon

Riolu is a lithe but powerful youngster with remarkable stamina, able to keep running through the
night and even cross great distances in a single night. Though still childlike in appearance, it is
a hard worker and can be a challenging companion to walk.

It senses the feelings and condition of people, Pokémon, and even its surroundings by reading aura
waves. Riolu can see emotions in those waves and use changing aura shapes to communicate silently
with others of its kind, helping it avoid dangerous foes.
