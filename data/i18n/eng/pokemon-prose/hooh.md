## Rainbow Pokémon

Ho-Oh is said to fly the world's skies continuously on magnificent seven-colored wings, and a
rainbow is said to trail behind it when it soars. Its body and feathers gleam in seven colors
depending on the light, giving it a radiant, rainbow-like appearance.

Legends say Ho-Oh reveals itself only to pure-hearted Trainers by shining its bright wings. Its
feathers are thought to bring joy and happiness to those who see or bear them, and it is said to
live at the foot of a rainbow.
