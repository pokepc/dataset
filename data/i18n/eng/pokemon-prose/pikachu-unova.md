## Mouse Pokémon

Pikachu stores electricity in the pouches on its cheeks, and its power can be strong enough to equal
a lightning bolt. When it is threatened, angry, or startled by something new, it releases crackling
shocks, and groups of Pikachu can build enough charge to help cause lightning storms.

This clever forest-dweller raises its tail to watch for danger, though the pose can draw lightning.
It also roasts hard berries with electricity to soften them, and may even use a shock to wake or
recharge a weakened fellow.
