## Cricket Pokémon

Kricketune crosses its knifelike arms in front of its chest when it cries, using them to help
produce sound. Its cry can resonate in the hollow of its belly, creating a captivating melody.

It can compose tunes ad lib, and its melodies vary greatly from one individual to another. These
changing cries also signal its emotions, drawing scientific study and even inspiring village
contests over the most amazing songs.
