## Axe Jaw Pokémon

Fraxure has tusks that can shatter rocks, and it uses them with a favored battle style that combines
tackling with stabbing strikes. Its skin is as hard as armor, and clashes between Fraxure can become
intensely violent when they fight over territory.

Because a broken tusk will never grow back, Fraxure carefully sharpens its tusks on river rocks
after battles. It takes great care to keep them intact, treating them as a vital part of its
strength.
