## Compass Pokémon

Probopass radiates intense magnetism from every part of its body, and it freely controls three small
units called Mini-Noses with that force. These mobile units help it battle from multiple directions
and catch prey, while the main body mostly directs them.

Its powerful magnetic field can even make nearby electrical devices unusable. It also commands the
iron sand that forms its mustache, shaping it into hard stones to strike down prey.
