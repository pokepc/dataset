## Lightning Pokémon

Jolteon is a sensitive Pokémon that can become sad or angry easily, and each mood change seems to
charge it with more power. Its cells produce weak electricity, which its static, bristling fur
amplifies into 10,000-volt lightning bolts.

When it is startled or agitated, every hair on its body stands up like sharp needles, creating a
constant crackle and sparking sound. It can also launch its bristlelike fur in small bunches, then
finish off foes with a sudden electric shock.
