## Balloon Pokémon

Jigglypuff uses its large, round eyes to captivate opponents before singing a soothing lullaby that
makes listeners drowsy and often sends them to sleep. Its voice can be adjusted to just the right
wavelength, and its excellent lung capacity lets it keep singing for a long time without pausing to
breathe.

When it inflates its body, it can sustain its song even longer. Jigglypuff’s singing varies by
region, and its impressive vocal range gives each individual a different style and skill.
