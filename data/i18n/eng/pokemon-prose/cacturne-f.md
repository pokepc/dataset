## Scarecrow Pokémon

Cacturne remains still during the day to avoid losing moisture to the harsh desert sun, then becomes
active after nightfall when the temperature drops. In the desert, it waits for prey that has been
exhausted by the day’s heat.

At night, it may trail travelers in ragtag packs, biding its time until they can no longer move.
After countless years in harsh deserts, its blood has become like sand.
