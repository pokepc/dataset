## Blade Quill Pokémon

Dartrix is a fastidious, dandyish Pokémon that spends much of its free time preening its wings. It
is so focused on staying clean that dirt on its plumage can distract it, and if it is not groomed
carefully, it may stop listening to its trainer.

It attacks by flinging sharp blade quills from its wings, and its throws are so precise that they
can strike weak points and even hit foes behind it. Regular sunlight helps it gather power, while
thorough preening keeps its feathers neat and its quills keen.
