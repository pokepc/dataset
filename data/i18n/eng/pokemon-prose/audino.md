## Hearing Pokémon

Audino has an extraordinary sense of hearing, picking up even the faintest sounds from far away. It
can understand its surroundings almost like radar, and its ears are so sensitive that even a pebble
rolling at great distance will not escape notice.

By touching others with the feelers on its ears, Audino can sense their feelings and physical
condition. It listens to heartbeats to gauge emotions, and it can even tell when an egg is close to
hatching.
