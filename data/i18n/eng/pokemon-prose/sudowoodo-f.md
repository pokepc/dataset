## Imitation Pokémon

Sudowoodo disguises itself as a tree to avoid attack, but its body is actually closer to rock than
to plant life. Its stiff pose leaves its arms strong yet supple, and the green parts that stay
bright all year make it easy to spot in winter.

It hates water and flees from rain or even a splash, sometimes seeming to vanish when wet weather
starts. Even so, it is a favorite among collectors, especially older fans who admire the length and
angle of its arms.
