## Prototurtle Pokémon

Carracosta lives and hunts both on land and in the sea, though it moves more slowly on land. It may
drag land prey into the water to finish it off, and a slap from its powerful front appendages can
knock a foe out.

Its jaws are fearsome enough to crush rocks, steel beams, shells, and bones, and it will devour prey
completely. By eating every last bit, Carracosta strengthens and thickens its sturdy shell.
