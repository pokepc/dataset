## Insect Pokémon

Venonat lives in the shadows of tall trees, where it waits for nightfall to hunt small bugs drawn to
light. It is active in darkness, and its large eyes act like radar, helping it find even minuscule
prey hiding out of sight.

Its big eyes are actually clusters of many tiny eyes, and its body is covered in a coat of thin,
stiff hair said to protect it. Poison oozes from all over its body, adding to the defenses it
carries as it feeds.
