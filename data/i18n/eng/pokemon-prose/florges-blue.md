## Garden Pokémon

Florges claims exquisite flower gardens as its territory and draws strength from the energy given
off by flowering plants. It is said to devote its long life, which can span several hundred years,
to protecting gardens, and in ages past castle governors invited it to create beautiful gardens for
their domains.

It controls the flowers it grows, unleashing petal blizzards that are as dazzling as they are
powerful. This Blue Flower form differs only in appearance, with its flower fixed as a Flabébé or
Floette
