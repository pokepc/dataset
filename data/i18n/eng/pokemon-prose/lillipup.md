## Puppy Pokémon

Lillipup is a brave yet cautious Pokémon that will face strong opponents boldly, but it quickly
retreats if it judges the battle is unwinnable. It is intelligent and easy to raise, which makes it
popular with beginning Trainers, and its obedience is especially valued.

The long fur around its face works like a radar, helping it sense subtle changes in its surroundings
and even probe an opponent’s condition. It does not yelp, making it well suited to apartment living,
and it remembers both kindness and abuse
