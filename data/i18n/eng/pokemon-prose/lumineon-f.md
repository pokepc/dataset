## Neon Pokémon

Lumineon lives on the deep-sea floor, where it moves over the seabed with its long front fins or the
two fins on its chest, almost as if it were crawling. In the darkness, schools of Lumineon can look
like stars shining in the night sky.

It uses the patterns on its four tail fins and its gleaming fins to lure prey close. But that same
light can draw in ferocious predatory fish Pokémon, and in the scarce depths it may even clash with
Lanturn over food.
