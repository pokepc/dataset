## Licking Pokémon

Lickitung uses its tongue like an extra limb, moving it with surprising dexterity to grab food,
attack, and even clean its body. The tongue is extremely long, sticky with thick saliva, and
sensitive enough to let it learn about things by texture and taste. It often licks anything
unfamiliar, though sour tastes bother it.
