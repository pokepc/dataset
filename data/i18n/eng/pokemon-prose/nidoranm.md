## Poison Pin Pokémon

Nidoran♂ is a small but very cautious Pokémon, always keeping its large ears upright and moving them
freely to catch even the faintest sound. It scans the grass and its surroundings for danger, and
will strike first if it senses a threat.

Its forehead horn contains a powerful poison, and larger horns are said to hold stronger venom. When
angered or cornered, it charges and stabs with the horn or poisonous sting to protect itself.
