## Tundra Pokémon

Amaura is an ancient Pokémon that was restored from a part of its body frozen in ice for over 100
million years. In ancient times, it lived in cold lands, often among others of its kind, where
dangerous predators were scarce.

This calm Pokémon is said to have lived where its whinnies could bring auroras to the night sky.
Although it was successfully brought back from a fossil, it is not expected to live long in today’s
warmer environment.
