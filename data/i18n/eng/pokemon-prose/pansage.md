## Grass Monkey Pokémon

Pansage dwells deep in forests, where it searches widely for berries and brings them back to share
with its friends. It is especially kind to weary-looking Pokémon, offering the leaf on its head as a
treat that can relieve stress and make fatigue seem to vanish.

Its leaf is prized for its soothing effect, and Pansage is said to share it with others in need.
This gentle Pokémon is known for its generosity as much as its talent for finding food.
