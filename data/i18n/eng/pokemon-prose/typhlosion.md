## Volcano Pokémon

Typhlosion unleashes intense fire and explosive blasts, sometimes rubbing its blazing fur together
to trigger huge explosions. When its rage peaks, it grows so hot that anything touching it can burst
into flames.

It can also cloak itself in a shimmering heat haze made by its own flames, hiding as it attacks. In
Hisui, its form is said to have been shaped by the sacred mountain’s energy and to purify lost souls
with its fire, guiding them to the afterlife.
