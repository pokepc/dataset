## Human Shape Pokémon

Jynx moves as though it is always dancing, walking with a rhythm of its own while swaying and
wiggling its hips. Its motions can be so alluring that people nearby may begin moving in unison with
it without realizing it.

It seems to use dance to communicate, and its cries sound remarkably like human speech, though their
meaning is unclear. Jynx may vary its rhythm with its mood, and some Jynx are known for especially
beautiful, delicate voices.
