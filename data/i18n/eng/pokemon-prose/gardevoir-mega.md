## Embrace Pokémon

Mega Gardevoir can read the future, and its psychic power peaks when it is defending a Trainer it
has bonded with. When danger threatens, it unleashes its psychokinetic energy at full force, even to
the point of creating a small black hole.

It supports itself with psychic power, seeming to ignore gravity. Dressed in a pure white form
reminiscent of noble attire, it is utterly devoted to protecting the one it adores, even at the risk
of its own life.
