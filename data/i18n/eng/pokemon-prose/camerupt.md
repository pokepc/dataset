## Eruption Pokémon

Camerupt has a volcano inside its body, with magma so hot that it courses through it like blood. The
humps on its back are formed from transformed bones and can blast out superheated magma or molten
lava, especially when it is angry.

It lives in volcanic craters, and its back volcanoes are said to erupt violently every 10 years.
When magma builds up inside it, Camerupt shudders before erupting in a powerful burst.
