## Beckon Pokémon

Dusclops’s body is completely hollow, like a black hole, and it can absorb anything that comes near
it, even huge objects. When it opens its mouth, it draws things in, but nothing ever comes back out.
It is also said to seek drifting will-o’-the-wisps and swallow them into its empty body, where
whatever happens remains a mystery.
