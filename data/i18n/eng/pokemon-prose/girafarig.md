## Long Neck Pokémon

Girafarig’s tail has a tiny brain of its own, letting the rear head react to smells and sounds with
sudden bites. It can even fight instinctively while facing backward, and it is wise to stay away
from behind this Pokémon.

Its tail does not need to sleep, so it can keep watch all day and night. When Girafarig is in
danger, the tail may also drive off enemies with mysterious powers.
