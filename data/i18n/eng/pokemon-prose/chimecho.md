## Wind Chime Pokémon

Chimecho’s cries echo through its hollow body, emerging as clear, beautiful notes. When enraged,
those sounds can become ultrasonic waves powerful enough to startle or knock foes flying, and it can
also emit waves of air strong enough to fell larger prey.
