## Bounce Pokémon

Spoink bounces constantly on its tail, and the shock of each bounce keeps its heart pumping. Because
of this, it cannot afford to stop; if it does, its heart will stop too.

It carries a pearl on top of its head that amplifies its psychokinetic powers, and it is always
searching for a bigger one. Even while asleep, it keeps bouncing, and it is said to enjoy mushrooms
that grow underground.
