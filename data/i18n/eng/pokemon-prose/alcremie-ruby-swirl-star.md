## Cream Pokémon

When Alcremie trusts a Trainer, it delights in sharing berries adorned with its creamy decorations.
Its mood clearly affects the cream it produces, with contentment making it sweeter and richer.

This sweet, decorative form reflects Alcremie's habit of expressing affection through the treats it
creates. As its feelings grow calmer and happier, the cream it secretes from its hands becomes
especially rich.
