## Megapede Pokémon

Mega Scolipede is highly aggressive and chases down foes with quick movements, attacking
relentlessly with its horns until it overpowers them. It clasps prey with the claws near its neck,
digging in and poisoning them with toxic spikes before finishing the battle.

It can latch onto prey and slam it into the ground, then jab again with its claws. Scolipede also
fights fiercely with Centiskorch over territory, and the winner may eat the loser.
