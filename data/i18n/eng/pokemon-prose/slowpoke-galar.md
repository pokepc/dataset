## Dopey Pokémon

Slowpoke is incredibly slow, dopey, and often lost in thought, spending long stretches lounging at
the water’s edge without worrying about time. It may not notice an attack or even a bitten tail for
a long while, and if its tail is lost, it grows back without bothering it much.

It uses its tail to fish by dipping it into the water to lure prey, drawing on the sweet sap that
seeps from it when soaked. Though it is often absent-minded, its tail is also valued as a tasty
ingredient in some places.
