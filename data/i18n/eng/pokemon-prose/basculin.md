## Hostile Pokémon

Basculin are savage, violent Pokémon that rarely get along, and schools of red and blue-striped
individuals may clash at once over territory. When they appear in a lake, they can drive away
everything else except Corphish and Crawdaunt, and anglers prize the fierce struggle they put up on
the hook.
