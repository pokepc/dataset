## Generator Pokémon

Helioptile makes its home in deserts, where it soaks up sunlight to fuel itself. It can generate
energy from basking in the sun, so it does not need to eat regularly.

The frills on either side of its head act like solar panels, with cells that turn sunlight into
electricity. That power drives its electric moves, and if its sunlight supply is interrupted,
Helioptile becomes weak and stressed.
