## Heel Pokémon

Incineroar is rough, aggressive, and wildly self-willed, often ignoring its Trainer when it does not
feel like obeying. It prefers fierce, no-holds-barred battles and grows motivated by strong
opponents, while finding easy fights dull.

After raining down punishing punches and kicks, it finishes foes by blasting fire from around its
waist. When its fighting spirit burns high, those flames intensify, and the heat from its navel can
exceed 3,600 degrees Fahrenheit.
