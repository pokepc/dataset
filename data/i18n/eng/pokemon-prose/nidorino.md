## Poison Pin Pokémon

Nidorino is quick to anger and even quicker to attack. It listens with its big ears, and the moment
it senses danger, it challenges the foe, bristling the barbs on its back and striking with all its
might.

Its horn is harder than diamond and contains a powerful venom that can ooze out when it pierces an
enemy. As its adrenaline rises, its poison becomes even more potent, making its violent charges
especially dangerous.
