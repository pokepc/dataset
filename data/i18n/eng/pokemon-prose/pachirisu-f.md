## EleSquirrel Pokémon

Pachirisu is a calm Pokémon that lives high in trees, where it uses electric cheek pouches to
generate and store power. It can release charges from its tail, and touching its tail or cheeks can
be dangerous.

It also makes fur balls that crackle with static electricity and keeps them with berries in tree
holes. When pairs meet, they may rub their cheek pouches together to share the electricity they have
stored.
