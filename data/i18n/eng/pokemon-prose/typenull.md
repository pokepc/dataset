## Synthetic Pokémon

Type: Null is a synthetic Pokémon created for a specific mission and modeled after a mighty mythical
Pokémon. In one account, an experiment caused it to go berserk, and it was cryogenically frozen;
another says stolen research notes led to a new instance being made in Galar.

Its heavy control mask suppresses its intrinsic capabilities and seals away its true power. Because
it is thought to be dangerous if it loses control, the mask is kept on to restrain the special power
it is said to hide.
