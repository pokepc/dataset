## Eruption Pokémon

Camerupt has a volcano inside its body, with magma so hot that it courses through it at extreme
temperatures. The humps on its back are formed from transformed bones, and they can burst out with
superheated magma or molten lava when pressure builds or the Pokémon becomes angry.

It lives in the craters of volcanoes, and its back volcanoes are said to have major eruptions every
10 years. When enraged, it is especially likely to erupt violently.
