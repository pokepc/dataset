## Lamp Pokémon

Lampent is feared as an ominous emissary of death, appearing in cities just before someone passes
away. It searches for the spirits of the fallen and quietly trails those whose death is near, often
lurking near hospitals or moving through city streets while pretending to be a lamp.

The spirits it steals from the body fuel its baleful fire, giving it strength as it waits for the
moment to strike.
