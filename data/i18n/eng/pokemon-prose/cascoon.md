## Cocoon Pokémon

Cascoon wraps itself entirely in fine silk from its mouth, forming a cocoon that hardens as it
prepares to evolve. Its soft silk body grows tougher over time, and when cracks appear, evolution is
near.

It hides motionlessly beneath leaves or among branches, sometimes covering itself with dead leaves
for camouflage. Even if attacked and badly hurt, it stays still and remembers the pain, enduring
patiently so it will emerge stronger.
