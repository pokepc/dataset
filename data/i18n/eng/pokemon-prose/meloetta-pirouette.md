## Melody Pokémon

Meloetta sings with a special vocal method that can sway the feelings of those who hear it. Its
melodies can leave Pokémon joyful or sorrowful, depending on the tune.

Many well-known songs are said to have been inspired by the melodies Meloetta plays. It can change
into Pirouette Forme in battle by using Relic Song, then return to Aria Forme when outside battle.
