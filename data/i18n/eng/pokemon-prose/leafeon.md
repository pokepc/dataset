## Verdant Pokémon

Leafeon’s body is closer to a plant than an animal, and it makes its own energy through
photosynthesis instead of eating food. It is often found resting in sunlight, where it produces
clean, pure air around itself.

It leads a quiet life in forests near clear rivers and generally avoids conflict, but it will fight
to protect its friends. Its leafy fur gives off a fresh scent when young and a fallen-leaf aroma as
it matures. Its sharp tail can cut through large trees in a single swing.
