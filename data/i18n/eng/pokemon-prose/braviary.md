## Valiant Pokémon

Braviary is a proud, brave bird that fights fiercely for its friends and will keep battling even
when injured. It is known as a hero of the sky, and its bold image is often used in emblems and
symbols.

This huge, ferocious hunter lets out a terrifying cry and can even blast lakes with shock waves to
drive prey to the surface. Among its kind, scars are a mark of honor, and those with the most are
respected, while the brave ones never retreat.
