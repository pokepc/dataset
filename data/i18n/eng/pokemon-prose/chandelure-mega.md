## Luring Pokémon

Mega Chandelure burns away absorbed spirits in its ominous flames, leaving only bodies behind. The
spirits it consumes are said to lose their way and wander the world forever.

It haunts dilapidated mansions, and by waving the flames on its arms, it can sway opponents into a
hypnotic trance. Homes lit by its glow are said to be haunted by frequent funerals.
