## Pumpkin Pokémon

Pumpkaboo’s pumpkin body is said to be inhabited by a spirit trapped in this world. As the sun sets,
it grows restless and active, and wandering spirits are placed into its body before being carried on
to where they belong.

Light streaming from the holes in its pumpkin can hypnotize and control those who see it, including
people and Pokémon. Its size is set when it is encountered and cannot be changed.
