## Swallow Pokémon

Swellow soars high in graceful arcs, circling the sky in search of prey. The moment it spots a
target, it dives steeply with uncanny precision and snatches it in its sharp, clawed feet so it
cannot escape.

It is very conscientious about keeping its glossy wings clean, and when two Swellow gather, they
meticulously groom each other. Its two tail feathers standing upright are a sign of good health, and
it is said to favor warm climates.
