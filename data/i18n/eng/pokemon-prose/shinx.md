## Flash Pokémon

Shinx generates electricity by contracting and extending its muscles, and its fur or body glows when
it is in trouble or senses danger. Its excited trembling can signal that it is building up a great
deal of electricity.

When threatened, its bright fur can dazzle foes long enough for Shinx to flee. It also communicates
with others of its kind by shaking the glowing tip of its tail, and despite its small size, it can
be quite aggressive.
