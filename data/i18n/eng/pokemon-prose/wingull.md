## Seagull Pokémon

Wingull rides sea winds and updrafts with long, narrow wings, gliding through the sky without much
flapping. Its hollow bones make it light, and it can fold its wings to rest after soaring high above
the ocean.

It builds nests on sheer sea cliffs and often circles over the water in search of prey, especially
fish Pokémon. Using its long beak, it carries food, prey, and even valuables, sometimes hiding them
in different places around its nest.
