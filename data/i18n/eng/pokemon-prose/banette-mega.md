## Marionette Pokémon

Mega Banette was once a discarded plush doll that became a Pokémon through lingering hatred and
resentment. It is said to roam dark alleys and garbage dumps, searching for the child or owner who
threw it away so it can exact its revenge.

Its cursed energy is stored in its stuffing, and if it opens its mouth, that energy escapes. Banette
can also build power for strong curses by sticking pins into its own body. Some say that treating it
kindly may calm its grudge and turn it back into a stuffed toy.
