## Meteor Pokémon

Minior once made its home in the ozone layer, where it became food for stronger Pokémon. When the
shell surrounding its body grows too heavy, or when it tries to flee, it hurtles down to the ground.

Its outer shell is unusually durable, but a strong impact or the shock of falling can smash it to
pieces and knock it out of the shell. This Pokémon was born from mutated nanoparticles.
