## South Sea Pokémon

Gorebyss lives in the southern seas at extreme depths, where its body can endure immense water
pressure and shrug off ordinary attacks. It is known for its graceful swimming form and striking
beauty, especially as its pink coloration grows more vivid in warmer spring waters.

Despite its elegance, Gorebyss is cruel. With its thin, tubular mouth, it feeds on prey by draining
bodily fluids, leaving the remaining meat to sink to the seafloor for other Pokémon. It also feeds
on seaweed growing between rocks.
