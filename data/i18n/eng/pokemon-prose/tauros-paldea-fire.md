## Wild Bull Pokémon

A rowdy, violent Pokémon with great stamina, Tauros is famous for its headlong charges. Once it
locks onto a foe, it whips its body with its long tails to raise its fighting spirit, then rushes in
at full speed and will not stop until it hits something.

It lives in herds and settles disputes by locking horns, with the boss usually bearing the longest,
thickest, most battle-scarred horns. If it has no opponent to face, it may ram thick trees to calm
itself.
