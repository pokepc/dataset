## Colossal Pokémon

Legends say Regigigas once towed continents with ropes, pulling landmasses together and binding them
into place. One story even claims it helped form the continent of Hisui, though the truth of that
tale is uncertain.

Other legends say Regigigas created Pokémon in its own image from many different materials,
including clay, ice, magma, rocks, and a special ice mountain. Across the world, it is remembered as
a creator of beings like itself before the continents took their current shape.
