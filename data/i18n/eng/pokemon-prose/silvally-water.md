## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally's hidden strength awakens through the trust it shares with its Trainer. Once that bond is
strong enough, it can break free of the control mask that once held it back and evolve into this
form, gaining a great increase in speed.

Freed from that burden, Silvally can change its type at will, showing remarkable flexibility in
battle. In this form, its true power is released only through a deep bond with a Trainer it trusts.
