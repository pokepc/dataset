## Cavalry Pokémon

Escavalier wears the shell it took from Shelmet as steel armor, which protects its whole body while
arming it with two lances. It charges at foes at high speed and strikes with its pointed spears,
using both offense and defense together.

Even when faced with danger, Escavalier meets opponents bravely. It is known for readying its lances
for battle, and its duels are famous enough to be depicted in a painting of Escavalier clashing with
Sirfetch’d.
