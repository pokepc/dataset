## Balloon Pokémon

Qwilfish inflates itself by swallowing water, and when it does, it can release toxic quills or
poison spikes from all over its body at once. Its small spikes, which evolved from scales, carry a
toxin that can cause fainting, and it must take in a large amount of water to fire them.

When facing a larger opponent, it swallows even more water to match the other Pokémon’s size. Its
round body makes swimming difficult, and fishers dislike it because its spines spray poison
everywhere.
