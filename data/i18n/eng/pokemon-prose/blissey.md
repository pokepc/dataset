## Happiness Pokémon

Blissey is a kindhearted Pokémon that senses sadness and the feelings of people and Pokémon with its
fluffy fur. When it finds someone distressed or weakened, it rushes over to offer care and will
nurse sick Pokémon and even sick people back to health until they recover.

It lays delicious eggs filled with happiness and good fortune. Even a single bite is said to make
others kinder, more pleasant, and smile with joy, and its eggs are prized as a rare delicacy.
