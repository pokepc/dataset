## Shadow Pokémon

Gengar hides in shadows and dark corners, where it waits for the chance to steal its prey’s life
force. A sudden chill is a sign that it is near, since it draws heat from the area around it and can
leave victims shaking.

On dark nights, it may slip into a person’s shadow, pretend to be that shadow, or make shadows move
and laugh on their own. It is also known for delighting in curses and frightening people, even
visiting naughty children in stories.
