## Coconut Pokémon

Known as the Walking Jungle, Exeggutor is said to have come from the tropics, where strong sunlight
helps its heads grow larger. Its three heads each have a will of their own and think independently,
but they are friendly and usually reach decisions by telepathy rather than squabbling.
