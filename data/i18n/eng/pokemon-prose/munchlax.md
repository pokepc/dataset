## Big Eater Pokémon

Munchlax stuffs itself with vast amounts of food and needs to eat about its own weight each day. It
swallows food whole with almost no chewing, and it does not care whether something is tasty or
bland, fresh or rotten.

It hides food under the long fur on its body and may carry a secret stash around, though it
sometimes forgets where it put it. Its sturdy stomach can handle even spoiled scraps, and it is
known to visit villages in search of food, sharing only a single bite with someone it truly trusts.
