## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally’s true strength awakens through its bond of trust with a Trainer. Once its control mask is
destroyed, it evolves beyond its earlier state and is freed from the burden that held it back,
greatly increasing its speed.

With that trust, Silvally can change its type at will, a versatility it displays clearly in battle.
The strong bond that released its hidden power is the final step in bringing out its full potential.
