## Desert Croc Pokémon

Krokorok lives and moves in small groups, often with a female leading while the males gather food.
Its thin eye membranes protect it from sandstorms and let it sense heat, so it can see clearly even
in darkness and hunt without getting lost.

It dislikes the cold and becomes less active after sunset, sometimes digging deep into desert sand
on chilly nights. Krokorok also buries some of its prey in the sand, saving them as emergency meals
if hunting goes poorly.
