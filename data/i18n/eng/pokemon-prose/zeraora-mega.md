## Thunderclap Pokémon

Mega Zeraora charges in at lightning speed, closing on its foes before slashing them apart with
claws crackling with electricity. Its attacks send sparks flying, and even those who evade the blow
may still be electrocuted by the scattered shock.

Electricity also pours from the pads on its limbs as it runs, making lightning flash and thunder
echo in its wake. Its sharp, high-voltage claws and overwhelming speed leave little chance for its
opponents to escape.
