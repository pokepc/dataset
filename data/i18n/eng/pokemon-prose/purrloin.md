## Devious Pokémon

Purrloin uses a deceptively cute act to lull people into lowering their guard, then steals their
valuables for its own amusement. It seems to enjoy the looks of frustration it causes, and its
victims are often unable to stay angry with it for long.

When its targets draw near, Purrloin can strike with sharp claws, leaving stinging scratches behind.
Opponents who are taken in by its adorable behavior may also be wounded by the Pokémon’s mocking
laughter.
