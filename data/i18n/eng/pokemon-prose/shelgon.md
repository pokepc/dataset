## Endurance Pokémon

Shelgon stays shut inside an armor-like shell while its cells rapidly transform, preparing it for
evolution. The shell is extremely hard, made of bone-like material, and can deflect attacks, but it
also makes the Pokémon heavy and sluggish.

It lives deep in caves, often motionless and hidden away. Shelgon scarcely eats, enduring hunger as
it stores up energy for the moment its shell peels off and it finally evolves.
