## Cherry Pokémon

Cherubi stores the nutrients it needs for evolution in the small ball growing beside its body, and
that fruit is said to be sweet and tasty. Sunlight can deepen its red color, a sign that it has
stockpiled more nourishment.

Bird Pokémon are eager to peck away at the fruit, so Cherubi nimbly dashes about to protect it. When
the fruit grows large and then is drained, it shrivels and detaches, providing nourishment for other
creatures as Cherubi evolves.
