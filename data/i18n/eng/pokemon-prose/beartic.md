## Freezing Pokémon

Beartic lives in cold northern seas and is highly skilled in the water, where it swims energetically
in search of prey. It can freeze its breath at will, using it to form fangs and claws of ice that
are said to be harder than steel.

With its frozen breath, Beartic also makes paths across the ocean and can freeze seawater into ice
when it grows tired, resting on the surface it creates.
