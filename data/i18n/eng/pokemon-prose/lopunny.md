## Rabbit Pokémon

Lopunny is extremely cautious and constantly watches its surroundings, quickly bounding away when it
senses danger. It keeps its ears neatly groomed and is very conscious of its appearance, using its
fluffy ear fur to cloak its body when threatened.

Its ears seem delicate, and if they are handled roughly, it answers with powerful, graceful kicks.
Though it dislikes fighting, its lithe legs can deliver striking blows with ease.
