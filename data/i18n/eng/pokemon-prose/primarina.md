## Soloist Pokémon

Primarina commands its water balloons through song, shaping sonic waves that sound like beautiful
singing. The melody is learned from others of its kind and passed down from one generation to the
next.

It treats every battle like a stage, using its singing and graceful dancing to mesmerize both foes
and onlookers. Its voice is its chief weapon, so a Trainer must care for its throat with great
attention. On moonlit nights, it leads its colony in song with a fantastical presence.
