## Symbol Pokémon

Unown (Q) is a flat, thin Pokémon shaped like ancient writing or a hieroglyph, and it is often found
stuck to walls. Its unusual form seems to carry meaning, but researchers still do not know whether
the writing or Unown came first.

Different Unown forms are said to have different abilities, and they may communicate telepathically.
When several are together, an odd power is said to emerge, while nothing happens when one is alone.
