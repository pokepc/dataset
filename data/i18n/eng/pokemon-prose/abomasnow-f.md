## Frost Tree Pokémon

Abomasnow lives quietly on mountains buried in snow, where it hides itself by whipping up blizzards
and blanketing wide areas in white. It is known as the Ice Monster and the abominable snowman.

A shake of its massive body can bring on whiteout conditions, and it can split huge boulders with
ease. Though it keeps to itself deep in the mountains, it will chase off packs of Darumaka that go
after Snover.
