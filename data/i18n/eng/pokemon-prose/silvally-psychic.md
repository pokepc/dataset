## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally (Type: Psychic) awakens its hidden strength through a deep bond of trust with its Trainer.
Once that bond is strong enough, it can break free of its control mask and emerge in its evolved
form, faster and more fully realized than before.

Freed from the mask, it is able to change its type at will. This flexibility is a sign of the trust
that awakened its true power, and the name it bears now reflects the form it took after that
transformation.
