## Heavyweight Pokémon

Hippowdon is quick to anger and can turn violent when roused. It holds its enormous mouth wide open
as a display of strength, and that gaping jaw can crush a car with ease.

It stores sand inside its body and blasts it from ports to raise huge clouds, whirlwinds, and
towering sand twisters in battle. When enraged, it spits out swallowed sand and whips up a sandstorm
to break its foes’ spirits before attacking.

Stones can become lodged in the ports on its body, and Dwebble help dislodge them.
