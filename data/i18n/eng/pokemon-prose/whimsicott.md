## Windveiled Pokémon

Whimsicott floats wherever whirling winds carry it, slipping through even the smallest gaps to sneak
into homes and cause mischief. It hides important things, moves furniture, and scatters balls of
cotton or white fluff behind as it goes.

It does not stay in one place for long, appearing and vanishing with the wind. Sunlight helps its
cotton grow, but if the fluff becomes too much, it tears some off and leaves it scattered about. If
it gets wet, it grows heavy and can no longer drift away.
