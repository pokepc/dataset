## Rock Peak Pokémon

Regirock’s body is made entirely of stone, with no sign of a brain or heart within. Scientists have
found that the rocks forming its body came from different places, and similar stones have been
discovered in ground layers around the world.

When parts of its body chip, erode, or are damaged in battle, it repairs itself by attaching new
rocks. Long ago, people sealed Regirock away, and it is said to seek out suitable stones on its own
to restore itself.
