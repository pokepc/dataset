## Armor Pokémon

Tyranitar is overwhelmingly powerful and can alter the landscape with its strength. Its body cannot
be harmed by ordinary attacks, which only makes it more eager to challenge foes, and it pays little
attention to anything it considers unworthy.

It roams mountain regions seeking opponents and is said to bring down entire mountains to make its
nest. Its steps cause quakes that can crumble great peaks, bury rivers, and force maps to be
redrawn.
