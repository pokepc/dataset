## Plasma Pokémon

Mow Rotom is a bizarre, mischievous Pokémon whose body is made of plasma. It can slip into
electronic devices and other machines, taking them over to cause havoc or surprise people.

Researchers continue to study Rotom because its unique power may even serve as the source for a
special motor. Its electric-like form is also said to be smart, like a will-o'-the-wisp charged with
electricity.
