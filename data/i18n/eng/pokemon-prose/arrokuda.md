## Rush Pokémon

Arrokuda reacts to the slightest movement by rushing straight toward it, leading the way with its
sharply pointed jaw. It seems especially proud of that jaw, and it uses it as it charges after
anything that catches its eye.

Once it has eaten its fill, Arrokuda becomes extremely sluggish. In that drowsy state, it is
vulnerable to being swallowed whole by Cramorant.
