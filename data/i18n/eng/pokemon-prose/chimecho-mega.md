## Wind Chime Pokémon

Mega Chimecho’s cries echo through its hollow body, emerging as clear, beautiful notes. When
agitated, those sounds can become ultrasonic waves strong enough to startle or even knock foes
flying, and it can also send out waves of air powerful enough to overwhelm larger prey.

It hangs from branches or building eaves with the suction cup on its head, and in strong winds it
cries while swaying there. It rides the wind to travel, plucking berries and nuts with its long
tail, and communicates with sevendistinct
