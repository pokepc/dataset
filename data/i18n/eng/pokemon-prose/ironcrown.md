## Paradox Pokémon

Iron Crown resembles a mysterious object once introduced in a paranormal magazine as a cutting-edge
weapon shaped like Cobalion. Its design gives the impression of something both artificial and
legendary, but very little about it is known for certain.

One account says it once unleashed shining blades that sliced everything around it to pieces. Beyond
that fearsome incident, reports offer few details, leaving Iron Crown shrouded in mystery.
