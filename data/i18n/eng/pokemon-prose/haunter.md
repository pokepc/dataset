## Gas Pokémon

Haunter is a frightful, malevolent spirit that can glide through walls and appear wherever it likes,
as if it came from another dimension. It lurks in darkness, keeps watch from inside walls, and may
beckon to anyone who wanders near.

Its gaseous tongue and hand are said to drain life from victims, leaving them shaking and growing
weaker until death. It fears the light and favors moonless nights, when it silently stalks prey and
turns a feeling of being watched into a real threat.
