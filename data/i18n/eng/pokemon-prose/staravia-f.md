## Starling Pokémon

Staravia lives in forests and fields, where it flies in huge flocks in search of bug Pokémon. It
prefers company because it seems to recognize its own weakness, and when left alone it cries out
noisily.

These flocks can be remarkably large, but they are rarely peaceful. Different groups often clash
over territory, and even members of the same flock are constantly fighting among themselves. Some
believe that those with the most magnificent head plumes are the strongest.
