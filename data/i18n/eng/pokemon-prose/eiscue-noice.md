## Penguin Pokémon

Eiscue (Noice Face) drifted in on ocean currents from a frigid place, and it keeps its head iced
over so it stays cold. The ice on its head helps it remain comfortable in warm waters.

It also uses its single hair to fish, dangling it into the sea to lure in prey. By keeping its
heat-sensitive head cool with ice, it can focus on feeding while drifting at sea.
