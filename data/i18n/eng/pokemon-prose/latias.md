## Eon Pokémon

Latias is highly intelligent and can understand human speech. It communicates by telepathy and is
very sensitive to human emotions, becoming wary and crying out sharply if it senses hostility.

Its body is covered in glass-like down that bends light, letting it alter its appearance and even
become invisible. Latias usually lives in small groups and keeps its distance from people and other
Pokémon, disappearing when it senses danger.
