## Dark Pokémon

Houndoom lets out eerie howls that make other Pokémon shiver and hurry back to their nests. Long
ago, people feared those calls and even thought they were the grim reaper’s voice.

Its flames are foul-smelling and mixed with toxins from its body. Burns from its mouth or when it is
angered are said to hurt without ever fully fading, and it uses those poisonous flames to finish off
opponents.
