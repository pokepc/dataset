## Punk Pokémon

When Toxtricity sounds as if it is strumming a guitar, it is really clawing at the protrusions on
its chest to generate electricity. Its performance-like display is tied to the powerful current it
can produce.

This short-tempered, aggressive Pokémon also drinks stagnant water, drawing in any toxins it
contains. It uses that ability to take in poison while fueling its volatile nature.
