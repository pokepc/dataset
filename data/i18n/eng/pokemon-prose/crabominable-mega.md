## Woolly Crab Pokémon

After getting lost on a snowy mountain, this Pokémon endured the bitter cold and evolved, growing a
thick coat of fur. Its fierce temper leaves it throwing punches without hesitation, and in desperate
moments it can even tear off its own pincers and launch them like rockets.

Mega Crabominable stores coldness in its pincers to drive its attacks. It can smash thick walls of
ice to bits, and records tell of it turning back avalanches with a relentless flurry of punches.
