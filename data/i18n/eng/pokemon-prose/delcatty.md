## Prim Pokémon

Delcatty prefers an unfettered life, doing as it pleases at its own pace, so its eating and sleeping
times are completely irregular. It is nocturnal, becoming active at dusk, and it looks for clean,
comfortable places to rest or groom itself rather than keeping a permanent nest.

If other Pokémon approach while it is sleeping, Delcatty does not fight back. It simply moves to
another spot. Its silky fur and stylish appearance also make it very popular with trend-conscious
female Trainers.
