## Poodle Pokémon

Furfrou with the Pharaoh Trim is an elegant Pokémon whose carefully trimmed fur makes it appear more
refined and helps it move more swiftly. In Kalos history, these Pokémon served as guardians of the
king, and their striking appearance later became a point of pride for aristocrats competing to
create the most exquisite styles.

Its fur grows longer and longer when left alone, but it will only let someone it trusts cut it.
