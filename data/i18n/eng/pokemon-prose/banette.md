## Marionette Pokémon

Banette is a discarded plush doll brought to life by resentment and cursed energy. It is said to
roam dark alleys and garbage dumps, searching for the child who threw it away so it can exact its
revenge.

It gathers power for strong curses by sticking pins into its own body, but if it opens its mouth,
its cursed energy escapes. Some stories say that treating it kindly can calm its grudge and let it
become a stuffed toy once more.
