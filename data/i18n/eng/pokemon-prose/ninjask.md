## Ninja Pokémon

Ninjask darts about so quickly that it is often impossible to see, and for a long time people
believed it was invisible. Even so, its distinctive cry can be heard clearly, and listening for too
long can cause a headache.

It is said to be able to avoid any attack, and it gathers around tree sap, which it loves to feed
on. If not trained properly, it may ignore its Trainer and cry loudly without stopping, putting that
Trainer’s skill to the test.
