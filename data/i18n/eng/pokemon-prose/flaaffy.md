## Wool Pokémon

Flaaffy’s fluffy fleece stores electricity with ease, and its wool quality lets it build up a great
deal of static even from a small amount of wool. Its rubbery, nonconductive skin protects it from
shocks, especially on the bare, slick patches where fleece does not grow.

When it holds too much electricity, those areas can become patchy and bald. If fully charged, its
tail glows, and it can fire hairs that zap when they strike an opponent.
