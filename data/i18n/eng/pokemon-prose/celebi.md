## Time Travel Pokémon

Celebi wanders across time, appearing only in peaceful times and in beautiful forests. Wherever it
shows itself, grass and trees flourish, and it is revered as a guardian of the forest or even a
deity in older records.

It is said to have come from the future by crossing time, with some tales claiming it leaves behind
an egg from that future when it vanishes deep in the woods. Its presence is also thought to promise
a bright future, and it can bolster plants and heal wounds.
