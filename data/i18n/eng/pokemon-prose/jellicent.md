## Floating Pokémon

Jellicent’s body is mostly seawater, and it drifts through the sea by expelling water from itself.
It is said to make sunken ships its lair, with a castle of wrecks resting on the seafloor where it
has left ships and crews lost and vanished.

Jellicent is often found around fancy cruise ships and tankers, waiting to drag away prey. It feeds
on life energy, and fishermen fear that it can pull them into the sea and steal their lives away. On
nights of the full moon, schools of Jellicent gather near the water'
