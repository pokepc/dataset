## Mushroom Pokémon

Shroomish lives in damp soil deep in forests, where it often stays motionless under fallen leaves or
in the shade during the day. It feeds on compost made from rotted leaves, and is often seen feasting
in forest ground softened by long rain.

When it senses danger, it shakes its body and scatters poison spores from the top of its head. The
spores are toxic enough to cause pain if inhaled, and even make trees and weeds wilt.
