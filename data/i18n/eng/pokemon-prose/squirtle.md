## Tiny Turtle Pokémon

After birth, Squirtle’s back swells and hardens into a resilient shell, though it starts out soft.
If poked, the shell bounces back, and the Pokémon can draw its limbs and neck inside it when
threatened.

Squirtle uses that shell for more than protection: its rounded shape and grooves reduce water
resistance, letting it swim swiftly. In the water, it shoots at prey and responds to danger with
forceful bursts of water or foam from its mouth.
