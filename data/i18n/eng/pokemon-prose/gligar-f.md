## Fly Scorpion Pokémon

Gligar clings to cliffs and builds its nest on steep ledges, then spreads its wings to glide down at
prey. It moves through the air almost soundlessly, as if sliding, and often aims straight for a
foe’s face from above.

It seizes its target with its clawed hind legs and large foreclaws, startling it by covering its
face with its body before driving in the thick stinger on its tail. The venom it injects helps bring
down its prey while it is stunned with surprise.
