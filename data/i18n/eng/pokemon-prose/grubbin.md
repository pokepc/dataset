## Larva Pokémon

Grubbin uses its strong jaws to scrape trees for sweet sap, and it often digs nests into the forest
floor. It normally lives underground and feeds by using its big mandibles to cut into wood and slurp
out the sap.

It spits sticky thread to stop opponents, then clamps down with its sharp, sturdy jaws. Grubbin may
gather near electric Pokémon to stay away from bird Pokémon, and its large mandibles can even make
would-be predators back off.
