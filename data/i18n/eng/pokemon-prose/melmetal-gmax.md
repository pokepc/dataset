## Hex Nut Pokémon

Revered long ago for its power to create iron from nothing, Melmetal returned to life after 3,000
years for reasons unknown. Its massive hex-nut arms drive devastating punches with centrifugal
force, and it is said to strike harder than any other Pokémon.

When Melmetal nears the end of its life, it rusts and breaks apart. The small shards it leaves
behind will eventually be reborn as Meltan.
