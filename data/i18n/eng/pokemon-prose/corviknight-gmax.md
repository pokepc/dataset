## Raven Pokémon

With a steel body that gleams with a dark luster, this Pokémon commands the skies of Galar and can
strike fear into would-be foes. Its imposing presence and armored form make it seem like a ruler of
the air.

Thanks to its keen intellect and exceptional flying skill, it also serves the people of Galar well
as a reliable airborne taxi service, carrying passengers swiftly through the region.
