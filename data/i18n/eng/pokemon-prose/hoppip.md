## Cottonweed Pokémon

Hoppip drifts and floats with the wind, its body so light that it must cling to the ground with its
feet to avoid being blown away. It often gathers in clusters, especially when strong winds are near,
and links its leaves with other Hoppip for extra stability.

Despite that, it enjoys gentle breezes. When Hoppip appear together in fields and mountains, people
say spring is on the way.
