## Legendary Pokémon

Admired since ancient times for its beauty and regal bearing, Arcanine is often described in legends
as a magnificent, almost legendary Pokémon. Its grand mane, proud presence, and powerful bark have
long captivated people, while its graceful movement leaves onlookers enchanted.
