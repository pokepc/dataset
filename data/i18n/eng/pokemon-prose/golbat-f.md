## Bat Pokémon

Golbat flies through dark, moonless nights in search of prey, attacking without warning from the
shadows. Its four sharp fangs, which are hollow like straws, puncture even tough hides and let it
drink blood with startling ease.

It can gulp down over 10 ounces at once and keep draining its victim until it is full, even if it
grows too heavy to fly well. If it feeds too much, it may become clumsy in the air or even fall to
the ground.
