## Roly-Poly Pokémon

Togedemaru’s spiny back fur usually lies flat, but when it is startled or agitated, its 14 spikes
stand up on their own and can stab at attackers. In trouble, it curls into a ball and releases
electricity indiscriminately.

Its long back hairs act like lightning rods, drawing in electricity from lightning and even from
other Electric Pokémon. It stores that power in an electric sac, since it cannot generate much
electricity on its own.
