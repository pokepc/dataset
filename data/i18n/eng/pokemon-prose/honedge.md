## Sword Pokémon

Honedge is said to be born when a departed spirit inhabits a sword, and its soul once belonged to a
person killed long ago by the blade that became its body. It attaches itself to people and feeds on
their life force.

The blue eye on its handguard is its true body, and the old cloth it carries can wrap around a
person’s arm. If someone grabs its hilt, Honedge drains that person’s life energy completely.
