## Evolution Pokémon

Eevee has an unstable genetic makeup, with irregular DNA that is easily influenced by its
surroundings. Because of this, it can change form quickly and adapt to harsh environments by
altering its body to match them.

Radiation from special stones can trigger its evolution, and studies suggest it may develop into
many different species. This rare Pokémon is thought to hold the key to understanding Pokémon
evolution.
