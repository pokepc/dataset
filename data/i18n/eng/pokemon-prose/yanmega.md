## Ogre Darner Pokémon

Yanmega churns its wings to create shock waves that can inflict severe internal injuries. Its
wingbeats are so powerful that they can accidentally knock down full-grown trees when it takes off.

This six-legged Pokémon has incredible jaw power and attacks at high speed, often aiming straight
for an opponent’s most vulnerable spots. It can carry an adult in flight, with tail wings helping it
stay balanced, and it is famously violent and difficult to tame.
