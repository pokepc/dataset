## Plasma Pokémon

Rotom’s body is made of plasma, giving it the look of a strange will-o'-the-wisp powered by
electricity. Smart and mischievous, it loves to surprise others and is known to slip into electronic
devices or machines to take control and cause havoc.

Researchers still study Rotom, since it may even be the power source for a unique motor. Its unusual
abilities have inspired many different machines built to take advantage of what it can do.
