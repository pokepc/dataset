## Alpha Pokémon

It is told in mythology that Arceus was born before the universe even existed, and that it emerged
from an egg in a place where there was nothing before shaping the world. Legends also describe it as
the Pokémon that shaped the universe with its 1,000 arms.

In Hisuian mythology, it is the heavenly fount from which the light across Hisui pours, and its
radiance is said to guide and protect all Pokémon. As an Ice Type form, it changes type to match a
held Plate through Multitype.
