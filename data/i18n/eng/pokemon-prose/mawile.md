## Deceiver Pokémon

Mawile’s cute, docile-looking face helps it lull foes into letting down their guard. Then it strikes
with the huge jaws attached to the back of its head, which are actually transformed steel horns.

Its massive jaws are relentless and can even chew through iron beams. Mawile is also known to have
two mouths, using the back jaws to eat foods it does not like.
