## Scarecrow Pokémon

Cacturne lives in deserts and stays still during the day to avoid losing moisture under the harsh
sun. When night falls and the temperature drops, it becomes active and hunts prey that has been worn
down by the day’s heat.

It may also trail travelers through the desert in ragtag packs, waiting until exhaustion leaves them
unable to keep moving. After countless years in harsh desert conditions, even its blood has changed
to resemble sand.
