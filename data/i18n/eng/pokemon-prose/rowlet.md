## Grass Quill Pokémon

Rowlet stores up energy through photosynthesis during the day, then becomes active at night. It
flies silently on delicate wings, gliding close to targets before they notice, and can launch its
sharp feathers like blades from a distance.

If prey gets too close, Rowlet switches to fierce kicks with its strong legs. It also feels at ease
in tight, dark places and has been known to nest in a Trainer’s pocket or bag.
