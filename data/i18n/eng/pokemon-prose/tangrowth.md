## Vine Pokémon

Tangrowth swings long bundles of vines as if they were arms, using them to wrap up and ensnare prey.
When it stands still, its many vines make it look like a shrub, and in warm seasons they can grow so
thick that its eyes are hard to see.

Its vine arms are tough, and if they are cut, eaten, or torn off by predators, they grow back
quickly. In warm climates, the vines grow especially fast, and Tangrowth may shorten them by tearing
off excess growth.
