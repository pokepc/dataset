## Hearing Pokémon

Mega Audino has superb hearing and can detect even the faintest sounds around it, letting it
understand its surroundings with remarkable precision. Its ear feelers are especially sensitive, and
it uses them to sense heartbeats, emotions, and even when an egg is close to hatching.

By touching others with these feelers, Mega Audino can gauge both their feelings and physical
condition. Its gentle nature makes it a caring Pokémon that often checks on the well-being of those
around it.
