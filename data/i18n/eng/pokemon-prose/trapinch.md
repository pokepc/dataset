## Ant Pit Pokémon

Trapinch lives in arid deserts, where it digs a sloped, bowl-like pit in the sand that serves as an
inescapable trap. It waits patiently at the bottom for prey to tumble in, making its nest both a
home and a hunting pit.

Its giant jaws are strong enough to crush rocks and even boulders as it digs. Trapinch can endure
long stretches without water or food, but if it tips onto its back, its oversized head makes it hard
to right itself.
