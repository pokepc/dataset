## Genetic Pokémon

Created through harsh genetic manipulation and years of DNA experiments, Mega Mewtwo X was made from
Mew’s genes, yet its size and nature differ greatly from Mew’s. Though its body was shaped by human
science, it was not given a compassionate heart.

Its battle power was raised to an extreme level, and it is said to think only of defeating its foes.
To conserve energy, it often stays motionless until it can unleash its full strength in battle.
