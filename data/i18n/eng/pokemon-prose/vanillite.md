## Fresh Snow Pokémon

Vanillite is said to have been born from an icicle touched by the morning sun, and it sleeps buried
in snow. In warm places, it becomes smaller little by little, so it makes itself comfortable by
breathing out freezing air.

Its breath is about -58 degrees Fahrenheit. Vanillite can create ice crystals and cause snow to fall
around it, and its frosty touch is said to feel pleasantly cool, making it valued in warm regions.
