## Poison Moth Pokémon

Dustox is a nocturnal Pokémon instinctively drawn to light, often gathering in the glow of
streetlights, city lamps, or bonfires. At night, swarms may descend on roadsides and boulevards,
where they strip leaves from trees as food.

When Dustox flaps its wings, it scatters a fine, highly toxic powder or dust. If threatened, it
flutters violently to spread the poison around, and even the dust alone can make others ill. It also
uses its antennae to search for food like radar.
