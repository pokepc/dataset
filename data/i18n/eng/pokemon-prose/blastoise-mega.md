## Shellfish Pokémon

Mega Blastoise is a brutal Pokémon that relies on pressurized water jets and rocket-like cannons
built into its shell. It deliberately makes itself heavy so it can withstand the recoil when firing,
and it plants its feet firmly before blasting water at tremendous force.

Its water shots are highly accurate and can strike targets at long range, with enough power to punch
through thick steel. It can also use its heavy body to crush foes, and in a pinch it withdraws
inside its shell.
