## Spirit Pokémon

Yamask is said to be the spirit of a person from a bygone age, now wandering through graves and
ruins while still remembering its former life. It carries a mask that was once its human face, and
it sometimes gazes at it and cries.

By night, it roams through old ruins searching for someone who knows its face, as if hoping to be
recognized. Its lingering memories and sorrowful nature remain from the life it left behind.
