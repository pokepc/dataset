## Face Pokémon

Mega Glalie’s true body is a rock wrapped in a hard armor of ice formed from moisture in the air.
This armor will not melt, even in fire, and it can shape the frozen moisture around it as it wishes.

By exhaling cold air from its huge mouth, it can instantly freeze prey or foes solid. It then
devours the immobilized prey at leisure, and its icy body can form a dazzling cloud of diamondlike
crystals around it.
