## Prism Pokémon

Necrozma (Dusk Mane) is thought to have come from another world long ago, and it seems to have slept
underground for ages. It survives by absorbing light, and when it cannot feed on it, impurities
build up in its body until it darkens and stops moving.

Driven by an insatiable need for light, it rages in a pained state as it searches for more. Its
violent nature and constant laser beams make it extremely dangerous, as those beams can cut through
nearly anything.
