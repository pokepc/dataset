## Fly Scorpion Pokémon

Gligar usually clings to steep cliffs, building its nest there and hopping back along the ground
after gliding. When it spots prey, it spreads its wings and sails in silently, aiming straight for
the target’s face.

It startles foes by clamping onto them with its clawed hind legs and large foreleg pincers, then
uses the thick stinger on its tail to inject venom. It covers its victim’s face with its body before
striking, making its attack even more sudden.
