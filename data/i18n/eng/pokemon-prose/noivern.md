## Sound Wave Pokémon

Noivern flies through moonless nights, attacking careless prey from the dark with ruthless speed.
Its ultrasonic waves can reduce boulders to pebbles and weaken enemies before it closes in to finish
the fight with its fangs.

Aggressive and cruel, it is especially dangerous in darkness, where few can match it in battle.
Despite its violent disposition, it can become tame if given a ripe fruit it loves.
