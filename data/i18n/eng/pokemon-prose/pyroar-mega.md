## Royal Pokémon

The male with the largest mane of fire leads the pride, while the females protect the cubs. Mega
Pyroar’s fiery breath can exceed 10,000 degrees Fahrenheit, making it a fearsome threat to any
challenger.

Although males are usually lazy, they will throw themselves into danger to protect their companions
when faced with a strong foe. Pyroar does not use its searing breath on prey and instead prefers raw
meat.
