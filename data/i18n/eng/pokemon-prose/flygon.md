## Mystic Pokémon

Flygon is known as the Desert Spirit, or the elemental spirit of the desert, because the flapping of
its wings sounds like singing. The sound can be so enchanting that those who hear it may be drawn in
and left stranded in the desert.

By beating its wings, Flygon whips up powerful sandstorms and hides within the blowing sand. It is
often enveloped in the storm it creates while flying, and red covers help shield its eyes from the
desert grit.
