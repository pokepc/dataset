## Poison Bee Pokémon

Beedrill flies at high speed, using the poisonous stingers on its forelegs and tail to jab enemies
again and again. Its tail barb is especially potent, and it can strike, then quickly pull away after
attacking.

It is extremely territorial and should not be approached near its nest. When angered, Beedrill may
attack in a furious swarm, using its powerful poison stingers to overwhelm opponents.
