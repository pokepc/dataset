## Mouse Pokémon

Raichu stores electricity in its body, and when the charge builds up, it becomes feisty and more
aggressive. Its muscles are stimulated by the buildup, and if its cheeks run empty, it raises its
tail to gather electricity from the air.

Its long tail acts as a ground, letting it discharge excess power safely and preventing shocks. The
body gives off a faint glow in darkness, and if it becomes overcharged, it can unleash bursts of
more than 100,000 volts that can fell much larger foes.
