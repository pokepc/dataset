## Meditate Pokémon

Meditite trains deeply in the mountains, spending hours each day in meditation and yoga to
strengthen its inner and spiritual energy. Its practice is so strict that it survives on only one
berry a day, using hunger itself as part of its discipline.

As it sharpens its spirit through meditation, Meditite can even begin to levitate. Yet its focus is
fragile, and it often loses concentration while meditating, so its training never truly ends.
