## Transport Pokémon

Lapras is a gentle, intelligent Pokémon that can understand human speech and carry people or even
other Pokémon across the sea on its back. It is known for providing a comfortable ride and for
gliding smoothly over the water, with icy seas posing no problem for its cold-resistant body.
