## Big Jaw Pokémon

Mega Feraligatr opens its huge mouth to intimidate foes, then kicks hard with its thick, powerful
hind legs to rush at incredible speed. Though it is massive, it can still move quickly on land, even
if it must sometimes drop onto all fours to support its weight out of water.

Its jaws are enormous and powerful, and when it bites, it shakes its head to tear at its prey with
savage force. It may seem slow at first, but it can suddenly strike with blinding speed when
attacking.
