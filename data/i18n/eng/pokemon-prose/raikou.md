## Thunder Pokémon

Raikou is said to have fallen with lightning, and the rain clouds it carries let it fire
thunderbolts whenever it chooses. This rough Pokémon stores energy within its body before sweeping
across the land and shooting off electricity.

It embodies the speed of lightning, racing across the ground with a cry like crashing thunder. Its
roars send shock waves through the air and shake the earth as though bolts had struck nearby.
