## Magnet Pokémon

Magnemite is a bizarre Pokémon with a single eye set in an iron sphere and horseshoe-shaped magnetic
units on its sides. It stays aloft by emitting electromagnetic waves that block gravity, and the
faster those units spin, the stronger its magnetic force becomes.
