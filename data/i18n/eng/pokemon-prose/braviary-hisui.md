## Valiant Pokémon

Braviary is a proud, fearless warrior of the sky that fights for its friends without concern for its
own safety. It never retreats, even when injured, and its fierce battle cry and harsh temper make it
a formidable hunter.

Among its flock, scars can be a mark of honor, especially on the front of its body, and it is widely
respected as a heroic symbol. Its power is great enough to carry a car while flying and to strike
lakes with shock waves before snatching up prey.
