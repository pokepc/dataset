## Scaly Pokémon

Kommo-o uses the rigid scales covering its body as both armor and offense, and it fights valiantly
with its fists, especially through powerful uppercuts. Its steel-scaled tail can jingle loudly to
threaten foes, and weaker opponents may panic and flee at the sound.

It is said to battle only those that remain steadfast when faced with this display. After defeating
prey, its howl can draw the metallic celebration of its comrades from far and wide, and its scales
were once used to make weapons and other goods
