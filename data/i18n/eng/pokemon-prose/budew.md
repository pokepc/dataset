## Bud Pokémon

Budew is highly sensitive to changing temperatures. In winter, it closes its bud to endure the cold,
but when warm sunlight returns, it opens and blooms, releasing pollen as a sign that spring is near.

It lives beside clear ponds and pools, where it scatters pollen containing poison. In some places,
the pollen is said to cause violent sneezing and runny noses, and clean water can make its toxicity
even stronger.
