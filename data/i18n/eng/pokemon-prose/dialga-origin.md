## Temporal Pokémon

Dialga is a legendary Pokémon revered in Sinnoh and Hisui as an ancient deity. Myths say that time
began moving when it was born, and that the flow of time follows the beat of its heart.

It is said to completely control time, able to move at will through the past and future. In Hisuian
legend, its birth is remembered as the moment the great river of time began to flow in the world.
