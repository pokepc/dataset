## Disguise Pokémon

Mimikyu is a lonely Pokémon that hides its true appearance beneath an old rag, using the disguise to
seem less frightening and draw closer to people and other Pokémon. It even fashions the rag into a
Pikachu-like costume, though this often makes it seem even creepier.

It is quiet and wary, but it becomes agitated and violently resists if anyone tries to look beneath
the rag. Its actual form remains unknown, and stories of people who have seen it have ended in fear,
illness, or death.
