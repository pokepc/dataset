## Spot Panda Pokémon

Every Spinda is said to have a unique pattern of spots, and the odds of two sharing the same one are
incredibly small. Collectors even enjoy comparing the tiny differences between them.

Spinda moves with shaky, tottering steps that can look like a dance or the staggering of someone
dizzy. It thinks it is walking straight, but its unsteady motion can throw off foes’ aim, confuse
them, and help it evade attacks.
