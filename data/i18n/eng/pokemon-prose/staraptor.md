## Predator Pokémon

Staraptor has a savage nature and will boldly challenge foes far larger than itself. It leaves the
flock to live alone after evolving, having gained the strength to survive on its own.

Its wings and legs are powerfully muscled, with sturdy wings that let it fly while gripping small
Pokémon. Staraptor keeps attacking even when injured, and its fierce flight can carry off even
large, burly targets.
