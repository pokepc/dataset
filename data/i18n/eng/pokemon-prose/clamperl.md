## Bivalve Pokémon

Clamperl lives protected inside a rock-hard shell that also serves as a trap for prey. It clamps
down with both halves of the shell and holds on until its catch stops moving, and older Clamperl
often show nicks and scratches from this life.

As it grows, its body eventually becomes too large for the shell, which is a sign it is nearing
evolution. Once in its lifetime, Clamperl makes a single, highly prized pearl said to contain
mysterious energy and amplify psychic power when held.
