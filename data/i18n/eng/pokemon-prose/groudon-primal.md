## Continent Pokémon

Groudon is said to embody the land itself and to have raised continents by driving away rain clouds
and evaporating water with intense heat. Legends tell of its fierce battles with Kyogre, after which
it fell into a long sleep in underground magma.

In Primal Reversion, Groudon returns to its true form with nature’s full power. It can cause magma
to erupt and is said to expand the world’s landmass.
