## Blaze Pokémon

Blaziken attacks with martial-arts punches and kicks, using intense flames that burst from its
wrists to scorch its foes. When it faces a powerful opponent, the fire burns even more fiercely, and
its courageous style makes it press the attack without hesitation.

Its legs are incredibly strong, letting it leap clear over buildings and even clear a 30-story
building in a single bound. Every several years, its old feathers burn away and are replaced by new,
supple ones.
