## Dark Pokémon

Houndoom’s eerie howls make other Pokémon shiver and hurry back to their nests, and long ago people
feared those cries as the call of the grim reaper. Its flames are no less dreaded, since they are
mixed with toxins from its body and can cause burns that never stop hurting.

In packs, Houndoom settle their leader through fighting, with the one whose horns rake sharply back
taking the role. They also share their prey evenly among the group.
