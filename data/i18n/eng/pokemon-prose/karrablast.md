## Clamping Pokémon

Karrablast reacts strangely to electrical energy, and its evolution is triggered when it is near
Shelmet and receives that stimulation. It is often seen targeting Shelmet, and the two are closely
linked in the course of its evolution.

When threatened, Karrablast spits an acidic liquid to drive off attackers. It also uses that liquid
to melt through Shelmet’s shell, though it eats only the contents inside rather than the shell
itself.
