## EleFish Pokémon

Mega Eelektross crawls out of the ocean on its arms to seize prey on shore, then drags it straight
back into the water. Its sucker mouth lets it latch on tightly before it sinks its fangs in and
shocks the prey with powerful electricity.

Once it has hold of its target, escape is difficult. It clamps on with its mouth, bites down, and
delivers repeated electric jolts as it hauls the prey beneath the waves.
