## Tumbleweed Pokémon

Born when a restless spirit was carried by the wind and entangled with dried grass, Bramblin drifts
through the world as a wandering bundle of weeds. Not even Bramblin knows where it is headed as it
tumbles across the wilderness.

It is moved along by the wind and dislikes getting wet, which would likely weigh down its dry,
tangled body.
