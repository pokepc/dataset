## Mouse Pokémon

Raticate has whiskers it uses to keep its balance, and if they are cut or touched, it may slow down
or grow angry and bite. Its fangs keep growing, so it constantly gnaws on hard things to wear them
down, even chewing through walls, rocks, and concrete.

Its webbed hind feet work like flippers, making it a strong swimmer that can cross rivers and search
wide areas for food. In Alola, people say it used those feet to flee from enemies by swimming from
island to island.
