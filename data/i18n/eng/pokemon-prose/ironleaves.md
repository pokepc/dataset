## Paradox Pokémon

Many of Iron Leaves’ physical traits resemble those of a Virizion said to come from the future, as
described in a paranormal magazine. The few eyewitness accounts that exist suggest it has a
mysterious, otherworldly appearance.

Witnesses also say it uses its shining blades with extraordinary precision, slicing through large
trees and boulders as if they were being cut into fine strips.
