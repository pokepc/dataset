## Avianoid Pokémon

Sigilyph are said to have guarded an ancient city, and some believe they may have served as its
emissaries. They fly the same route without variation, as if their memories of that duty have never
faded.

They use their psychic power to fly and to repel intruders that enter their territory. Ruins that
may have belonged to that ancient city have been found beneath the desert sands where they are seen
flying.
