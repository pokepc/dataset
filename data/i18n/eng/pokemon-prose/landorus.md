## Abundance Pokémon

Landorus is hailed as the Guardian of the Fields, because lands it visits are blessed with bountiful
harvests. It draws on the forces of lightning and wind to create energy that enriches the soil,
helping crops grow large and abundant.

When the incarnations of wind and lightning clash, Landorus appears to quell the conflict. After the
storm has passed, the land is left fertile and ready for a rich harvest.
