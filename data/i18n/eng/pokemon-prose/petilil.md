## Bulb Pokémon

Petilil lives where the soil is moist, clean, and rich, often near fresh water or in well-tended
fields and flowerbeds. Places where it thrives are said to be especially good for growing plants,
and if the environment turns poor, it will move elsewhere.

Its head leaves are very bitter, but they grow back if they fall out and are valued for their
ability to restore tired bodies. Dried or boiled, they are used in remedies for fatigue, and the
darker the leaves, the healthier Petilil is thought to be.
