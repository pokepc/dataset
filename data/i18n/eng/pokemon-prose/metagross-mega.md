## Iron Leg Pokémon

Mega Metagross is formed by the fusion of two Metang, giving it four linked brains that can process
calculations with supercomputer speed and analyze opponents with remarkable accuracy. It relies on
psychic power and great strength to back foes into a corner.

When hunting, it can pin prey with its four legs and claws and then crush or chew it with the mouth
on its stomach. It can also float by tucking in its legs, and its magnetic power grows stronger in
freezing temperatures.
