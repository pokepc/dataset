## Slacker Pokémon

Slakoth spends over 20 hours a day lolling about or sleeping, and it is rarely seen in motion. Its
slow, motionless lifestyle means it needs very little food; three leaves can satisfy it for the day.

Its heart beats only once a minute, and it is content to loaf around no matter what happens.
Watching it can even make others feel drowsy or want to lie down too.
