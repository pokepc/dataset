## Arm Thrust Pokémon

Hariyama loves to challenge others to tests of strength and to match power with big-bodied
opponents. It practices its straight-arm slaps in many places, and a single blow can snap a
telephone pole, stop a train, or send a 10-ton truck flying.

Its thick body may look bulky, but it is packed with solid muscle. When it tightens every muscle,
its body hardens like rock. Despite its fierce strength, Hariyama is polite in battle, praising
opponents, and older ones focus more on training Makuhita.
