## Weather Pokémon

Castform’s appearance changes with the weather, as its sensitive cells react to temperature and
humidity. It can borrow the power of nature to shift into forms that match the sun, rain, or snow
clouds, and its form changes along with its type in battle.

For Sunny Form, that weather-linked change takes on a sunlit guise. Its form seems tied to chemical
reactions rather than its own free will, and its mood is said to grow rougher when the weather does.
