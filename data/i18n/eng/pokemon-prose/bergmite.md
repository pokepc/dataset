## Ice Chunk Pokémon

Bergmite lives in areas of frigid, perpetual snow on mountains, often in herds above the snow line.
It chills the air around itself to extreme temperatures, freezing water vapor into the ice it wears
as armor or a helmet for defense.

Its icy shield blocks attacks, and if the armor cracks, Bergmite repairs it with fresh ice made from
cold air. It can also freeze opponents solid, and some cling to the back of an Avalugg by freezing
their feet in place.
