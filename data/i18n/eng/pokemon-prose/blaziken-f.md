## Blaze Pokémon

Blaziken attacks with courage, blasting intense flames from its wrists when it faces a tough
opponent. The fiercer the foe, the hotter its wrists burn, and its punches leave enemies scorched
and blackened.

It also has incredibly strong legs, letting it clear a 30-story building in a single leap. It
studies martial arts that rely on punches and kicks, and its old feathers burn off every few years
as new, supple ones grow in their place.
