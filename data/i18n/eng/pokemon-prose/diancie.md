## Jewel Pokémon

Diancie is said to be a sudden transformation of Carbink, taking on a pink, glimmering form many
consider the loveliest sight in the world. Its appearance is dazzling and rare, making it stand out
as something almost impossibly beautiful.

It can create many diamonds at once by compressing the carbon in the air between its hands. This
remarkable power adds to its legend as a jewel-like Pokémon of extraordinary elegance.
