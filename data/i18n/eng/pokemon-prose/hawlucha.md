## Wrestling Pokémon

Although its body is small, Hawlucha’s skilled fighting allows it to stand up to powerful opponents.
It uses its wings to control its position in the air and often attacks from above, making its
strikes hard to defend against.

In battle, it darts around nimbly, targeting blind spots and using superb submission holds. It
overwhelms foes with quick moves, then builds to a flashy finishing move, though its tendency to
pose can sometimes leave it open to a counterattack.
