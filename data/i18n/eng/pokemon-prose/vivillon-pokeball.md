## Scale Pokémon

Vivillon with many different patterns are found all over the world, and this form’s Poké Ball
Pattern is one of the rare variants that does not correspond to any real-world region. Its pattern
is fixed and cannot be changed, remaining tied to how it was obtained.

Like other Vivillon, its wing pattern is influenced by climate and topography in the wild, and it
scatters colorful scales. Forms differ only in appearance, while the Poké Ball Pattern is available
only through special distributions.
