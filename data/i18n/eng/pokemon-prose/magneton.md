## Magnet Pokémon

Magneton is formed when three Magnemite link together by powerful magnetism. Its combined body emits
strong electrical and magnetic energy, and the three share one mind rather than becoming any
smarter.

It sends out strange radio waves and can raise the temperature in its vicinity. Groups may trigger
