## Drowsing Pokémon

Musharna is always drowsing and dreaming, and it can be very grumpy when first awakened. Its
forehead gives off a mist filled with the dreams of people and Pokémon, and that mist shifts into
many colors depending on what it has eaten in its dreams.

Using that mist, Musharna can form shapes from the dreams it has consumed. If dark mist pours from
its body, it is best to keep your distance, since nightmares may begin to come true.
