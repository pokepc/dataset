## Stag Beetle Pokémon

Vikavolt builds up electricity in an organ in its abdomen, then focuses that power through its
strong jaws to fire concentrated beams and powerful jolts at its foes. It can overwhelm bird Pokémon
with these shocking attacks, and when it spots an opening, it zips in quickly to strike.

Its acrobatic flying maneuvers help fluster opponents and create that opening for a beam attack. If
it carries a Charjabug as a spare battery, Vikavolt can unleash high-powered electrical beams even
more rapidly.
