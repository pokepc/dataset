## Spiny Nut Pokémon

Chespin has a thick shell of wood covering its head and back, making it tough enough to shrug off
even a direct hit from a truck. Its head quills are usually soft, but when it flexes them, their
tips become hard and sharp enough to pierce rock.

This defense gives Chespin a sturdy, armored appearance. Beneath that protective shell, its quills
can quickly stiffen into powerful points when it needs to strike.
