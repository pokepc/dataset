## Hostile Pokémon

Basculin are savage, violent Pokémon that usually cannot get along with one another and will start
fighting at once when they meet. Schools of them can overwhelm a lake, driving away everything
except Corphish and Crawdaunt, and anglers prize the fierce struggle they put up on the hook.

The White-Striped Form is noted as gentle, setting it apart in demeanor while still sharing many
traits with other Basculin. In the past, it was often served as food, and its meat was considered
fatty and especially popular
