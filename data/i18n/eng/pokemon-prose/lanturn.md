## Light Pokémon

Lanturn lives deep in the ocean, where its bright glow can rise from the depths and even illuminate
the sea’s surface from far below. At night, its light can make the water look like a starlit sky,
earning it the nickname “the deep-sea star.”

Its illuminated antennae and light-emitting body parts are formed from its dorsal fin and produce
light through a chemical reaction involving bacteria and its bodily fluids. Lanturn uses that sudden
flash to blind prey, sometimes following up with an electrical attack
