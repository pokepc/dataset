## Parent Pokémon

Kangaskhan raises its young in a protective belly pouch, where the infant usually stays until it is
about three years old. The mother lets the child out to play only when it feels safe, and keeps a
close watch nearby. If its young is threatened, Kangaskhan becomes fiercely enraged and will never
back down from a fight.
