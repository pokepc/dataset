## Aura Pokémon

Mega Lucario Z can sense the auras of all things and understand human speech. By reading the aura it
catches from others, it can tell how they feel, read their thoughts and movements, and even detect
beings it cannot see from over half a mile away.

It also gained the power to control aura, using precise waves in battle to strike prey or crush huge
rocks to dust. This keen awareness can leave it stressed by what it learns, and it trusts only
Trainers with justice in their hearts.
