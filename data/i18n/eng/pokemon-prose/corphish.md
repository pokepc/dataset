## Ruffian Pokémon

Corphish was originally brought over from distant lands as a pet, but many escaped or were released
and later established themselves in the wild. Hardy and resilient, it has spread widely and can
thrive even in polluted streams and filthy water.

It catches prey with its sharp claws and once its large pincers lock on, it will not let go.
Corphish is not picky about food and will eat almost anything, showing a strong will to survive in
harsh conditions.
