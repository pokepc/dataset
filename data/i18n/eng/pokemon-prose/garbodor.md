## Trash Heap Pokémon

Garbodor eats trash, and the garbage it consumes becomes new poisons inside its body. The exact
make-up of these toxins depends on what it has eaten, and the resulting liquid can be dangerously
virulent.

It uses its left arm or hand to pin opponents in place, then overwhelms them with foul-smelling
poison gas or by dousing their bodies with poisonous liquid from its right arm. Even a small amount
of that liquid can cause severe effects.
