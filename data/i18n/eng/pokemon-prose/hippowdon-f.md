## Heavyweight Pokémon

Hippowdon is surprisingly quick to anger and can turn violent when roused. It holds its huge mouth
open as a show of fearsome strength, and its jaws are powerful enough to crush a car.

It stores sand inside its body and can blast it from ports to whip up towering twisters and
sandstorms. It uses those whirlwinds to batter its foes and overwhelm them with swirling sand.

Stones can get stuck in the ports on its body, and Dwebble help dislodge them, so Hippowdon are
known to look after those Pokémon.
