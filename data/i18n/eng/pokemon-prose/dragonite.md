## Dragon Pokémon

Dragonite is a rarely seen, intelligent Pokémon said to live somewhere in the sea and to fly over it
with ease. Its powerful build lets it cross raging waters freely, and stories say it can circle the
globe in just 16 hours.

Kindhearted by nature, Dragonite is often said to rescue drowning people and Pokémon and to guide
lost ships and shipwrecked crews safely to land. Its calm manner can be deceptive, though, as it is
said to become devastating when angered.
