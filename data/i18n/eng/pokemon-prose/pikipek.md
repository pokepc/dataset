## Woodpecker Pokémon

Pikipek can peck at trees with its hard beak at a rate of 16 times a second, drilling holes it uses
for both food storage and nesting. Though it looks spindly, its neck muscles are powerful enough to
support this rapid pecking.

It feeds on berries, and the leftover seeds become the ammunition for attacks it fires from its
mouth. The rhythm of its pecking can also reveal its mood or condition.
