## Rock Inn Pokémon

Dwebble uses a liquid secreted from its mouth to bore holes in hard rock, letting it crawl inside
and make the stone its home. It first looks for a rock of suitable size, and when it finds one, it
opens a hollow in it to live in.

If its home rock breaks, Dwebble becomes agitated until it finds another. When no suitable rocks are
available, it may take shelter in the ports of a Hippowdon, and it is a natural enemy of Roggenrola
and Rolycoly.
