## Mega Fire Pig Pokémon

Mega Emboar can ignite its fists with the flame from its fiery chin, unleashing powerful fire
punches while relying on a wide range of mastered fighting moves. Its beard of fire flares when it
is fully fired up.

Despite its fierce strength, it cares deeply about its friends. The blaze around its face is both a
sign of its intense spirit and a mark of its many abilities.
