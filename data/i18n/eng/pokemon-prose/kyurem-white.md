## Boundary Pokémon

White Kyurem generates an intense freezing energy within itself, and when that power leaks out, its
body becomes frozen. It can release ultracold air, and its icy power is used to freeze its own body,
helping stabilize its cellular structure.

This legendary ice Pokémon is said to hold a power greater than that of Reshiram or Zekrom, though
extreme cold keeps it bound. It waits for a hero to complete the missing parts of its body with
truth or ideals.
