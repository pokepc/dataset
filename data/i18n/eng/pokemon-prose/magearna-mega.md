## Artificial Pokémon

Constructed more than 500 years ago by a scientist, Mega Magearna has a mechanized body that serves
only as a vessel. Its true life-form is the Soul-Heart, an artificial soul that gives it
consciousness and allows it to understand human speech, though it cannot speak itself.

By synchronizing its consciousness with others, it can sense their feelings. This makes it
especially helpful in caring for people.
