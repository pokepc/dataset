## Sea Slug Pokémon

Shellos lives along seashores and other bodies of water, where its body shape and coloration shift
with its habitat and even the quality of its food. In Sinnoh, two forms are known, and warm ocean
waters are thought to influence this variation.

Its squishy body can ooze a mysterious purple fluid when it is handled roughly or senses danger. The
sticky mucus is harmless but hard to wash off, and Shellos can also crawl toward people with
surprising friendliness.
