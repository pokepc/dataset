## Constraint Pokémon

Meowstic’s ear interiors bear eyelike patterns that emit psychic energy, and it keeps them tightly
covered because that power is overwhelming. When it is threatened, it raises its ears and unleashes
enough force to grind a 10-ton truck into dust.

Female Meowstic is treated as a separate form for practical purposes, alongside the male. Unlike its
counterpart, it normally hides the patterns inside its ears, revealing them only when it needs to
release its full psychic power.
