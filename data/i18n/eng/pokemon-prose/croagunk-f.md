## Toxic Mouth Pokémon

Croagunk has poison sacs in its cheeks and can inflate them to make an odd, frightening sound that
startles foes. It waits for openings, then catches opponents off guard with toxic fingers or a
poison jab, especially when they flinch.

It rarely fights fairly, but only to survive. Its poisonous fluid can be diluted for medicinal use,
and its peculiar cry and comical features have made it a popular mascot.
