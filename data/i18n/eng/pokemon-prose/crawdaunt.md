## Rogue Pokémon

Crawdaunt is a brutish, rough-tempered Pokémon that loves to battle and will fiercely drive away any
intruder with its giant pincers. It often pinches Pokémon that enter its territory, tosses them out
of its pond, and even crashes into foes that approach its nest, leaving the waters it claims
strangely desolate.
