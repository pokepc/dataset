## Land Spirit Pokémon

Tapu Fini is the guardian deity of Poni Island, and it manipulates water with power said to create
pure water that can wash away impurity. It is both feared and revered, for it lives deep within a
thick fog that surrounds it.

The fog it produces can bring ruin to confused enemies, and those who recklessly approach it may
suffer terrible calamities. Ocean currents are said to be the source of its energy.
