## DNA Pokémon

Deoxys was born when the DNA of a space virus changed after exposure to a laser beam. Said to have
come from beyond Earth, it is highly intelligent and uses psychokinetic powers, while the
crystalline organ on its chest seems to serve as its brain.

It can fire lasers from that organ, and its body is known to shift between forms that change its
abilities. When it changes form, it may even alter its appearance to mislead foes, and an aurora can
appear as it does so.
