## Single Horn Pokémon

Mega Heracross is usually docile, but when disturbed while sipping honey or searching for sweet tree
sap, it drives intruders away with its prized horn. Proud of its strength, it uses powerful legs and
sharp claws to brace itself, then charges straight in to slip beneath an opponent and hurl it with
tremendous force.
