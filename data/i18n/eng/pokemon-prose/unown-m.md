## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is always found stuck to walls, and its shape appears to carry
some meaning. Its form resembles ancient writing or hieroglyphs, leaving it a mystery whether human
language or Unown came first.

Different Unown shapes are said to have different abilities, and they seem to communicate
telepathically among themselves. When one is alone, nothing happens, but if two or more gather, an
odd power is said to emerge.
