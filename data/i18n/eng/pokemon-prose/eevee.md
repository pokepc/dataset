## Evolution Pokémon

Eevee has an unstable genetic makeup that makes it highly adaptable. Its irregular genes are easily
influenced by its surroundings, and changes in the environment can trigger it to evolve into many
different forms.

Because of this rare and unbalanced genetic code, Eevee can alter its body to suit harsh conditions
and hide many possible evolutions. Radiation from special stones is also known to cause it to
change.
