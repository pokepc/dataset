## Sea Lily Pokémon

Lileep is an ancient Pokémon restored from a fossil that once lived in warm seas about 100 million
years ago. It clings permanently to rocks on the seafloor, using powerful suckers that keep it from
being washed away, even in rough surf.

From its immobile perch, it watches for prey with its two eyes. By swaying its petallike tentacles
or disguising them as seaweed or flowers, it lures nearby prey close before swallowing them whole.
