## Sky Squirrel Pokémon

Emolga lives in treetops and glides on its cape-like membrane, storing electric energy in its cheeks
and releasing it as it moves through the air. While fluttering, it crackles with electricity and may
shock friends and foes alike, which keeps bird Pokémon away and helps it keep its food to itself.

It is especially fond of sweet berries, sometimes stuffing its cheeks so full that it can hardly
fly. It may even grill berries and bug Pokémon with shocks before eating them, and it often nests in
tree holes.
