## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally's hidden strength awakens through a deep bond of trust with its Trainer. Once its control
mask is destroyed and it evolves into this form, it becomes far faster and can change its type at
will, showing remarkable flexibility in battle.

Freed from the heavy mask it once wore, this Pokémon has fully awakened. Its true power emerges only
when it trusts its partner completely, and that bond is the final key to releasing its strength.
