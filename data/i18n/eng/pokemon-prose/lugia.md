## Diving Pokémon

Lugia is said to be the guardian of the seas, appearing when storms begin or on stormy nights. It
has an incredible ability to calm raging storms, yet its power is so great that it stays hidden deep
beneath the sea.

Its wings are devastatingly powerful; even a light flutter can destroy houses. Because of that
strength, it is said to sleep in a deep-sea trench, out of sight from the world above. It is also
rumored that when it flaps its wings, a long storm can follow.
