## Donkey Pokémon

Mudbray eats dirt to make mud, then smears it over its hooves to improve its grip and keep its
powerful running gait steady on rough ground. It is skilled at using mud to move without slipping,
even in difficult terrain.

This Pokémon has a stubborn, individualistic nature and spends its days eating dirt, making mud, and
playing in the mire. It needs to be covered in mud every day, or it becomes stressed and may stop
listening to its Trainer.
