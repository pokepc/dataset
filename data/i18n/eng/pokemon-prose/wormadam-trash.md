## Bagworm Pokémon

When Burmy evolves, its cloak becomes part of Wormadam’s body and is never shed. Its form is set by
the cloak it had at evolution, and it can no longer change afterward.

Wormadam’s appearance reflects the materials it absorbed and can vary by where it evolved. The Trash
Cloak form has different stats and movepools from the other forms, and some are said to develop a
thicker cloak if they evolve on a cold day.
