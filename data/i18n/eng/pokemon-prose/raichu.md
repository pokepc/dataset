## Mouse Pokémon

Raichu stores electricity in its cheek pouches, and when they are fully charged, its ears stand
straight up. If its reserves run low, it raises its long tail to draw power from the atmosphere,
then uses that same tail as a ground to protect itself from its own high-voltage charge.
