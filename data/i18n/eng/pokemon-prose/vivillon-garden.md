## Scale Pokémon

Vivillon’s wings display a pattern shaped by the climate and topography of its habitat, and it
scatters colorful scales as it flies. Around the world, Vivillon can appear in many different
patterns, with this form reflecting the Garden Pattern.

The form is purely a matter of appearance. Its pattern is fixed by the region tied to its original
Trainer and cannot be changed, even if it is traded or the system region setting is altered.
