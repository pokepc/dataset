## Plasma Pokémon

Fan Rotom’s body is made of plasma, and it can slip into machines to take control and cause
mischief. It is smart and likes to surprise others, making it a troublesome presence wherever it
appears.

This strange Pokémon has also drawn research interest because of its unique capabilities and its
possible use as a power source for special motors. By inhabiting appliances and other devices, Rotom
has inspired the development of many different machines.
