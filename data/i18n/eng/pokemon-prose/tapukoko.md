## Land Spirit Pokémon

Tapu Koko is the lightning-wielding guardian deity of Melemele, and it is said to appear before
people from time to time. Brimming with curiosity, it summons thunderclouds and stores their
lightning inside its body.

It flies so quickly that it can confuse enemies before they can track its movements. Though usually
a guardian, it has a hair-trigger temper and may become a malevolent attacker if angered by a person
or Pokémon.
