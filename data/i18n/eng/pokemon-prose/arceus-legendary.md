## Alpha Pokémon

Arceus is spoken of in mythology as the Pokémon that came before the universe and emerged from an
egg in emptiness to shape the world. Legends of Sinnoh say it formed all that exists, and some
stories credit it with 1,000 arms.

In Hisui, it is revered as the heavenly source of light that shines across the land, guiding and
protecting all Pokémon. Its form changes with a held Plate, allowing its type to match through
Multitype.
