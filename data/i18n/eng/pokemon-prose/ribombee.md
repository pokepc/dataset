## Bee Fly Pokémon

Ribombee rolls pollen and nectar into puffs, making many varieties with different effects depending
on the ingredients and their proportions. Some are nutritious enough to be sold as supplements,
while others are used in battle.

It hates rain and anything that would make its pollen damp. When it senses ominous clouds, it hides
in a hollow tree and waits motionless. Ribombee can also predict the weather from moisture and wind,
and it is usually seen only after several clear days in a row.
