## Cotton Candy Pokémon

Swirlix’s fluffy fur carries the sweet smell of cotton candy, and its body feels sticky and sugary
from a diet of sweets. It eats its own weight in sugar every day, and if it does not get enough, it
becomes incredibly grumpy.

In battle, Swirlix can extrude white, sticky threads that are as sweet as cotton candy. It uses
these strings to tangle up its opponents.
