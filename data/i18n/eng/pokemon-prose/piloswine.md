## Swine Pokémon

Piloswine is covered in a thick, shaggy coat that helps it endure freezing weather, with long fur
that can even hide its eyes and limbs. Its tusks are used to dig food out from under ice, and in
snowy conditions they can grow thicker. With excellent smell, it can locate buried mushrooms and
other food beneath frozen ground.
