## Wood Gecko Pokémon

Grovyle lives in dense jungles and thick forests, where the leaves growing from its body help hide
it from enemies. It is a master climber and moves through trees with great skill, leaping from
branch to branch as it closes in on prey.

Its strongly developed thigh muscles give it astounding agility and jumping power. Grovyle can dart
through the canopy so swiftly that, in a forest, almost nothing can catch it once it starts fleeing.
