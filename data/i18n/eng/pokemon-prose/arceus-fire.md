## Alpha Pokémon

In Sinnoh and Hisui legend, Arceus is said to have appeared before the universe existed, emerging
from an egg in emptiness and then shaping the world. Myth also credits it with the power to shape
the universe with its 1,000 arms.

As the heavenly source of light in Hisui, it is believed to pour out a radiance that guides and
protects all Pokémon. This Fire Type form changes with a held Plate, matching its type through
Multitype.
