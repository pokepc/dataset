## Nebula Pokémon

Cosmog has a frail, gaseous body that can be blown about by even a gentle breeze, yet it seems
unconcerned by its own vulnerability. It slowly grows by gathering dust from the atmosphere, and its
curious nature often leads it into danger.

Long ago, it was known as the child of the stars, and some say it came from another world or even
another universe. When trouble arises, Cosmog can warp or teleport away to a safer place to hide.
