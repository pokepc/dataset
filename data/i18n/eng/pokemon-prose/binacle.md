## Two-Handed Pokémon

Two Binacle share a single rock, adhering to it after finding one of a suitable size. They cooperate
to gather food when the tide is high, and if their partnership breaks down, both their offense and
defense suffer.

They stretch out and then contract to yank their rock along in bold hops, feeding on seaweed washed
onto the shore. If the two quarrel, one may move to another rock, leaving the pair split apart.
