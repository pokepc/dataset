## Head Butt Pokémon

Rampardos is an ancient Pokémon built around a thick, iron-hard skull and a powerful head butt.
Repeated headbutts made its skull even harder over time, while its brain stayed small, and two of
them colliding would scarcely be stunned by the impact.

It can smash down massive trees while hunting prey, and its head has been known to shatter even
durable objects. Ancient people even used fossils from this Pokémon to make helmets, since its skull
was harder than steel.
