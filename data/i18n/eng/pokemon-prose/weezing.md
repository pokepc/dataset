## Poison Gas Pokémon

Weezing lives by feeding on poison gas, dust, germs, and fumes from garbage, and it grows as it
absorbs them. It is especially drawn to rotted kitchen trash and may make its home in a dirty house,
raiding the garbage while people sleep.
