## Rabbit Pokémon

Buneary keeps its ears rolled up, and the way it does so can reveal its mood. When it is scared,
both ears stay curled, while danger makes it perk up and remain alert. Its ears are also believed to
guard its keen hearing from too much sound.

By sharply uncoiling those ears, Buneary can strike with surprising force, even shattering boulders
or smashing them to dust. It can also use that stored power to bound high, sometimes surprising
itself. If both ears are rolled up, it may be a sign that it needs care.
