## Tiny Bird Pokémon

Pidgey is a very docile Pokémon commonly seen in forests, woods, grassy areas, and tall grass. It
usually avoids conflict and searches for food such as small bugs while staying hidden among the
plants.

When threatened, it relies on quick wingbeats close to the ground to kick up sand or dust, creating
a cloud that can drive enemies back and even flush out insect prey. It is also known for an
excellent sense of direction and can return unerringly to its nest, no matter how far away it has
been taken.
