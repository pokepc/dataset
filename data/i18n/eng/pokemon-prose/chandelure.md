## Luring Pokémon

Chandelure haunts dilapidated mansions, where it sways the flames on its arms to draw opponents into
a hypnotic trance. Its eerie fire is said to consume spirits, leaving only their bodies behind.

Those burned by its ominous flame are thought to lose their way and wander this world forever. In
homes lit by Chandelure instead of lamps, funerals were said to become a constant occurrence.
