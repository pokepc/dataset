## Fox Pokémon

Ninetales is a highly intelligent, long-lived Pokémon with gleaming golden fur and nine long tails,
each said to hold supernatural power. Legends say it may have been formed from noble saints or
sacred wizards, and that it can live for a thousand years.

light and hypnotize foes. Its flames can leave opponents dazed before it attacks, and touching one
