## Shadow Pokémon

Mega Gengar lurks in shadows, and a sudden chill often signals its presence. It may seem to become a
person’s own shadow, slipping in unnoticed to wait for a chance to steal heat, cast a curse, or take
life force from its target.

On dark nights and under a full moon, it is said to make shadows move on their own and laugh. It can
even appear in rooms or mountain darkness, lurking where prey will not notice until it is too late.
