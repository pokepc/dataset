## Iron Will Pokémon

Cobalion is a calm, composed legendary Pokémon with a body and heart of steel. From the moment it is
born, it gives off a natural air of leadership, and even vicious foes can be calmed by its presence.

Its glare is said to be enough to make unruly Pokémon obey. In legend, it fought alongside Terrakion
and Virizion against humans to protect Pokémon, punishing those who harmed them in defense of the
Unova region.
