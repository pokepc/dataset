## Gloomdweller Pokémon

Marshadow can conceal itself in any shadow, slipping into the shadows of people and Pokémon alike.
Hidden there, it observes them, understands their feelings, and copies their movements and powers.

It once went undiscovered for a long time, and its existence was thought to be only a myth. By
shadowing martial arts masters, it has learned ultimate techniques, becoming stronger as it improves
beyond those it imitates.
