## Sea Weasel Pokémon

Buizel keeps its head above water by inflating the flotation sac around its neck like a collar,
letting it watch for movement on the surface. When it dives, the sac collapses.

It propels itself through the water by spinning its two tails like a screw, moving with ease and
even slicing through clinging seaweed. Its forked tail resembles a steamboat screw, though only by
coincidence.
