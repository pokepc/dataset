## Land Spirit Pokémon

Tapu Bulu is a guardian deity of Ula’ula that controls plants, causing vegetation to flourish before
drawing energy from the growth. It can pull up large trees by the roots and swing them as weapons.

Though it often avoids unnecessary conflict by making ringing sounds with its tail to signal its
location, it can be violent toward those it considers enemies. It may command plants to immobilize
foes before striking them with its horns.
