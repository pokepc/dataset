## Mantle Pokémon

Born deep underground and found in fissures, Roggenrola has a hexagonal ear cavity that helps it
follow sounds. If the noise stops, it can panic and topple over.

Its body is compressed rock that is as hard as steel, though a long soak in water can soften it a
little. An energy core inside keeps it slightly warm, and it grows angry if anyone puts a hand into
its ear.
