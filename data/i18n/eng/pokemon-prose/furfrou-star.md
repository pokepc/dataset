## Poodle Pokémon

Furfrou’s Star Trim shows off carefully shaped fur that makes it look more elegant and helps it move
more quickly. Its coat can be styled into exquisite forms, and in Kalos, aristocrats once competed
to create the most impressive trim.

Its fur keeps growing if left alone, but it will only let someone it trusts cut it. In earlier
times, these Pokémon were even chosen as guardians of the king.
