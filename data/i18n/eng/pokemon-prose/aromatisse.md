## Fragrance Pokémon

Aromatisse creates a variety of scents, from sweet aromas that can strengthen its allies to foul
ones that can weaken an opponent’s fighting spirit. It uses these smells strategically in battle,
releasing whatever scent will give it the advantage.

The fragrance that constantly pours from its fur is so overpowering that companions may eventually
lose their sense of smell. Even walking beside it can be difficult unless a Trainer truly enjoys the
scent.
