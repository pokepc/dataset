## Collective Pokémon

Mega Barbaracle is formed when two Binacle multiply into seven, bringing the strength of seven
Binacle to battle. Its seven limbs are controlled by a head that gives the orders, though the hands
and legs can also move on their own when needed.

With eyes on each palm, it can keep watch in every direction. In a tight spot, its limbs act
independently to help secure victory, even as they usually follow the head’s commands.
