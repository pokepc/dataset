## Carefree Pokémon

Ludicolo begins dancing as soon as it hears cheerful, festive music, and it is said to appear when
it hears children singing on hiking outings. The sound of a bright rhythm stirs its body, making its
cells and muscles surge with energy.

Once it starts moving in time with the music, Ludicolo grows far more powerful. Even in battle, it
keeps dancing and faces tough opponents without hesitation.
