## Dopey Pokémon

Slowpoke is slow-witted and oblivious, content to loll around without worrying about time. It often
loafs at the water’s edge, lost in vague thought, and may not notice if its tail is bitten or even
eaten for a long while.

It dips its tail in water to lure and catch prey, though it is so forgetful that it may abandon the
task and drift off. Sweet sap seeps from the tail, making it pleasant to chew, and the tail grows
back if it breaks off.
