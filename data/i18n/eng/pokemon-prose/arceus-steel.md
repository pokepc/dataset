## Alpha Pokémon

Arceus is said in mythology to have existed before the universe and to have emerged from an egg in a
place where nothing existed, then shaped the world. Legends also tell of it as the Pokémon that
formed all things with its 1,000 arms.

In Hisui, it is revered as the heavenly source of light that shines across the land, guiding and
protecting all Pokémon. Its form changes with a held Plate to match its type through Multitype.
