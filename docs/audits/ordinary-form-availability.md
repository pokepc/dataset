# Ordinary and battle form availability audit

Reviewed **2026-09-22**. Apply the [canonical field definitions](../pokemon-availability.md). These
are researched acquisition decisions for the current main-series games, not permission to copy every
field from a species to all of its forms. GO remains independently parsed from its exact
form/release rows. Future games and Champions roster support require separate evidence.

## Battle transformations

For the following forms, the underlying Pokémon's acquisition classification can be reused in a
supported main game because the transformation is available through ordinary battle mechanics. This
includes transfer-only classification when an imported base is required. It does **not** copy the
base's storage or HOME/Bank acquisition: the temporary state does not remain in boxes or transfer as
a separate Pokémon. GO may implement the same appearance as a permanent form, an independent
release, or a different battle mechanic.

LZA's lack of Abilities is therefore not a blanket reason to reject a transformation. Its
[battle mechanics](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_battle_(Legends:_Z-A)>)
specifically preserve Aegislash, Mimikyu, and Morpeko mechanics. Species absent from LZA must remain
absent; these exceptions do not grant LZA support to the other families.

### Darmanitan needs a Hidden Ability check

Unovan and Galarian Zen Mode require the **Zen Mode Hidden Ability**, rather than the ordinary
ability of every individual. Both are battle-only. Their current main-game classifications can match
the respective regional base, with the following evidence and prerequisites:

| Games                          | Route / limit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Black/White                    | The static Desert Resort Darmanitan have Zen Mode.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Black 2/White 2                | N's Darmanitan has Zen Mode. This encounter requires Memory Link; retain that prerequisite in the explanation instead of claiming every wild Darumaka supplies the Hidden Ability. This audit follows the main table's native-encounter classification rather than creating a new blanket policy for save-data connectivity. |
| X/Y                            | The species itself requires trade; a qualifying Hidden Ability individual must be imported.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ORAS                           | DexNav can supply Darmanitan with Zen Mode, so a prior-game import is not required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Sun/Moon, Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon | Import a qualifying individual; no native species route.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Sword                          | Unovan Darumaka from Regina's Isle of Armor trade has Inner Focus and evolves into Zen Mode Darmanitan. Galarian individuals have native routes; Ability Patch also supplies the Hidden Ability.                                                                                                                             |
| Shield                         | Retain the regional base's external acquisition classification. Ability Patch can enable Zen Mode after the required individual is present.                                                                                                                                                                                  |

Sources:
[Darmanitan locations and form data](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Darmanitan_(Pok%C3%A9mon)>),
[released Hidden Abilities in generation V](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_with_released_Hidden_Abilities_(Generation_V)>),
[released Hidden Abilities in generation VI](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_with_released_Hidden_Abilities_(Generation_VI)>),

No Darmanitan form is supported in Scarlet/Violet or LZA. Do not infer GO Zen releases from either
Standard Mode release or the main-game transformation.

## Two justified GO identity aliases

These are narrow identity mappings backed by research, not permission for general species fallback:

- **Xerneas Active:** the normal Xerneas release also supplies its automatic Active appearance.
  There is no separate GO release to wait for. The
  [GO form-differences reference](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_with_form_differences_in_Pok%C3%A9mon_GO#Xerneas)
  identifies Active in battles/capture encounters and Neutral in other contexts.
- **Low Key Gigantamax Toxtricity:** both ordinary Toxtricity styles share the same Gigantamax
  appearance. Crucially, release of both is independently confirmed: the official
  [GO Fest 2025 Max Finale schedule](https://pokemongo.com/gofestmaxfinale) lists Amped Gigantamax
  on August 23 and Low Key Gigantamax on August 24. Mapping both dataset Gmax records to the shared
  released Gigantamax row is justified.
  [Toxtricity form data](<https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Toxtricity_(Pok%C3%A9mon)#Form_data>).

All other GO results must continue to come from their exact parsed GO rows. A species page's GO
stats, assets, or move list alone does not establish that a form has been released. This audit does
not change shiny-release fields or claim to verify every game's storage/Champions roster.
