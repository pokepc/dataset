## Bat Pokémon

Zubat makes its home in dark caves and other places where sunlight does not reach. During the day,
it hangs upside down and sleeps in colonies, sometimes gathering together to keep warm.

Its eyes are undeveloped or absent, so it relies on ultrasonic cries from its mouth to sense
obstacles and find its way while flying. Those sound waves let it move skillfully through even
tight, pitch-black spaces.

Sunlight can burn its thin skin and make it unhealthy, so it avoids the sun and stays hidden until
nightfall
