## Apple Core Pokémon

Applin spends its entire life inside an apple, where it hides from bird Pokémon by posing as nothing
more than the fruit itself. It rarely leaves this shelter, relying on its disguise to stay safe from
natural enemies.

From the moment it is born, it burrows into an apple that becomes both its home and its food source.
The apple’s flavor even influences how it will evolve.
