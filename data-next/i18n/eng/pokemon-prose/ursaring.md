## Hibernator Pokémon

Ursaring is a skilled climber despite its large body, and it often lives among towering trees. It
spends much of its time in the treetops, eating and sleeping there, though it will also snap trees
with its forelegs to reach fallen berries.

In its territory, it marks trees that bear delicious berries or fruits and wanders its forest every
day in search of food. With a keen sense of smell, it can find food buried deep underground. When
cold weather comes, it may roam fields and mountains in a fierce search,.
