## Bee Fly Pokémon

Cutiefly feeds on nectar and pollen, often hovering around flowers and seeking out their pollen. It
can sense auras and even tell which flowers are about to bloom, helping it find food at just the
right time.

By reading the auras of living things, Cutiefly can predict a foe’s next move and slip around
attacks before striking back. It is sometimes seen fluttering above people with flowerlike auras,
and it may clash with other Pokémon over pollen.
