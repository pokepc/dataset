## Gratitude Pokémon

Shaymin in its Sky Forme lives among flower patches and can hide by curling up to resemble a
blooming plant. When Gracidea flowers bloom, it gains the power of flight, and it is said to deliver
a message of gratitude.

Its body flowers burst into bloom when it is hugged with affection and senses thanks. It can also
dissolve toxins in the air, turning ruined ground into a lush field of flowers, and appears when
thankful people bring winter to an end.
