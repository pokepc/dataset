## Judo Pokémon

Throh tightens its belt to grow stronger, and wild ones weave their own belts from vines. As they
train through many battles, the belt darkens as it absorbs sweat, and they replace it as they become
stronger.

Throh is skilled at throwing moves and likes to hurl opponents bigger than itself. It also trains in
groups of five, and any member that cannot keep up will discard its belt and leave the group.
