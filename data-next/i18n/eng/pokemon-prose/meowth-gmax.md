## Scratch Cat Pokémon

Meowth is most active at night, when it prowls streets and cities in search of loose change and
other shiny, round things. It loves coins and glittering objects, often picking them up to add to a
secret hoard, and its eyes or forehead coin may gleam when it spots something bright.

It moves quietly by drawing its sharp claws into its paws, and by day it tends to lie low or sleep.
It regularly washes its face to keep the coin on its forehead spotless, though it can be fickle and
hard to keep as a friend.
