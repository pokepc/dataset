## Weed Pokémon

Gloom’s mouth oozes a sticky honey or nectar that is not drool at all. It is sweet, clings
stubbornly when touched, and is used to attract prey, though its odor is so awful it can curl noses
from far away.

Its flower pistils also give off a foul fragrance, and the stench grows worse when it is threatened.
When Gloom feels calm and secure, it does not release its usual reek, but its noxious scent can
still make opponents faint, and a few people oddly enjoy it.
