## Floating Pokémon

Frillish wraps its veil-like arms around prey swimming by, using invisible poison spikes to paralyze
them. Once a foe is stunned, it drags the helpless victim down into the depths of the ocean,
sometimes to lairs far below the surface.

Legend says the residents of a sunken ancient city became these Pokémon. Their victims are drawn
ever deeper, where they may never return.
