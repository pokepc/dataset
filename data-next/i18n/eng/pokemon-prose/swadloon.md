## Leaf-Wrapped Pokémon

Swadloon lives in dark, damp forests, where it spends the day moving about and eating fallen leaves.
It wraps itself in leaves to protect against the cold, staying tucked away while it forages.

By feeding on leaf litter, Swadloon helps enrich the soil. Forests where it lives are said to grow
especially lush and healthy, with superb foliage nourished by the nutrients it produces.
