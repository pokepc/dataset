## DNA Pokémon

Deoxys is said to have mutated from a space virus after exposure to a laser beam, and it is highly
intelligent, with a crystalline organ on its chest that seems to serve as its brain. It can also
fire lasers from that organ and wield psychokinetic power.

In its Defense Forme, Deoxys alters its cellular structure to absorb attacks and may change its
appearance to confuse foes. It is an alien Pokémon associated with auroras, appearing where they are
seen.
