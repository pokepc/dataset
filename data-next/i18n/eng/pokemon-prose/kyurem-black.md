## Boundary Pokémon

Black Kyurem generates a powerful, freezing energy within itself, and the intense cold has frozen
its body in place. It can produce ultracold air, and that icy power seems to help stabilize its
cellular structure.

Though it holds power greater than Reshiram or Zekrom, the extreme cold keeps it bound. Kyurem can
fuse with Zekrom to become Black Kyurem, with the other Pokémon disappearing until the fusion is
undone.
