## Formidable Pokémon

Hard of heart and deft of blade, this rare form of Samurott is a product of evolution in Hisui. Its
front-leg seamitars can be drawn and sheathed in an instant, and one swing of the sword built into
its armor can fell an opponent.

A mere glare from this Pokémon can quiet a crowd. Its turbulent blows crash into foes like ceaseless
pounding waves, and its cry alone is enough to intimidate most enemies.
