## Trap Pokémon

Stunfisk’s skin is very hard, so even being stepped on does it no harm. It hides in seashore mud and
waits motionless until prey comes close, then shocks them with a sudden jolt of electricity.

It can emit strong currents from the yellow markings on its body, and it seems to smile slightly
while doing so. If an opponent cannot be paralyzed, it contorts with surprising speed and flops
away.
