## Ember Pokémon

Fletchinder scatters embers from its beak into tall grass or prey dens, driving out frightened
Pokémon so it can pounce with its sharp talons. It even grills its catch at high heat before eating
it.

Its flame sac must build up heat before it can fly at full speed, but once it takes off, it is among
the fastest bird Pokémon. Fletchinder is highly territorial and aggressive, defending feeding
grounds over a wide area.
