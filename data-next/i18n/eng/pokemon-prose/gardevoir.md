## Embrace Pokémon

Gardevoir can sense the future, and its power grows strongest when it is protecting a trusted
Trainer. If danger threatens, it unleashes its psychokinetic energy at full force, even risking its
own life.

By supporting itself with psychic power, it seems to defy gravity. In extreme devotion, it can
distort space and create a small black hole to shield the one it has come to adore.
