## Wild Duck Pokémon

Farfetch’d is almost always seen carrying a plant stalk, and it treats that stalk as both a weapon
and something worth defending with its life. It uses the stalk much like a sword, and different
individuals have been observed using it in distinct ways.

It lives where reedy plants grow and is rarely seen, leading to concern that its numbers are
declining. The stalk can also serve as emergency food, but if it eats it, Farfetch’d will go off in
search of a new one. It may even fight others over a choice stalk
