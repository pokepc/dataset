## Humming Pokémon

Mega Altaria soars gracefully through the sky among billowing, cloudlike wings, often blending into
white clouds on sunny days. It sings and hums in a beautiful soprano, and its clear melodies can
leave listeners feeling dreamy wonderment.

This Pokémon is known for its calm, relaxed flight and kind disposition. If it bonds with a person,
it may gently wrap them in its soft wings and hum, but when provoked, it answers with shrill cries
before attacking fiercely.
