## Single Bloom Pokémon

Floette flutters through flower fields and tends blossoms that are beginning to wilt, giving its own
power to flowers with heartfelt care. It is said to draw out the hidden power of flowers in battle,
and the more beautiful a bloom is, the stronger it becomes.

When a well-kept flower bed comes into bloom, Floette may appear to celebrate with an elegant dance.
It is especially protective of flowers and never forgives anyone who ruins a flower bed.
