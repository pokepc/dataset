## Alpha Pokémon

In mythology, Arceus is said to have been born before the universe existed, emerging from an egg in
a place where there was nothing. Legends tell that it then shaped the world and all that exists
within it.

As the heavenly source of light in Hisui, its radiance is said to guide and protect all Pokémon.
Arceus is revered as the creator of all things, and its form changes with a held Plate to match its
type.
