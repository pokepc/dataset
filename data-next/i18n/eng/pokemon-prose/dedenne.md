## Antenna Pokémon

Dedenne’s whiskers act like antennas, letting it send and receive electrical waves to communicate
over long distances. Its upper whiskers sense its surroundings, while the lower ones fire
electricity.

Because it cannot generate much power on its own, Dedenne steals electricity from outlets, power
plants, or even other Electric Pokémon, storing it in its tail before blasting it from its whiskers.
Dedenne also uses those signals to share the locations of food or electricity with others of its
kind.
