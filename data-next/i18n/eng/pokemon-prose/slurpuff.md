## Meringue Pokémon

Slurpuff has an extraordinarily keen sense of smell, far surpassing a human's, and can detect the
faintest scents to learn about its surroundings. It even uses this ability to help pastry chefs, as
if it can see with its nose.

By taking in a person's scent, it can gauge their physical and mental condition, a talent thought to
have promising medical uses. Its fur is packed with air, making it soft to the touch and lighter
than it looks.
