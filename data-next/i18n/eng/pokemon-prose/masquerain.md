## Eyeball Pokémon

Masquerain intimidates foes with the eyelike patterns on its antennae, which can make it look angry.
If the eyespots droop and seem sad, it is said to signal that heavy rain is coming.

Its four wings let it hover and fly in any direction, even sideways and backwards, with surprising
agility. However, its wings and antennae absorb moisture easily, so it waits out rainy days in tree
hollows or under trees and eaves, then faces the sun to dry off.
