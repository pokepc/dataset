## Iron Armor Pokémon

Mega Aggron claims a mountain as its own territory and patrols it relentlessly, striking down
anything that intrudes. It is fiercely defensive of that land, yet also restores it after damage,
hauling soil and planting trees when disaster strikes.

Its steel horns are sharp enough to punch through thick iron, and it uses them to ram opponents and
tunnel through bedrock in search of iron. The length of its horns and the scars on its armor reflect
its age and the battles it has survived.
