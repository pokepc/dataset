## Pincer Pokémon

Kingler has an enormous, overgrown claw that is as hard as steel and incredibly powerful, with
crushing force of 10,000 horsepower. Yet its size makes the pincer unwieldy, difficult to aim, and
hard to lift without losing balance.

It waves the huge claw to communicate with others, but the weight quickly tires it. Outside of
battle, the oversized limb often just gets in the way.
