## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally’s hidden power awakens through the trust it shares with a Trainer, and that bond allows it
to change its type at will. Once its control mask is broken and it evolves beyond its former state,
it is freed to move with much greater speed.

The strength inside Silvally only emerges when its partnership is strong enough. In this form, its
true power is released by the final key of trust, and it is known by the name it received after
evolving.
