## Loyal Dog Pokémon

Herdier is a loyal, intelligent Pokémon that faithfully follows its Trainer’s orders and helps raise
and care for other Pokémon. It is said to have lived alongside people for so long that images of it
appear in ancient cave art, and some even think it may have been the first Pokémon to partner with
humans.

Its dense black fur forms a cape-like covering that grows continuously. The more it grows, the
harder and springier it becomes, reducing damage and making it difficult for claws and fangs to bite
