## Poodle Pokémon

Trimming its fluffy fur makes Furfrou more elegant and also helps it move more swiftly. In Kalos,
these Pokémon were once trusted as guardians of the king, and nobles later competed to see whose
trim was the most exquisite.

Its fur grows longer and longer if left alone, but it will only let someone it trusts cut it. This
Diamond Trim differs only in appearance and can be changed at any time; it returns to its Natural
Form after five days unless trimmed again or deposited in the PC.
