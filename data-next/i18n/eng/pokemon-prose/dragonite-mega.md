## Dragon Pokémon

Mega Dragonite is an extremely rarely seen marine Pokémon said to live somewhere in the sea and fly
over the open ocean. With its impressive build, it can soar over raging waves without trouble and
circle the globe in just 16 hours.

It is known for its humanlike intelligence and kindhearted nature. Legends tell of it guiding lost
ships to shore and rescuing drowning people or Pokémon, and sailors have even called it the sea
incarnate. Though usually calm, it can become devastating when angered.
