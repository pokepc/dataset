## Triple Bird Pokémon

Dodrio uses its three brains to make complex plans, though it may overthink things and freeze up. If
one head eats, the others are satisfied too, and when its three heads look in different directions
it is on guard and may peck at anyone who comes near.

This rare species evolves from Doduo when one of its heads splits in two. It races across grassy
plains at nearly 40 mph, even in dry seasons, and is said to have three sets of hearts and lungs,
letting it run long distances without rest.
