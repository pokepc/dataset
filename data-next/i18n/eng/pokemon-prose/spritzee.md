## Perfume Pokémon

Spritzee emits a fragrance that can enrapture those who smell it, and the scent it produces changes
depending on what it has eaten. A pouch inside its body lets it create a variety of aromas, with
diet influencing the fragrance it wafts.

In former times, noble and royal ladies cherished Spritzee and even carried it instead of perfume so
they could enjoy the scent it gave off.
