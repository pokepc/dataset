## Fossil Pokémon

Mega Aerodactyl is a ferocious Pokémon from the age of dinosaurs, restored from amber and said to
have ruled ancient skies. It flew freely and fearlessly through prehistoric air, sometimes gliding
overhead while shrieking high-pitched cries.

Its sawlike fangs are built to tear at prey and even shred skin to tatters. Though its body is a
relic of the distant past, it remains vicious and powerful, with a savage nature that modern
technology cannot perfectly restore.
