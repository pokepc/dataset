## Scaly Pokémon

Hakamo-o sheds and regrows its scales continuously, and each new set becomes harder and sharper than
the last. It clangs its scales together to make noise, building up to a fierce attack, while its
scaly punches and sharp claws can shred opponents.

Its courage shows when it leaps at prey with a shout, and the scaleless, scarred parts of its body
are signs of strength that it proudly displays to defeated foes. The scales it sheds can even be
molded into heat-conducting pots and pans.
