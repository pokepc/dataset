## Freeze Pokémon

Articuno is a legendary bird Pokémon said to appear to doomed travelers lost in icy mountains, and
it is often associated with snowy peaks riddled with permafrost. Its beautiful blue, seemingly
translucent wings are said to be made of ice, and its long tail trails gracefully behind it as it
flies.

By chilling the air with the flapping of its wings, Articuno can control ice and freeze moisture in
the atmosphere. As it flies, it is said to create snow and even blizzards, turning the winter sky
white.
