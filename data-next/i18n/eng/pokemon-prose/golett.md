## Automaton Pokémon

Golett was fashioned from clay by an ancient, mysterious civilization, and it has remained active
for thousands of years. A strange energy burns within it and powers its movement, though no one has
been able to identify the source.

It is said to still carry out orders given to it many thousands of years ago. Some Golett are also
known to endlessly line up boulders, as if following a purpose from long ago.
