## Jewel Pokémon

Carbink was born deep underground under intense heat and pressure, and it has slept there for ages.
It is sometimes uncovered during cave excavations, where its glittering, jewel-covered body quickly
attracts attention.

It defends itself by firing powerful beams from the gems in its body. Reports also say that Carbink
live in large underground packs, where they polish one another’s jewels with their soft manes and
may even dwell with a queen in a hidden kingdom of gemstones.
