## Royal Pokémon

Among Pyroar, the male with the largest mane of fire leads the pride. It is usually lazy, but when
threatened by a strong foe, it will defend its companions without regard for its own safety.

Pyroar’s breath exceeds 10,000 degrees Fahrenheit, and it uses that fierce heat to threaten
challengers rather than prey. It prefers to eat raw meat, while the females protect the pride’s
cubs.
