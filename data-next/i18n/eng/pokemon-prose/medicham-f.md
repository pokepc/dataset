## Meditate Pokémon

Through meditation and harsh yoga training, Medicham sharpens its sixth sense and heightens the
energy within its body. It can sense what others are thinking, read an opponent’s aura, and even
predict a foe’s next move.

Its movements are elegant and dance-like, letting it avoid attacks and counter in the same motion
with devastating force. Medicham is also said to meditate for a whole month without eating, and it
can hide its presence by blending with fields and mountains.
