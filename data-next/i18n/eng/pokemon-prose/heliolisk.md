## Generator Pokémon

Heliolisk flares its frill to generate energy, and a single one can produce enough electricity to
power a skyscraper. When basking in the sun, it spreads its frill wide and can generate even more
power, enough to supply a city.

It also uses electricity to stimulate its muscles, strengthening its legs and letting it dash 100
yards in five seconds. A vanished desert culture prized these Pokémon, and treasure was said to come
with them when they arrived in Galar.
