## Plasma Pokémon

Frost Rotom is a bizarre Pokémon with a plasma-like body that can slip into certain machines and
take control, causing all kinds of mischief. It is clever and loves to surprise others, and people
have long been wary of its habit of wreaking havoc in electronic devices.

Research into Rotom continues because of its unusual abilities, and some believe it could even power
a unique motor. Its strange nature has also inspired the development of many different machines that
make use of what Rotom can do.
