## Tiny Raccoon Pokémon

Zigzagoon is endlessly curious, wandering restlessly and zigzagging about as it takes interest in
everything it sees. It rubs its nose along the ground as it searches, and its zigzag path is often
marked by the footprints it leaves behind.

It is skilled at finding items in grass and even underground. This Galarian form is friendlier and
calmer than the kind native to Galar, and it marks its territory by rubbing its bristly fur on
trees. It may also play dead to fool foes in battle.
