## Mach Pokémon

Garchomp is built for overwhelming speed on land and in the air. Its body is covered in fine scales
that cut drag, and when it folds up its body and spreads its wings, it resembles a jet plane. It can
fly at sonic speed, and its sharp wing motion can whip up blades of wind strong enough to fell
trees.
