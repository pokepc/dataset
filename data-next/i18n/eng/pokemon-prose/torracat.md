## Fire Cat Pokémon

Torracat has a fire bell at its throat, and it rings brightly whenever it spits flames. In battle,
this fire pouch can ring like a bell and burn hotter than usual, especially when Torracat faces a
strong opponent and its fighting spirit rises.

It has powerful front legs and can bend an iron bar with a single punch. When it grows close to its
Trainer, it may act spoiled, and its sharp claws can leave scratches. Its standing mane shows when
it feels good, while flat fur can signal that it is unwell.
