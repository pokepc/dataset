## Triple Bird Pokémon

Dodrio is a rare three-headed Pokémon that evolved when one of Doduo’s heads split again. It races
across grassy plains and prairies at nearly 40 mph, and its extra hearts and lungs help it run long
distances without rest.

Its three brains let it make complex plans and gather information more wisely, though it may
overthink and become immobile. The heads can work together so that if one eats, the others are
satisfied, and if they look in different directions, it is a sure sign they are on guard.
