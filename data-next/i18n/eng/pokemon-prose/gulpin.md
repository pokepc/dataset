## Stomach Pokémon

Virtually all of Gulpin’s body is devoted to its stomach, leaving its heart and brain very small by
comparison. With that oversized stomach and its harsh digestive fluids, it can swallow prey or
objects as large as itself and dissolve nearly anything it takes in.

Its stomach’s contents are powerful enough to break down even scrap iron. As it digests, Gulpin
gives off vile, overpowering gases.
