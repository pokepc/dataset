## Aqua Mouse Pokémon

Marill’s round, oil-filled tail acts like a float or life preserver, keeping it from drowning even
in strong or vicious currents. If only its tail is bobbing on the surface, it is usually diving
below to feed on aquatic plants.

Its body is covered in water-repellent fur, so it stays dry and can swim quickly without being
slowed by the water. When feeding near fast streams, it may wrap its flexible tail around a tree
trunk for support.
