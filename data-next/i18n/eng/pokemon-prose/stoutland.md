## Big-Hearted Pokémon

Stoutland is a wise, good-natured, and valiant Pokémon that people trust on rescue teams. It rescues
people stranded by blizzards in the mountains or even at sea, and its shaggy, long fur protects it
from the cold. Being wrapped in that warm coat can be so comfortable that someone could spend a
wintry night outdoors.
