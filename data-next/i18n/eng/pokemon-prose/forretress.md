## Bagworm Pokémon

Forretress is encased in a steel-hard shell that hides its inner body, and even when it opens to
catch prey, the motion is so quick that no one has seen what lies inside. Its peering eyes are often
the only visible clue to the mystery within.

It usually remains fixed to a tree trunk, and when something comes near, it scatters or fires
fragments of its shell to drive enemies away. This defensive burst is described as a conditioned
reflex, making its shell both armor and weapon.
