## Puppy Pokémon

Growlithe is friendly and faithful to people, but it becomes fiercely protective when anything
enters its territory. It will bark, bite, and even roar to drive away intruders, and its loyalty
makes it fearless in defending its Trainer from harm.

It is brave and trustworthy, standing up to stronger foes without hesitation. Growlithe has a superb
sense of smell, remembers every scent it encounters, and can use that sharp nose to detect the
emotions of others.
