## Dancing Pokémon

This Oricorio has sipped red nectar, and its passionate dance can win over its enemies before it
lashes out with blazing flames. As it moves through its beautiful steps, it beats its wings together
to create fire and bathe foes in intense heat.

If its Trainer gives the wrong order, this passionate Pokémon becomes fiercely angry. Its dance is
both graceful and dangerous, drawing opponents in and then burning them up when they let their guard
down.
