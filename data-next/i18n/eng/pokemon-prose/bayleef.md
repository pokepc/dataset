## Leaf Pokémon

Bayleef’s neck is ringed by curled-up leaves that give off a spicy aroma. The scent from these
leaves can perk people up and make them feel energetic.

Inside each tubular leaf is a small tree shoot, and its fragrance is said to act as a stimulant that
restores health. The aroma is so invigorating that it can even stir some listeners into a fighting
spirit.
