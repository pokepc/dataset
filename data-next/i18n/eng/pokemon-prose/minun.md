## Cheering Pokémon

Minun is devoted to cheering on its partners, even more than to its own safety. It releases the
electricity in its body as bright showers of sparks, using them like pom-poms to encourage teammates
in battle.

When a partner is in trouble or losing, Minun lets off even more sparks. It dislikes water and will
shelter under the eaves of houses when it rains. Together with Plusle, its electricity is said to
help promote circulation and relax muscles.
