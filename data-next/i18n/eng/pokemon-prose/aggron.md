## Iron Armor Pokémon

Aggron claims an entire mountain as its territory and patrols it vigilantly, driving off any
intruders with relentless force. Its iron horns grow longer with age, and the scratches and gouges
in its armor stand as proud marks of battle.

While seeking iron for food, it breaks through bedrock and digs tunnels with its steel horns. Those
horns are sharp enough to pierce thick iron sheets, and Aggron often brings down foes by charging
horn first.
