## Mouse Pokémon

Mega Raichu X stores powerful electricity in its body, and when it builds up, it becomes feisty and
more aggressive. The charge also makes it glow faintly in the dark, and its muscles seem to be
stimulated by the overflow.

Its long tail acts as a ground, protecting it from its own high-voltage power. When its cheek
pouches run low, it raises its tail to draw electricity from the atmosphere, and if it becomes
overcharged, it plants its tail in the ground to discharge safely.
