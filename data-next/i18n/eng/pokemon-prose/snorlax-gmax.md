## Sleeping Pokémon

Snorlax spends most of its time eating and sleeping, and its huge appetite leaves it satisfied only
after consuming nearly 900 pounds of food a day. It is so lazy and docile that it may doze off while
eating, and its breathing or hungry rumbling can sound like a cry.

Its stomach is famously powerful, letting it eat moldy, rotten, or even poisonous food without harm.
