## Balloon Pokémon

Qwilfish inflates itself by swallowing a great amount of water, using the pressure to fire its toxic
quills all at once from all over its body. Its round shape makes swimming somewhat difficult, but it
can also swell up to match a larger opponent's size.

The small spikes covering its body developed from scales, and they inject a toxin that can cause
fainting. Fishers dislike this troublesome Pokémon because its poisoned spines spray everywhere.
