## Aura Pokémon

Lucario senses the auras of all things and can understand human speech. By catching the aura emitted
by others, it can read their thoughts, movements, and feelings from more than half a mile away, even
detecting foes it cannot see.

It also controls aura in battle, shaping those waves with great precision. Its power can pulverize
large rocks, and it may use it to hunt in packs. Lucario is said to be stressed by the thoughts it
picks up, and only Trainers with justice in their hearts can earn its trust.
