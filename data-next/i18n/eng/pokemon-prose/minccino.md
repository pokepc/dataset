## Chinchilla Pokémon

Minccino is a tidy Pokémon that keeps its tail well groomed and clean. It greets others by rubbing
them with its tail, and the fluffier tail is often a point of pride.

It is a fastidious cleaner that dislikes even the slightest mess, sweeping and dusting with its tail
like a broom or mop. If its tail gets dirty from cleaning itself or its nest, it may spend a whole
day washing it in clean spring water.
