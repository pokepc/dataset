## Five Star Pokémon

Ledyba is very timid and grows uneasy when it is alone, but becomes active in a group. It clusters
with others for warmth when the weather turns cold, and its back pattern varies slightly from one to
another.

It communicates by secreting scented fluid where its legs join its body, using changes in the odor
to share its feelings. That scent can even turn sour when it is angry. In warm places, it appears
especially lively.
