## Loud Noise Pokémon

Exploud is covered with sound-generating organs and tubes that let it produce whistles, howls, and
other noises. It communicates by changing the tone and volume of its cries, but it raises its voice
most fiercely in battle.

Its battle roar can shake the ground like an earthquake, and its loud bellowing has been known to
create tremors. When it draws in air through the ports on its body, it is preparing to unleash a
huge cry that can be heard from more than six miles away.
