## Cricket Pokémon

Kricketot shakes its head back and forth so its antennae strike together, making a bright,
xylophone-like sound. It uses these colliding antennae to chat with others, and the sounds are a
familiar sign of autumn.

Its legs are short, so it stumbles often, and each misstep makes its stiff antennae clack. As the
trees change color, more Kricketot appear, and the tones they make can even resemble a marimba.
