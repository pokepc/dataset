## Ball Whale Pokémon

Wailmer stores seawater inside its body, filling out like a big, bouncy ball. The more water it
swallows, the higher it can bounce, and on sunny days it may come onto beaches to play this way.

Its nostrils sit above its eyes, and it likes to surprise people by forcefully snorting or spraying
the water it has stored. Though it usually lives at sea, it can survive on land for a short time,
but it quickly loses vitality if it dries out. It also eats a great deal of food every day.
