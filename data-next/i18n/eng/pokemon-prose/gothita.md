## Fixation Pokémon

Gothita is always staring at something, and its attention can be so fixed that it may fail to notice
attacks. It intently observes Trainers and Pokémon alike, as if watching something only it can see.

Even as a baby Pokémon, it stores psychic power in its ribbonlike feelers, which strengthen that
power and can be used in battle. It can also be heard making a muted cry when no one seems to be
nearby, as though speaking to an unseen presence.
