## Karate Pokémon

Sawk trains in the mountains with single-minded dedication, working without sleep in pursuit of the
strongest karate chop. Its punches against boulders and trees can echo far off, and a Sawk pushed to
its limit is said to be able to cleave the ocean itself.

Tying its belt pumps it up and makes its punches even more destructive. It dislikes being disturbed
during training, so it is best to quietly pass by when one is focused on its workouts.
