## Polka Dot Pokémon

Azurill lives by water, where it often gathers at the edge on sunny days to splash and play. Its
large, rubbery tail is packed with nutrients needed for growth, and it uses that bouncy tail like a
ball to support itself on land and even as a flotation aid in water.

Normally docile, Azurill can move quickly by bouncing on its tail, but when angered it swings the
oversized tail around to battle opponents bigger than itself. It may even spin the tail like a lasso
and launch itself forward with surprising 힘.
