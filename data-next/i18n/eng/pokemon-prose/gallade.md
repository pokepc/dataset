## Blade Pokémon

Gallade is a courteous, honorable warrior that fights with blades extending from its elbows. Its
self-taught swordsmanship is astonishingly impressive, and the blades are said to be sharper than
the finest swords.

It senses what its foe is thinking, allowing its attacks to strike first with speed and force. True
to its protective nature, it uses those blades only to defend someone or to aid those in need in
battle.
