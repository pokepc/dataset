## Fresh Snow Pokémon

Glaceon can lower its body temperature at will, freezing the moisture around it and creating
glittering diamond-dust flurries. The sudden cold can also turn nearby air into powdery snow, making
it a striking sight in wintry places.

When threatened, Glaceon freezes its fur into sharp needles or icicle-like spines, which it can use
to protect itself or strike prey. Its icy display can be so beautiful that those who watch too long
may not notice the danger until it is too late.
