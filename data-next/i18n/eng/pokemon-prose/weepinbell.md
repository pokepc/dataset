## Flycatcher Pokémon

Weepinbell appears to be a plant, but it captures unwary prey by showering it with Poison Powder
before spraying acid to finish the attack. Its leafy parts act like sharp cutters, and if prey is
too large to swallow whole, it slices it up first.

When hungry, it will swallow anything that moves, dissolving its catch with strong acids. It also
oozes a protective fluid that keeps it from being harmed by the acid inside. At night, it hooks onto
a tree branch to sleep, but if it shifts in its sleep, it may fall
