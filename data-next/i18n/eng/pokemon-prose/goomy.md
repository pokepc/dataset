## Soft Tissue Pokémon

Goomy is the weakest Dragon-type Pokémon, and it lives in cool, shady places where the air stays
damp. Its body is mostly water, so if its skin or slime dries out, it quickly becomes lethargic and
may even die.

A slimy membrane coats its body and helps attacks slide off harmlessly. Its sensitive horns act as
powerful sensors, and when they detect danger, Goomy hides away at once to stay safe.
