## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is always found stuck to walls, and its shape seems to carry some
kind of meaning. Its form resembles ancient writing or hieroglyphs, leaving it a mystery whether
human language or Unown came first.

Different forms of Unown are said to have different abilities, and they may communicate
telepathically with one another. When one is alone, nothing seems to happen, but when two or more
are together, an odd power is said to emerge.
