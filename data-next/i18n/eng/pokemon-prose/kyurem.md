## Boundary Pokémon

Kyurem generates a powerful freezing energy within itself, but the energy leaked out and left its
body frozen. It can produce ultracold air, and the extreme cold also seems to keep its own cellular
structure stable while binding a power greater than that of Reshiram or Zekrom.

This legendary ice Pokémon is said to wait for a hero to fill in the missing parts of its body with
truth or ideals. It may also be able to freeze itself in order to control that immense power.
