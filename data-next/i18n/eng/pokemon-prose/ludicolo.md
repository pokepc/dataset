## Carefree Pokémon

Ludicolo begins dancing as soon as it hears cheerful, festive music, and it is even said to appear
when it hears children singing on hiking outings. The sound of a bright, upbeat rhythm makes its
body’s cells become lively and active, filling its muscles with energy.

Once stirred by festive music, Ludicolo moves in rhythm to amplify its power. Even in battle, it
becomes especially vigorous and does not back down when facing a tough opponent.
