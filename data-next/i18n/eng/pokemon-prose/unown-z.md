## Symbol Pokémon

Unown’s flat, thin body is usually found stuck to walls, where its shape seems to carry some kind of
meaning. Its many forms resemble ancient writing or hieroglyphs, and it remains a mystery whether
these markings or Unown came first.

These Pokémon are said to communicate telepathically, and when several are together, an odd power
may emerge. Forms only change Unown’s appearance, leaving its nature the same.
