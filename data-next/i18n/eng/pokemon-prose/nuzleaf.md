## Wily Pokémon

Nuzleaf lives deep in densely overgrown forests, often in holes bored into large trees, and is
skilled at climbing them. It sometimes wanders out to startle people, especially those who are lost
in the woods.

It can pull off the leaf on its head and use it to make a flute. The eerie sound of that grass flute
makes listeners uneasy, fearful, or filled with dread. Nuzleaf’s long, pointed nose is a weak point,
and it loses power if the nose is gripped.
