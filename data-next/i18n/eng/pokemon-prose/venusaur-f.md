## Seed Pokémon

Venusaur’s large flower blooms as it absorbs solar energy, so it keeps moving to seek sunlight. By
spreading its broad petals, it catches the sun’s rays and turns them into strength, becoming
especially powerful in summer.

Its flower can take on vivid colors when it has enough nourishment and sunlight. The sweet fragrance
drifting from it soothes people, calms battle-hungry foes, and can grow stronger after rain, drawing
other Pokémon in.
