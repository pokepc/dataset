## Scratch Cat Pokémon

Meowth is a nocturnal Pokémon that sleeps through the day and becomes active around dusk, roaming
streets or territory at night with its eyes glinting. It silently tucks away its claws to move
without a sound, and if it spots something shiny, it cannot resist picking it up.

It is especially drawn to round, sparkling objects and coins, which it often hoards. Meowth
regularly washes its face to keep the coin on its forehead spotless, and when in a good mood it may
even show a Trainer its treasures.
