## Shedding Pokémon

Scraggy has rubbery, durable skin that it can pull up to its neck to soften blows, and its more
stretched skin is seen as a sign of respect. It is proud of its sturdy skull and will suddenly
headbutt anything that meets its gaze, though its heavy head makes it a bit unsteady.

It often moves in small, lazy groups, but anyone who locks eyes with it should be careful: its
reckless headbutts are hard to escape. It is thought to evolve once its skin has stretched out
completely.
