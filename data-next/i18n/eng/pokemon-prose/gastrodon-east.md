## Sea Slug Pokémon

Gastrodon’s soft, boneless body is highly resilient, and if it is torn or bitten, it can regenerate
the missing parts within hours. In ancient times, its back was covered by a sturdy shell, and today
only a thin vestigial plate remains. It lives in shallow tidal pools and feeds mainly on plankton
and beach sand.
