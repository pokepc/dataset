## River Crab Pokémon

Krabby lives in burrows dug into sandy beaches and can be found near the sea. On shores where food
is scarce, it may squabble with others over territory.

Its large pincers are powerful weapons and also help it keep its balance when it walks sideways.
They break off easily in battle or if torn away, but they grow back quickly.

When food is hard to find, it can swallow sand to absorb nutrients. If danger approaches, it cloaks
itself in bubbles from its mouth to make itself look bigger.
