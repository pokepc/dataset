## Royal Heir Pokémon

Restored from a fossil, Tyrunt is an ancient Pokémon that lived 100 million years ago. It has a
violent, spoiled nature and likes to be pampered, but if something upsets it, it throws a tantrum
and runs wild.

Its hefty jaws are incredibly powerful, able to tear apart objects and even chew up an automobile.
When it tries to play, it can badly injure others, including its Trainer.
