## Puppy Pokémon

Growlithe is friendly and faithful to people, but it is very protective of its territory and will
bark, bite, or roar at intruders. It is brave, trustworthy, and loyal enough to stand up to stronger
foes and protect its Trainer from harm.

Its superb sense of smell lets it remember every scent and even sense the emotions of others. Once
it has grown used to people, it can be quite affectionate, though it remains alert and fierce when
guarding its space. It has lived alongside humans since ancient times.
