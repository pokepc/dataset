## Toxic Mouth Pokémon

Toxicroak’s knuckle claws secrete a deadly toxin, and even a scratch can be enough to bring down an
opponent. The poison is produced in sacs in its body and carried through tubes in its arms, then
churned to greater potency when it croaks before battle.

It fights by swaying and dodging to get close, then bouncing in to gouge foes with its poisonous
claws. After a victory, it lets out a booming croak, while the toxin in its crimson claws can also
be diluted and processed into a tonic.
