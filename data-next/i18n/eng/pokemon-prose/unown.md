## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is shaped like ancient writing or hieroglyphs, and its many forms seem to resemble letters and
symbols from old tablets. It is still a mystery whether the ancient scripts inspired Unown or the
other way around.

Its flat, thin body is usually found stuck to walls, where its shape appears to carry some meaning.
Unown are said to communicate telepathically, and when two or more gather, an odd power is said to
emerge.
