## Ore Pokémon

Boldore’s body holds such great energy that orange crystals form where it leaks out, and these
crystals can glow when power overflows. The crystals are dense masses of energy, hard enough to
smash diamonds and valuable enough to attract attackers.

It explores caves in search of underground water, though it is uneasy around water and drinks
carefully. Boldore monitors its surroundings by sound, and when angered it can attack without ever
changing the direction it faces.
