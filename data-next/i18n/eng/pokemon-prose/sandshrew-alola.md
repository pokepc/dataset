## Mouse Pokémon

Sandshrew lives in deep burrows in dry, sandy places with little rainfall and little water. It
usually comes out only to hunt, and it even uses the sand to bathe itself and shake off dirt and
moisture. When night turns cold, its dry hide may gather a light dew.

Its body is built for arid life, with skin that absorbs water without wasting it and hardens into a
tough shield. When threatened, it quickly curls into a ball, protecting its soft underside and
letting blows bounce away as it rolls or drops safely.
