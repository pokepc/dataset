## Legendary Pokémon

Admired in ancient Eastern legends, Arcanine is celebrated for its beauty, grace, and proud, regal
bearing. Its grand mane and majestic bark have long captivated people, and stories say even its
movement across the prairies could draw crowds.

Arcanine is also famed for its remarkable speed, said to let it cover over 6,200 miles in a day and
night. The fire blazing within its body powers that relentless run, while its foes are met with
flaming fangs and quick, deceptive feints.
