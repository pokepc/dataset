## Hex Nut Pokémon

Revered long ago for its ability to create iron from nothing, Melmetal has somehow returned to life
after 3,000 years. It is said to deliver the strongest punches of all Pokémon, using the centrifugal
force of its heavy hex-nut arms to power devastating blows.

When its life span ends, Melmetal rusts and falls apart. The small shards it leaves behind are
eventually reborn as Meltan.
