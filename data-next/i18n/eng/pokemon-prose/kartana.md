## Drawn Sword Pokémon

Kartana is an Ultra Beast from an Ultra Wormhole, an alien life-form that is considered dangerous in
this world. Its body is as thin as paper and shaped like a sharpened sword, making its very form a
weapon.

It does not seem to attack on its own, but its blade-like body is deadly enough to cut down enormous
structures in a single stroke. In its own world, however, it is apparently a common organism.
