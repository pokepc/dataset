## Weather Pokémon

Castform’s form shifts with the weather, and in its snowy state it reflects cold conditions. Changes
in temperature and humidity affect its cells, causing its appearance to change on its own as a
chemical reaction rather than by choice.

It can borrow nature’s power to take on the guise of a snow cloud. Its feelings seem to follow the
weather as well, growing more unsettled in rough conditions, while its tiny body is protected by
this remarkable ability.
