## Bash Buffalo Pokémon

Bouffalant charge wildly and headbutt anything in their path, with blows strong enough to derail a
train or crush a car. Their fluffy fur helps absorb the damage even as they strike with fierce
force.

They live in herds of about 20, and those with more hair on their heads hold higher positions within
the group. Bouffalant that betray the herd will lose the hair on their heads for some reason.
