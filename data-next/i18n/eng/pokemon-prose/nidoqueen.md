## Drill Pokémon

Nidoqueen’s body is armored with hard, needle-like scales that give it strong protection and make it
hard to drive off in battle. Its sturdy build lets it deliver forceful tackles, and its scales may
grow in cycles, with the spines bristling when it becomes excited.

It is especially protective of its young, using its rugged body to guard a nest or burrow and shield
its children from attacks. Nidoqueen is said to be at its strongest when defending them, even
placing offspring in the gaps between the spines.
