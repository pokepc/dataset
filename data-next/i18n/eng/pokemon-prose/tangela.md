## Vine Pokémon

Tangela is cloaked in a mass of thick blue vines that hide its true appearance, and the vines seem
to grow without stopping. As it walks, the vines sway and jostle constantly, giving it a restless,
writhing look.

It tangles anything that moves with those vines, and their fine, jiggling motion can be oddly
ticklish or unnerving to foes. If the vines are grabbed, they can snap off easily and without pain,
letting Tangela escape while new growth replaces what was lost soon after.
