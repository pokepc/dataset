## Intertwining Pokémon

Sylveon emits a soothing aura from its ribbonlike feelers, calming quarrels and easing hostility in
those around it. Its ribbon-shaped appendages can even be wrapped around quarrelers to quickly
restore peace.

It also wraps its feelers around its beloved Trainer’s arm, a touch that lets it read the Trainer’s
feelings as they walk together. When it sights prey, it may swirl those feelers as a distraction
before pouncing, and it will bravely charge dragon Pokémon far larger than itself.
