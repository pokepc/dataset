## Cricket Pokémon

Kricketot is a small Pokémon with short legs and stiff antennae that make a xylophone-like clack
whenever it stumbles or shakes its head. By striking its antennae together, it produces sounds that
resemble a xylophone or marimba.

It uses these clicks to chat with others, and the sounds are a familiar sign of autumn. More of
these Pokémon appear when the trees change color.
