## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that stays stuck to walls, and its shape seems to carry some meaning.
People have long compared it to ancient writing, hieroglyphs, or old text characters, yet it remains
a mystery whether those markings or Unown came first.

It is also thought that different Unown may have different abilities, and they seem to communicate
telepathically with one another. When one is alone, little happens, but when two or more gather, an
odd power is said to emerge.
