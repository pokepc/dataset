## Mischief Pokémon

Hoopa Unbound is said to possess immense power in its true form, with six huge arms and six rings
that can seize almost anything it desires. Legends tell of its greed, saying it once carried off an
entire castle to claim the treasure hidden inside.

Using its loop, it can warp space and send objects far away. It gathers things it likes and passes
them through the loop, teleporting them to a secret place.
