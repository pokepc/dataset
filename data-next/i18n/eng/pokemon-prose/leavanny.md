## Nurturing Pokémon

Leavanny weaves clothing and warm wrappings for small Pokémon from leaves, using the cutters on its
arms and sticky silk from its mouth. It is especially caring toward Sewaddle, making leaf wrappings
to keep them warm.

It also keeps its own eggs warm with heat produced by fermenting leaves. Leaf-making and
leaf-working are central to how Leavanny protects and tends to other Pokémon.
