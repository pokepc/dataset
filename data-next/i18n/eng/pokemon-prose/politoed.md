## Frog Pokémon

Politoed is a leader among Poliwag and Poliwhirl, and its echoing cry draws them in from far away.
Its voice can command them to gather, and when several join together, they raise a loud chorus that
can sound harsh and bellowing.

The curled hair on its head is a sign of kingly status, and the longer and more luxurious it is, the
more respect and obedience it earns. At night, Politoed are seen along lake shores, announcing their
territory with loud calls. Male Politoed have especially deep, booming voices.
