## Iron Armor Pokémon

Lairon lives in mountains rich in iron ore and spring water, where it feeds on iron drawn from rocks
and water. It makes its nest near clean springs, and its steel body is tempered by drinking mineral
water until it becomes bloated.

It often clashes with humans mining iron ore, and with other Lairon over territory. These battles
are fought by slamming their steel bodies together, leaving scratches and dents. The impact can send
sparks flying, and the size of those sparks is one way it shows off its power.
