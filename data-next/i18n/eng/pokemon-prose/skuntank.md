## Skunk Pokémon

Skuntank sprays a vile, repugnant fluid from the tip of its tail to attack, and it can shoot it from
more than 160 feet away. The stench is strongest when the fluid has had time to fester, though
repeated use can dull its odor.

It stores this stinky fluid in its belly and releases it in battle, with its diet affecting the
smell. Skuntank digs holes in the ground for nests, and its powerful spray can take down prey,
though it may leave uneaten food behind if the reek becomes too much for it.
