## Psi Pokémon

Alakazam is famed for its extraordinary intelligence and memory, with an IQ said to reach 5,000 or
more. It can remember everything it learns, and some say it recalls every event it has experienced
from birth.

Its brain keeps growing throughout its life, making its head so heavy that it must rely on
psychokinetic power to hold it up and move its weak body. It also uses powerful psychic abilities to
create the spoons it carries, and closing its eyes can heighten its other senses.
