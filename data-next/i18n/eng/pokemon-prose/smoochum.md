## Kiss Pokémon

Smoochum is a very curious Pokémon that uses its highly sensitive lips to examine anything it
encounters before touching it with its hands. By testing things this way, it decides what it likes
and dislikes, and its lips remember those impressions. It also rocks its head slowly back and forth
as though trying to kiss someone.
