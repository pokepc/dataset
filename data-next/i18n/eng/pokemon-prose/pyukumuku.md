## Sea Cucumber Pokémon

Pyukumuku lives in warm, shallow seas and along beaches, where its body stays moist thanks to a
thick layer of slime. This coating lets it survive on land for days, and the sticky mucous on its
body is even said to soothe sunburned skin.

If it washes ashore, people often throw it back into the sea, a habit that led to the tradition of
Pyukumuku chucking. When threatened, it can spit out its internal organs to strike back at foes, and
stepping on one will make it slip and anger it enough to smack you.
