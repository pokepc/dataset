## Pumpkin Pokémon

On nights of the new moon, Gourgeist wanders town streets singing in eerie voices, and those who
hear are said to be cursed. In the dark, its cries are believed to be the wails of suffering
spirits, and it may come knocking to carry away whoever answers.

It wraps prey in its hairlike arms and sings joyfully while watching their struggle. This Super Size
form has a fixed size, set like a Pumpkaboo, and cannot be changed.
