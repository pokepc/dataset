## King Pokémon

Calyrex is remembered as a merciful ruler of ancient Galar, a legendary king said to have reigned
there in times long past. Its presence was associated with compassion and benevolence, and it was
believed to bring healing and blessings to those around it.

It is also said to have the power to mend hearts and make plants spring forth, reflecting a gentle
influence over both living things and the land itself.
