## Sea Drifter Pokémon

Phione drifts in warm seas, often floating offshore when the water is warm. It inflates the
flotation sac on its head to glide languidly across the ocean, sometimes traveling in packs while it
searches for food.

Its azure body blends in with the sea, which may help protect it from natural predators. No matter
how far it drifts, Phione always returns to the place where it was born.
