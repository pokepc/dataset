## Alpha Pokémon

Arceus is described in myth as the Pokémon that existed before the universe and emerged from an egg
in a place of nothingness to shape the world. Legends say it formed all that exists, and that its
many arms helped it mold the universe.

In Hisuian lore, it is the heavenly source of the light that shines across the region, guiding and
protecting all Pokémon. This form changes type to match a held Plate through Multitype.
