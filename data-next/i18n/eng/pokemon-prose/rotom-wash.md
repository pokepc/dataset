## Plasma Pokémon

Wash Rotom is a bizarre Pokémon made of plasma that can slip into electronic devices and other
machines, taking control to cause havoc or playful mischief. It is said to be both smart and
mischievous, and some researchers even suspect it could serve as the power source for a unique
motor.

This form can inhabit all sorts of machines, and its unusual abilities inspired many inventions. It
appears like a will-o'-the-wisp fueled by electricity, surprising others with its strange, lively
presence.
