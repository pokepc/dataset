## Spatial Pokémon

Palkia is a legendary Pokémon of Sinnoh and a feared deity in local legend. It is said to live in a
gap in a spatial dimension parallel to ours, where it has total control over the boundaries of space
and can send itself to faraway places or even other dimensions.

Its power is said to distort space and steady it with every breath. In Hisuian legend, Palkia’s
birth is blamed for the disappearance of the world’s walls, leaving behind a sky that seems to
stretch on forever.
