## Mouse Pokémon

Sandslash’s body is covered in tough spines made from hardened hide, and it can curl into a spiny
ball to protect itself or roll into foes. It often uses this posture to strike from above, and its
spines can make opponents flinch before it lashes out with sharp claws.

Its claws and spikes may break during hard digging or fighting, but they grow back quickly. It is
also agile enough to climb trees and scurry about at speed, sometimes kicking up a blinding dust
storm while trying to hide.
