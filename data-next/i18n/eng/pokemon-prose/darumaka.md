## Zen Charm Pokémon

Darumaka draws its power from the fire burning inside its body. When that flame is strong, it
becomes restless and runs around, but if the fire dwindles, it falls asleep at once.

When Darumaka sleeps, it pulls its limbs into its body and its internal fire drops to 1,100 degrees
Fahrenheit. Its hot droppings were once used to keep people warm, and it is regarded as a popular
symbol of good fortune that never topples over in sleep.
