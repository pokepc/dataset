## Emotion Pokémon

Known as the Being of Emotion, Mesprit is said to have given humans the heart’s feelings, including
joy and sorrow. Legends say that when it flew, people first came to know the full range of living
emotions, and that it taught the nobility of pain as well as happiness.

Mesprit is said to sleep at the bottom of a lake, while its spirit leaves its body to glide over the
water’s surface. In legend, it was feared, because anyone who treated it without respect could have
their emotions thrown into disarray.
