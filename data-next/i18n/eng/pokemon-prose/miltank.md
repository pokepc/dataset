## Milk Cow Pokémon

Miltank produces more than five gallons of sweet, highly nutritious milk each day, and people have
relied on it since ancient times. Its milk is often given to the sick or weary, and children who
drink it are said to grow into hearty, healthy adults. Depending on conditions such as pastures and
seasons, the milk’s flavor can change, and if Miltank has recently had a baby or is around babies,
it becomes even richer.
