## Digging Pokémon

Bunnelby uses its large, shovel-like ears to dig burrows and nests, working through the night with
remarkable speed. In a single night, it can carve a nest deep underground, and the repeated motion
strengthens its ears until they can even cut through thick roots.

It is also highly sensitive to danger. At the sound of a Corviknight's wings, Bunnelby will quickly
dig itself underground to hide.
