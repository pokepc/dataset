## Manipulate Pokémon

Gothorita uses hypnosis and psychic power to control people and Pokémon, and tales of it leading
people astray are told widely. Its power is strongest on starry nights, when it draws strength from
starlight and floats stones to mark the positions of the stars.

Many old stories say it creates friends by controlling sleeping children, and some claim it spirits
them away beneath the night sky. Its strange connection to the greater universe remains unknown.
