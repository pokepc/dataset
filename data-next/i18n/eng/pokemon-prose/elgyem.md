## Cerebral Pokémon

Elgyem is shrouded in mystery and was first discovered about 50 years ago after appearing in a
desert. Rumors link its origin to a UFO crash, and many supposed alien sightings may actually be
people mistaking Elgyem for an extraterrestrial visitor.

Its highly developed brain gives it tremendous psychic power. It can use that power to squeeze an
opponent’s brain and cause severe headaches. When it stands near a TV, strange scenery may appear on
the screen, said to be a glimpse of its home.
