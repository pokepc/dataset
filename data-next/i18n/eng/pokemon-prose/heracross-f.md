## Single Horn Pokémon

Heracross is usually docile, but if it is disturbed while sipping honey or searching for sweet sap
and nectar in forests, it drives intruders off with its proud horn. It loves to keep sweet food for
itself, and its horn is its chief weapon for flinging rivals away.

With powerful legs, sharp claws, and a steel-hard shell, Heracross keeps a firm footing before
charging straight in. It can slip beneath an opponent’s grasp, scoop it up, and hurl it far away.
