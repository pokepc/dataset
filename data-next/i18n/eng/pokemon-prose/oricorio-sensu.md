## Dancing Pokémon

Oricorio (Sensu Style) beats its wings together to create fire, and as it moves through its
beautiful dance, it bathes opponents in intense flames. After sipping red nectar, it becomes
especially passionate, and its dancing can leave enemies overwhelmed in both body and mind.

It wins the hearts of its foes with graceful, fiery motions, then turns that opening into a blaze.
If its Trainer gives the wrong order, this passionate Pokémon becomes fiercely angry.
