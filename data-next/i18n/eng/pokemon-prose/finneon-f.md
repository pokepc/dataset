## Wing Fish Pokémon

Finneon stores sunlight in the line running down its side and in the pink areas of its skin, which
then glow when darkness falls. After long exposure to sunlight, the patterns on its tail fins shine
vividly at night, and the soft light may also help it lure prey.

As it swims, its two fluttering tail fins give it a graceful, Beautifly-like look. Its energetic
double tail fins can propel sudden jumps, though it is often seen near the surface by day and deeper
waters at night.
