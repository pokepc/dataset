## Symbol Pokémon

Unown (!) has a flat, thin body that clings to walls and resembles ancient writing or hieroglyphs,
as if its shape itself carries meaning. Researchers still do not know whether ancient text or Unown
came first, and its many forms are said to resemble writing from other lands.

These Pokémon seem to communicate telepathically, and some accounts say an odd power emerges when
more than one is present. By itself, however, nothing unusual happens. Its form only changes its
appearance and cannot be changed.
