## Dragon Pokémon

Salamence is said to have gained its magnificent wings after a long-held wish to fly triggered a
sudden mutation in its cells. At last able to realize its dream, it soars through the sky in joy,
often staying aloft until exhaustion forces it to rest.

Its happiness can turn fierce if it is angered. Then it becomes uncontrollable, flying about while
spouting flames and using its shredding claws and fire to destroy everything in its path, scorching
fields and mountains as it goes.
