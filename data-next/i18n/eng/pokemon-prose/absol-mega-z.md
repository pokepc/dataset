## Disaster Pokémon

Absol lives deep in harsh mountains and rarely comes down, but it rushes through fields and slopes
swiftly when it senses danger. Its curved horn is keenly attuned to subtle changes in the sky and
land, letting it warn people of approaching natural disasters.

Because it often appears before calamity, people once feared it as a bringer of doom. In truth,
Absol has a calm disposition and is said to protect fields by alerting others to looming crises.
