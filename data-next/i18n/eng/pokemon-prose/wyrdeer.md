## Big Horn Pokémon

Wyrdeer’s black orbs shine with an eerie light when it raises invisible barriers. This striking
display hints at a calm but formidable power, as it can ward off danger without any visible sign of
the defense it creates.

The fur shed from its beard holds heat exceptionally well, making it a valued material for winter
clothing. Even after it is shed, this warm fur remains useful, adding a practical quality to
Wyrdeer’s rugged coat.
