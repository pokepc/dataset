## Imitation Pokémon

Sudowoodo disguises itself as a tree to avoid attack, but its body is actually closer to rock than
to plant matter. It may stand motionless along paths, and if a branch seems to shake with no wind,
it is probably a Sudowoodo in disguise.

Its green hands or forelegs stay green all year, making it easy to spot in winter. It hates water
and will flee, or even seem to vanish, when rain begins or it is splashed.
