## Fairy Pokémon

Granbull is timid and easily spooked, despite its fearsome look. Its heavy fangs and well-developed
lower jaw make it tilt its head for balance, and it usually avoids biting unless it is startled or
provoked.

When it does bare its mouth or become enraged, its huge fangs and powerful jaws can scare off foes
and deliver a strong bite. Even so, it dislikes conflict and is often seen as a sensitive,
unreliable watchdog.
