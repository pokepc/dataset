## Cobra Pokémon

Arbok uses the frightening face-like pattern on its belly to intimidate foes and prey alike, and the
exact markings are said to vary by area. Studies have confirmed several variations, with even more
possible arrangements reported.

It can spread its chest wide and hiss with eerie sounds to frighten enemies, then strike quickly by
stunning them with its belly pattern. Once it wraps its powerful body around a victim, it constricts
so tightly that escape is impossible, and it may even flatten steel oil drums.
