## Live Coal Pokémon

Magby is a small, timid Pokémon that lives near volcanoes and in volcanic craters. Its body heat
stays around 1,100 degrees Fahrenheit, and embers or sparks leak from its mouth and nose with each
breath. When it gets excited or surprised, it may exhale flames, and its blood is said to circulate
like magma.
