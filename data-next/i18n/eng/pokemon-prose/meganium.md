## Herb Pokémon

The fragrance rising from Meganium’s flower soothes and calms emotions, and even its petals give off
an aroma that eases aggressive feelings. In battle, it releases more of this becalming scent to
blunt its foe’s fighting spirit.

Meganium’s breath can revive dead grass, plants, and flowers, restoring them to health. Those who
stand near it often feel refreshed, as if resting in a sunny forest.
