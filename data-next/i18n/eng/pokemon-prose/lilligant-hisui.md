## Flowering Pokémon

Lilligant is admired for its beauty and is even popular with celebrities, but its flower crown is
hard to bring into bloom. Even skilled gardeners have trouble caring for it, and if it is neglected,
the flower can wither and rot.

Its garland gives off a soothing fragrance that can calm the mind, and the scent varies a little
with the soil it grew in. Its strong legs are thought to come from life in snowy mountains, and its
flowers are especially beautiful when they bloom in the wild.
