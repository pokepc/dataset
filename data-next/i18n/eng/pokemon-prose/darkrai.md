## Pitch-Black Pokémon

Darkrai is active on new-moon nights, when it can lull people to sleep and stir dreams. Folklore
says that on moonless nights it appears in nightmares, and some villagers have claimed to see it in
the dreams that afflicted them.

It is said to protect itself by filling nearby people and Pokémon with terrible nightmares, driving
them from its territory. Despite this, it is said to mean no harm.
