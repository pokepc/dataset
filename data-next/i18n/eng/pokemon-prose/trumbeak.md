## Bugle Beak Pokémon

Trumbeak stores berry seeds in its beak after feeding, then fires them out in a sudden burst when it
meets prey or an enemy. The scattered seeds can sprout into new plants.

It also bends the tip of its beak to make many different calls, even more than a hundred at will.
Its loud, varied cries often make it a nuisance to nearby Pokémon and people.
