## Trash Heap Pokémon

Garbodor eats trash, and that garbage becomes new kinds of poison gases and liquids inside its body.
The main makeup of its toxins depends on what it has swallowed, and it can even absorb garbage into
its body and make it part of itself.

In battle, it clamps foes with its left arm or hand and overwhelms them with foul-smelling gas or a
poisonous liquid fired from its right arm. Its toxic liquid is so virulent that even a small amount
can cause serious effects.
