## Tiny Swallow Pokémon

Taillow is a small, gutsy Pokémon that bravely challenges foes far larger and stronger than itself.
Even after a defeat, it stays defiant, though its spirit can weaken when it is hungry, and it may
cry loudly.

Having only recently left its nest, Taillow can become lonely and cry at night. It feeds on Wurmple
in forests and dislikes cold seasons, migrating to warmer lands and flying more than 180 miles a
day.
