## Punk Pokémon

Toxtricity is a short-tempered, aggressive Pokémon that chugs stagnant water to absorb any toxins it
may contain. It seems to strum like a guitar, but the sound actually comes from clawing at the
protrusions on its chest to generate electricity.

It uses this unusual method to build up power, turning its body into a source of electric energy
while handling toxins it takes in.
