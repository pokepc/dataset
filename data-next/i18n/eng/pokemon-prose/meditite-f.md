## Meditate Pokémon

Meditite undertakes rigorous training deep in the mountains, practicing yoga and meditation every
day to heighten its inner energy and spiritual strength. It can even levitate or float in the air
when its power is sharpened.

It survives on just one berry a day, treating minimal eating and even hunger as part of its
discipline. Yet whenever it meditates, it loses focus, so its training never truly ends.
