## Garden Pokémon

Florges claims exquisite flower gardens as its territory and draws power from the energy given off
by blooming plants. In times long past, castle governors invited it to create lavish gardens to
adorn their domains.

It can control the flowers it grows, and the petal blizzards it unleashes are said to be as
overwhelming as they are beautiful. Its life may span several hundred years, and it is devoted to
protecting gardens throughout that long existence.
