## Centipede Pokémon

Venipede uses the feelers on its head and tail to detect vibrations in the air, letting it sense
where prey is and even judge its condition. It is brutally aggressive and will leap at prey it
believes it can eat without considering the danger.

Its bite delivers a potent poison from highly venomous fangs, strong enough to paralyze large bird
Pokémon that would otherwise prey on it. When Venipede meets a similar species, a fierce fight is
likely to break out.
