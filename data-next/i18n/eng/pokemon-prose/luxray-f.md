## Gleam Eyes Pokémon

Luxray can see through solid objects, spotting prey hidden behind walls or other cover and even
keeping track of lost young. Its gold-shimmering eyes are said to reveal anything it looks upon, and
this sharp sight also helps it scout for danger.

Using this power consumes a great deal of electricity, so Luxray rests for long periods to build up
energy. It is also known to live in packs led by a single male.
