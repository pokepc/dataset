## EleSquirrel Pokémon

Pachirisu is a calm Pokémon related to the Pikachu line, but its cheeks and tail still carry a
dangerous charge. It makes electricity with pouches in its cheeks and can shoot that power from its
tail, living high atop trees.

It also gathers fur balls that crackle with static electricity and stores them with berries in tree
holes. Pairs may rub their cheek pouches together to share the electricity they have stored.
