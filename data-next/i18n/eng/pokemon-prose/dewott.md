## Discipline Pokémon

Dewott’s flowing double-scalchop technique is the result of strict, daily training. Through constant
practice, it develops its own unique forms for using its scalchops, so no two Dewott handle them
exactly alike.

It never neglects to maintain its scalchops, keeping them in excellent condition. Its exquisite
technique is so refined that it can even drive seasoned blade masters to retreat in defeat.
