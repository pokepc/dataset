## Scout Pokémon

Patrat are extremely cautious, and they keep a constant watch over their nest by taking turns
standing guard. They feel uneasy if no one is on lookout, so one of them is usually watching for
danger at all times.

They can keep this vigilance up for days by eating food stored in their cheek pouches. They also use
their tails to communicate with one another, though they can be caught off guard by threats coming
from behind.
