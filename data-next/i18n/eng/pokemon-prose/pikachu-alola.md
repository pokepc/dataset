## Mouse Pokémon

Pikachu stores electricity in its cheek pouches and can release it in a burst as powerful as a
lightning bolt. When it is angry, threatened, or encounters something unfamiliar, it may discharge
that energy at once. If several gather together, their combined electricity can build into
thunderstorms.
