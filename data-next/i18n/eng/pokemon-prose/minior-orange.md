## Meteor Pokémon

Minior once lived in the ozone layer, where it drifted high above the world until its outer shell
grew too heavy and it plunged to the ground. It is born from mutated nanoparticles, and in its
native habitat it can even become food for stronger Pokémon.

Its shell is tough, but a hard impact can knock it loose from the body inside. When it falls, the
shock can smash that outer shell to pieces.
