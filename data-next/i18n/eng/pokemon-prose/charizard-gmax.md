## Flame Pokémon

Charizard flies the sky in search of powerful opponents, but it will not unleash its fiery breath on
a foe weaker than itself. Its wings carry it to great heights as it blows out extremely hot fire,
hot enough to melt boulders, almost anything, or even any material it strikes.
