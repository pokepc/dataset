## Blazing Pokémon

When Darmanitan is injured in fierce battle, it hardens into a stone-like form and meditates,
sharpening its mind to continue fighting mentally. In this state, its power changes with the
intensity of its internal fire.

That fire can reach 2,500 degrees Fahrenheit, and its strength is enough to obliterate a dump truck
with a single punch. In battle, it shifts into this form when its health drops low, then returns to
its normal state if it recovers.
