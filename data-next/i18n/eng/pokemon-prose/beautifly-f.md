## Butterfly Pokémon

Beautifly’s colorful, patterned wings are its most striking feature, and it drifts on spring winds
through flower-covered fields in search of sweet pollen and nectar. Leave a potted flower by an open
window, and it may come looking for food.

Its long, thin mouth, like a coiled needle, is used to collect pollen and suck nectar from flowers.
Though beautiful, Beautifly is aggressive and greedy, and it will jab fiercely when disturbed or
chase off other pollen gatherers.
