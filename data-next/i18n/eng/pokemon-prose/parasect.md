## Mushroom Pokémon

Parasect is a host-parasite pair in which a large mushroom has taken control of the bug host,
draining its energy and seeming to do all the thinking. The bug is mostly dead, and if the mushroom
comes off, it stops moving. Mushroom-lacking specimens lie motionless, suggesting the fungus directs
its actions.
