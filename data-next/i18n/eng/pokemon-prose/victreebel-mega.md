## Flycatcher Pokémon

Mega Victreebel uses a sweet, honeylike aroma and a long vine waved like a living lure to draw prey
close. When victims approach, it swallows them whole and dissolves them with a fluid so strong that
even hard objects melt away.

Its mouth pools an acid that can become sweeter after dissolving prey, making the lure even more
effective. Reports also say it lives in huge colonies deep in jungles, though few ever return from
there.
