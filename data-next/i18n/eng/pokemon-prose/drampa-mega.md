## Placid Pokémon

Mega Drampa is usually kind and friendly, especially toward people and children, and it may come
down from its mountain home to play in towns. It is known to protect children in danger, and even to
seek out bullies when a friend has been harmed.

When angered, however, its gentle nature gives way to overwhelming force. It can unleash intense
breath and whip up fierce gales that devastate everything around it.
