## String Spit Pokémon

Spinarak spins webs from fine, strong thread and waits motionlessly for prey to wander into its
trap. It may remain in the same pose for days, patiently waiting until something is caught in its
web.

Its web is like a second nervous system, letting Spinarak sense tiny vibrations and tell what kind
of prey is touching the strands. Though the poison in its fangs is not very strong, it can still
weaken trapped prey. Some people even use its sturdy thread to weave nets.
