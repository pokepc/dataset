## Scarf Pokémon

Cinccino’s white fur is coated in a special oil that makes it feel remarkably smooth and pleasant to
touch. The fur also repels dust and keeps static electricity from building up, while the oil helps
this Pokémon slide away from attacks and even deflect blows.

Cinccino is very particular about cleanliness and will not tolerate even a speck of dust. It spreads
the oil from its body over its nest for protection, and the oil is valued highly at market for its
smooth, skin-soothing qualities.
