## Eon Pokémon

Mega Latios is a highly intelligent Pokémon that understands human speech and telepathy lets it
sense others' locations and emotions even while hidden. It can project images of what it sees or
imagines into another's mind, and it opens its heart only to a compassionate Trainer.

This tender, docile Pokémon dislikes fighting. By folding back its wings and forelegs to reduce air
resistance, it can fly faster than a jet plane.
