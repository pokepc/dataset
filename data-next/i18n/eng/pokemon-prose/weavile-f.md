## Sharp Claw Pokémon

Weavile live in cold, snowy regions and often travel in groups of four or five. They leave
claw-marked signals on trees, rocks, and even frost-covered surfaces so others can find them, and
the number of different signs is said to be vast.

These Pokémon hunt with remarkable coordination, often under a leader. Working together, they can
surround and bring down prey much larger than themselves, and they share food among the group rather
than fighting over it.
