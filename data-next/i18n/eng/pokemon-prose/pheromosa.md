## Lissome Pokémon

Pheromosa is one of the dangerous Ultra Beasts, known to race across the land at terrific speeds.
Its body is thin and supple, yet it still possesses great power.

Although it is alien to this world and seen as a danger here, it is apparently a common organism
where it normally lives. It also refuses to touch anything, perhaps sensing some uncleanness in this
world.
