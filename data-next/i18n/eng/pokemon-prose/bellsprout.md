## Flower Pokémon

Bellsprout is a carnivorous Pokémon that prefers hot, humid places, where it ensnares tiny bugs with
its vines and devours them. Though its body is very skinny, it can move blindingly fast when
catching prey, and it immediately lashes out at anything that moves.
