## Big Jaw Pokémon

Totodile is small but rough and tough, with powerful, well-developed jaws that can crush almost
anything. It often snaps at anything that moves, and even when it seems to be playfully nipping, its
bite can cause serious injury.

Because of this habit, even its Trainer has to be careful around it. Its eager chomping makes it a
scrappy little Pokémon that should never be underestimated.
