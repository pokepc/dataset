## Spatial Pokémon

Palkia is a legendary Pokémon of Sinnoh feared as a deity in mythology. It is said to dwell in a gap
of the spatial dimension parallel to ours, where it can distort space and cross to faraway places or
even other dimensions.

Its every breath is said to make space more stable, yet its birth was also blamed for the
disappearance of the world’s walls, leaving a sky that stretches on forever.
