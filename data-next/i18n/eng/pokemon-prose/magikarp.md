## Fish Pokémon

Magikarp is a famously weak and unreliable Pokémon that flops and splashes more than it battles. It
is so powerless that even mild currents can carry it away, and its reckless jumping makes it easy
prey, though it may occasionally leap high for no clear reason.

Despite that pathetic image, Magikarp is remarkably hardy and can survive in all kinds of water,
even if it is dirty or polluted. It lives in seas, lakes, rivers, and shallow puddles, and in great
numbers, making it a common sight across the world.
