## Poison Pin Pokémon

Nidorina has a calm, caring, and gentle temperament, and its horn develops slowly, so it prefers not
to fight. It relies on physical attacks like clawing and biting, and when danger is near, it raises
the barbs all over its body. Its ultrasonic cries can befuddle foes, and groups may even use a
chorus of these waves to drive off enemies.
