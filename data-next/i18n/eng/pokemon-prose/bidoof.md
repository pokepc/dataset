## Plump Mouse Pokémon

Bidoof has a calm, unsophisticated look and is rarely flustered by anything. With nerves of steel,
it is more agile and active than it seems, and it may even saunter into villages without a care.

It lives in groups near water and nests there. Bidoof constantly gnaws on logs, rocks, boulders, and
trees to wear down its front teeth, which grow as fast as Rattata’s.
