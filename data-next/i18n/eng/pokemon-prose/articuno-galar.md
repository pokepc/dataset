## Freeze Pokémon

Articuno is a legendary bird Pokémon said to appear to people lost in icy mountains. It can control
ice at will, and the flapping of its wings chills the air so deeply that snow begins to fall.

By freezing moisture in the atmosphere, it can create blizzards while flying. Its beautiful blue,
seemingly translucent wings are said to be made of ice, and its long tail trails behind it as it
glides over snowy mountains and permafrost.
