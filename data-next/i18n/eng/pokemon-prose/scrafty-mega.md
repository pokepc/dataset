## Hoodlum Pokémon

Mega Scrafty is a tough territorial Pokémon that attacks intruders in groups and never leaves its
home range. Though it acts like an aggressive ruffian and taunts foes by spitting acidic liquid, it
also takes very good care of its family, friends, and turf.

It protects itself by pulling up its shed skin while kicking, and its kicks are strong enough to
smash concrete blocks and even shatter solid pillars. Among its group, the one with the largest
crest is the leader and earns the most respect.
