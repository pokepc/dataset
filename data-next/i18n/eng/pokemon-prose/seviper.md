## Fang Snake Pokémon

Seviper has long shared a bitter feud with Zangoose, and the scars on its body show how vicious
those battles have been. It is known as Zangoose’s archrival and will not give up the conflict that
has lasted for generations.

Its swordlike tail is its main weapon. Seviper constantly sharpens and polishes it on hard rocks
until the blade grows extremely sharp, then uses it to slash foes while coating them with a deadly
poison. It also hides in tall grass and strikes unwary prey with venomous fangs.
