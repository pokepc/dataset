## Iceberg Pokémon

Avalugg’s body is covered in ice that is harder than steel, giving it a heavy, cumbersome frame that
can crush anything in its path. Its lower jaw is armored with especially tough ice, strong enough to
shatter rocks and cut through deep snow on steep mountain paths.

In cold regions, clusters of Bergmite may huddle on its back, making it look like an ice-built
carrier as it swims among icebergs. As it moves through the day, cracks spread across its body, but
it restores itself to a pristine state overnight.
