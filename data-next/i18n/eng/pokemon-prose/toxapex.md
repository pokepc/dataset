## Brutal Star Pokémon

Toxapex crawls along the ocean floor on its 12 legs, leaving a trail of Corsola bits in its wake. It
can also form a protective dome with those legs, sheltering itself from the tides and trapping its
own body heat in cold waters.

It attacks by hurling toxic spikes at foes, and anything that comes too close is cut down by the
claws on its feet. Its poison is extraordinarily potent, leaving victims in intense pain for days
and causing lingering aftereffects even after recovery.
