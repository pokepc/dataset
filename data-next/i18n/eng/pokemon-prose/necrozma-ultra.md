## Prism Pokémon

Ultra Necrozma is a life-form thought to have come from another world long ago, and it was
apparently found slumbering underground. It survives by absorbing light, and when it is deprived of
it, impurities build up within and around its body, causing it to darken and stop moving.

Driven by its need for light, it rages about in a painfully frantic state, searching for more
energy. Its disposition is said to be extremely vicious, and it constantly fires laser beams that
can cut through nearly anything.
