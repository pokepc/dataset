## Trash Bag Pokémon

Trubbish was born when garbage bags and industrial waste reacted together, leaving it drawn to
filthy, unsanitary places. If trash is left lying around, it may even turn up in a room, and in
Alola it is often seen with Grimer in pursuit.

It follows people who litter in search of more garbage, gorging on trash until its stomach is full.
It then belches poisonous gas that can put people in the hospital, and a child or young Pokémon who
breathes it in could be in serious danger.
