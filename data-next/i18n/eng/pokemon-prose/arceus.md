## Alpha Pokémon

In mythology, Arceus is said to have existed before the universe and to have emerged from an egg in
an empty void. Legends tell that it shaped the world, and even all of creation, with its thousand
arms.

In Hisuian lore, it is the heavenly source of the light that shines across the region, guiding and
protecting all Pokémon. Its form changes with its held Plate to match its type.
