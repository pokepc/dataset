## Mouse Pokémon

Sandshrew lives in dry, sandy regions with little rainfall, where it digs deep burrows and sleeps
underground at night. It avoids water and uses the sand around it to protect itself, even bathing in
grit to shake off dirt and moisture.

When danger threatens, it quickly curls into a ball to shield its soft belly and make attacks bounce
off. Its tough, dry hide helps it survive harsh desert life, and it can even roll away at high speed
or bounce safely after a fall.
