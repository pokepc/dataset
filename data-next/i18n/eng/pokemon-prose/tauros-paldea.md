## Wild Bull Pokémon

Tauros is a rowdy, highly resilient Pokémon that thrives on conflict and is rarely satisfied unless
it is charging or rampaging. When it sights an opponent, it whips its body with its long tails to
build up its will to fight, then rushes in at astounding speed and keeps going until it strikes
something.
