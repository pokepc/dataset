## Chick Pokémon

Torchic sticks close to its Trainer, following behind with unsteady steps and a fluffy coat of down.
A small flame burns in a sac inside its body, making it feel warm to hug like a hot-water bottle.

When threatened, it spits fireballs from its stomach. Its flames can reach over 1,800 degrees
Fahrenheit and leave foes scorched black. Torchic also dislikes darkness because it cannot see well
in it.
