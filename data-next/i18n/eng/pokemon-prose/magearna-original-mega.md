## Artificial Pokémon

Built more than 500 years ago by a scientist, this artificial Pokémon cannot speak, though it can
understand human speech. Its mechanized body serves only as a vessel for the real life-form within
it: the Soul-Heart, an artificial soul.

It can synchronize its consciousness with others to understand their feelings, making it especially
helpful in caring for people. Even in its Original Color Mega Form, its true self is still said to
be the Soul-Heart.
