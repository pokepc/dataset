## Continent Pokémon

Groudon is revered in mythology as the embodiment of the land itself, credited with raising
continents and expanding the world’s landmass. Legends also say it can drive away rain clouds and
evaporate water with intense light and heat, even coming as a savior to people suffering from
floods.

It is said to sleep in underground magma and awaken to trigger volcanic eruptions. Ancient tales
tell of its titanic clashes with Kyogre, after which it fell into slumber, and that with nature’s
full power it can return
