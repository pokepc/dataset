## Chill Pokémon

Cubchoo’s nose is always running, and it keeps sniffing the snot back up because that mucus helps
supply the raw material for its moves. It sniffles before it acts, using its frosty snot to add an
icy element when needed.

Its snot is also a barometer of health. When Cubchoo is well, it becomes thick and sticky and can
even be smeared on things it dislikes, but when it is unwell, the mucus turns watery and its
Ice-type power weakens.
