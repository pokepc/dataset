## Discharge Pokémon

Mega Manectric constantly discharges strong electricity from its mane, which gathers power from the
atmosphere. When it battles, it forms thunderclouds overhead and can send down lightning bolts,
leaving it beneath unnatural storm clouds.

It is rarely seen near people and is said to nest where lightning has struck. Its crackling sparks
can even start forest fires, and it uses electricity to stimulate its muscles, helping it move
swiftly and recover from soreness.
