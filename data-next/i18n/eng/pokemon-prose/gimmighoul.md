## Coin Chest Pokémon

Gimmighoul was born inside a treasure chest about 1,500 years ago, and it still lives in old chests
today. It often goes unnoticed, so it can be left in shop corners where people mistake it for
ordinary treasure.

When would-be thieves try to steal the chest’s contents, Gimmighoul drains the life-force from those
scoundrels to protect its treasure.
