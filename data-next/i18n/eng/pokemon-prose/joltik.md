## Attaching Pokémon

Joltik cannot generate electricity on its own, so it clings to large-bodied Pokémon and draws off
static charge, storing it in an electric pouch. It is often found attached to other Pokémon, and
some are even seen stuck to Yamper’s hindquarters.

Joltik that live in cities have adapted to siphon electricity from household outlets. By latching on
and feeding on nearby power, they keep themselves charged.
