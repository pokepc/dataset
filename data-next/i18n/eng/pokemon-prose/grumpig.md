## Manipulate Pokémon

Grumpig stores psychic power in the black pearls on its body, using them to intensify its mental
waves and exert control over opponents. When it focuses its special power, its breathing becomes
labored and snorting.

To use this power, Grumpig performs an odd, bizarre dance that can influence foes’ minds. Its
distinctive style of dancing became popular overseas, and the black pearls on its forehead are
prized as valuable gems and works of art.
