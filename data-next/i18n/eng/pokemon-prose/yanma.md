## Clear Wing Pokémon

Yanma can see all around without moving its large eyes, so it quickly notices prey and attackers
alike. It uses that wide vision to hunt insects and to keep watch over its territory.

A skilled flier, Yanma can hover in place, make sudden stops, and turn sharply in midair. When it
beats its thin wings at high speed, it can even churn the air with enough force to create shock
waves strong enough to shatter windows.
