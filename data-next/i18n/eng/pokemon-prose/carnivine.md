## Bug Catcher Pokémon

Carnivine binds itself to trees in marshes with its tentacles and even walks around on them to find
a branch to dangle from. Once it has a perch, it hangs like a plant and waits with its mouth wide
open.

It lures prey close with sweet-smelling drool or saliva, then chomps down and gulps it whole. Though
it looks like a plant Pokémon, it has a gluttonous, unruly temperament and can take a whole day to
finish eating.
