## Tiny Bee Pokémon

Combee is a set of three that stays together from birth, spending all its time with the other two
members of its trio. It busily gathers nectar from flowers and delivers honey to its colony, working
from sunrise to sundown to please Vespiquen and the swarm.

Each trio is devoted to a queen Pokémon, and every swarm makes honey with its own flavor. At night,
Combee gather closely with others to form a beehive-like mass and sleep, sometimes clustering in
great numbers.
