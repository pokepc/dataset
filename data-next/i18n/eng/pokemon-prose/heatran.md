## Lava Dome Pokémon

Heatran dwells in volcanic caves, where it uses its cross-shaped feet to dig in and crawl along
ceilings and walls. Stories say it was born from the boiling magma within Mount Coronet, and its
molten-steel body still holds many mysteries.

Its body is made of rugged steel, but some parts are partially melted by its own heat. Boiling blood
like magma flows through it, giving it a fierce, otherworldly presence.
