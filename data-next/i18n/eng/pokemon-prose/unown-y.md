## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is always found stuck to walls, and its shape appears to carry
some meaning. The many forms resemble ancient writing or hieroglyphs, leaving it a mystery whether
human language or Unown came first.

These strangely shaped Pokémon are often thought to be linked to ancient tablets and other lands’
writing. They also seem to communicate telepathically, and when two or more are present, an odd
power is said to emerge.
