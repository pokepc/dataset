## Turtle Pokémon

Wartortle often hides in water to stalk unwary prey, and it swims with careful balance by
controlling its furry ears and tail. When tapped on the head, it pulls itself into its shell, though
the tail still sticks out a little.

Its thick, fur-covered tail is a symbol of longevity and grows darker with age, making it especially
popular among older people. Scratches on its shell show its toughness in battle, and algae on the
shell is a sign of great age.
