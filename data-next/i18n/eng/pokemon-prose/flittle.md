## Frill Pokémon

Flittle’s toes hover just above the ground, lifted by psychic power that comes from the frills on
its belly. It can stay lightly suspended as it moves, giving it an airy, almost weightless way of
running.

It spends its days darting around wastelands. If someone takes its cherished berries, Flittle will
relentlessly chase the thief down and get its revenge.
