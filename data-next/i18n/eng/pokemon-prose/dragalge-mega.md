## Mock Kelp Pokémon

Mega Dragalge lurks among seaweed and uses its camouflage to ambush prey. When enemies draw near, it
sprays a poisonous liquid that can corrode or even melt metal, and it unleashes it without
hesitation on anything entering its territory.

Its toxin is so powerful it can eat through a tanker’s hull, and ships that wander into waters where
it lives are said to vanish without return. It also generates dragon energy by raising the plume on
its head above the ocean’s surface to bathe it in sunlight.
