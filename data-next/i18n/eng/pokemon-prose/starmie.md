## Mysterious Pokémon

Starmie has a symmetrical, star-shaped body that it spins rapidly to swim through the water. Its
central core, sometimes called the gem of the sea, glows in seven colors and may appear different
each time it is seen.

People have long thought the bright core is used to communicate, since it seems to send mysterious
radio waves or signals into the night sky and even into space. Its unusual geometric shape and
luminous core have also led some to imagine it as an alien creature or a reflection of the stars.
