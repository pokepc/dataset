## Sword Pokémon

When Honedge evolves, it splits into two swords that act together through telepathy, coordinating
their attacks with uncanny precision. Their intricate attack patterns can overwhelm even highly
skilled swordfighters.

The twin blades also rub against each other to produce a metallic sound that unsettles foes. By
quickly switching between offense and defense, they work in tandem to bring down their prey.
