## Parent Pokémon

Mega Kangaskhan raises its young in a belly pouch, and the infant rarely leaves it until about three
years old. The mother lets the child out to play only when it feels safe, but keeps a close watch
and never strays far.

Its devotion to its offspring is fierce enough to make it stand its ground in battle no matter how
badly hurt it is. Even while carrying its baby, it remains swift and can batter opponents with quick
jabs and powerful punches.
