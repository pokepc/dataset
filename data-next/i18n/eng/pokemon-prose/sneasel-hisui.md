## Sharp Claw Pokémon

Sneasel is a cunning, savage Pokémon that blends into darkness to ambush prey. It hides sharp,
hooked claws in its paws, then suddenly extends them to startle foes or strike vulnerable spots.
Those claws also help it scale trees and steep cliffs with ease.
