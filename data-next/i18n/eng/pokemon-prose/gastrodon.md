## Sea Slug Pokémon

Gastrodon has a soft, boneless body that can quickly grow back if torn or bitten, making it far more
resilient than it looks. Its body is so pliable that it can absorb impacts, and when threatened, it
oozes purple fluid to escape.

It lives in shallow tidal pools and often comes onto land while searching for food, leaving a sticky
slime trail behind. Once protected by a large shell, it now has only a hard, thin plate on its back,
with traces of that ancient shell still left in its cells.
