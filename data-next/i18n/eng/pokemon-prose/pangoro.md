## Daunting Pokémon

Pangoro is a rowdy, quick-tempered Pokémon with tremendous physical strength, and it refuses to let
itself or others be bullied. It often settles disputes with its fists, driving into battle with
savage force and overwhelming opponents with punishing blows.

By watching the slight twitches of the leaf in its mouth, Pangoro can sense and predict an enemy’s
movements. Though it is eager to tussle and can be fierce in combat, it is also kindhearted toward
its companions.
