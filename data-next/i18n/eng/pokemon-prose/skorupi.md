## Scorpion Pokémon

Skorupi lives in arid regions and buries itself in sand to wait patiently for prey to come near. It
can go without food for a year while it lies hidden, ready to strike.

It attacks with the claws on its tail, gripping prey tightly so it cannot move while poison seeps
in. Its sharp claws secrete venom as soon as they close, and it will hang on tenaciously until the
poison takes effect. Cold temperatures seem to weaken it.
