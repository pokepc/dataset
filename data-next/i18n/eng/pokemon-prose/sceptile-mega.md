## Forest Pokémon

Mega Sceptile is a remarkably agile forest dweller that darts through tree branches and strikes from
above or behind. The leaves growing on its body and forelegs are sharp enough to slice down thick
trees, making it formidable in jungle combat.

Seeds grow on its back and are said to be packed with nutrients that help revitalize trees. It also
tends forest growth with care, and its body is said to regulate itself by basking in sunlight.
