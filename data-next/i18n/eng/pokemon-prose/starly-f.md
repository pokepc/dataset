## Starling Pokémon

Starly gather in large flocks across fields and mountains, and though each one is small, they are
strongest when flying together. Their wings are surprisingly powerful, and a strike from them can
leave lasting pain.

They rely on groups because they are weak on their own, but too large a flock soon turns restless
and quarrelsome. Their cries and singing are loud, strident, and often quite obnoxious.
