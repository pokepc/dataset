## Meteor Pokémon

Minior was born from mutated nanoparticles and originally made its home in the ozone layer. There,
it can serve as food for stronger Pokémon, and if it tries to flee, it simply falls to the ground.

Its outer shell is remarkably durable, but once the shell enclosing its body grows too heavy or it
takes a strong impact, it hurtles downward. The shock of the fall smashes the shell to smithereens.
