## Cave Pokémon

Gabite loves shiny things and is drawn to treasures hidden in caves. It digs to expand its nest,
uncovering gems and other loot to hoard, and its cave homes are often packed with jewels. The moment
intruders enter, its claws and fangs make the place dangerous, and its stolen cache is a constant
target for thieves.
