## Mach Pokémon

Mega Garchomp Z folds its body and extends its wings to resemble a jet plane, then tears through the
sky at sonic speed. Its fine scales cut drag and can injure foes that strike it, while the
protrusions on its head act as sensors to detect prey far away.

It is a fierce hunter that races after food and never lets prey escape. Flying at jet-fighter speed,
it can dive into flocks of bird Pokémon, and its wing-driven wind can even fell nearby trees. In the
wild, it is a fearsome sight that demands great care.
