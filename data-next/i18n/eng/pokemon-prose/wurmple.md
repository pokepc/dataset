## Worm Pokémon

Wurmple lives in tall grass and forests, where it feeds on leaves and peels bark from trees to drink
the sap that oozes out. Its feet have suction pads, letting it cling to glass without slipping.

When threatened, it raises the spikes on its rear to ward off attackers and can leak poison from
them. It is often preyed on by bird Pokémon, and it also spits a white silk that turns sticky in the
air to slow and immobilize foes.
