## Overturning Pokémon

Mega Malamar wields extraordinarily powerful hypnosis, able to overwhelm others and force them to
act as it wishes. Its glowing spots and flashing pattern can quickly draw observers into a trance,
and its hypnotic motions are used to lure prey close.

Once prey is within reach, it wraps its tentacles around the target and finishes it with digestive
fluids. Its sharp fin edges add to its danger, and its formidable control has even been used for
nefarious purposes and linked to history-changing events.
