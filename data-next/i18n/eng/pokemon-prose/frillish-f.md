## Floating Pokémon

With its veil-like arms and legs, Frillish wraps prey that swims by, stuns it with invisible poison
spikes, and drags it down into the deep sea. Victims are pulled miles below the surface to its lair,
where they are said never to return.

Legend says the residents of a sunken ancient city changed into these Pokémon. They are most often
associated with the ocean floor and the depths far beneath the waves.
