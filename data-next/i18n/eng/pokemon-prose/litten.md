## Fire Cat Pokémon

Litten keeps its feelings hidden and prefers to be alone, so earning its trust takes time. Even
after it warms up to someone, it still dislikes being fussed over, and trying to pet it too soon can
earn a scratch from its sharp claws.

It spends nearly all of its spare time grooming its coat with its tongue. Loose fur gathers in its
stomach and becomes fuel for fiery attacks, which vary depending on how it coughs them out. Its coat
is shed and regrown twice a year, and at that time it burns away the old fur.
