## Scratch Cat Pokémon

Meowth is nocturnal and tends to sleep through the day, becoming active at dusk and patrolling
streets or territory at night. It silently withdraws its claws to sneak about, often with its eyes
glinting when something shiny catches its attention.

It is fascinated by round, shiny objects, especially coins, and will pick them up, hoard them, or
play with them until it tires. It even keeps its face clean so the coin on its forehead stays
spotless, and it does not get along with Galarian Meowth.
