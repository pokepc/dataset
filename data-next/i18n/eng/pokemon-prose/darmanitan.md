## Blazing Pokémon

Darmanitan’s internal fire burns at 2,500 degrees Fahrenheit, and the hotter it burns, the greater
its power becomes. Its thick arms can throw punches strong enough to obliterate a dump truck.

When it is injured in fierce battle, it hardens into a stone-like form and meditates, sharpening its
mind. If it has Zen Mode, it can change into that form while fighting and return to its usual state
afterward.
