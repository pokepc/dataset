## Illuminating Pokémon

At night, Shiinotic’s strange, flickering lights make deep forests dangerous to enter, and anyone
lured closer may fall asleep and lose their way. Its glowing spores can drowsily charm prey before
it feeds on their energy.

Once prey is asleep, Shiinotic drains vitality through the tips of its arms and fingertips. It is
also known to help its own kind by sending vitality to weakened companions, though conflicts with
Parasect can fill an area with spores.
