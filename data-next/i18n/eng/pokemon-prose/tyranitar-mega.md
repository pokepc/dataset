## Armor Pokémon

Mega Tyranitar is so overwhelmingly powerful that its steps shake the earth, crumbling great
mountains and reshaping the surrounding terrain. Its body is difficult to harm, and that
invulnerability only makes it more eager to challenge strong enemies.

It is said to bring down whole mountains to make a nest, and when it rampages, rivers can be buried
and maps must be redrawn. Insolent and unconcerned with weaker foes, it roams the mountains seeking
worthy opponents to fight.
