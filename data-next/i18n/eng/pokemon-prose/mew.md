## New Species Pokémon

Mew is so rare that many experts still consider it a mirage, and only a few people have ever seen
it. It is said to appear only to those with a pure heart and a strong desire to see it.

Its DNA is believed to contain the genetic codes of all Pokémon, which lets it learn any move and
has led some scientists to think it may be the ancestor of Pokémon. It can also make itself
invisible at will, slipping by unnoticed.

When viewed under a microscope, its short, fine, delicate hair can be seen.
