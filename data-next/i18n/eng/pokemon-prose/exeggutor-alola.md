## Coconut Pokémon

Called the Walking Jungle, Alolan Exeggutor is a tropical Pokémon whose heads grow larger in strong
sunlight. Its three heads think independently, but they are friendly and can use telepathy to reach
a joint decision.

The heads often make it noisy, since each may be thinking about something different. In battle, they
work together to unleash powerful psychic energy, though cloudy days can make it sluggish.
