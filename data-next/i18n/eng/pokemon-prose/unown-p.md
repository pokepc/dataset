## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is always found stuck to walls, and its shape appears to carry
some kind of meaning. Its form resembles ancient writing or hieroglyphs, leaving it a mystery
whether the writings or Unown came first.

Different Unown forms are said to have different abilities, and they may communicate telepathically
with one another. When several are together, an odd power is said to emerge, but when alone, nothing
happens.
