## Seafaring Pokémon

Manaphy is born on the cold seafloor and is said to travel great distances through the oceans to
return to its birthplace. It is rarely seen in coastal waters, and its link to Phione is still
unclear despite their resemblance.

Water makes up most of its body, so it is strongly affected by changes in its surroundings. From
birth, it possesses a wondrous power that allows it to bond with any kind of Pokémon.
