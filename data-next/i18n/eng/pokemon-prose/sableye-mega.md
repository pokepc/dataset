## Darkness Pokémon

Mega Sableye dwells deep in caves, where it hunts for gems and gemstone crystals with sharp claws
and thick, pointy teeth. It is especially drawn to Carbink, though it may lose out to Gabite when
both are after the same treasure.

The minerals it eats rise to the surface of its body, creating countless patterns, while its
gemstone eyes gleam in the dark. Long feared for the sinister glow of those eyes, Sableye is said to
steal spirits away when it lurks unseen in the darkness.
