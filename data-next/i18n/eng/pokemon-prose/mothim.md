## Moth Pokémon

Mothim loves floral honey, but it never gathers any itself. Instead, it searches fields and
mountains for honey already collected by Combee, and at night it flutters around their hives to
steal it.

It does not keep a nest of its own, and it is not often seen in flower gardens despite feeding on
nectar. As it flies, Mothim scatters steel-colored scales from its wings.
