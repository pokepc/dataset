## Sun Pokémon

Sunflora converts sunlight into energy and nutrition, so it stays pointed toward the sun and moves
about in search of warm rays. On bright, warm days it is active and even rushes around in a lively,
hectic way.

When the sun goes down, Sunflora closes its petals and becomes still at once. As the hot season
approaches, the petals on its face grow more vivid and lively.
