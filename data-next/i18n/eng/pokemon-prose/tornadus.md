## Cyclone Pokémon

Tornadus soars through the sky at incredible speed, its lower body wrapped in a cloud of energy. As
it flies, it whips up winds in every direction strong enough to blow away everything in its path,
and the blasts from its tail can trigger severe storms.

This storm-stirring Pokémon is said to help turn the seasons by agitating the air. Its humanlike
shape is believed to be only a false form.
