## Flame Pokémon

Moltres is a legendary bird Pokémon of fire, known for wings that seem to blaze with orange light.
Each flap scatters embers and sends a dazzling red flare across the sky, sometimes making the night
itself glow.

It is also said to herald spring, migrating from the south as the seasons change and bringing early
warmth to wintry lands. Stories tell of it lighting mountain paths for the lost.
