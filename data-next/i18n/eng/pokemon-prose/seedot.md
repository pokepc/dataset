## Acorn Pokémon

Seedot attaches itself to tree branches with the top of its head and hangs there like an acorn or
real nut, making it hard to tell from the tree. While it dangles, it sucks moisture and absorbs
nutrients from the branch, and strong winds can sometimes knock it down.

By staying still, Seedot can surprise foraging Pokémon or suddenly startle them when it moves. It
also polishes its body with leaves once a day, and the more water it drinks, the glossier it
becomes.
