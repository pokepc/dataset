## Twin Bird Pokémon

Doduo has short wings that make flying difficult, so it relies on its strong legs to run at high
speed across grassy plains. It can race along with powerful strides, leaving deep footprints behind,
and its body stays balanced by alternately raising and lowering its two heads.

Its two heads never sleep at the same time, so one can always watch for danger while the other
rests. When threatened, it flees swiftly, and the brains in its heads are said to share emotions
with each other.
