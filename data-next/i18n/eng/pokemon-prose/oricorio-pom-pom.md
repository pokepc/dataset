## Dancing Pokémon

Oricorio's passionate dancing can win over its foes, leaving them open to be struck by blazing
flames. It beats its wings together to create fire, and as it moves through its elegant steps, it
bathes opponents in intense heat.

This Pom-Pom Style Oricorio has sipped red nectar, which fuels its fiery spirit. If its Trainer
gives the wrong order, it can become fiercely angry.
