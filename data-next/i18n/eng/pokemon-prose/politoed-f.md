## Frog Pokémon

Politoed gathers Poliwag and Poliwhirl with its echoing cry, and its voice can make them obey or
cower in fear. When several come together, they form a loud chorus, especially on moonlit nights by
lake shores, where their calls sound harsh and territorial.

The curled hair on its head is a sign of royal status. The longer and curlier it is, the more
respect and obedience Politoed earns from its peers. A deeper, more menacing cry is said to make it
especially popular.
