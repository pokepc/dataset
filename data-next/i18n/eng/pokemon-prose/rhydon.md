## Drill Pokémon

Rhydon walks on its hind legs, and its development gave it a smarter, though still very forgetful,
mind. Its forelegs were freed by this change, while its thick, armor-like hide makes it extremely
tough and able to endure lava and magma without feeling the heat.

Its drill-like horn can bore through solid rock, shatter boulders, and even crush uncut diamonds,
while a sweeping blow of its tail can topple buildings. It is known to roam volcanic regions,
protected by its rugged hide from molten lava.
