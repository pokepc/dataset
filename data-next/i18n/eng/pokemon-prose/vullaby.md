## Diapered Pokémon

Vullaby’s wings are still too short for flight, so it hops about friskily while waiting for them to
develop. It wears bones to protect its rear, often using ones given by Mandibuzz or found among prey
remains, and it may squabble with others over a comfortable bone.

As Vullaby grows, its appetite drives frequent growth spurts, so it must keep replacing bones that
become too small. Bones outgrown by older Vullaby are often passed down to younger ones in the nest.
