## Genetic Pokémon

Mewtwo was created through brutal genetic manipulation and years of gene-splicing and DNA
engineering, with Mew’s genes repeatedly recombined to form its body. Its DNA is almost the same as
Mew’s, but its size and temperament are vastly different, and it was made solely for battling.

Its power was raised to the ultimate level, and it is said to have a savage heart. Though its body
was shaped by human science, it was not given a compassionate heart, so it thinks only of defeating
its foes.
