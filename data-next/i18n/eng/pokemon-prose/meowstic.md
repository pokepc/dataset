## Constraint Pokémon

Meowstic keeps the eyelike patterns inside its ears tightly hidden, since they emit psychic energy
so intense it can be overwhelming. When it senses danger, it raises its ears and unleashes that
power with frightening force.

The males are especially defensive, and they show their full strength when protecting themselves or
their partners. Its male and female differences are significant enough that they are treated as
separate forms for practical purposes.
