## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is usually found stuck to walls, where its shape appears to carry
some kind of meaning. Its many forms resemble ancient writing or hieroglyphs, and researchers still
do not know whether the symbols inspired Unown or the other way around.

It is also said that Unown can communicate telepathically with one another. When several are
together, an odd power is said to emerge, but when alone, nothing happens.
