## Butterfly Pokémon

Butterfree flits from flower to flower, seeking nectar and honey and even finding blooms far from
its nest. It can locate patches with only a trace of pollen, and its legs help it carry honey home
after rubbing it onto their hairs.

Its wings are coated with poisonous powder that repels water, letting it fly even in heavy rain.
When threatened, it beats its wings rapidly to scatter toxic dust and scales into the air, and its
many tiny eyes give it keen awareness of its surroundings.
