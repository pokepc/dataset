## Axe Jaw Pokémon

Haxorus is usually kindhearted, but it can become terrifying when angered or defending its
territory. It faces foes with tusks so sharp and strong they can cut through steel beams and even
thick steel columns.

Its armored body adds to its toughness, and its resilient tusks are a source of pride. It licks up
dirt to gather the minerals it needs to keep them in top condition, preserving their destructive
edge.
