## Snow Land Pokémon

Mega Froslass freezes foes with icy breath that can plunge to around -60 degrees Fahrenheit, and its
hollow-looking body gives it an eerie, insubstantial appearance. It is said to be born from the
lingering regrets or soul of a woman who died on a snowy mountain, and legends of its presence in
blizzards and mountain settlements inspire fear.
