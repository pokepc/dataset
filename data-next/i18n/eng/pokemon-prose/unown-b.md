## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is always found stuck to walls, where its form resembles ancient
writing or hieroglyphs on old tablets. Its shape seems to carry some meaning, but researchers still
do not know whether human writing or the many Unown came first.

Different forms of Unown are said to have different abilities, and they seem to communicate
telepathically with one another. When several are together, an odd power is said to emerge, while
nothing happens when one is alone.
