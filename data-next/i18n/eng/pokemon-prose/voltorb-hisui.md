## Ball Pokémon

Voltorb is an enigmatic Pokémon that closely resembles a Poké Ball, and its origin remains a mystery
tied to the appearance of Poké Balls and their manufacture. It is often found in power plants, where
it has startled many by shocking anyone who mistakes it for a harmless object.

It moves by rolling, and even a small jolt or touch can make it discharge electricity or explode.
When excited, it releases the electricity stored in its body and is said to let out a loud,
uproarious laugh.
