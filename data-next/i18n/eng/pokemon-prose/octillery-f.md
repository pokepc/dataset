## Jet Pokémon

Octillery likes to hide in rocky holes, boulders, and other snug places, and it may even steal a
fellow Octillery’s nest or sleeping spot. It often waits there with only its mouth showing, ready to
spray ink before attacking.

It grabs prey with its suction-cupped tentacles, trying to hold it still before finishing it off
with its rock-hard head. If an opponent is too strong, it can spit murky ink to escape, and that ink
can also dull scent or be used in cooking.
