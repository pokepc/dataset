## Young Fowl Pokémon

Combusken toughens its legs and thighs by running through fields and mountains, building the speed
and power needed to deliver up to 10 kicks in a second. Its kicking mastery is paired with a fierce
fighting instinct, so it keeps attacking until its opponent gives up.

In battle, it fights with both intensely hot flames from its beak and outstandingly destructive
kicks. It also uses harsh, loud cries to distract and intimidate foes, sharpening its concentration
as the flame within its body grows hotter.
