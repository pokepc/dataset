## Sky High Pokémon

Rayquaza is said to have lived for hundreds of millions of years in the ozone layer, far above the
clouds, where it cannot be seen from the ground. It flies endlessly through the sky, and its long,
meteorlike form was long thought to be hidden from human view.

It is said to feed on water and particles in the atmosphere, and on meteoroids as well. Legends say
it descended to calm the clash between Kyogre and Groudon, and the meteoroids in its body provide
the energy it needs to Mega Evolve.
