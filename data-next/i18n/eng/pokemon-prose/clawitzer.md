## Howitzer Pokémon

Clawitzer’s oversized claw acts like a cannon, using water it has sucked up and compressed into
powerful blasts. It can fire projectiles of seawater strong enough to punch through thick iron or
even sink a tanker.

The feelers on its large claw help it detect prey before it launches its attack. Water expelled from
the nozzle in the back of the claw also propels it through the sea at speeds of up to 60 knots. Its
right arm is packed with meat, and detached pincers are even used as a cooking ingredient.
