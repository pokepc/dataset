## Big Jaw Pokémon

Croconaw attacks by opening its huge jaws wide and biting down hard, and once it clamps onto a foe,
it will not let go. Its backward-slanted, forked fangs work like barbed fishhooks, making them
difficult to remove once they sink in.

It has 48 fangs lining its mouth, and if any are lost while it is biting, new ones quickly grow back
in their place. This constant regrowth helps keep its grip powerful and persistent.
