## Mouse Pokémon

Pikachu stores electricity in the pouches on its cheeks, building it up while it sleeps and
releasing it in crackling shocks when threatened, startled, or angered. If it is sleepy or not fully
rested, its power may be weaker, and if it cannot discharge enough energy, it may become stressed.
