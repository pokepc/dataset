## Bubble Frog Pokémon

Froakie covers its body in delicate bubbles to protect its skin and soften the damage it takes when
attacked. It secretes flexible bubbles from its chest and back, using them as a natural shield.

Though it gives off a happy-go-lucky air, Froakie is always keeping a careful watch on its
surroundings. Its playful appearance hides a wary, alert nature.
