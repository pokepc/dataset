## Sand Heap Pokémon

Born from a sand mound playfully built by a child, Sandygast is said to be possessed by the grudges
of the departed. It mainly lives on beaches and is fond of the shovel perched on its head.

If someone puts a hand in its mouth, it can take control of that person and force them to keep
adding sand to its body. It will even get serious and fight children who try to take its shovel
back.
