## Worm Pokémon

Caterpie has a voracious appetite, often devouring leaves larger than its body and even eating a
hundred leaves a day. It clings to trees and other surfaces with suction-cupped feet, letting it
climb slopes, walls, and foliage as it forages among leaves that blend with its green body.
