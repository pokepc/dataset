## Gigantic Pokémon

Eternatus (Eternamax) is sustained by the core on its chest, which absorbs energy from the lands of
Galar to keep it active. This strange source of power is tied to the Dynamax phenomenon.

It is said to have arrived inside a meteorite that fell 20,000 years ago, hinting at a long-standing
connection to the events that shape Galar's unusual energy.
