## Water Fish Pokémon

Wooper usually lives in cold water, sometimes half-buried in mud as it rests. When the temperature
drops or evening comes, it may leave the water and move along the shore in search of food.

When it goes onto land, Wooper coats its body with a slimy, poisonous film. This gooey layer helps
keep its skin from drying out, but touching it barehanded can cause a painful sting.
