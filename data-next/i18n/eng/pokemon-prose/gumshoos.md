## Stakeout Pokémon

Gumshoos is an extremely patient Pokémon that will wait for long periods after catching the faintest
trace of prey. Once it spots Rattata, its favorite meal, it can lose control and pounce at once.

It is active by day and asleep by night, so it rarely encounters the Rattata and Raticate it most
adores eating. Gumshoos was brought to Alola long ago during a massive Rattata outbreak.
