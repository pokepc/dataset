## Poison Pin Pokémon

Nidoran♀ is a small, mild-mannered Pokémon that would rather avoid fighting, but it becomes
dangerous when threatened. Its barbs and small horn secrete powerful venom, and even a tiny scratch
or a single drop of its poison can have severe results.

Its toxic barbs are thought to have developed as protection for its small body. When enraged, it can
release a horrible toxin from its horn. Females are especially sensitive to smells and use their
whiskers to sense wind direction while foraging.
