## Brutal Pokémon

Sharpedo is widely feared as the bully of the sea, a vicious bandit that attacks any ship in its
waters without exception. It can hurtle forward at about 75 mph by drinking in seawater and jetting
it from its rear, though it cannot travel long distances this way.

Its cruel fangs can crunch through iron and tear apart even a supertanker, and if they break, they
grow back quickly. Sharpedo is also very sensitive to the scent of blood, and its skin is specially
textured to reduce drag in the water.
