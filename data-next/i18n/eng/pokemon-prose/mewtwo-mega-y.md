## Genetic Pokémon

Created through years of brutal gene-splicing and DNA engineering, Mega Mewtwo Y was made by
recombining Mew’s genes. Though its DNA is nearly the same as Mew’s, its size and nature are very
different, and the result is said to be among the most savage of Pokémon.

Its battle power is raised to an extreme level, and it thinks only of defeating opponents. To
conserve energy, it often stays perfectly still so it can unleash its full strength in battle.
