## Muscular Pokémon

Gurdurr is so powerfully built that even a group of wrestlers could not make it budge. It
strengthens its body by carrying steel beams, and it likes to show off its muscles to other Pokémon,
especially Machoke and other Gurdurr.

With its strengthened body, Gurdurr skillfully wields its metal beam to bring down buildings. It
excels at demolition rather than construction, and if it feels it has not measured up, it will lie
low for a while.
