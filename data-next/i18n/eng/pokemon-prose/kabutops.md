## Shellfish Pokémon

Kabutops is a slim, fast swimmer whose sleek body is built for moving through water. It tucks in its
limbs to become more compact, folds its razor-sharp sickles close, and can close on prey at great
speed before striking with its claws and scythes.

In ancient warm seas, it hunted underwater and drained the fluids of the prey it cut apart, leaving
the remains for other Pokémon. Its body also shows signs of a shift toward life on land, but it did
not fully adapt before going extinct.
