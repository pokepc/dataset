## Poodle Pokémon

Furfrou’s fluffy coat can be trimmed to make it look more elegant, and the grooming also makes it
move more swiftly. Left on its own, its fur keeps growing longer, but it will only let someone it
trusts cut it.

In Kalos, these Pokémon were once trusted as guardians of the king. There was also a time when
aristocrats competed to create the most exquisite trim for their Furfrou, treating its appearance as
a mark of status.
