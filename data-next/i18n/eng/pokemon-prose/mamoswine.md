## Twin Tusk Pokémon

Mamoswine flourished around the world during the ice age, and traces of it can still be found in
ancient wall paintings. It was once thought to have vanished, but frozen specimens have been dug up
from 10,000-year-old ice or soil, showing how long this Pokémon has existed.

Its impressive tusks are made of ice, and they grow longer and thicker as the weather gets colder.
When the climate warmed after the ice age, its population thinned and most Mamoswine disappeared.
