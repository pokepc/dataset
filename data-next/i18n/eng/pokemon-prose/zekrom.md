## Deep Black Pokémon

Zekrom flies through the Unova region concealed within lightning clouds, and it creates electricity
in its tail. Legends say its tail contains a giant generator, and when the inner part spins like a
motor, it can unleash many bolts of lightning around it.

This legendary Pokémon is said to scorch the world with lightning. Mythology claims it aids those
who seek to build an ideal world, but if people lose righteousness in their hearts, their kingdoms
may be razed by Zekrom’s lightning.
