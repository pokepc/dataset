## Strong Arm Pokémon

Bewear is immensely strong and widely feared, with a reputation as one of the most dangerous Pokémon
in Alola. Its habitat is generally off-limits, and it often waves its arms in a wild display meant
to intimidate anything that gets too close.

Despite its frightening power, Bewear shows affection by hugging those it accepts as friends. That
embrace can crush bones, and many who fail to escape are badly hurt. It also uses martial arts-like
moves to overpower prey, then tucks the subdued prey under its arms.
