## DNA Pokémon

Deoxys is said to have been born when the DNA of a space virus mutated after exposure to a laser
beam. Highly intelligent and psychokinetic, it has a crystalline organ on its chest that seems to
serve as its brain and can fire lasers.

In its Attack Forme, Deoxys is built for superior agility and speed, and it takes on an aggressive
guise. It can also alter its appearance to deceive foes and change its cellular structure to absorb
attacks. It is often linked with sightings of auroras.
