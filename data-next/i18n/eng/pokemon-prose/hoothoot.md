## Owl Pokémon

Hoothoot stands on one foot and can switch feet so quickly that the motion is barely seen, even when
it is attacked. It also keeps a precise rhythm, tilting its head and crying out at the same time
every day.

It has an internal organ that senses the earth’s rotation, giving it a perfect sense of time.
Because of this accuracy, people have long used it as a living clock and sometimes regarded it as a
wise or even divine messenger.
