## Shed Pokémon

Shedinja is a peculiar Pokémon that appears in a Poké Ball when a Nincada evolves. It is completely
motionless, not even breathing, and its hard body seems to be nothing more than an empty shell.

Though its wings never move, Shedinja floats in the air. The hollow space inside it is dark, and a
crack on its back is said to steal the spirit of anyone who peers into it.
