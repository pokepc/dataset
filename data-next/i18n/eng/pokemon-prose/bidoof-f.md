## Plump Mouse Pokémon

Bidoof is rarely flustered and has a calm, unsophisticated expression, with nerves of steel that
leave it unfazed by almost anything. It also seems more agile and active than it looks.

It lives in groups near water and nests there, constantly gnawing on logs, rocks, boulders, and
trees around its home to keep its front teeth worn down. Those teeth grow quickly, and Bidoof has
even wandered into villages to chew on houses without a care.
