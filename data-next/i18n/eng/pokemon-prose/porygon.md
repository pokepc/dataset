## Virtual Pokémon

Porygon is a man-made Pokémon created with advanced research and programming. Made entirely of code
and digital data, it can convert itself back into program form and move freely through cyberspace.
It was the first artificial Pokémon to be created this way, though many of its parts are now
obsolete.

Because it does not breathe and shows no discernible heartbeat, it can function in many
environments. Scientists who made it even dreamed it might one day travel into space, but that feat
has not yet been seen
