## Woolly Crab Pokémon

Crabominable is said to have aimed for the top, only to become lost on a snowy mountain. Forced to
endure the cold, it evolved and grew a coat of fur.

It attacks by throwing wild punches without much thought, and in desperate moments it can even tear
off its own pincers and launch them like rockets. Its pincers store cold and let it smash thick ice
walls to bits.

It has been known to halt avalanches by unleashing a furious flurry of blows.
