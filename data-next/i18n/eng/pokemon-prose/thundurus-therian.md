## Bolt Strike Pokémon

Thundurus flies over Unova hurling immense bolts of lightning from the spikes on its tail. Its
passage leaves behind charred ground and can spark forest fires, which has made it widely disliked.

Stories say this wielder of electricity has battled its nemesis, Tornadus, since ancient times. Yet
the lightning it drives into the earth is also said to enrich the soil.
