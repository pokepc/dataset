## Paleozoic Pokémon

An ancient bug Pokémon from 300 million years ago, Genesect was once feared as a powerful hunter.
Team Plasma later altered it and fitted a cannon on its back, upgrading its weapon into a more
formidable engine of attack.

In this Chill Drive form, Genesect changes to match its held Drive. Its appearance shifts in the
color of its weapon, and Techno Blast takes on a different type.
