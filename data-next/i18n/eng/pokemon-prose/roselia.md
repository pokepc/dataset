## Thorn Pokémon

Roselia’s beautiful flowers give off a relaxing fragrance, and the stronger its aroma, the healthier
it is. Its scent can calm living things and even make foes careless.

It protects the flowers on its arms with sharp, highly poisonous thorns, and it can fire those
thorns at anyone who tries to steal them. It uses different poisons in each hand when attacking.

Roselia raised on clean, nutritionally rich springwater are said to bloom in vivid or rare colors.
