## Stone Home Pokémon

Crustle is highly territorial and fights viciously over land, with the one whose boulder is
shattered losing the battle. If its boulder is broken, it becomes uncertain and starts to weaken.

Its powerful legs can carry heavy slabs for many days, even across arid ground, and it prefers dry
climates. On rainy days, it stays inside its boulder. Its thick claws are its greatest weapons,
strong enough to crack Rhyperior’s carapace.
