## Illusion Fox Pokémon

Hisuian Zoroark forms strong bonds with its own kind and fiercely protects its pack. It uses
illusory scenery and terrifying visions to mislead foes, guarding its den by making intruders lose
their way or trapping those who try to catch it in deception.

It can fool large groups at once, even showing hundreds of people the same illusion. With its
disheveled white fur and bitter energy, it has a grim, deathlike appearance, and it attacks without
regard for its own safety.
