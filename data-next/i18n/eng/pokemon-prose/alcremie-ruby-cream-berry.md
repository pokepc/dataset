## Cream Pokémon

When Alcremie trusts a Trainer, it delights in offering berries decorated with its cream. This
graceful sweetness is part of its bond, as if it is sharing a special treat with someone it relies
on.

When it is content, the cream it secretes from its hands becomes even sweeter and richer. Its calm
mood seems to deepen the cream’s flavor, making it especially indulgent.
