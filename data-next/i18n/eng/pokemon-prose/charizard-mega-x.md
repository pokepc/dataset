## Flame Pokémon

Mega Charizard X soars high on powerful wings in search of worthy opponents, breathing flames hot
enough to melt almost anything. It refuses to scorch foes weaker than itself, but its fire grows
fiercer after harsh battles and with battle experience.

When it unleashes a blast of superhot flame, the fire at the tip of its tail burns more intensely,
sometimes shifting to a bluish-white hue when it becomes furious. Its heat can melt boulders and may
start forest fires unintentionally.
