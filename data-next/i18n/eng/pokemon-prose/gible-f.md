## Land Shark Pokémon

Gible prefers narrow holes and crevices in cave walls, especially places warmed by geothermal heat,
where it can stay warm even in bitter weather. It once lived in much hotter lands, and when the cold
sets in it may huddle with others of its kind.

It lashes out at anything that moves, pouncing from its lair to bite and drag prey inside. Its huge
mouth makes its attacks powerful, but its clumsy strikes can hurt it, chip its teeth, or make them
fall out before they regrow soon after.
