## Sound Wave Pokémon

Noibat lives in pitch-black caves by day and emerges after nightfall to search for ripe fruit. It
uses sonar from its enormous ears to pick out only the ripest fruit, flying through the dark as it
hunts for food.

Its ultrasonic waves can reach 200,000 hertz, and no wavelength of sound is beyond its ability to
produce. These sounds can even make much larger Pokémon dizzy and unable to stand.
