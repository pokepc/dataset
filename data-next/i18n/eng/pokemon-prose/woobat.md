## Bat Pokémon

Woobat lives in dark forests and caves, where it flutters about while emitting ultrasonic waves from
its nose to sense its surroundings and search for bug Pokémon prey.

When it rests, suction from its nostrils lets it cling to cave walls, leaving behind a heart-shaped
mark. Those marks are sometimes seen as signs that Woobat live there, and they are said to bring
good fortune.
