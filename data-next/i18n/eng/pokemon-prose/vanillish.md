## Icy Snow Pokémon

Vanillish is a hardy Pokémon that has existed since the Ice Age and is still found in snowy
mountains. It can create ice particles or spray grains of ice to cool the air around it, hide
itself, and freeze opponents before shattering them with a headbutt.

On hot days, its body can melt, though refreezing can restore it with some warping. It grows by
drinking pure water, can be hard to spot in warm sunlight, and is especially popular in very hot
regions. Despite its chilling attacks, it is considered kind.
