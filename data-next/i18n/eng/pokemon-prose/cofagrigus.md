## Coffin Pokémon

Cofagrigus is said to lure in those who come too close, trapping them inside its body and leaving
them mummified. It is known to pose as an ornate coffin to punish grave robbers, and its body is
covered in sparkling pure gold.

It is also said to enjoy eating gold nuggets. Ancient tombs are often decorated with images of
Cofagrigus, where it serves as a symbol of the wealth once held by old kings. People say it has
forgotten that it was once human.
