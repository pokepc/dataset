## Sickle Grass Pokémon

Fomantis spends the day basking in sunlight and sleeping peacefully, spreading its four leaves to
soak up the light. Regular exposure to the sun helps it develop vivid coloration, and the tip of its
head gives off a pleasant aroma.

When night falls, it wakes and moves around in search of a safer place to rest. Bathed in sunlight,
it releases a sweet, refreshing scent that often draws Bug Pokémon, including Cutiefly, to the tall
grass where it hides.
