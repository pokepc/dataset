## Mock Kelp Pokémon

Dragalge blends in with seaweed to ambush prey, then sprays a poisonous liquid that can corrode
metal. It attacks anything that enters its territory, and its poison is so strong it can eat through
a tanker’s hull.

It is said that ships wandering into seas where Dragalge live may never return. To generate dragon
energy, it raises the plume on its head above the ocean’s surface and bathes it in sunlight.
