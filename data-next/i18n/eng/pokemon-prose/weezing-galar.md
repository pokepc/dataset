## Poison Gas Pokémon

Galarian Weezing lives and grows by absorbing poison gas, dust, and germs found in garbage. It is
drawn to the fumes from rotted trash, and it may settle in a dirty house to search through waste at
night.

Its twin bodies constantly shrink and inflate to mix the gases inside, making the toxins stronger
and the stench even fouler. When different poison gases meet, two Koffing can fuse into a Weezing
over many years, and very rarely, a sudden mutation can join the twins into this form.
