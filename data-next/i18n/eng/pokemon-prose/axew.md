## Tusk Pokémon

Axew uses its tusks to crush hard berries, and the repeated regrowth of those tusks makes them
stronger, sharper, and sturdier each time. Its tusks can break, but new ones grow back quickly.

It nests in the ground and marks its territory by gashing trees with its tusks. Axew also tests its
strength and plays with others by knocking tusks together, without worrying much when they snap.
