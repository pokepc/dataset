## Prism Pokémon

Necrozma (Dawn Wings) is said to have come from another world and to have lain asleep underground
for ages, much like the Ultra Beasts. It survives by absorbing light, and when it cannot feed on it,
impurities build up within and around it, leaving its body darkened and motionless.

Driven to find light, it rages in a pained state and unleashes laser beams with terrible force. Its
vicious disposition and constant need for light make its attacks capable of cutting through almost
anything.
