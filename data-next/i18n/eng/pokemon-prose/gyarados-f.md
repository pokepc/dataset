## Atrocious Pokémon

Gyarados is said to become wildly violent after evolution, when its brain changes and its ferocity
is greatly stirred. Once it ramps up, it does not calm until everything around it has been burned or
demolished, and records say its rampages have lasted for a month.

Huge and vicious, it is rarely seen in the wild. Old tales describe it appearing in times of
conflict, leaving blazing ruins behind, destroying villages, cities, and fields, and firing
white-hot Hyper Beams that incinerate all in their path.
