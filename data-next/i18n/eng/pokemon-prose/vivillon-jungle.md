## Scale Pokémon

Vivillon with many different patterns are found all over the world, and this one is the Jungle
Pattern. Its wing pattern is shaped by the climate and topography of its habitat, and it scatters
colorful scales.

Forms only differ in appearance, and Vivillon’s pattern is fixed by the region tied to its original
Trainer. It cannot be changed, even if it is traded or the system region is altered.
