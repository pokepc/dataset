## Life Pokémon

Legends say Xerneas can share eternal life, and that it once slept for a thousand years as a tree
before awakening again. In its Neutral Mode, it is known by its resting appearance outside of
battle.

When the horns on its head shine in seven colors, it is said to be sharing everlasting life. Its
forms differ only in appearance, with Neutral Mode outside battle and Active Mode during battle.
