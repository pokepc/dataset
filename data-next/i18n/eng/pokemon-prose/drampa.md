## Placid Pokémon

Drampa is a kind, friendly Pokémon that especially loves children, and it often comes down from the
mountains to play with them in town. It is protective of kids and will stand guard when they are in
danger.

Its mountain home lies very high above the towns below, and it visits only on rare occasions. But if
Drampa is angered, its gentle nature gives way to fierce breath and violent gales that can devastate
everything nearby.
