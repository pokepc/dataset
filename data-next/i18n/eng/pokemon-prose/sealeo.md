## Ball Roll Pokémon

Sealeo lives on drift ice in herds, and when it hunts it swims out to sea, relying on scent to find
prey. Its powerful flippers can break ice, and it is said to dive into the water several times a
day.

This Pokémon has a very sensitive nose and uses it to examine new things by touch, smell, and feel.
It often balances and spins round objects on the tip of its nose, including Spheal and even Poké
Balls, as a form of play and a way to judge them.
