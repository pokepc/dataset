## Pitch-Black Pokémon

Mega Darkrai can lull people and Pokémon to sleep and draw them into dreams, and it is said to be
active on nights of the new moon. On moonless nights, it is associated with terrifying nightmares,
and it may appear in those dreams as part of strange incidents.

It is known to drive intruders from its territory by wrapping them in deep, nightmarish slumber.
Though this defense unsettles those around it, it is not meant to cause harm.
