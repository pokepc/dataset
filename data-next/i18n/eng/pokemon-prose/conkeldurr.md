## Muscular Pokémon

Conkeldurr is said to have taught humans how to make concrete more than 2,000 years ago, and the
concrete it mixes is far more durable than ordinary material even when the ingredients are the same.

It uses concrete pillars as walking canes and can swing them freely in battle. Rather than depend on
raw strength alone, it favors moves that make use of centrifugal force, and when it goes all out, it
throws aside its pillars and leaps at opponents to pummel them with its fists.
