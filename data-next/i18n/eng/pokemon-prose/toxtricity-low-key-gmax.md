## Punk Pokémon

When Toxtricity sounds as if it is strumming a guitar, it is actually clawing at the protrusions on
its chest to generate electricity. Its performance-like displays come from this unusual way of
building up power.

Short-tempered and aggressive, it also chugs stagnant water to absorb any toxins it may contain. It
can turn even polluted water to its advantage while feeding its electric nature.
