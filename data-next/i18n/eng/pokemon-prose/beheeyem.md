## Cerebral Pokémon

Beheeyem has strong psychic powers that let it control opponents and rewrite their memories, even
tampering with their minds. It may leave those it faces with memories changed, as though they had
already been altered before.

It communicates by flashing its three fingers in three different colors, though the meaning of those
patterns has never been decoded. Sometimes seen drifting above wheat fields, it has also been linked
to a mysterious disappearance when it visits farms.
