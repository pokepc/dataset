## Cocoon Pokémon

Kakuna is almost incapable of moving, spending most of its time clinging to trees or hiding under
leaves and in the nooks of branches while it waits for evolution. Though it appears still, its shell
is hardening as it prepares for its next stage, and the shell can feel hot to the touch from all the
activity inside.
