## Abundance Pokémon

Landorus in its Therian Forme is hailed as the Guardian of the Fields, because lands it visits are
blessed with abundant harvests. It draws power from lightning and wind, creating energy that
enriches the soil and helps crops grow large and plentiful.

When the incarnations of wind and lightning clash, it appears to calm the conflict. After the storms
subside, the land is left fertile and ready for bountiful crops.
