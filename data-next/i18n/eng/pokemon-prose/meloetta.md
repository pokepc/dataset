## Melody Pokémon

Meloetta sings melodies with a special vocalization method that can influence the feelings of those
who hear them. Its songs can make Pokémon happy or sad, and many famous songs are said to have been
inspired by the music it plays.

Meloetta changes form when it uses Relic Song in battle, then returns to its Aria Forme outside of
battle. Its melodies are notable for the emotional power they carry.
