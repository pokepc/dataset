## Bronze Pokémon

Bronzor is found in ancient tombs and ruins, where its strange shape has often been compared to
implements from long ago. Researchers have also noted that X-ray photos reveal nothing inside it,
adding to its mystery.

Its back bears a pattern that ancient people believed held sacred or mysterious power, and it may
even have once reflected like a mirror. Common lore says polishing it can make it reflect the truth,
though Bronzor itself dislikes being polished.
