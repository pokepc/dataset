## Loitering Pokémon

Yungoos is a relentless forager with sharp fangs that will bite at anything it encounters. It was
brought to Alola from another region, where it now marches through streets in search of prey.

It will eat almost anything, but it prefers fresh, living food. Its long torso is dominated by a
large stomach, and its appetite is so great that feeding one can become very costly. After wandering
all day in search of food, it often collapses from exhaustion and falls asleep at dusk.
