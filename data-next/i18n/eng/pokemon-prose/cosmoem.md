## Protostar Pokémon

Cosmoem lies motionless, seeming almost dead, yet its body is faintly warm. Ancient people called it
the cocoon of the stars, and some believed it had come from another world, even worshipping it in
old Alola.

It grows by absorbing light and starlight, building up energy within the black core hidden by its
hard shell. It also sucks in dust at an astounding rate as it prepares for evolution, and its golden
shell is said to be harder than any known material.
