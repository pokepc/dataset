## Collective Pokémon

Seven Binacle come together to form one Barbaracle, and it fights with the strength of all seven.
The Binacle that serves as the head gives orders to the limbs, but the legs and hands can also move
on their own when needed.

With an eye on each palm, Barbaracle can keep watch in every direction. In a tight spot, its limbs
will act independently to help ensure an enemy’s defeat.
