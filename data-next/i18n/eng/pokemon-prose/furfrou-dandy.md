## Poodle Pokémon

Trimming this Furfrou’s fluffy fur makes it look more elegant and also lets it move more swiftly. In
Kalos, these Pokémon were once the designated guardians of the king, and aristocrats competed to
have their coats cut into the finest styles.

Its fur keeps growing if left alone, but it will only let someone it trusts trim it. This trim
changes only its appearance, and it can return to Natural Form after five days unless it is trimmed
again.
