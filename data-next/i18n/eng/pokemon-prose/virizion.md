## Grassland Pokémon

Legends tell of Virizion working with Cobalion and Terrakion to protect the Pokémon of the Unova
region, and of Virizion fighting humans to defend its friends. These stories continue to be passed
down.

Its head bears horns as sharp as blades. With whirlwind-like, rapid movements, it darts around
opponents, confounding them before slicing them with its horns.
