## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally’s hidden strength is awakened by a strong bond of trust with its Trainer. Once its control
mask is destroyed and it evolves into this form, it is freed from its burden and its speed increases
greatly.

Its trust in its partner strengthens its will and allows it to change its type at will, a
flexibility it can display clearly in battle. In this awakened form, it has become the Pokémon known
as Silvally.
