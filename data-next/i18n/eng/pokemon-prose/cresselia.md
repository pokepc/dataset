## Lunar Pokémon

Cresselia is said to represent the crescent moon, and its graceful form is reminiscent of it. As it
flies across the night sky, it leaves a brilliant trail of light behind it, and shiny particles
drift from its wings like a veil.

Around the quarter moon, the aurora from its tail lengthens and undulates beautifully. Those who
sleep holding Cresselia’s feather are assured of joyful dreams, and it is even likened to a heavenly
maiden associated with the Milky Way.
