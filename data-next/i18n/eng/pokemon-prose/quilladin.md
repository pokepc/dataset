## Spiny Armor Pokémon

Quilladin relies on its sturdy shell to deflect predators’ attacks, then counterattacks with the
sharp quills it hides within. Its tough protection makes it well suited to defend itself when
threatened.

It also strengthens its lower body by charging into others, though it is very kind and does not
start fights. Rather than seeking conflict, it seems to use its rough training to stay ready for
danger.
