## Spark Pokémon

Luxio lives in small groups and communicates by resting its forelegs on others, sending rhythmic
electric pulses through its claws. Its sharp claws hold strong electricity; even a light scratch can
cause foes to faint, and it may extend them in battle to unleash tremendous voltage.
