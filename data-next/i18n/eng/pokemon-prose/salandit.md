## Toxic Lizard Pokémon

Salandit lives in volcanoes and dry, craggy places. It heats a fluid from its venom sacs with the
flame in its tail to make a sweet-smelling poisonous gas, then uses it to disorient and immobilize
prey and foes before they realize it is there.

The gas can also attract Bug Pokémon, which it then attacks. Males are devoted to females, giving
them most of their food and following their commands so closely that poor nutrition keeps the males
from evolving.
