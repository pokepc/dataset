## Catty Pokémon

Glameow is a very fickle Pokémon, but that unpredictability is part of its charm. It purrs when
affectionate and claws when displeased, and its sharp glare can leave foes in a mild hypnotic state.

When happy, it moves its tail with graceful, ribbonlike beauty. It also hides sharp claws and a
spiteful streak, sometimes hooking its Trainer’s nose if it is not fed, making it a tricky Pokémon
to antagonize.
