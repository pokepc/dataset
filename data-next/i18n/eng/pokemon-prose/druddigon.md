## Cave Pokémon

Druddigon lives in caves and narrow tunnels, often taking over nests or burrows dug by other
Pokémon. It lies in wait for prey in these cramped passages, then races after it with sharp claws,
and it is vicious enough to confront enemies head-on.

Its red facial skin is harder than rock, letting it charge straight into foes. Druddigon also relies
on sunlight to warm its body; if it gets too cold, it can no longer move.
