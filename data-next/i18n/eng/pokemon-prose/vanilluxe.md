## Snowstorm Pokémon

Vanilluxe makes snow clouds inside its body by swallowing large amounts of water, then blasts
violent blizzards from its two mouths. When both heads are in agreement, or when its anger reaches a
breaking point, it can bury everything around it in snow, freezing friend and foe alike.

Each head has a brain, and the two-headed Pokémon can live relatively well even if it loses one of
them. People believe it formed when two Vanillish froze together, and its body temperature is about
21 degrees Fahrenheit.
