## Megaton Pokémon

Golem’s boulder-like body is extraordinarily hard, able to withstand dynamite blasts without a
scratch, though it dislikes rain and humidity. It lives on mountains and is known to roll downhill
in great numbers after earthquakes, so mountain grooves are sometimes cut to guide its path.

Once a year, it sheds its shell, which hardens and then crumbles away into soil that can enrich
fields. When it molts, its body becomes soft and whitish before its hide hardens again in the air.
