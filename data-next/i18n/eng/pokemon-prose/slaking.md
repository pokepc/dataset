## Lazy Pokémon

Slaking spends most of its day lying on its side or lounging on the ground, reaching only for grass
that grows within its reach. It rarely bothers to move, and only rolls or shifts to another spot
when it has eaten everything nearby.

Its feeding leaves broad rings in grassy fields, marked by the grass it has cleared while still
prone. It is known as one of the laziest Pokémon, but it can unleash terrifying power when it
finally releases the energy it has been storing.
