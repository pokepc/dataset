## Single Bloom Pokémon

Floette flutters through fields of flowers, tending to blossoms that are starting to wilt and
pouring its own energy into their care. It is deeply devoted to flower beds and is quick to take
offense at anyone who ruins them.

When a well-tended bed comes into bloom, Floette appears to celebrate with an elegant dance. It can
also draw out the hidden power of flowers and use them in battle, with more beautiful blossoms
holding greater power.
