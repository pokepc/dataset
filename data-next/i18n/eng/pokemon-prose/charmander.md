## Lizard Pokémon

Charmander prefers hot places, and when rain falls, steam is said to rise from the flame at the tip
of its tail. That flame has burned since birth, and its life would end if it ever went out.

The tail flame also reflects Charmander's condition. When it is healthy, the fire burns brightly and
intensely, even if it gets a little wet, but if it is weak, the flame burns faintly.
