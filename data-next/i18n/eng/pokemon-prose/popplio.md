## Sea Lion Pokémon

Popplio uses water bubbles and balloons in battle, practicing diligently each day to make them
larger and more powerful. Its balloon-making skill grows through persistent training, and it is
known for being a hard worker.

It snorts body fluids from its nose to inflate its bouncy balloons, then sends them at opponents.
Popplio can also ride on top of these balloons to leap higher, making it a surprisingly acrobatic
fighter.
