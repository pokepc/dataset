## Hard Scale Pokémon

With giant claws it can flash-cut through seawater or even the air, and it battles skillfully with
its six arms. Its claws can be extended or retracted at will, and it uses even the small claws on
its front legs to finish off opponents, seizing every opening to win.

Its body is protected by a shell as hard as diamond. Golisopod usually spends its time peacefully
meditating in caves deep beneath the sea, or dwelling in sunken ships and holes in the seabed. It is
relentless in battle and will do anything it.
