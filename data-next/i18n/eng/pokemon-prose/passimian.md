## Teamwork Pokémon

Passimian live in groups of about 20, and each member has an assigned role. Their remarkable
teamwork keeps the troop together, and they will not abandon a comrade when trouble comes.

They battle with hard berries, which serve as both weapons and a staple of their diet. The troop
follows the boss’s orders when searching for berries, and the one that can throw a berry farthest
leads the group. Techniques are passed down from boss to troop over generations.
