## Licking Pokémon

Lickilicky can wrap objects with its amazingly extensible tongue, which can stretch to many times
its body length and even reach over 10 times its height. It uses that tongue far more skillfully
than its hands or feet, able to pick up tiny objects with surprising precision.

Its tongue is always wet with slobber, and its saliva contains corrosive and numbing components that
can dissolve or decompose things. Getting too close may leave you soaked in drool, and its lick can
leave numbness that does not fade.
