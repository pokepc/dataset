## Ruinous Pokémon

Chien-Pao is said to have been formed from the hatred of those who once perished by the sword, that
resentment wrapped in snow and given life as a Pokémon.

It can command vast amounts of fallen snow, enough to move 100 tons of it at once. Despite that
fearsome power, it is known to play innocently, leaping in and out of the avalanches it creates.
