## Scaly Pokémon

Jangmo-o lives high in remote mountains where humans are absent, and its metallic scale-smacking
echoes through the peaks. It uses the noises to communicate with others of its kind and to express
its feelings, while its scales are actually fur hardened like metal.

It trains by battling other Jangmo-o, growing little by little as the duels strengthen both skill
and spirit. In battle, it fiercely protects itself and never shows an enemy its back, a valiant
disposition that ancient warriors admired.
