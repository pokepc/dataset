## Mole Pokémon

Diglett spends most of its life just below the surface, moving through shallow tunnels and leaving
raised earth behind. It prefers dark places, sometimes popping up in caves or at night, and it is
repelled by bright sunlight.

It feeds on plant roots underground, and its burrowing can be either troublesome for farmers or
useful, since the soil it turns over is left neatly tilled and rich for crops. Its thin skin makes
it especially weak when exposed to light.
