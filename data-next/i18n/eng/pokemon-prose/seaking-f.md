## Goldfish Pokémon

In autumn, Seaking gather in rivers and creeks to spawn, and their numbers can make the water seem
brilliantly red. Their bodies grow richer in color during this season, especially the males, who
perform courtship dances on the riverbed to attract mates.

A sharp, drill-like horn lets Seaking bore holes in boulders and hollow out nests in streambeds.
These nests help keep the eggs from being washed away, and both parents take turns guarding them for
more than a month. They will defend their family fiercely.
