## Transport Pokémon

Lapras is a gentle, highly intelligent Pokémon that can understand human speech. It enjoys carrying
people and Pokémon across the sea on its back, offering a smooth and comfortable ride, and in some
places it is an important means of transportation over water.

Once driven close to extinction by overhunting and poaching, Lapras has become more common in some
regions under protection. It is rarely aggressive, and when it is in a good mood it may sing a
beautiful, plaintive cry that carries over the water.
