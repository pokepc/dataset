## Alpha Pokémon

Myth says this Pokémon was born before the universe existed and emerged from an egg in a place of
nothingness, then shaped the world and all that exists. It is also described as the being that
created the universe with its 1,000 arms.

In Hisuian lore, Arceus is the heavenly source of the light that shines across the region, guiding
and protecting all Pokémon. This form changes its type to match a held Plate through Multitype.
