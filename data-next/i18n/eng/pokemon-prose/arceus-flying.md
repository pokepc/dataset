## Alpha Pokémon

It is said in mythology that this Pokémon was born before the universe even existed, emerging from
an egg in a place where there was nothing before shaping the world. Legends of Sinnoh tell that
Arceus created all things, and that it shaped the universe with its 1,000 arms.

In Hisui, it is revered as the heavenly source of light that shines across the land, with a radiance
said to guide and protect all Pokémon. This Flying Type form changes its type to match a held Plate
through Multitype.
