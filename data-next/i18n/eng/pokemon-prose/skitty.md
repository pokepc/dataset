## Kitten Pokémon

Skitty is easily captivated by moving things and will chase them in circles, often pursuing its own
tail until it gets dizzy. Its playful, adorable behavior makes it very popular as a pet, though it
is said to be difficult to earn its trust.

In the wild, Skitty lives in holes in forest trees. When it feels threatened, it puffs out its tail
and gives a sharp growl, but its cute antics remain what it is best known for.
