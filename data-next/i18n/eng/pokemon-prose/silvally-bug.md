## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally’s hidden strength awakens through a deep bond of trust with its Trainer, allowing it to
change its type at will. Once it evolves and sheds the heavy control mask, its true power is
released and its speed increases greatly.

With its will strengthened by that bond, it is no longer held back by the restraints it once wore.
In battle, its adaptable nature is especially striking, as it can shift its type to meet the moment.
