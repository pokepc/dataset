## Single Bloom Pokémon

Flabébé draws out and controls the hidden power of flowers, and the flower it carries is likely part
of its body. Its pollen crown is said to have hidden healing properties, and its flower’s color and
shape are set when it is encountered.

When it finds a flower it likes, it stays with it for life and floats along on the wind with an
untroubled heart. Until it finds the right flower, it keeps traveling in search of one that matches
what it wants.
