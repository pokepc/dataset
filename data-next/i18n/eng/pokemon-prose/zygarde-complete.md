## Order Pokémon

When the Kalos region’s ecosystem falls into disarray, Zygarde appears and reveals its hidden power.
From deep within its cave, it is thought to watch over those who would destroy the balance of
nature.

In this Complete Forme, it is formed from about half of its cells and serves as a guardian of the
ecosystem. It shows no mercy to those who oppose it, and some believe an even greater power lies
hidden within it.
