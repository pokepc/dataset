## Float Whale Pokémon

Wailord is the largest of all identified Pokémon, a giant that moves languorously through the open
sea while taking in massive amounts of food with its enormous mouth. Its huge size makes it a
popular sight, and watching it is a favorite activity in many places.

Wailord travels and hunts in pods, leaping from the water to herd prey together before swallowing
the gathered mass in one gulp. By raising its nostrils above the surface, it can breathe while
diving to nearly 10,000 feet on a single breath.
