## Playful Pokémon

Pancham does its best to look intimidating, often fixing others with a fierce glare and a scary
face. Yet it cannot hide its cheerful side for long, and if it loses focus or receives a pat on the
head, it may break into a grin.

It chews on a leaf mainly to look cool, though the leaf has no real purpose. Mischievous by nature,
Pancham follows Pangoro like a henchman and learns by imitating the master it chooses to look up to.
