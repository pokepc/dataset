## Alpha Pokémon

In mythology, Arceus is said to have existed before the universe and to have emerged from an egg in
a place where nothing yet existed. Legends tell that it shaped the world, and even all of creation,
with its 1,000 arms.

In Hisuian lore, it is the heavenly source of light that shines across the region, guiding and
protecting all Pokémon. As Dragon Type, this form changes with a held Plate through Multitype.
