## Puppy Pokémon

Yamper is a popular herding dog in the Galar region, valued for helping people with their work. It
is especially motivated by treats, and its gluttonous nature means it often lends a hand only when
food is promised.

As it runs, Yamper gathers electricity at the base of its tail and crackles with stored power. This
lively charge seems to accompany its constant motion, making it as energetic as it is useful.
