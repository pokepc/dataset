## Hoodlum Pokémon

Scrafty is an aggressive, territorial Pokémon that guards its turf for its whole life. It may seem
rude and unruly, but it takes very good care of its family and friends, and it has a leader with the
largest crest in the group.

It protects itself by pulling up its shed skin while it kicks, and its attacks are strong enough to
smash concrete blocks and even shatter concrete pillars. It also taunts opponents by spitting and
can spray acidic liquid from its mouth.
