## DNA Pokémon

Deoxys is said to have arisen when the DNA of a space virus mutated after exposure to a laser beam.
Highly intelligent and wielding psychokinetic power, it shoots lasers from the crystalline organ on
its chest, which is said to function as its brain.

In its Speed Forme, Deoxys’s body is configured for superior agility and quick movement. It can also
alter its appearance to confuse enemies, and when it changes form, an aurora appears as it adjusts
its cellular structure.
