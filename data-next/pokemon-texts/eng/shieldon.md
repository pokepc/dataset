## Shield Pokémon

Shieldon lived in primeval jungles millions of years ago, and its fossils suggest a calm,
mild-mannered, herbivorous Pokémon with an exceptionally tough face. Its heavily armored hide made
it hard to challenge, and it could dig up tree roots to eat without much trouble.

It likes polishing its face against trees and rocks, and that sturdy facial armor has rarely shown
damage in fossil finds. Yet it is vulnerable to attacks from behind, so few enemies would have dared
to face it head-on.
