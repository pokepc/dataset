## Mouse Pokémon

Raichu stores electricity in its body, and when the charge builds up, it becomes feisty and more
aggressive. Its muscles are stimulated by this buildup, and even its Trainer must be careful around
it. It can unleash bursts of electricity that reach 100,000 volts or more, enough to fell much
larger foes.

Its long tail acts as a ground to protect it from its own power. When its pouches are full, its ears
