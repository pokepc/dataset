## Sharp Blade Pokémon

Pawniard’s entire body is made of blades, which it keeps sharp by rubbing them on stones by the
river. Each Pawniard favors its own sharpening stone, and its cutting edges help it bring down
opponents.

It serves a Bisharp boss and follows orders precisely in battle. Pawniard often attacks in groups,
clinging to prey or enemies and sinking its blades into them, then clanging its two blades together
after a slash. Each one trains hard, hoping to lead someday.
