## Fire Horse Pokémon

Rapidash is a highly competitive runner that loves to chase anything faster than itself, eager to
turn speed into a race. It is often seen cantering through fields and plains, but when it becomes
serious, it breaks into a fierce gallop that can reach 150 mph in only a few steps.

At full speed, its four hooves barely seem to touch the ground, and its fiery mane blazes and
sparkles in the wind. Among its herd, the fastest runner leads the others and sets the pace and
direction of travel.
