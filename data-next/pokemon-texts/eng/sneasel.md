## Sharp Claw Pokémon

Sneasel is a cunning, savage Pokémon that hides in the dark and waits to ambush prey. It can scale
trees and steep cliffs with its hooked claws, and it uses those claws to strike vulnerable spots or
make opponents flinch when suddenly revealed.

It is especially known for raiding nests. Sneasel drives bird Pokémon away, steals eggs while the
parents are gone, and may work with another Sneasel to lure guardians off before taking the food.
