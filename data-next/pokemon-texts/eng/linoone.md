## Rushing Pokémon

Linoone is built for explosive speed, sprinting full out in perfectly straight lines and topping 60
miles per hour. It has trouble with curved roads and winding paths, and when it must avoid an
obstacle, it needs to make a sudden right-angle turn or come to a screeching halt before changing
direction.
