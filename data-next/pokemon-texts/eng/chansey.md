## Egg Pokémon

Chansey is a gentle, kindhearted Pokémon that is said to bring happiness to those lucky enough to
catch one. Rare and elusive, it is also known for fleeing quickly and walking carefully so the egg
it carries will not break.

Chansey lays several highly nutritious eggs each day. These eggs are delicious, eagerly eaten even
by those with little appetite, and are prized by many Pokémon and people as a food ingredient.

This purehearted Pokémon shares its eggs with injured Pokémon or people, but may withhold a.
