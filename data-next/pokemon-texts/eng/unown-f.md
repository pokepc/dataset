## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is shaped like ancient writing or hieroglyphs, and its many forms resemble letters from old
texts. Researchers still do not know whether the writings inspired Unown or the other way around,
and its strange appearance has led to endless mystery.

Its flat, thin body is usually found stuck to walls, where its shape seems to carry some meaning.
Unown are also said to communicate telepathically, and when several are together, an unusual power
is said to emerge.
