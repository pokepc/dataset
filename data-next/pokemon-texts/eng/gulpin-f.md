## Stomach Pokémon

Almost all of Gulpin’s body is its stomach, while its heart and brain are tiny in comparison.
Because of this, it can swallow prey or objects as large as itself, and its harsh digestive fluids
or enzymes quickly dissolve anything it ingests.

Its stomach can even break down scrap iron and leaves nothing undigested. While digestion is under
way, it gives off vile, overpowering gases.
