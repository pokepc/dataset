## Bat Pokémon

Golbat is a stealthy night hunter that flits through the darkness in search of fresh blood. It
sneaks up on sleeping prey or attacks from behind, using its four sharp fangs to puncture even tough
hides and drain blood quickly.

Its fangs are hollow like straws, and it can gulp down over 10 ounces in a single feeding. Once it
starts biting, it may keep draining its victim until it is full, even if the heavy meal makes flying
clumsy or impossible.
