## Gear Pokémon

Klink is made of two minigears that form a fixed pair and are closer than twins. They mesh only with
each other, and when they interlock and spin around one another, they generate the energy they need
to live.

The two gears are predetermined to fit together, and they will rebound from other Klink instead of
meshing. It is even suspected that ancient people were inspired by Klink when they invented the
first gears.
