## Spikes Pokémon

Rhyhorn is a strong but not very bright Pokémon with a one-track mind. It charges in a straight line
and cannot turn well, so once it starts running, it keeps going until it falls asleep or crashes
into something.

Its massive bones and thick hide let it smash through obstacles, topple buildings, and even shatter
skyscrapers or mountains with its tackles. It may feel the impact later, but in the moment it barely
notices what is in its way.
