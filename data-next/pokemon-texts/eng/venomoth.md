## Poison Moth Pokémon

Venomoth is nocturnal and becomes active only at night, when it hunts small insects drawn to
streetlights. Its large wings are covered with dustlike scales that are hard to remove and can leak
poison on contact.

When Venomoth flaps its wings, it scatters poisonous powder or highly toxic dust all around. The
color of the scales can matter too: darker powder is poisonous, while lighter scales can cause
paralysis and leave those who touch them unable to stand.
