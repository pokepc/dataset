## Disaster Pokémon

Mega Absol is known for sensing the warning signs of natural disasters with its curved horn and
appearing before people to signal danger. Swift and sure-footed, it ranges through mountains and
fields, though it usually lives in harsh mountain terrain and seldom comes down.
