## Leaf Pokémon

A sweet aroma drifts from the leaf on Chikorita's head. It is gentle and docile, and it loves to
bask in warm sunlight, using that leaf to find cozy places. Its leaf can also sense temperature and
humidity.

When threatened, Chikorita waves its leaf to keep foes at bay. The same fragrant scent spreads
around it, calming nearby Pokémon and giving battles a strangely friendly, peaceful feeling.
