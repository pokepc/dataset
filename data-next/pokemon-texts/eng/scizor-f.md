## Pincer Pokémon

Scizor has a steel-hard body and is not easily fazed by ordinary attacks, with even its muscles made
of metal. Its shell has been shown to be stronger than forged steel, while its heavy pincers can
crush hard objects and even boulders into scraps.

It raises its eye-patterned pincers to intimidate foes, making it look as though it has three heads.
Its wings are not for flying; it flaps them quickly to regulate its body temperature and keep its
metal body from overheating.
