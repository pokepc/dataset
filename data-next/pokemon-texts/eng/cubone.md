## Lonely Pokémon

Cubone always wears the skull of its dead mother, hiding its true face from view. When it feels sad,
lonely, or remembers her, it cries loudly, and its tears leave marks on the skull it wears.

Its sobs echo hollowly inside the skull, sometimes sounding like a sad melody or a plaintive rattle.
On nights when it sees a likeness of its mother in the full moon, it weeps even more for the loss it
cannot forget.
