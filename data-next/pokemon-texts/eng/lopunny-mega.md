## Rabbit Pokémon

Mega Lopunny is intensely wary and keeps a close watch on its surroundings, quickly bounding away at
the first sign of danger. It is also highly attentive to its appearance, grooming its ears
carefully, and its soft, light fur changes seasonally to provide warmth when the weather turns cold.

Though it dislikes fighting, it can answer a threat with forceful, graceful kicks from its lithe
legs. Its ears seem delicate, and rough handling may provoke an immediate attack.
