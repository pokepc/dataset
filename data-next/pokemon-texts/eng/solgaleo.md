## Sunne Pokémon

Solgaleo is said to live in another world, and when the light radiating from its body bursts forth,
even the darkest night can shine like midday. Its brilliance can make it seem almost like the sun
itself, dispelling darkness wherever it goes.

Ancient writings call it the Beast That Devours the Sun. Said to be a male evolution of Cosmog, it
is also linked to the opening of Ultra Wormholes, through which energy and life-forms from other
worlds may be drawn here when its third eye awakens.
