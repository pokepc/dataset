## Seven Spot Pokémon

Orbeetle is famous for its high intelligence, and the great size of its brain is said to reflect
immense psychic power. It uses that power to observe and study its surroundings with remarkable
precision.

By releasing psychic energy, Orbeetle can keep track of what is happening around it, even at
distances of more than six miles. Its keen awareness makes it an exceptional watcher and
investigator.
