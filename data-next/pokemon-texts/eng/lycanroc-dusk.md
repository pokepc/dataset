## Wolf Pokémon

Lycanroc’s quick, swift movements can confuse its enemies while it steadily drives prey into a
corner. Calm and cautious, it keeps its fangs aimed at an opponent’s weak spots and uses its claws,
fangs, and the sharp rocks in its mane as weapons.

The rocks jutting from its mane are razor sharp, and fragments that break off are sometimes
treasured as good luck charms. When raised well from a young age, it can become a loyal partner that
will never betray its Trainer.
