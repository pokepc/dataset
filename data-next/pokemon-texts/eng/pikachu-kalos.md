## Mouse Pokémon

Pikachu stores electricity in the sacs on its cheeks, building up enough power that a burst can
equal a lightning bolt. It may discharge when threatened, when awakened, or when it encounters
something unfamiliar, and groups of Pikachu can even help spark lightning storms.
