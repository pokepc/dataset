## Scorching Pokémon

Talonflame soars into battle with embers scattering from the gaps between its feathers, especially
when it is stirred up by an exciting fight. Its tough wings keep fire from passing through them, and
long ago, people even used its wings to make firefighters’ outfits.

It mainly hunts bird Pokémon, including Wingull and Pikipek. Diving toward prey at speeds of up to
310 mph, it strikes with powerful kicks, seizing its target firmly in its talons and leaving little
chance to escape.
