## Transform Pokémon

Ditto can reorganize its cells to transform into almost any shape or life-form it sees, even copying
an opponent’s genetic code into a near-perfect duplicate. Its transformations are remarkably
flexible, though the result can be flawed if it must rely on memory or if it relaxes and returns to
normal.

When it meets another Ditto, it speeds up to match the other exactly. Its disguise is usually
dependable, but it may fail if startled into laughter. When sleeping, it changes into a stone to
avoid attack.
