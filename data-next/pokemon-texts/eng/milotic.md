## Tender Pokémon

Milotic is admired as one of the most beautiful Pokémon, with rainbow-colored scales that shift hue
with the viewing angle. Many works of art have been inspired by its elegant appearance, and those
who behold it are said to have their combative or hostile feelings calmed.

It lives at the bottom of large, clear lakes and is said to rise when people are in conflict. When
its body glows a vivid pink, it releases a soothing wave of energy that quiets troubled hearts and
eases bitter feuding.
