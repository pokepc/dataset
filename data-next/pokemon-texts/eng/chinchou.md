## Angler Pokémon

Chinchou lives in the dark depths beyond sunlight, where its flashing antennae are its main way to
communicate with others. Those antennae originally evolved from fins and carry both positive and
negative charges, creating strong electricity that can even make the Pokémon tingle slightly.

It can discharge electricity from the tips of its two antennae to shock or stun prey and enemies.
Its softly glowing antennae may also lure prey, making it useful for night fishing.
