## Tiny Robin Pokémon

Fletchling is an amiable Pokémon that is easy to train, and it uses beautiful chirps and
tail-feather movements to signal others. Its melodious cries can also serve as warnings, as it is
merciless toward intruders and will peck at anything that enters its territory.

When excited, Fletchling’s body temperature rises sharply, and it can burn anyone who touches it
unexpectedly. Its body is always warm, which even makes it welcome in cold climates.
