## Starling Pokémon

Starly gather in large flocks across fields and mountains, where they are often seen chasing Bug
Pokémon. Though each one is small and weak on its own, they fly with strong wings and can deliver a
painful strike that lingers for days.

Alone, a Starly is easy to overlook, but its cries are sharp and obnoxious. When too many gather,
they begin to bicker, even as their noisy singing carries across the area.
