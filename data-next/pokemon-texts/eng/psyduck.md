## Duck Pokémon

Psyduck is constantly troubled by headaches, and as the pain intensifies, its latent psychic power
may begin to stir. It often seems to use these abilities without meaning to, and it is not clear
whether it intends to do so at all.

When Psyduck taps into its strange power, it often appears vacant or puzzled, as if it cannot
remember what just happened. Its brain activity becomes unusually high in these moments, and the
resulting psychokinesis can even seem to bring brief relief from the pain.
