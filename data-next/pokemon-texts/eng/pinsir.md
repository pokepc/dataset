## Stag Beetle Pokémon

Pinsir is astoundingly strong, using its massive thorned pincers to clamp onto prey and either tear
it apart or fling it far away if the grip does not finish the job. It can even lift foes that weigh
twice as much as it does, and a single strike from its horns can split a large tree.

temperatures drop. On chilly nights it burrows or hides under leafage, roots, or deep in forests,
