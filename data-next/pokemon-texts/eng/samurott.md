## Formidable Pokémon

Samurott is a hard-hearted warrior whose armor hides blades that can fell an opponent in a single
swing. Its cry and even a simple glare are enough to unsettle and quiet most foes.

The seamitars attached to its front legs can be drawn and sheathed in the blink of an eye, and part
of the armor on its anterior legs becomes a giant sword. A rare Hisuian form is said to strike with
turbulent blows that crash over opponents like ceaseless pounding waves.
