## Gas Pokémon

Gastly is a barely visible Pokémon made mostly of gas, with no real shape to speak of. It can slip
into almost any place and often lurks in deserted or abandoned buildings, where even a faint sweet
scent or flickering light may betray its presence.

Its gaseous body is poisonous and can make opponents faint, suffocate, or fall asleep when it cloaks
them. Strong wind can quickly disperse it, so groups of Gastly often gather under house eaves for
shelter.
