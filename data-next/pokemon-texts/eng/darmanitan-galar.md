## Blazing Pokémon

Darmanitan’s blazing internal fire can reach 2,500 degrees Fahrenheit, and when that heat rises, its
power grows with it. Its thick arms can land punches strong enough to obliterate a dump truck.

When badly hurt in fierce battle, it hardens into a stone-like form and meditates, sharpening its
mind before fighting on. With Zen Mode, it changes form at low health and returns to normal when it
recovers.
