## Cotton Puff Pokémon

Cottonee drifts wherever the wind carries it, and when it gathers with others of its kind, the group
can look like a cloud or even a cumulonimbus mass. It often seeks shelter under large trees when
rain makes its body heavier and harder to move.

To defend itself, Cottonee shoots cotton from its body to distract attackers, and the cotton quickly
grows back. Its cotton is soft, light, and highly valued for stuffing and fine cloth, though strong
winds can send it far away.
