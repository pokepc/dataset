## Eruption Pokémon

Mega Camerupt has a volcano inside its body, and magma so hot it can reach 18,000 degrees Fahrenheit
courses through it. Its back humps, formed from transformed bones, can blast out superheated magma
or molten lava when pressure builds or it becomes enraged.

It is said to live in the craters of volcanoes, and its back volcanoes are known for violent
eruptions at intervals of about 10 years. When angered, those eruptions become even more frequent
and forceful.
