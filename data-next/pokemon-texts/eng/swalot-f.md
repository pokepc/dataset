## Poison Bag Pokémon

Swalot has no teeth, so it swallows its food whole, even when it is large enough to fit an
automobile tire. Its cavernous mouth can open wide, and once something fits inside, it gulps it down
in one go.

When Swalot spots prey, it sprays a hideously toxic fluid from its pores to weaken the target first.
Its powerful stomach acids and special enzymes can dissolve almost anything it eats.
