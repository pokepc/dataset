## Caring Pokémon

Alomomola lives floating in the open sea, where it gently holds injured or weakened Pokémon in its
fins and brings them to shore. Its special membrane and mucus are said to close wounds and restore
health.

Many Water Pokémon gather around it, and fishermen value its presence on long voyages because it can
take the place of a doctor or medicine. The reason it helps others is unclear, though it may do so
because predators that follow wounded Pokémon could attack Alomomola as well.
