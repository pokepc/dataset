## Sage Pokémon

Oranguru is known for its extreme intelligence and calm, meditative nature, and it tends to look
down on inexperienced Trainers. It spends most of its time in the treetops, where it knows the
forest well and can gather medicinal herbs to help wounded Pokémon.
