## Single Bloom Pokémon

Flabébé draws out and controls the hidden power of flowers, and the bloom it carries is likely part
of its own body. It wears a crown of pollen gathered from that flower, which is said to have hidden
healing properties.

When it finds a flower it likes, it stays with it for life and floats along with an untroubled
heart. Without a suitable flower, it keeps traveling until it finds one with the color and shape it
wants. Its flower is set when encountered and cannot be changed.
