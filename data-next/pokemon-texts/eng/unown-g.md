## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon whose shape resembles ancient writing, hieroglyphs, or text
characters, making it hard to tell whether the markings or the Pokémon came first. Its many forms
seem to carry meaning, and researchers still do not know much about their origin.

Unown are usually found stuck to walls, and they are said to communicate telepathically. Alone, they
are inactive, but when two or more are together, an odd power is said to emerge.
