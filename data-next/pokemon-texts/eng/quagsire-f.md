## Water Fish Pokémon

Quagsire has a carefree, easygoing nature and a sluggish way of life. While swimming, it often bumps
into boat hulls, boulders, and even the river bottom, but it seems not to care.

It often lies at the bottom of the river with its mouth wide open, waiting for prey to wander in.
Because it hardly moves, it does not get very hungry, and its body is always slimy.
