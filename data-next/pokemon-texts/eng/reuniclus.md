## Multiplying Pokémon

Reuniclus controls its arms with psychic power, and those arms are made of a special liquid that can
crush boulders with surprising force. Remarkably intelligent, it may even prefer to swing its
powerful arms to batter opponents rather than rely on psychic attacks alone.

When Reuniclus shake hands, a network forms between their brains that boosts their psychic power.
The liquid surrounding its body is said to grant wisdom, but it is highly toxic to anything other
than Reuniclus itself.
