## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally’s hidden strength awakens through a deep bond of trust with its Trainer. Once its control
mask is destroyed, it is freed from its former burden and becomes much faster, able to enter battle
with its power fully released.

This awakened form can change its type at will, a flexibility that is especially striking in combat.
It marks the moment when its true power is unlocked by the trust it shares with the partner it
believes in.
