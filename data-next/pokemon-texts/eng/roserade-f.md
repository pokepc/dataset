## Bouquet Pokémon

Roserade entices prey with a sweet aroma or scent, then attacks with thorny whips hidden in its arms
or bouquets. It moves with dancer-like grace as it lashes, binds, and finishes off its victims with
poison-loaded whips.

Each hand holds different toxins, with one poison acting quickly and the other slowly, yet both are
deadly. Its graceful movements and sweet fragrance help it corner prey before striking mercilessly
with virulent, thorn-lined whips.
