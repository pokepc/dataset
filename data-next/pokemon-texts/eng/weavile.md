## Sharp Claw Pokémon

Weavile lives in cold, snowy regions and usually hunts in groups of four or five, using impressive
teamwork to surround and bring down prey much larger than itself. A leader guides the pack, and
their coordinated attacks can even overpower Mamoswine.
