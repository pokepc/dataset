## Bivalve Pokémon

Shellder is encased in an extremely hard shell that can withstand nearly any attack, though its
tender body is exposed when the shell opens. It swims backward by opening and closing its two
pieces, and can be surprisingly fast. Its tongue is usually left hanging out, even when it closes
up.
