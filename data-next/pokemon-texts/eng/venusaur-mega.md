## Seed Pokémon

Mega Venusaur’s broad flower spreads wide to catch the sun’s rays, turning sunlight into energy. It
keeps moving to find more light, and it grows stronger when the weather is warm and bright.

Its flower also gives off a rich, soothing fragrance. When the plant has plenty of nutrition and
sunlight, the bloom can take on vivid colors, and its scent is said to calm both people and Pokémon,
even in battle.
