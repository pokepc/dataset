## Penguin Pokémon

Eiscue drifted in on ocean currents from a frigid place, and it keeps its head iced at all times to
stay cold. Its ice-covered head is key to its survival, helping it remain in a chilled state.

It fishes by dangling its single hair into the sea, using it to lure prey close enough to catch.
This simple method lets it feed while keeping its heat-sensitive head cool.
