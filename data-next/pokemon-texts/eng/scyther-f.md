## Mantis Pokémon

Scyther moves with blinding, ninja-like speed, leaving only a blur and sometimes the illusion that
more than one is present. It can hide in grass with protective coloration, then leap out to slash
prey before it can react.

Its twin scythes are fearsome weapons that grow sharper through hard use and battle. With a single
wicked stroke, it can slice through thick logs or even fell a massive tree, and it is said to leave
cut-down trees wherever it makes its territory.
