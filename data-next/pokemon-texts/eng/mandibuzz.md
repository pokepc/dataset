## Bone Vulture Pokémon

Mandibuzz circles high above the ground, watching for weak Pokémon to strike and carry back to its
nest. It is especially drawn to Cubone, and it skillfully arranges the bones of its prey to build a
nest of bones.

These Pokémon decorate themselves with bones, and there seem to be fashions that rise and fall among
them. Though rough around the edges, Mandibuzz also looks after lost Vullaby and cares for them
until they are ready to leave the nest.
