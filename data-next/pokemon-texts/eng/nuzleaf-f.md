## Wily Pokémon

Nuzleaf live deep in densely overgrown forests, often making their homes in holes bored into large
trees. Skilled climbers, they sometimes slip out of the woods to startle people, and they are known
to dislike having their long noses pinched or gripped, since it weakens them.

Using the leaf on its head, Nuzleaf can make a flute whose eerie sound unsettles listeners. In
particular, people lost in a forest may feel fear, dread, or uncertainty when they hear its music.
