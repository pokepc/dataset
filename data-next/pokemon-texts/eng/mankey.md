## Pig Monkey Pokémon

Mankey is a light, agile Pokémon that lives in treetop colonies and can turn violent without
warning. It angers easily, and even a small irritation can send it into a sudden frenzy.

When one Mankey becomes enraged, the others around it often follow suit, so a whole troop may
rampage at once. In its fury, it may strike at anything nearby and cannot tell friend from foe.
