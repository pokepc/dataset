## Penguin Pokémon

Piplup lives along coastal shores in cold northern places, where its thick down helps shield it from
the chill. It is a skilled swimmer that can dive for over 10 minutes to hunt, but it is a poor
walker and often falls down.

Despite its clumsy footing, Piplup puffs up its chest with great pride and refuses to act dependent.
It dislikes being cared for, will not easily listen to its Trainer, and even hates accepting food
from people.
