## Meteor Pokémon

Minior originally lived in the ozone layer, where it became prey for stronger Pokémon. When its
shell grows too heavy, it hurtles down to the ground, and a hard impact can knock it free of its
shell.

Its outer shell is unusually durable, but the shock of the fall can smash it to pieces. This Pokémon
is said to have been born from mutated nanoparticles.
