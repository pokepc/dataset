## Muscular Pokémon

Timburr is often seen at building sites, where it helps with construction while always carrying a
squared log. As it grows, it carries larger logs, and once it can handle a log about three times its
size, it is nearly ready to evolve.

It fights by swinging its lumber around, and it seems to enjoy construction so much that it may get
upset if rain stops the work. When that happens, it can throw a tantrum and whirl its log angrily.
