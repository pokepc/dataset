## Trainee Pokémon

Nincada lives underground for many years in complete darkness, often remaining motionless as it
waits for evolution. It absorbs moisture and nutrients from tree roots, and can sometimes stay below
ground for more than 10 years.

Nearly blind from life in the soil, it relies on its whiskerlike antennae to sense its surroundings.
Its sharp claws carve through roots, and it avoids bright sunlight whenever possible.
