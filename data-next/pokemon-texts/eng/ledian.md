## Five Star Pokémon

Ledian uses starlight as energy, and its star-like spots are said to change size with the number of
stars in the night sky. On clear nights, it flutters through the sky and scatters a glowing, sparkly
powder.

It is often found where the air is clean and the stars shine brightly. By day, when it is warm, it
curls up in grass or inside a big leaf and sleeps deeply. It also likes to eat berries.

In battle, Ledian strikes with all four arms, relying on many quick blows rather than strength in
each one.
