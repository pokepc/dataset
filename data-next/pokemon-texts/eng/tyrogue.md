## Scuffle Pokémon

Tyrogue is always bursting with energy and eager to fight. Even though it is small, it can’t be
ignored, since it may strike at any handy target without warning.

It challenges anyone to sharpen its skills and has a strong competitive spirit. It keeps fighting
even after losing, determined to become stronger, and often nurses injuries from taking on larger
foes.

Tyrogue grows stressed if it does not train every day, so it needs a regular training schedule and
varied methods from its Trainer.
