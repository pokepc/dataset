## Spiral Pokémon

Omanyte is an ancient Pokémon revived from fossils, and in rare cases it can also be genetically
regenerated. Its fossils are found in places that were once seas, and some show bite marks,
suggesting it was preyed on long ago.

In prehistoric oceans, Omanyte swam by twisting its 10 tentacles, seeming to drift as it paddled
along. It could also use air stored in its shell to sink and rise in water. When threatened, it
withdraws into its hard shell.
