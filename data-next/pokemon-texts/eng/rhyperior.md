## Drill Pokémon

Rhyperior loads rocks into the holes in its palms and fires them by tightening and releasing its
muscles with great force. It can launch a held stone like a missile, and at times it has been known
to shoot out Geodude, or even Roggenrola, from its hands.

Its heavy carapace can withstand volcanic eruptions and even shrug off a blow from a masterwork
sword. Rhyperior uses that sturdy hide to deflect attacks, unbalance foes, and then drive its drill
into them.
