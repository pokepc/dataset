## Lightning Pokémon

Electrike stores electricity in its long fur and body hair, building up static power as it runs. The
friction of its movement and even the air against its coat can generate electricity, and its fur may
crackle or spark as it races along.

It uses the stored charge to stimulate its leg muscles, giving it explosive acceleration and a
reaction speed that stands out for its incredible swiftness. In dry weather or when a storm is near,
sparks can scatter from all over its body.
