## Mold Pokémon

Shuckle stores berries inside its vase-like shell, then keeps itself hidden beneath rocks and
remains completely still to avoid attacks. Its hard shell conceals its body while it waits quietly
in place.

Over time, the berries it stores decompose and ferment into a thick, pulpy juice. Fluid secreted by
its toes can carve holes in rocks for nesting, and that same fluid may mix with the berries to
create a drink.
