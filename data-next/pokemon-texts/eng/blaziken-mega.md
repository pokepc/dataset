## Blaze Pokémon

Mega Blaziken attacks with courage, releasing intense flames from its wrists when it faces a strong
foe. The fiercer the opponent, the hotter its wrists burn, and its flaming punches leave enemies
scorched and blackened.

Its incredibly powerful legs let it leap clear over buildings, even a 30-story one in a single
bound. It also learns martial arts focused on punches and kicks, making it a swift and formidable
fighter.
