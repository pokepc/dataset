## Stump Pokémon

Phantump are born when a spirit takes hold of a rotten tree stump, and old tales say they may be the
spirits of children who died after becoming lost in the forest. They live in abandoned woods, where
their eerie cries can sound like a child’s voice.

By imitating a child, they lure adults deeper into the trees and leave them hopelessly lost, though
they are only trying to make friends. Their green leaves are also said to yield a brew that can cure
any illness.
