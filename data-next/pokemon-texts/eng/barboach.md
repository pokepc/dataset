## Whiskers Pokémon

Barboach lives in muddy swamps and riverbeds, where poor visibility helps hide it from predators. It
keeps most of its body buried in mud, exposing only its two long whiskers while it waits for prey,
and those whiskers act as a sensitive radar that can detect and even taste what is around it.
