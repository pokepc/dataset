## Starling Pokémon

Staravia lives in forests and open fields, where it flies in large flocks in search of bug Pokémon.
These groups are very large, but they are often in conflict, and fierce territorial scuffles can
break out when flocks meet.

Staravia seems to rely on group life because it recognizes its own weakness. When left alone, it
cries noisily. In a flock, those with especially magnificent head plumes are thought to be the
stronger ones.
