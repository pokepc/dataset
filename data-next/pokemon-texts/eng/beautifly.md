## Butterfly Pokémon

Beautifly is drawn to the sweet pollen and nectar of flowers, and may come to a potted bloom left by
an open window. It flits through flower-covered fields on spring winds, using its long, needlelike
mouth to collect pollen and sip nectar.

Its vividly patterned wings are its most striking feature, but its beauty hides an aggressive
streak. If disturbed while feeding, it may jab with its long mouth, and it can even chase away other
pollen-gathering Pokémon to keep the food for itself.
