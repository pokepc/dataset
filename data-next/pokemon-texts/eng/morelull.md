## Illuminating Pokémon

Morelull lives in dark, damp forests and spends the day drowsing away. It nourishes itself by
drawing from tree roots, then wakes at night to wander in search of a new tree.

Its mushroom caps fill with glowing spores that flicker in the dark. These spores can put those who
see them into a deep sleep, even though forest Pokémon eat the caps and they regrow overnight.
