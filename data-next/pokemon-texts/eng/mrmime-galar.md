## Barrier Pokémon

Mr. Mime is a master of pantomime, always practicing its act to make others believe something unseen
is really there. Its broad hands and fingertips help it shape invisible walls, using a strange force
to harden the air and block attacks.

Its performance can be so convincing that an imagined object may seem to become real once others
accept it. If its act is interrupted or unappreciated, it may retaliate with a sharp Double Slap.
