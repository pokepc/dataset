## Cyclone Pokémon

Tornadus in its Therian Forme soars through the sky with the lower half of its body wrapped in a
cloud of energy, racing at great speed as it drives powerful winds in every direction.

By expelling massive energy from its tail, it can stir severe storms and create gales strong enough
to blow houses away. It is said to whip up the air and help bring about the changing seasons.
