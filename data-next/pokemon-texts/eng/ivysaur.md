## Seed Pokémon

Ivysaur has a bud on its back that grows by drawing in energy and nutrients, and sunlight makes it
stronger and larger. As the bud swells, it gives off a sweet, pleasant aroma that signals its flower
is close to blooming.

To support the bud’s weight, Ivysaur’s legs and trunk become thick and sturdy. When the bulb grows
especially large, it may seem unable to stand on its hind legs.
