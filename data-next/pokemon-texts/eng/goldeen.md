## Goldfish Pokémon

Goldeen is a beautiful Pokémon whose dorsal, pectoral, and tail fins wave gracefully in the water,
giving it a queenly, princess-like air. Its dress-like tail fin and elegant swimming have earned it
names like the Water Queen and Water Dancer.
