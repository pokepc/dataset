## Gear Pokémon

Klinklang is made of three gears that spin at high speed, including a new spiked gear that is not a
living creature. The minigear is rapidly spun and then charged by the red core, preparing it to
fire.

Its red core acts as an energy tank, storing an enormous amount of power. Klinklang releases that
charged energy through its spikes, launching powerful electric blasts into the surrounding area.
