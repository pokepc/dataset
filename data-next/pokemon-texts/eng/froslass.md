## Snow Land Pokémon

Froslass is said to have been born from the soul or regrets of a woman who died on a snowy mountain
and possessed an icicle. Its hollow body releases icy breath near -60 degrees Fahrenheit, freezing
foes and quarry alike.

It drags frozen prey or favored humans back to its chilly lair, where it arranges them as
decorations. Legends also warn that it comes down during blizzards and may target handsome men or
those who misbehave.
