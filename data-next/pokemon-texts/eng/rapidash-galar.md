## Fire Horse Pokémon

Rapidash is a very competitive runner that will chase anything faster than itself just for the
chance to race. It is often seen cantering through fields and plains, but when it gets serious, it
surges into a blazing gallop.

Its speed is astonishing, reaching nearly 150 mph with great acceleration from a standstill. At full
gallop, its hooves seem barely to touch the ground, and its mane flares and sparkles as it races
like an arrow. The fastest runner leads the herd and sets its pace and direction.
