## Tiny Bee Pokémon

Combee is a trio that stays together from birth, with each member having a slightly different taste
in nectar. From sunrise to sundown, it tirelessly gathers nectar and honey from flowers, carrying
them back to its colony and serving Vespiquen, its queen Pokémon.

It works diligently for the sake of the swarm, and each group produces honey with its own flavor. At
night, Combee gather closely in huge clusters to sleep, forming a hive-like mass.
