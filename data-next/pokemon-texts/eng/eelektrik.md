## EleFish Pokémon

Eelektrik coils around its foes and shocks them with electricity from the round spots on its sides,
which act as electricity-generating organs. It uses this power to paralyze prey before biting down.

It has a big appetite, and when it spots prey, it attacks at once. By wrapping tightly around its
target, it can immobilize it with electricity, then chomp away.
