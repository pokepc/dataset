## Numb Pokémon

Numel stores intensely hot magma in the hump on its back and throughout its body, where it can reach
almost 2,200 degrees Fahrenheit. That heat gives it great strength, though its hump shrinks a little
when it uses Fire-type moves.

If it gets wet or the weather turns rainy, the magma cools and hardens, making its body heavy and
its movements sluggish. Numel is also very dull-witted and seems not to notice being hit, but it
cannot tolerate hunger for even a moment.
