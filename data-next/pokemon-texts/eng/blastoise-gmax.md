## Shellfish Pokémon

Blastoise is a brutal Pokémon whose shell carries pressurized water jets and rocket-like cannons. It
plants its feet firmly before firing, using the recoil to launch powerful attacks with heavy force.

Its water shots are highly accurate, able to strike distant targets with great precision. These
blasts can punch through thick steel, and it can use its heavy body to crush foes or withdraw inside
its shell when pressed.
