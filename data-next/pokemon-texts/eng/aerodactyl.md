## Fossil Pokémon

Aerodactyl is a ferocious Pokémon from the age of dinosaurs, restored from genetic material found in
amber. It is imagined to have ruled ancient skies, flying freely and fearlessly above the open land,
and its shrieking cries were said to carry through those old heavens.

With wings that seem made for gliding, it was a vicious hunter that struck at the throat with
serrated fangs like saw blades. Those teeth could shred prey to tatters, and its ferocity was so
great that even modern technology cannot produce
