## Dragon Pokémon

Kingdra sleeps deep on the seafloor or in underwater caves, building its energy in places where no
other life can survive. It is said that when it yawns or even moves, spiraling currents and giant
whirlpools form around it.

When storms arrive, Kingdra may rise to the surface and wander in search of prey. Its appearance has
long been linked with tornadoes and fierce seas, and its gleaming scales have even been prized as
gifts for royalty.
