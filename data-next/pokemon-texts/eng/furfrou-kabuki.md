## Poodle Pokémon

Furfrou in its Kabuki Trim is known for its carefully styled fur, which makes it look more elegant
and helps it move more swiftly. In Kalos, these Pokémon were once regarded as guardians of the king,
and there was a time when nobles competed to create the most exquisite trim.

Its fur keeps growing if left alone, but it will only permit a trusted person to cut it. This form
is only a change in appearance, and its trim can be altered again at any time.
