## Fire Mouse Pokémon

Cyndaquil is timid and usually stays hunched over or curled into a ball. When it is startled, angry,
or attacked, it raises the flames on its back for protection, and those flames can shoot out
vigorously to intimidate foes.

Its fire burns hottest when it is angry, but if Cyndaquil is tired, the flames sputter with
incomplete combustion. Hailing from the Johto region, it may look shy, yet it harbors tremendous
firepower.
