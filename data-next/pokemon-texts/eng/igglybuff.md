## Balloon Pokémon

Igglybuff has a very soft, marshmallow-like body that moves by bouncing instead of walking. Once it
starts rolling or bouncing, it is hard to stop, and its flexible body lets it keep bouncing almost
anywhere.

It loves to sing, though its vocal cords are not yet fully developed, so too much singing can hurt
its throat. It keeps practicing, even in its dreams, and responds well to praise and encouragement.
Its body gives off a faint sweet scent, and it can turn deeper pink when it grows warm.
