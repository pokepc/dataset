## Mask Pokémon

Ogerpon is a curious, mischief-loving Pokémon that battles by drawing out the type-based energy
stored in its masks. Its form can change with the mask it wears, and it uses that power to keep
opponents guessing.

It confounds enemies with quick, nimble movements and sharp kicks, making it hard to predict. The
Wellspring Mask shape reflects its reliance on mask-bound energy to fight.
