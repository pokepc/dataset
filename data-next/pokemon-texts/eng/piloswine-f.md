## Swine Pokémon

Piloswine is wrapped in a thick, shaggy coat that helps it withstand freezing weather, though the
long fur also covers its eyes and makes sight difficult. It relies on its sensitive nose and keen
sense of smell to find food, even digging up buried prey under ice or frozen ground.

Its short legs are steadied by rugged hooves that keep it from slipping on icy terrain. When it
charges, the hairs along its back bristle, and its tusks, which can thicken in snow, are used both
for digging and for battle.
