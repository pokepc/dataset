## Tiny Raccoon Pokémon

Zigzagoon is endlessly curious, so it wanders restlessly and zigzags about wherever it goes. It
keeps its nose to the ground, often searching back and forth as it follows whatever catches its
attention.

Its bristly back fur is used to rub against trees and mark its territory. Zigzagoon is also good at
finding items in grass or even in the ground, and it may play dead to fool foes when threatened.
