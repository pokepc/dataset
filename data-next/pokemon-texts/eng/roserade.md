## Bouquet Pokémon

Roserade lures prey with a sweet fragrance, then lashes it with thorny whips hidden in the bouquets
on its hands. It moves with dancerlike grace as it corners and binds its target, finishing it off
with poison-laden strikes.

Each hand carries different toxins, and both can deliver life-threatening jabs. Its right hand’s
poison works quickly, while the left acts more slowly. The stronger its poison, the sweeter its
aroma seems to become.
