## Seed Pokémon

Venusaur’s broad flower blooms as it absorbs sunlight, and it keeps moving to find more. The petals
catch the sun’s rays and turn them into energy, making it especially strong in warm weather.

Its flower can grow vivid with good nutrition and sunlight, and it gives off a soothing, sometimes
bewitching fragrance. After rain, the scent becomes stronger and can attract other Pokémon.
