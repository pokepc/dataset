## Volcano Pokémon

Entei races across the land with such overwhelming power that it cannot be restrained. Legends say a
new volcano is born whenever one appears, and that its roar or bark can trigger an eruption
somewhere in the world.

This Pokémon embodies the passion of magma and is thought to have been born from a volcanic
eruption. It sends out huge bursts of fire, with flames hot enough to consume everything they touch.
