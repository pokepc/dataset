## Wushu Pokémon

Urshifu in its Rapid Strike Style believes in ending battles with a single decisive hit. It closes
in on its foes with swift leaps and strikes with a hardened fist, delivering powerful blows at close
range.

Living in the mountains of a distant region, it races across steep cliffs as part of its training.
Constant movement helps it strengthen its legs and sharpen its techniques.
