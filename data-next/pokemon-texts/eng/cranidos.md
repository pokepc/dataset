## Head Butt Pokémon

A primeval Pokémon from about 100 million years ago, Cranidos lived in jungles and used its powerful
head butts to snap obstructing trees and even combat other ancient Pokémon. Fossils are often found
near broken tree trunks, a sign of the force behind its attacks.

Its skull is rock-hard and ironlike, and it is said to lack intelligence. Cranidos also toughens its
already sturdy head by ramming others of its kind, with the loud clash echoing like a bell.
