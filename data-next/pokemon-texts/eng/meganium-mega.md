## Herb Pokémon

Mega Meganium’s flower gives off a soothing fragrance and a petal-scented aroma that can calm
aggressive feelings. In battle, it releases even more of this becalming scent to blunt an opponent’s
fighting spirit.

Its breath is said to revive dead grass, plants, and flowers, restoring them to health. Those who
stand nearby feel refreshed, as if they were resting in a sunny forest.
