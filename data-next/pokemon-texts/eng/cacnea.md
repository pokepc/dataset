## Cactus Pokémon

Cacnea lives in arid deserts and other harsh places with little rain. It stores water in its body,
allowing it to survive for about 30 days without drinking, and its yellow flower blooms only once a
year.

Its flower gives off a strong, fragrant aroma to lure prey. When something comes close, Cacnea
attacks by wildly swinging its thorny arms and firing sharp thorns from its body.
