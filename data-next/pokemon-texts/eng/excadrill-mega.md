## Subterrene Pokémon

Known as the Drill King, Mega Excadrill can tunnel through the ground at speeds of over 90 mph. Its
steel claws and head form a drill powerful enough to bore through iron plates and even thick steel
without slowing down.

It is also useful in tunnel construction, but its digging can be so intense that it creates mazelike
nests deep underground. Tunnels that look natural may actually be the result of its rampant
excavation, and its work can damage subway lines.
