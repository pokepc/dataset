## Brutal Star Pokémon

Mareanie crawls along beaches and seafloors, where it feeds on the coral branches of Corsola and is
disliked by those who work with fallen Corsola growths. Its poison sting first causes numbness, then
an intense itching that is hard to ignore.

It uses the poison spike on its head to weaken prey before striking with its ten tentacles to finish
the job. Most of its body can regrow quickly if cut off, and after a good night’s sleep it is back
to normal.
