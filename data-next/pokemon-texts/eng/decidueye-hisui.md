## Arrow Quill Pokémon

Decidueye’s wing feathers hide arrow quills that it nocks and fires with remarkable speed and
precision, striking weak points before foes can react. Its shots are so accurate they can even
pierce a pebble from far away, and when every attack must count, it tugs the vine on its head to
sharpen its focus.

Usually cool and cautious, it can panic if caught off guard. In Hisui’s extreme cold, air stored
inside its feather rachises helps insulate it, showing how strongly environment can shape its form.
