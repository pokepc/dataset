## Bite Pokémon

Poochyena is a persistent scavenger that will eat almost anything, and its oversized fangs give it a
fierce look despite its small body. It often tries to intimidate opponents by bristling the hair on
its tail.

Once it picks a target, Poochyena chases it relentlessly, guided by a keen sense of smell, until the
prey is worn out. However, if the prey fights back, Poochyena may turn and run.
