## Mach Pokémon

Mega Garchomp folds its body and spreads its wings to look like a jet plane, then flies at sonic
speed. Its fine scales reduce drag, and their sharp edges can injure anything that strikes it,
helping it race through the sky and even hunt down distant prey.

It can also unleash wind blades as it rushes past, felling nearby trees. Fierce and relentless, it
is said to dive into flocks of bird Pokémon and swallow them whole, never allowing prey to escape.
