## Hard Shell Pokémon

Pupitar’s body is encased in a shell as hard as bedrock or sheet rock, and it can still move freely
inside it. Even as a pupa, it already has arms and legs beneath the solid shell, and its thrashing
can unleash great destructive power.

It propels itself by compressing gas inside its body and forcefully venting it like a jet or rocket.
This lets it dash around wildly, even into solid steel, while its durable body avoids damage. It is
often restless, as if eager to evolve.
