## Spike Ball Pokémon

Togepi’s shell is said to be filled with happiness and joy, making it a symbol of good luck. When
treated kindly, it is believed to share that good fortune with others, and its cheerful presence can
soothe the soul.

It draws energy from the compassion and pleasure people and Pokémon express, transforming those
positive feelings into happiness stored inside its shell. It then passes those happy feelings along
to others.
