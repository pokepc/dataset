## Thorn Seed Pokémon

Ferroseed clings to cave ceilings or walls and draws in iron and other minerals from the rock around
it. Mossy caves are its preferred home, since enzymes in the moss help its spikes grow big and
strong.

When it is threatened, Ferroseed launches a barrage of spikes to drive off attackers and create a
chance to roll away. Its aim is poor at first, but with practice it becomes more accurate.
