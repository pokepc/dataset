## Dark Pokémon

Houndour are smart pack hunters that use a variety of cries and barks to communicate with one
another and coordinate the pursuit of prey. They can check each other’s location through calls only
their own kind understands, allowing them to work together with remarkable teamwork.

Around dawn, they give repeated eerie howls that echo through the area, announcing their territory.
If one becomes a partner, it is said to be skillful, loyal to its Trainer, and obedient to orders.
