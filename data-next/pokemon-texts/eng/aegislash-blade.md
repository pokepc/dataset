## Royal Sword Pokémon

Aegislash has long been said to attend generations of kings, using its spectral power to influence
people and Pokémon. Legends claim it can sense leadership in others, and that anyone it recognizes
is destined to become king.

In Blade Forme, it shifts away from its defensive stance and prepares to strike. Its form can change
with its ability, becoming Blade Forme before a damaging move and returning to Shield Forme for
King’s Shield or when it leaves battle.
