## Irate Pokémon

Deino cannot see, so it learns about the world by bumping into things and biting whatever it
touches. Its constant gnawing helps it keep track of its surroundings, and it will bite anything it
encounters, even eating anything that moves.

This Pokémon is not picky about food, but approaching it carelessly is dangerous. Its body is often
covered in wounds from its habit of tackling and biting to explore, and if it likes the taste of
something, it remembers the scent.
