## Vine Pokémon

Tangrowth is draped in long vines that make it look like a large shrub when it stays still. It
swings these vine bundles like arms to wrap around prey and ensnare it, and its vines can grow so
thick that its eyes are hidden in warm weather.

Its arms are really bundles of vines that bind to things and grow back quickly if cut. Even if a
predator tears them away or eats one, Tangrowth is not troubled and soon returns to normal. In warm
