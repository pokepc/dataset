## Armor Pokémon

Donphan has sharp, hard tusks and a rugged, rock-hard hide that resists ordinary attacks. The longer
and larger its tusks grow, the higher its rank in the herd, though they take a long time to develop.

It often curls into a ball and charges by rolling at high speed, and once it starts it is hard to
stop. With its massive strength, it can smash houses and even help clear rock and mud slides
blocking mountain trails.
