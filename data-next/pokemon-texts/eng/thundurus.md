## Bolt Strike Pokémon

Thundurus flies around the Unova region hurling immense lightning bolts from the spikes on its tail,
and its passage leaves charred remains and even forest fires in its wake. Because of this reckless
scattering of electricity, it is widely disliked.

Stories say it has long battled its nemesis, Tornadus. The bolts it strikes into the earth can
pierce deep below the surface and enrich the soil.
