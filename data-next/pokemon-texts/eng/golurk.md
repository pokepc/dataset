## Automaton Pokémon

Golurk is an ancient construct said to have been made to protect people and Pokémon, and it follows
orders faithfully. Some accounts also describe it as a laborer built to serve its master, while its
internal workings are thought to draw on a mysterious, possibly limitless energy.

It can fly across the sky at astonishing speed, and when the seal on its chest is removed, its power
goes out of control. In that state, it may rampage indiscriminately and reduce everything around it
to rubble.
