## Requiem Pokémon

Duskull roams the deep darkness of midnight, slipping through even thick walls as it silently stalks
prey. Once it chooses a target, it pursues it doggedly until sunrise, though the chase ends at dawn.

Its single scarlet eye can freeze even stout adults in fear. Folklore says it loves children’s
crying and will startle or spirit away badly behaved children, a story often used to warn the unruly
before the witching hour.
