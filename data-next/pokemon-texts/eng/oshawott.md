## Sea Otter Pokémon

Oshawott fights with the scalchop on its stomach, striking back immediately when attacked. The shell
is made from the same material as claws, and it can detach it to use as a blade.

This belly shell is useful for more than battle. Oshawott also uses it to break open hard berries,
and it has even been named a scalchop for the way it cuts down foes.
