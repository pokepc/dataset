## Bird Pokémon

Pidgeotto is a vigorous Pokémon that constantly patrols a broad territory in search of prey. It
flies in slow, circling paths while keeping a sharp lookout, and its keen vision lets it spot
movement from far above.

Highly territorial, it fiercely pecks at intruders and strikes with its well-developed claws. When
it catches prey, it can immobilize it and carry it to its nest, even over long distances of more
than 60 miles.
