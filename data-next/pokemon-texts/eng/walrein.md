## Ice Break Pokémon

Walrein swims through icy seas and frigid seawater, using its large tusks to shatter drift ice and
even smash heavy ice blocks and icebergs. Its tusks keep growing throughout its life, and even when
they break in battle, they grow back over time.

A thick coat of blubber insulates Walrein from subzero temperatures and helps repel enemy attacks.
It lives in herds, and the leader will fight to protect the group and territory, even at great
personal cost.
