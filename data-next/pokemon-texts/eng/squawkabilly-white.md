## Parrot Pokémon

Squawkabilly with white plumage live in cities and gather into flocks sorted by feather color. These
groups are highly territorial, and they often clash with one another over control of their space.

Their flocks can become very loud, especially in the morning and evening when they are out searching
for food. Among the differently colored groups, the green-feathered flocks are said to hold the most
influence.
