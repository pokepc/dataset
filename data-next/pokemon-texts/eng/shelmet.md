## Snail Pokémon

Shelmet defends itself by tightly closing the lid of its shell when attacked, and it can also spit a
sticky, poisonous liquid. Its unusual body responds to electricity, and when it is together with
Karrablast and both are exposed to electric-like stimulation, it evolves for reasons that are still
unknown.

This defense is not always enough, though, because Karrablast can still get inside the shell.
Shelmet’s strange physiology and its puzzling link to Karrablast remain a mystery.
