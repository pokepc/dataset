## Long Neck Pokémon

Girafarig is a herbivore that feeds on grass and tree shoots, and while it eats, its tail makes
chewing and swallowing motions as if it were eating too. The head on its tail has a tiny brain of
its own, enough to react to smells and sounds and bite if something approaches from behind.

The rear head does not need sleep, so it can keep watch around the clock, even while Girafarig
rests. When threatened, it can also lash out instinctively or drive foes away with mysterious power.
