## Tadpole Pokémon

Poliwhirl can live on land or in water, but it prefers the safety of water, where its prey is
plentiful and its enemies are fewer. Its body stays moist and slick with a slimy, oily fluid, and
when it is out of water it sweats constantly to keep from drying out.

The spiral on its belly subtly undulates and can make viewers drowsy. When threatened, Poliwhirl may
use that swirl to put a foe to sleep before slipping free and escaping from its grasp. Its
well-developed legs also let it walk on land with skill.
