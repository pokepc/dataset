## Lava Dome Pokémon

Mega Heatran dwells in volcanic caves, where it uses its cross-shaped feet to dig in and crawl along
ceilings and walls. Its rugged steel body is partly melted in places by its own intense heat, giving
it a molten, unfinished look.

Boiling blood like magma flows through it, and stories say this Pokémon was born from the boiling
magma within Mount Coronet. Its molten-steel body still holds many mysteries.
