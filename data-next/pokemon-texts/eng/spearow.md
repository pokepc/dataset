## Tiny Bird Pokémon

Spearow flits about restlessly on its short wings, unable to fly high or far, but quick enough to
dart around at speed. It uses its beak to search tall grass for insects and peck at Bug-type prey,
and its vigorous appetite can even make it welcome around troubled fields.

It is fiercely protective of its territory and will stand up to others, even larger Pokémon, while
flying ceaselessly overhead and crying out loudly. Its high, keening calls can be heard from far
away and may warn others of danger.
