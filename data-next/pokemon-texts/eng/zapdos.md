## Electric Pokémon

Zapdos is a legendary bird Pokémon said to live in thunderclouds and appear when the sky turns dark,
especially as clouds part and lightning begins to fall. It freely controls electricity and is often
described dropping enormous bolts from the clouds.

When Zapdos beats its glittering wings, it can unleash savage thunderstorms, and stories say the
crackling sounds of flight are followed by lightning almost at once. It is also said to grow
stronger when struck by lightning.
