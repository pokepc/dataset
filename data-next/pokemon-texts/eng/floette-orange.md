## Single Bloom Pokémon

Floette flutters through fields of flowers, tending to blossoms that are beginning to wilt and
sharing its own power with them. It is said to give its heart to flower care, and it does not
forgive anyone who damages a flower bed.

When flowers bloom in a well-kept bed, Floette appears to celebrate with an elegant dance. It can
also draw out the hidden power of flowers and use flowers as weapons, with more splendid blossoms
holding greater power.
