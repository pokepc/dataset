## Seed Pokémon

Venusaur’s broad flower blooms as it absorbs solar energy, so it stays on the move to seek sunlight.
By spreading its petals to catch the sun’s rays, it converts that light into power and becomes
especially strong in summer.

Its large flower can deepen in vivid color with plenty of nutrition and sunlight. The sweet
fragrance it gives off soothes people, calms those in battle, and can grow stronger after rain, when
it also attracts other Pokémon.
