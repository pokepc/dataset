## Mushroom Pokémon

Amoonguss lures prey by dancing and waving the Poké Ball-like caps on its arms in a swaying display.
It prefers damp places, though many Pokémon are not fooled by its trick.

It usually stands still, sometimes near discarded Poké Balls, and releases poisonous spores at foes.
If the spores are not washed off quickly, mushrooms will grow where they land.
