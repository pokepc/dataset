## Music Note Pokémon

Chatot can learn and speak human words, and it is a skilled mimic of human speech. Its tongue is
shaped much like a human’s, and older, more experienced Chatot are said to understand the meaning of
the words they copy.

It also imitates the cries of other Pokémon to make them think it is one of their own, helping it
avoid attacks. When several gather, they pick up the same saying, and they keep rhythm by flicking
their tail feathers like a metronome.
