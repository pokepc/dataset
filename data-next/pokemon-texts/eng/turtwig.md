## Tiny Leaf Pokémon

Turtwig’s shell is really hardened soil or silt, and when it is healthy it feels moist, slightly
damp, and warm to the touch. It lives along lakes and takes in water to keep that shell firm.

Under sunlight, Turtwig photosynthesizes across its body and makes oxygen, becoming more energetic
as the light grows stronger. If it gets thirsty, the leaf on its head wilts.
