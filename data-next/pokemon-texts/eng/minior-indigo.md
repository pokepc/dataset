## Meteor Pokémon

Minior once lived in the ozone layer, where stronger Pokémon fed on it. When its enclosing shell
grows too heavy or it tries to flee, it plummets to the ground.

Its outer shell is unusually durable, but a strong impact can knock it free of the core inside. The
fall shatters the shell to smithereens. It is said to have been born from mutated nanoparticles.
