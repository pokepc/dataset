## Moonlight Pokémon

Umbreon is a nocturnal Pokémon that blends into darkness with its black fur and waits silently for
prey to move. Even in total darkness, its large eyes can see clearly, and moonlight or the moon’s
aura makes the rings on its body glow, often with a faint yellow light that can unsettle those
nearby.
