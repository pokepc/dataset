## Season Pokémon

Sawsbuck migrates with the changing seasons, and people can judge the time of year by the shape of
its horns. The plants growing on them also shift with the season, and the herd’s leaders are said to
have especially magnificent horns.

Because it appears with the seasons, some people regard Sawsbuck as a harbinger of spring. Its forms
change to match the current season, though only its appearance is affected.
