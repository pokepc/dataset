## Penguin Pokémon

Prinplup lives a solitary life, keeping away from others because each one believes it is the most
important and cannot form a group. It carries itself with great dignity while swimming gracefully
through the frigid sea and singing with a voice like the roaring tide.

Its sturdy wings can strike with such force that they can snap even thick trees. It also searches
icy seas for prey, relying on both its strength and its composed manner.
