## Single Bloom Pokémon

Flabébé draws out and controls the hidden power of flowers, and the bloom it carries is likely part
of its own body. It wears a crown made from pollen it has gathered, and that crown is said to have
hidden healing properties.

It wanders until it finds a flower with the color and shape it wants, then stays with it for life,
floating along in the wind with a calm heart. In this form, the flower it is encountered with is
fixed and cannot be changed.
