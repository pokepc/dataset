## Dancer Pokémon

A single kick from Quaquaval can send a truck rolling. It relies on its powerful legs to perform
striking dances inspired by far-off lands, combining graceful movement with overwhelming force.

Its elaborate water feathers add to the spectacle, mesmerizing onlookers as it dances. Those same
feathers can lash out at foes, turning each flourish into a sharp, cutting attack.
