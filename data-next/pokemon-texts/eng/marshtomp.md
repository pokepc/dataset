## Mud Fish Pokémon

Marshtomp’s body is covered by a thin, sticky film that helps it live on land, and it likes to play
in mud on beaches when the tide is low. It moves through mud much faster than it swims, and its
developed hind legs let it walk upright.

Living on muddy ground has made its legs sturdy and given it sure footing, even where footing is
poor. It is strongest in mud, where it can quickly overwhelm foes, and it burrows into dirt to
sleep. Because its skin can dry out, it replenishes moisture by playing in mud.
