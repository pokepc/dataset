## Superpower Pokémon

Machoke’s power is so overwhelming that it wears a belt to restrain its energy and regulate its
motions. If the belt is removed, it can unleash its full strength, a state so dangerous that it
would be unstoppable.

Its body is covered in steel-hard muscles and it never seems to tire, even when working at full
power. It loves training, grows stronger through constant workouts and battle, and willingly takes
on heavy labor to help people with demanding tasks.
