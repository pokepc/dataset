## Intimidation Pokémon

Krookodile is a fiercely aggressive predator that lurks beneath the sand, sometimes waiting for days
for prey to appear. It can hide within sandstorms, see tiny targets from more than 30 miles away,
and spot objects at great distance as if using binoculars.

Once it locks onto prey, it rushes in with startling speed and clamps down with jaws strong enough
to bite through iron or crush an automobile. After grabbing its target, it may twist its body to
tear the prey apart, and it never lets escape.
