## Classy Cat Pokémon

Persian carries itself with the grace and elegance of a proud queen, and its forehead gem glows on
its own. Its refined air and luxurious fur win many admirers, though its haughty nature makes it
difficult to raise or befriend.

Despite its poised appearance, Persian can turn vicious without warning, scratching or pouncing for
little reason. It moves so silently that it can attack in an instant, and its lithe body and sharp
claws let it strike with deadly speed.
