## Chick Pokémon

Torchic stays close to its Trainer, following along with unsteady steps. A small flame burns inside
its belly, making it feel warmly cozy to hug, almost like a hot-water bottle, and its fluffy down
helps give it a soft, downy look.

When threatened, it fights back by spitting fireballs from the flame it keeps inside. Its fire can
reach over 1,800 degrees Fahrenheit, scorching foes black. It also dislikes darkness, since it
cannot see well in dim places.
