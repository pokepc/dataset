## Subterrene Pokémon

Known as the Drill King, Excadrill can tunnel through the ground at speeds of over 90 mph. It forms
a drill with its steel claws and head, allowing it to bore through even thick steel plates, and its
drill has become strong enough to pierce iron plates.

Deep below the surface, Excadrill builds mazelike nests more than 300 feet underground. Its digging
can be so relentless that it helps in tunnel construction, but it can also damage subway tunnels and
make natural-looking tunnels that are actually its work.
