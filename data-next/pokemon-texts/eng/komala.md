## Drowsing Pokémon

Komala is born asleep and remains so throughout its life, from birth to death. Its constant motions
are only the turning and tossing of its dreams, and when it falls into especially deep sleep, it
becomes completely still.

The log it carries is passed down by its parents at birth, and it may even cling to the arm of a
friendly Trainer. Its lifelong slumber is linked to the sedative properties of the leaves it eats.
