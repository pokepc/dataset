## Land Spirit Pokémon

Tapu Lele is the guardian deity of Akala, nourished by the fragrant aroma of flowers. Though it is
worshiped as a protector, it is known for a guileless yet cruel nature, seeming to embody nature
itself without any sense of guilt.

As it flutters about, it scatters strangely glowing scales. These sparkling scales are said to heal
the wounds of both people and Pokémon, and even touching them is believed to restore good health at
once.
