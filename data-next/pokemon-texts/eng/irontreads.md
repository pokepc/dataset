## Paradox Pokémon

Iron Treads closely resembles a strange scientific weapon said in a paranormal magazine to have been
sent to this planet by aliens. Its sleek, uncanny design gives it an air of mystery that has fueled
speculation about its origin.

Recent sightings have added to that mystery. It is also said to resemble an enigmatic object
described long ago in an old expedition journal, linking modern reports with an even older account.
