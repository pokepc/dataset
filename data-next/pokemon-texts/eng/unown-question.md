## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is usually found stuck to walls, and its shape appears to carry
some kind of meaning. Its many forms resemble ancient writing, text characters, or hieroglyphs,
leaving it a mystery whether human writing inspired Unown or the other way around.

Different forms are said to have different abilities, though little is known for certain. Unown are
also thought to communicate telepathically, and when several are together, an odd power is said to
emerge.
