## Mysterious Pokémon

Mega Starmie has a symmetrical, geometric body that it spins rapidly to move through the water. Its
shining core, often called the gem of the sea, glows in seven colors or appears in different colors
when seen, and some people even prize it as a jewel.

The core is thought to send and receive mysterious signals, sometimes seeming to communicate with
the night sky or even outer space. Its unusual, star-like form has also led people to imagine it as
something from beyond the sea, or as a reflection of stars.
