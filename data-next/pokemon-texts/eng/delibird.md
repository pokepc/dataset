## Delivery Pokémon

Delibird carries food wrapped in its tail and spends the day gathering more, always ready to share
it with people and Pokémon in need. Its generous habit has inspired tales of lost travelers being
saved by its stored provisions, and one explorer even reached the world’s highest mountain with
Delibird’s help.

It nests at the edges of sharp cliffs and carries food for its waiting chicks. When attacked, it may
throw its food at an opponent, and the biggest-tailed Delibird is said to lead the flock.
