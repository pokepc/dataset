## Discharge Pokémon

Manectric constantly discharges electricity from its mane, which gathers electrical energy from the
atmosphere. The sparks it gives off can even start forest fires, and it is said to be found beneath
unnatural thunderclouds or where lightning has struck.

When it enters battle, Manectric creates thunderclouds overhead and can call down lightning bolts.
It stimulates its muscles with electricity to move quickly, and uses that same electricity to ease
soreness and recover.
