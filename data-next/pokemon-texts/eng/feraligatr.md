## Big Jaw Pokémon

Feraligatr has massive jaws that it uses to bite down and tear at prey, shaking its head fiercely as
it attacks. It usually moves slowly, but when it goes on the offensive, it can burst forward with
blinding speed.

Out of water, it has trouble supporting its heavy body and sometimes lowers itself to all fours, yet
it still moves fast. It also opens its huge mouth to intimidate foes before charging in with
powerful hind legs.
