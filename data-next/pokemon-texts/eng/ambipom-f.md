## Long Tail Pokémon

Ambipom uses its two tails for nearly everything, deftly handling food and other tasks so it rarely
relies on its arms. It can even shuck nuts with its tails, and consecutive strikes from both tails
are difficult to dodge.

This species lives in large colonies high in trees, where members link their tails to form rings as
a sign of friendship or kinship. On rare occasions, humans have even been accepted into these
circles.
