## Poodle Pokémon

Furfrou’s Heart Trim gives it a refined look and can also make its movements swifter. In Kalos,
these Pokémon were once known as guardians of the king, and there was a time when aristocrats
competed to create the most exquisite trim styles.

Its fur keeps growing if left alone, but it will only let someone it trusts cut it. The trim is only
a change in appearance, and it can be altered again at any time.
