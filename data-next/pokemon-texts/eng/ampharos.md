## Light Pokémon

Ampharos gives off a brilliant light from the tip of its tail that can be seen from far away, even
from space. Since ancient times, people have treasured this light as a beacon, using it to send
signals and guide sailors across the sea.

Its shining tail also helps lost people find their way, and its glow can be visible on the ocean’s
surface at night. Because its light is so easy to spot, Ampharos often keeps it turned off.
