## Blimp Pokémon

Drifblim drifts silently at dusk, often in large swarms that seem to vanish from sight as quickly as
they appear. It is drowsy by day, but evening brings it aloft on the wind, and its aimless drifting
can carry people and Pokémon anywhere.

It controls its altitude by generating and releasing gas within its body, and the gas is said to
come from souls. Its melancholy, mysterious presence has inspired songs and poems, while some people
whisper that a ride on a Drifblim can lead to the afterlife.
