## Hermit Crab Pokémon

Slowbro lives lazily by the sea, with a Shellder firmly attached to its tail. The Shellder feeds on
the tasty flavor that seeps from the tail, and its poison has made Slowbro even duller and unable to
feel pain. Because the tail can no longer be used for fishing, it reluctantly swims after prey
instead.
