## Blade Pokémon

Mega Gallade is a master of courtesy and swordsmanship, fighting with blades that extend from its
elbows. Those blades are said to be sharper than the finest swords, and its self-taught skill is
astonishingly impressive.

Sharply attuned to the wishes of others, it seeks out those in need and aids them in battle. True to
its honorable nature, it uses its blades only to defend someone, striking first, fast, and fiercely
when it senses danger.
