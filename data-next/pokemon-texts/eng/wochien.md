## Ruinous Pokémon

Wo-Chien is said to have formed from the grudge of someone punished for recording a king’s misdeeds
on wooden tablets, its body cloaked in dead leaves. This eerie presence gives it a sorrowful,
ancient air.

It drains the life force from plants around it, and the vegetation nearby quickly withers. Forests
can fall barren in its wake, leaving the land dry and lifeless.
