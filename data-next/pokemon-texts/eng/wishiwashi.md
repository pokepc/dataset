## Small Fry Pokémon

Wishiwashi is incredibly weak on its own, and it is often targeted because it is considered tasty.
To survive, it gathers into schools, and even those that have strayed alone can be seen joining
others of their kind.

When danger or trouble appears, its eyes tear up and begin to shine. That light can be seen from far
away and draws other Wishiwashi together, letting the school stand against enemies and punish
bullies with a painful lesson.
