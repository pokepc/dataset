## Fruit Pokémon

Tropius loves fruit so much that it eats it constantly, and that devotion seems to have caused the
bunches of fruit that grow around its neck. These sweet, delicious fruits are popular with children
and are often eaten as snacks in tropical areas; in warm regions, ranches even raise Tropius for
them.
