## Sludge Pokémon

Grimer is made of congealed sludge and is found in filthy places, where it thrives on polluted waste
from factories and sewers. Its rubbery body can squeeze through even tiny openings, and it
constantly leaks germ-infested fluid as it moves.

It is said to have formed from sludge exposed to the moon’s X-rays or from polluted seabed sludge.
As it travels, it leaves behind germs so dense that plants will not grow, and pieces of its body can
break off to form more Grimer. Without filthy food, it cannot survive
