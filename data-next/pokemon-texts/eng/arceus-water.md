## Alpha Pokémon

In myth, Arceus is the being that came before the universe and emerged from an egg in a place of
nothingness, then shaped the world and all that exists. It is also said to have fashioned the
universe with its 1,000 arms.

As the heavenly source of light in Hisui, it is believed to spread a radiance that guides and
protects all Pokémon. This Water Type form changes to match a held Plate through Multitype.
