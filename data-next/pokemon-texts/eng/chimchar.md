## Chimp Pokémon

Chimchar is very agile and can scale sheer cliffs to live atop craggy mountains. Full of vigor and
always in high spirits, it was once known as "Lantern-Tail" and feared as a kind of apparition.

Gas made in its belly feeds the flame on its rear end, and even rain cannot easily put it out. When
Chimchar feels sick, that fire burns weakly, and before it sleeps, it extinguishes the flame to
prevent accidents.
