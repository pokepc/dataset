## Rock Pokémon

Geodude are commonly found in fields, along roads, and near mountain trails, where they are so
rocklike that people often mistake them for boulders and step on them by accident. When disturbed,
they become angry and swing their fists, though they often stay half buried or motionless at rest.

They climb steep mountain paths and cliffs with their arms, using both hands on the hardest routes.
