## EleSpider Pokémon

Galvantula spins electrically charged threads into webs and barriers that can stun foes. These
silken defenses serve as both protection and a weapon, helping it immobilize prey or fend off
attackers.

It traps its prey in electrified webs, then feeds while they remain shocked. It can also launch
electrified fur from its abdomen, and it is known to set its traps near bird nests to catch chicks
that cannot fly well.
