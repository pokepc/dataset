## Rock Pokémon

Graveler’s slow, uncaring nature shows in the way it moves: instead of walking, it curls up and
rolls down mountain paths, cliffs, and steep trails. Once it gains momentum, it barrels straight
through obstacles, even smashing aside rocks and trees without changing direction, and nothing can
easily stop it.

It lives in holes it digs into sheer stone walls and feeds on rocks, especially moss-covered ones.
