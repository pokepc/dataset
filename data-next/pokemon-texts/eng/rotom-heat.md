## Plasma Pokémon

Heat Rotom has a plasma body that can slip into machines and take them over, causing all kinds of
mischief. It is smart, playful, and often surprises people by inhabiting appliances or other
devices.

Research has long focused on Rotom’s unusual power, since it may even serve as the source for
special motors. Its electric-like form is tied to many different machines, and its various forms are
known for having distinct traits.
