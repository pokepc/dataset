## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a strange Pokémon shaped like ancient writing or hieroglyphs, and its flat, thin body is
usually seen stuck to walls. Its form seems to carry meaning, but researchers still do not know
whether the old writings or Unown came first.

These Pokémon are said to be linked somehow, and different forms may have different abilities. They
also seem to communicate telepathically, and when two or more are together, an odd power is said to
emerge.
