## Pond Skater Pokémon

Surskit secretes oil from the tips of its feet, letting it move across water as if skating. It lives
in ponds, lakes, and marshes with plenty of plant life, and after evening rain it may even appear on
puddles in towns.

It feeds on microscopic organisms and often draws prey with the sweet scent of a thick fluid from
the tip of its head. When threatened, it oozes a sugary syrup there instead, which some Pokémon
enjoy eating. It also often competes with Dewpider for food.
