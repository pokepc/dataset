## Long Tail Pokémon

Aipom lives high in the treetops, where it leaps from branch to branch and swings with ease. Its
powerful tail can grip branches, keep it balanced, and even hold it up in the air while it sleeps.

The tail ends in a handlike appendage that is more skillful than its real hands, which have grown
clumsy from overuse. It also uses that tail to reach fruit and spot prey from above, and it gets
especially excited when it finds Bounsweet.
