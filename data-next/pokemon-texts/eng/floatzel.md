## Sea Weasel Pokémon

Floatzel floats with a well-developed flotation sac that can also serve as a raft, keeping it from
drowning even in stormy seas. It is often seen around fishing ports and hamlets, where it helps
rescue people from the water.

The sac developed from its pursuit of aquatic prey, and when inflated it can carry people on its
back. Before diving, it deflates the sac, then slips underwater to chase prey or carry off a catch.
