## Hibernator Pokémon

Although Ursaring is large, it is a skilled climber that can eat and sleep high in the treetops. It
roams forests with streams and tall trees, gathering food each day and scratching marks into trees
that bear berries or fruit.

With a keen sense of smell, it can find food buried deep underground. It often snaps trees with its
forelegs to reach fallen berries, and in the cold season it wanders fields and mountains in search
of its favorite berries, becoming especially ferocious when hungry.
