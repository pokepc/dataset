## Savage Pokémon

Carvanha has powerful jaws and sharply pointed fangs that can shred boat hulls and splinter wooden
craft, so sailors avoid its dens at all costs. It lives in massive jungle rivers, where schools or
packs guard their own territory and attack intruders together.

Though it can be merciless in a group, Carvanha is timid when alone. It will not hunt by itself and
often waits for others to join before striking en masse. A faint trace of blood can send it rushing
into a fierce swarm attack.
