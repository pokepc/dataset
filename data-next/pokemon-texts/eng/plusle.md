## Cheering Pokémon

Plusle always acts as a cheerleader for its partners, flashing with electric sparks all over its
body when they do well in battle. It shorts out its electricity to make crackling noises and
spark-like pom-poms, and if its partner loses, it cries loudly.

To keep up its cheering, Plusle climbs telephone poles to absorb electricity and then scatters
sparks from its body as it encourages its friends.
