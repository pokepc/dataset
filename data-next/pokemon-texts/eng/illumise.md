## Firefly Pokémon

Illumise is a nocturnal Pokémon that becomes active at nightfall. Its sweet fragrance attracts
swarms of Volbeat, which it then leads in drawing signs and geometric patterns with light across the
night sky.

More than 200 patterns have been confirmed, and scientists study the designs it creates. Illumise is
said to earn greater respect from its peers by composing especially complex displays.
