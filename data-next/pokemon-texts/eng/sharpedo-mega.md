## Brutal Pokémon

Mega Sharpedo is widely feared as the bully of the sea, a vicious ruffian that attacks any ship
entering its waters. It propels itself by jetting seawater from its rear, reaching about 75 mph,
though it cannot travel long distances.

Its cruel fangs can crush iron, rip through sheet metal, and grow back if broken. Sharpedo is
especially sensitive to the scent of blood and lunges at prey the instant it detects one.
