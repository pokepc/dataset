## Land Shark Pokémon

Gible makes its home in narrow holes and crevices in cave walls, especially places warmed by
geothermal heat. It once lived in much warmer regions, and it keeps to these sheltered lairs to stay
comfortable even in cold weather.

It lunges at anything that moves near its nest, biting and dragging prey into its hiding place. Its
huge mouth makes its attacks powerful, but its clumsy rushes can chip or even knock out its teeth,
which soon grow back.
