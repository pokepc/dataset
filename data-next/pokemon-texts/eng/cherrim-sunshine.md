## Blossom Pokémon

As a bud, Cherrim sits still and waits placidly for sunlight. Its folded petals are tough enough to
shrug off pecking, and they give off a scent that can draw many other Pokémon nearby.

When strong sunlight shines on it, Cherrim blooms, opens its petals wide, and becomes lively and
cheerful. In this Sunshine Form, it absorbs the sun with its whole body; if the light fades, it
folds back into a bud.
