## Wolf Pokémon

Lycanroc’s swift, calm movements can confuse foes and slowly drive its prey into a corner. It is
cautious in battle, using its claws, fangs, and the razor-sharp rocks in its mane to wear opponents
down before striking at their weak points.

When raised properly from a young age, it can become a loyal partner that would never betray its
Trainer. Its mane rocks are sharper than knives, and fragments that break off are treasured as good
luck charms.
