## Hostile Pokémon

Zweilous wanders its territory while its two heads bicker over which way to go. The heads never get
along, and whichever one eats more becomes the leader.

Because they compete for every bit of food, Zweilous often eat too much and are covered in scars
even without fighting other Pokémon. After they exhaust the food in one area, they move on to
another.
