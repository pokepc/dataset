## Light Pokémon

Mega Ampharos gives off an intense light from the tip of its tail, bright enough to be seen from far
away and even from space. Since ancient times, people have treasured this light as a beacon and used
it to send signals over long distances.

At sea, its shining tail can guide sailors and serve as a navigation light to distant shores.
Because its glow makes it easy to locate, it often keeps the light off when it can.
