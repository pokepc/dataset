## Prototurtle Pokémon

Restored from an ancient fossil, Tirtouga is said to have lived in warm seas about 100 million years
ago and may be an ancestor of many turtle Pokémon. It swam skillfully in ancient oceans and could
dive to depths of more than half a mile.

Although it mainly lived at sea, Tirtouga also came onto land to search for prey. Its hunting range
stretched from shore to deep ocean, and even now it can only crawl as it does so.
