## Alpha Pokémon

In mythology, this Pokémon is said to have been born before the universe existed, emerging from an
egg in a place of nothing and then shaping the world. Sinnoh legend also credits it with forming all
there is with its thousand arms.

In Hisui, it is revered as the heavenly source of the light that shines across the land, guiding and
protecting all Pokémon. Its form changes with a held Plate, and its type changes to match.
