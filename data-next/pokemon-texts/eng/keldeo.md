## Colt Pokémon

By blasting water from its hooves, Keldeo can glide across the surface of water and race over oceans
and rivers. It often appears at scenic waterfronts as it crosses the world.

Cobalion, Terrakion, and Virizion taught it how to battle, and it keeps dashing in search of more
chances to train. When Keldeo is resolute, its body fills with power, making it swifter and its
jumps too fast to follow. Its true power is said to awaken only after harsh battles and full
development of the horn on its forehead.
