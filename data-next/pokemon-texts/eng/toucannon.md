## Cannon Pokémon

When Toucannon battles, its beak heats up to extreme temperatures and can cause severe burns on
impact. It can also ignite internal gas to launch seeds with explosive force strong enough to
pulverize boulders.

Toucannon communicates with others of its kind by smacking beaks together. The number and strength
of the hits convey its feelings. It is also known for forming harmonious pairs and is sometimes
brought to wedding ceremonies as a good-luck charm.
