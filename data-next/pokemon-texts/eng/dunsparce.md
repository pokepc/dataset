## Land Snake Pokémon

Dunsparce uses its drill-like tail to burrow backward into the ground whenever it is spotted,
slipping away through tunnels it makes with surprising speed. It lives deep underground, where it
builds complex, maze-like nests and never seems to lose its way.

In dark places it may remain nearly motionless, and its tiny wings let it hover or float only a
little. Some believe those wings hint at an ancient time when it could fly, though today it travels
mainly by digging through the earth.
