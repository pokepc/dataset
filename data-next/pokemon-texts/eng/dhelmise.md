## Sea Creeper Pokémon

Dhelmise is a ghost Pokémon born when seaweed merged with debris from a sunken ship. What looks like
green seaweed is actually its body, and it keeps itself together by taking in more seabed detritus
and seaweed.

It swings a massive anchor and can stretch its chain-like seaweed far into the distance. Dhelmise
prefers large prey such as Wailord, waiting with its anchor lowered before wrapping them in seaweed
and draining their life-force.
