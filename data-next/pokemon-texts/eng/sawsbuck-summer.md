## Season Pokémon

Sawsbuck migrate with the changing seasons, and its horns reveal the time of year at a glance. The
plants growing on them shift with the season, and the leaders of the herd are said to have
especially magnificent horns.

Because it appears as spring arrives, some people call Sawsbuck a harbinger of spring. In this form,
it changes appearance to match the current season, though its seasonal form is only a matter of
appearance.
