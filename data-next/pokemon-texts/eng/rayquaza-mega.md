## Sky High Pokémon

Mega Rayquaza is said to have lived for hundreds of millions of years in the ozone layer, far above
the clouds, where it was long unseen from the ground. It is known to fly endlessly through the sky,
and its shape in flight resembles a meteor.

It feeds on water and particles in the atmosphere, and even meteoroids are said to sustain it.
Legends also tell of it calming the clash between Kyogre and Groudon, while the energy stored in its
body lets it Mega Evolve.
