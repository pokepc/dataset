## Hermit Crab Pokémon

Mega Slowbro lives lazily by the sea, still linked to the Shellder clamped on its tail. The Shellder
feeds on sweet fluid from the tail and will not let go, while the poisoned bite leaves Slowbro
unable to feel pain and even more spaced out than before.

Because its tail can no longer be used for fishing, it reluctantly swims after prey. Though usually
dim-witted, the bite can briefly spark inspiration. If the Shellder is thrown off or the tail
breaks, it reverts to Slowpoke.
