## Garden Pokémon

Florges claims exquisite flower gardens as its territory and draws power from the energy given off
by flowering plants. It has long been said that castle governors invited it to create beautiful
gardens to adorn their domains.

It can control the flowers it grows, and the petal blizzards it unleashes are said to be both
beautiful and overwhelming. Florges may live for several hundred years, and it is devoted to
protecting gardens throughout its long life.
