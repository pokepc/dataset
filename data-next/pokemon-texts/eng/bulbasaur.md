## Seed Pokémon

Bulbasaur is born with a seed on its back, and the plant sprouts and grows along with it. The seed
is packed with nutrients, helping the young Pokémon grow, and it slowly becomes larger as Bulbasaur
does.

It can go for days without eating because it stores energy in the bulb on its back. Bulbasaur is
often seen napping in bright sunlight, soaking up the rays to help the seed continue to grow.
