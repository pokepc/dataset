## Wood Gecko Pokémon

Treecko has small hooks and tiny spikes on the bottoms of its feet, letting it scale vertical walls
and even cling to ceilings. It can also hang from above, and it uses its thick tail to strike foes.

Cool, calm, and collected, Treecko never panics, even when challenged by larger opponents. It is
said to guard the trees of its forest home and makes its nest in a giant tree.

Treecko can sense humidity with its tail, allowing it to predict the next day’s weather.
