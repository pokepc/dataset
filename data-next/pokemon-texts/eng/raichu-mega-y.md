## Mouse Pokémon

Raichu’s long tail acts as a ground, shielding it from its own powerful electricity. When its
pouches or body build up too much charge, it becomes feisty and more aggressive, then plants its
tail to discharge the excess and calm itself. Its body gives off a faint glow in the dark, and if
its pouches run low, it raises its tail to draw electricity from the air.
