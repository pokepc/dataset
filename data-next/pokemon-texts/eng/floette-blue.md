## Single Bloom Pokémon

Floette flutters through flower fields, tending to blossoms that are starting to wilt and giving
them its own power. It pours its heart into caring for flowers, and it never forgives anyone who
ruins a flower bed.

When a well-tended bed comes into bloom, it appears to celebrate with an elegant dance. It also
draws out the hidden power of flowers to battle, using especially beautiful blossoms as stronger
weapons.
