## Predator Pokémon

Staraptor is a fierce, solitary Pokémon that leaves the flock after evolving and becomes strong
enough to survive on its own. Its sturdy wings and powerful muscles let it fly with great force,
even while carrying away a small Pokémon or other heavy prey.

It has a savage nature and will boldly challenge foes much larger than itself. Even when injured, it
keeps attacking without hesitation, and it is unusually particular about the shape of its comb.
