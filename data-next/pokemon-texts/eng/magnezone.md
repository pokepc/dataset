## Magnet Area Pokémon

Magnezone evolved when a special magnetic field altered Magneton’s molecular structure, and three
units generate its powerful magnetism. Its own magnetic force can become so strong that Magnezone
are drawn together and unable to move.

It can fly through the sky, where it has often been mistaken for a UFO. It uses radar to watch over
its territory and quickly drives away intruders with hyper beam, while its antenna appears to send
and receive strange signals and radio waves of unknown origin.
