## Iron Snake Pokémon

Steelix is said to form when an Onix lives long enough for its body to become diamond-like, though
iron taken in with swallowed soil is also said to transform it. Its body is compressed deep
underground, making it harder than any metal and even harder than diamond.

It lives even deeper than Onix and is known to dig toward the earth’s core, with records of it
reaching more than six-tenths of a mile below ground. Its sturdy jaws chew through boulders, and its
eyes can see in the darkness underground.
