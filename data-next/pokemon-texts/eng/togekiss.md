## Jubilee Pokémon

Togekiss is rarely seen, and it avoids places touched by strife, conflict, or turmoil. In times of
peace, it is said to appear in quiet regions, where sightings have become scarce in recent years.

Known as a bringer of blessings, Togekiss is described as sharing kindness and good fortune with
people who respect one another. It has even been depicted on good-luck charms since ancient times,
and is believed to reveal itself when peace reigns.
