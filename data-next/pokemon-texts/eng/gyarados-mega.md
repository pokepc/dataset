## Atrocious Pokémon

Mega Gyarados is brutally vicious and enormously destructive, with a rage that does not settle once
it begins to rampage. It is said to appear amid conflict and leave blazing ruins behind, burning
through everything around it even in harsh storms.

Its violent nature is tied to the transformation brought on by evolution, which is believed to make
it far more ferocious. With searing power from its mouth, it can incinerate targets and reduce
towns, villages, and even entire cities to ashes.
