## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that clings to walls, and its shape resembles ancient writing or
hieroglyphs on old tablets. Its letter-like form seems to carry meaning, but it remains a mystery
whether human language or Unown came first.

Unown are often found stuck to walls and appear to communicate telepathically. When several are
together, an odd power is said to emerge, while one alone does nothing noticeable. Different forms
are thought to have different abilities, though their true nature is still not知
