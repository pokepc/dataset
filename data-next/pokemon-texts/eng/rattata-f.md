## Mouse Pokémon

Rattata is cautious and hardy, able to live almost anywhere and even in dirty places as long as food
is available. It is small, very quick, and commonly seen in many areas, often with many others
nearby.

It bites and chews on almost anything with its sharp fangs, which grow continuously throughout its
life. To keep them worn down, it gnaws on hard objects and even wooden surfaces while searching for
food all day. It also stays alert by moving its ears, even while asleep.
