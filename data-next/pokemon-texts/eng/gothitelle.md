## Astral Body Pokémon

Gothitelle’s intense psychic power distorts space, making starry skies thousands of light-years away
visible around it. By reading the placement and movement of the stars, it can predict the future
with uncanny accuracy.

It can even see a Trainer’s life span, and when it learns this, it cries in sadness. Though it has
tremendous power, Gothitelle dislikes conflict and is said to quietly reveal a criminal’s fate.
