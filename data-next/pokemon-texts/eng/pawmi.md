## Mouse Pokémon

Pawmi has underdeveloped electric sacs on its cheeks, and it must rub them furiously with the pads
on its forepaws to build up enough electricity to use. Its paw pads are themselves
electricity-discharging organs, helping it generate power through constant motion.

It often releases electricity from its forepaws while balancing unsteadily on its hind legs, giving
its actions a shaky but determined look.
