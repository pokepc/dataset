## Vast White Pokémon

Reshiram appears in legend as a Pokémon whose tail sends flames into the air, scorching everything
around it. As it flies through the sky, fire pours from its tail like a jet’s exhaust, and it is
said to be powerful enough to scorch the world.

When its tail flares, the heat can disturb the atmosphere and even change the weather. Myths say
Reshiram supports those who seek a world of truth, but if people turn from truth and are consumed by
greed, it may come to burn their kingdoms down.
