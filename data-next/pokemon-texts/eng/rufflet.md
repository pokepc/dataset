## Eaglet Pokémon

Rufflet is a hotheaded, combative Pokémon that will challenge almost anything it sees, including
much stronger opponents. It is reckless rather than truly courageous, but each fight helps it grow
stronger, and it becomes more powerful through frequent battles, even after being injured or
fainting.
