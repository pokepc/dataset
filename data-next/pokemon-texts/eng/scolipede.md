## Megapede Pokémon

Scolipede is a highly aggressive Pokemon that chases down foes with quick movements and attacks
relentlessly with its horns until it prevails. It can also latch onto prey with the claws near its
neck, dig in, and poison it with their toxic spikes.

Once it has seized its target, Scolipede may clamp on until the prey stops moving, then finish it
with deadly poison. It is known to slam victims into the ground and to engage in fierce territorial
battles, sometimes eating the loser when it wins.
