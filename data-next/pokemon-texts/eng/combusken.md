## Young Fowl Pokémon

Combusken toughens its legs and thighs by running through fields and mountains, building the speed
and power needed to unleash ten kicks in a second. Its kicking mastery gives it destructive force,
and its strong fighting instinct drives it to keep pressing the attack until its opponent gives up.
