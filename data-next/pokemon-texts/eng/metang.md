## Iron Claw Pokémon

Metang is formed when two Beldum fuse together, their brains linked by a magnetic nervous system.
This union doubles its psychic power, letting it unleash strong psychokinetic energy, though its
intelligence does not increase.

By using magnetism to float, Metang can travel at high speed, even turning its arms backward for
flight. Its steel body is extremely tough, and its claws can tear through thick iron while it grips
prey firmly and chases magnetic minerals such as Nosepass.
