## Grove Pokémon

Grotle lives along clean water in forests and is said to know where pure spring water wells up. It
will even carry other Pokémon on its back to these hidden sources, and some Grotle living in the
forest are believed to have a spring of their own.

Its shell is hardened soil with trees and shrubs growing from it, and the fruit there is sweet,
nutritious, and delicious. By day, Grotle leaves the forest to sunbathe its treed shell, while other
Pokémon sometimes peck at the berries on its back.
