## Cruel Pokémon

Liepard is admired for its beautiful fur and elegant figure, which come from its well-developed
muscles. It moves silently through the night and can vanish and appear unexpectedly, making it hard
to notice until it is close.

This stealthy Pokémon sneaks up behind its prey without making a sound, then strikes before its
victim can react. Despite its gorgeous appearance, it is moody, vicious, and fiercely territorial.
