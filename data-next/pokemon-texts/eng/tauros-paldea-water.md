## Wild Bull Pokémon

Tauros is a rowdy, highly durable Pokémon that lives in herds. The one with the longest, thickest,
most battle-scarred horns leads the group, and herd members even lock horns with one another to
prove their strength.

When Tauros sights a foe, it whips itself with its long tails as a warning before bursting into a
headlong charge. It is famous for its violent nature and unstoppable momentum, and if it has no
opponent, it may ram thick trees until it calms down.
