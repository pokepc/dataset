## Fruit Pokémon

Steenee is always bouncing about with lively energy, and its pleasant aroma draws the attention of
other Pokémon. As it twirls like a dancer, it spreads a sweet scent that can fill those nearby with
a surge of happiness.

The sepals on its head have grown hard to protect its body, so even pecking bird Pokémon do not
bother it. It swings those sepals around freely, and anyone hit by them may find the blow
surprisingly painful.
