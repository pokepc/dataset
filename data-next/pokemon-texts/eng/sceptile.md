## Forest Pokémon

Sceptile is an agile forest dweller that leaps through tree branches and attacks from above or
behind. The sharp leaves on its body and arms can cut down thick trees, making it a formidable
fighter in jungle combat.

It also carries seeds on its back said to be rich in nutrients that help revive trees. Sceptile
carefully tends the plants and trees around it, and it is said to bask in sunlight to regulate its
body temperature.
