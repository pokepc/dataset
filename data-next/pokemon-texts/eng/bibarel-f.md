## Beaver Pokémon

Bibarel busily builds its nest by damming streams and rivers with bark, mud, branches, and roots it
has cut with its sharp incisors. Its work is so steady and thorough that the dams it makes help keep
nearby waters from overflowing, which people appreciate.

Though it moves sluggishly on land, Bibarel swims very well. Its fur repels water and also helps
retain heat, making it well suited to a life spent around the dams it creates.
