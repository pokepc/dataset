## Wicked Pokémon

Shiftry lives quietly in deep forests, often in places people do not venture, and is said to dwell
atop towering trees that are centuries old. Long feared as a forest guardian, it is associated with
chilly winter winds and mysterious appearances on wintry gusts.

With the large leafy fans it holds, Shiftry can whip up fierce winds that can level houses and blow
almost anything away. It is also said to sense an opponent’s thoughts, letting it act before its
foe.
