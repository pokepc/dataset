## Pumpkin Pokémon

On dark, new-moon nights, Gourgeist wanders town streets and comes knocking at doors. Its eerie
singing and cries are said to curse anyone who hears or answers, and the sounds are whispered to be
the wails of suffering spirits.

It wraps prey in its hairlike arms and sings joyfully while watching their struggle. This Small Size
form has the same body size as a Pumpkaboo, and its size cannot be changed.
