## Mud Fish Pokémon

Mega Swampert is tremendously strong, able to drag boulders weighing more than a ton and even tow a
large ship through the water. Its thick, rock-hard arms can smash foes with a single swing or break
boulders apart, and it can see clearly even in murky water.

Using its fins to sense subtle changes in waves and tidal winds, it can predict approaching storms.
When rough weather or a tidal wave is near, it protects its seaside nest by piling up boulders, and
it is known to swim with jet-ski-like speed.
