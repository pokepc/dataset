## Little Bear Pokémon

Teddiursa keeps its paws soaked in honey, and it spends much of its time licking the sweet taste
from them. When it finds honey, the crescent mark on its body glows, and the honey can be so
absorbed into its paws that each one tastes a little different.

It also makes its own honey by blending fruits with pollen gathered by Beedrill. Cunning and
careful, Teddiursa hoards food in hidden places before winter makes it scarce, and it may even slip
into Combee nests to take the honey they have stored.
