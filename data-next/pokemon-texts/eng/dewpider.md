## Water Bubble Pokémon

Dewpider normally lives underwater, but it comes onto land in search of food carrying a water bubble
on its head. The bubble lets it breathe by keeping dissolved oxygen with it and also protects its
soft head.

It forms the bubble at the rear of its body and then covers its head with it. When it meets enemies
or prey, it may smash its bubble-covered head into them.

When two Dewpider meet, they compare the sizes of their bubbles, and the one with the smaller bubble
gives way.
