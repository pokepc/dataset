## Wool Pokémon

Mareep’s soft, fluffy fleece rubs together to build static electricity, and the more charge it
stores, the brighter the bulb at the tip of its tail glows. When that charge builds up or the
weather turns cold, its wool can swell to twice its usual size, and touching it barehanded may give
a painful shock.
