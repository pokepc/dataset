## Bubble Jet Pokémon

Vaporeon lives near clean, beautiful waters, where its long finned tail and graceful shape can make
it seem like a mermaid. Its cells resemble water molecules, letting it blend into water and even
disappear from sight while submerged.

It is well adapted to aquatic life, with fins and gills that allow it to live underwater and move
through the water smoothly. It can also detect moisture with its fins; when they begin to tremble or
vibrate, rain is likely to fall within a few hours.
