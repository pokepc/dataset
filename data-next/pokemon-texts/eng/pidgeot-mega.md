## Bird Pokémon

Mega Pidgeot races through the skies at Mach 2, using its outstanding vision to spot prey from far
above. It can skim low over water to snatch unwary Magikarp, and its large talons make it a feared
hunter.

Its powerful chest muscles and strong wings can whip up fierce gusts with a few flaps, even bending
tall trees. It also spreads its gorgeous, glossy wings and striking head feathers to intimidate
enemies and catch the eye of Trainers.
