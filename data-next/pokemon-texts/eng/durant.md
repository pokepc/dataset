## Iron Ant Pokémon

Durant live in mountain nests made of complicated, interconnected tunnels that form maze-like
colonies. They lay their eggs deep inside these nests, and when threatened they work together to
defend them from Heatmor and other attackers.

These Pokémon attack in groups, with each individual taking on a different role in driving predators
away. Their steel armor helps protect them, and their large mandibles can crunch through rock when
they retaliate.
