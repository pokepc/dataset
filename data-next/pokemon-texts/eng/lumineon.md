## Neon Pokémon

Lumineon lives on the deep-sea floor, where it moves by crawling along the seabed with its long
front fins. In the darkness of the deep ocean, the patterns on its four tail fins and its shining
light can make it seem like stars scattered across the night sky.

It uses those gleaming fins to lure prey close, but the light can also draw in fierce predator
Pokémon. Because food is scarce in the depths, Lumineon often competes with Lanturn for prey, and it
is said to search for favorite meals such as Starmie.
