## Lava Pokémon

Magcargo’s shell is actually cooled skin or hardened magma, and it is so brittle that even a light
touch can make it crumble. It can restore its body by dipping into magma.

Its body burns at about 18,000 degrees Fahrenheit, with flames bursting from gaps in its shell and
circulating through it. Water vaporizes on contact, and rain turns instantly to steam, wrapping the
area in thick fog.
