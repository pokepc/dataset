## Eon Pokémon

Latios is highly intelligent and understands human speech, and it can even sense the emotions and
locations of others through telepathy. It can project images of what it has seen or imagined into
another’s mind, making its powers as subtle as they are remarkable.

This tender Pokémon dislikes fighting and has a docile temperament. It opens its heart only to a
Trainer with a compassionate spirit, and when it folds its forelegs and wings to cut air resistance,
it can fly faster than a jet plane.
