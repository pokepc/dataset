## Mouse Pokémon

Pikachu stores electricity in the sacs on its cheeks, building it up while it sleeps and releasing
it as crackling shocks when it is threatened, angry, or curious about something new. If it does not
discharge the power often enough, it can become stressed, and lack of sleep leaves it able to use
only weak electricity.
