## Barnacle Pokémon

Cradily roams the ocean floor and the shallows of warm seas in search of prey, and it can be seen on
beaches when the tide goes out. Its heavy body anchors it against rough seas, while its short legs
keep it from moving quickly.

It extends its tree trunk-like neck and eight tentacles to catch unwary prey, even reaching far
beyond its usual length. Once it has something in its grip, it secretes a strong digestive fluid and
dissolves the prey before feeding on it.
