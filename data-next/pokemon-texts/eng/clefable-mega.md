## Fairy Pokémon

Mega Clefable is a timid fairy Pokémon that is rarely seen, and it will hide at once when it senses
people. Its sensitive hearing can pick up even the faintest sounds from far away, so it prefers
quiet places and often lives deep in remote mountains.

It moves by skipping so lightly that it seems to fly, and its bouncy steps can even carry it across
water. On calm, moonlit nights, it may stroll over lakes or linger under the full moon, as if
listening for the voices of its kin.
