## Royal Pokémon

Female Pyroar live in prides where the males with the largest fiery manes lead, while the females
guard the cubs. They can unleash breath hotter than 10,000 degrees Fahrenheit and use it to fiercely
warn off challengers.

Pyroar are known to prefer raw meat and do not use their searing breath on prey. While males are
often lazy, they will defend their companions without hesitation when faced with a powerful foe.
