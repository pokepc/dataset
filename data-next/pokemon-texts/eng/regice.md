## Iceberg Pokémon

Regice’s body is made of ice from the ice age, and it is said to have been born beneath thick ice or
slept in a glacier for thousands of years. Researchers believe its ice is the same kind found at the
South Pole.

It cloaks itself in frigid air as cold as -328 degrees Fahrenheit, freezing anything that comes
near. Its body is so deeply frozen that it cannot melt, even in fire or magma.
