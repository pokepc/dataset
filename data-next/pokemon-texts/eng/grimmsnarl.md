## Bulk Up Pokémon

Grimmsnarl’s hair acts like muscle fibers, wrapping around its body to strengthen it and letting it
overpower even Machamp. When its hair unfurls, it can latch onto opponents and bind them like
tentacles.

This Pokémon uses its tangled hairs both for strength and for ensnaring foes, turning its body into
a formidable weapon in battle.
