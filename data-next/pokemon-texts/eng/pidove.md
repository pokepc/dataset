## Tiny Pigeon Pokémon

Pidove lives in cities and is very accustomed to people, often gathering in parks and plazas in
flocks that can grow very large when food is around. It follows where people go and is known for its
friendliness and sincerity, which makes it appealing to many Trainers.

It tries hard to obey its Trainer, but its forgetful nature and modest intelligence can make
complicated commands hard to understand. Even when it has already received an order, it may still
wait for another one.
