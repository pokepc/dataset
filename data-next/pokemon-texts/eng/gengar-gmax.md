## Shadow Pokémon

Gengar lurks in shadows, where a sudden chill may reveal its presence. It is said to steal heat from
the area around it, leaving those nearby shivering, and it waits in dark corners or even within a
person’s shadow for a chance to strike.

On dark nights and under a full moon, it may mimic passing shadows or make them seem to move and
laugh on their own. Tales also say it delights in casting curses and preys on the lost, pursuing
travelers and other victims from the darkness.
