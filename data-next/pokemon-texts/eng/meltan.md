## Hex Nut Pokémon

Meltan melts particles of iron and other metals found in the subsoil, absorbing them into its body
of molten steel. By dissolving and eating metal, it sustains itself and generates energy through the
liquid metal that circulates inside its body.

Meltan usually lives in groups, but when the time comes, a single strong Meltan will absorb the
others and evolve.
