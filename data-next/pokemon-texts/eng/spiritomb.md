## Forbidden Pokémon

Spiritomb was formed when 108 spirits gathered together, and some accounts say a few of them were
ill-natured. It is bound to an odd keystone or a fissure within it, held there by a mysterious
spell.

Stories say this binding was a punishment for its constant mischief and misdeeds, including wicked
thoughts that lay curses. Writings also describe it as being born from the assembly of five score
and eight malevolent spirits.
