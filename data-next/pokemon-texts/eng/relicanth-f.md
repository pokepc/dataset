## Longevity Pokémon

Relicanth is a rare living fossil discovered during deep-sea exploration, thought to have gone
extinct long ago. Its form has remained unchanged for 100 million years, and its rock-hard scales
and oil-filled body help it endure the crushing pressure of the ocean depths.

With atrophied teeth and a toothless mouth, it feeds on microscopic organisms. It also moves along
the seafloor using its pectoral fins, like a creature that has carried the same ancient shape since
prehistoric times.
