## Tiger Cat Pokémon

Purugly is a brazen, impudent Pokémon that will barge into another Pokémon’s nest and claim it if
the place seems comfortable enough. It is difficult to tame and keeps up a hard glare, staring
without relenting when it locks eyes with something.

To look more intimidatingly beefy, it tightly binds its body with its twin tails and cinches its
waist. Despite its rude nature, Purugly is popular for its fur, whose beauty is said to surpass even
velveteen.
