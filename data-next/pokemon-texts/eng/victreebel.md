## Flycatcher Pokémon

Victreebel is said to live in huge colonies deep in jungles, though no one who has gone there has
ever returned. It waves the long vine that hangs from its head like a living lure, drawing in
unsuspecting prey with its motion and a sweet, honeylike aroma.

When a victim comes close, Victreebel swallows it whole and traps it in a fluid that dissolves
anything, even hard objects. The acid in its mouth can melt prey away in a day, and the more it has
dissolved, the sweeter it seems to become.
