## Ice Dragon Pokémon

Baxcalibur blasts cryogenic air from its mouth, and the frigid breath can instantly freeze even
liquid-hot lava. Its icy power is so intense that it can overwhelm extreme heat without hesitation.

When it charges into battle, Baxcalibur flips upside down to launch itself forward on a rush of
freezing air. It then brings the fight to a close with the blade on its back, using that dorsal
weapon to finish off its opponents.
