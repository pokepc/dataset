## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a strangely shaped Pokémon that resembles ancient writing or hieroglyphs, and its many
forms seem to carry some kind of meaning. Researchers still do not know whether the ancient
characters or Unown came first.

Its flat, thin body is usually found stuck to walls, and different forms are said to communicate
telepathically. When several are together, an odd power is said to emerge, though nothing happens
when one is alone.
