## Long Tail Pokémon

Ambipom uses its two tails for almost everything, deftly handling food and other tasks while rarely
relying on its arms. It can even shuck nuts with its tails, and its quick consecutive tail strikes
are hard to avoid.

These Pokémon live in large colonies high in trees, where they link tails to form rings as a sign of
friendship and kinship. They may also communicate this way, and on rare occasions humans have been
accepted into their circles.
