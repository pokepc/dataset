## Bite Pokémon

Mightyena travels and hunts in packs, sometimes of around ten, and defeats foes through tightly
coordinated teamwork. It never defies its leader’s orders, and this old pack life is why it will
obey only Trainers it judges to be truly skilled.

When Mightyena is preparing to attack, it gives clear warning signs. It growsl deeply, flattens its
body, and then snaps at its target with sharply pointed fangs.
