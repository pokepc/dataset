## Meteorite Pokémon

Lunatone was discovered at the site of a meteor strike, leading some to wonder if it came from
space, though that has never been proven. It is closely tied to the lunar cycle, becoming especially
active on nights of the full moon and brimming with power in its light.

Instead of walking, Lunatone floats through the air. Its red eyes are said to frighten and mesmerize
those who meet its gaze, and its stare can even lull foes to sleep.
