## Vibration Pokémon

Seismitoad powers its bumps to create strong vibrations, and it uses those tremors to hurt
opponents. The vibrating bumps on its fists greatly strengthen its punches, enough to reduce a
boulder to rubble with a single blow.

It can also shoot a paralyzing liquid from the bumps on its head. The vibrations from the lumps all
over its body can cause earthquake-like tremors, and some people, especially the elderly, appreciate
those soothing vibrations as a massage.
