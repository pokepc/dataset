## Paradox Pokémon

This mysterious Pokémon bears a striking resemblance to the Slither Wing described in an old book,
though the account itself leaves its identity uncertain. It is also said to look somewhat like an
ancient form of Volcarona reported in a dubious magazine.

Taken together, these accounts suggest a Pokémon whose appearance recalls an ancient creature from
conflicting old sources, making its true nature feel mysterious and poorly documented.
