## Water Gun Pokémon

Clauncher fires compressed water from its claw like a bullet, using controlled bursts of gas to
launch powerful shots. At close range, the force can even shatter rock, and it can bring down flying
prey with these sudden attacks.

It moves by jetting water from its right pincer, though its poor balance makes it bad at swimming
straight. Its claws may fall off during battle and later grow back, so it keeps a low profile while
they regenerate. The meat inside them is said to be delicious.
