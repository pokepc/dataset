## Rushing Pokémon

Linoone always runs at full speed in a straight line, and it has a hard time following curved roads
or winding paths. If it needs to avoid an obstacle, it must brake hard before making a sharp turn,
which can make hunting difficult.

When it does charge at prey, it can top 60 mph and uses its sharp claws to catch its target, even
leaping into water to snatch pond-dwelling prey. Its fur is strong and supple, and shed hairs are
prized for making shaving brushes.
