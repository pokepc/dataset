## Owl Pokémon

Noctowl’s eyes are specially adapted to gather even the faintest light, letting it see clearly in
darkness or murky conditions. With this superior vision, it can catch prey where others cannot, and
some call it the emperor of dark nights.

Its soft, supple feathers and wings make it nearly silent in flight, so it can sneak up on prey
without being noticed. When it needs to think hard, it turns its head 180 degrees or upside down, a
sign of deep concentration.
