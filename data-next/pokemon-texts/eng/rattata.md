## Mouse Pokémon

Rattata is a cautious, hardy Pokémon that can live in almost any environment, even dirty places, and
it often makes its nest wherever food is available. Small and very quick, it is commonly seen
scavenging all day, and its numbers can multiply rapidly where conditions suit it.

It attacks by biting anything in reach, using long, sharp incisors that keep growing throughout its
life. To keep them worn down, it gnaws on hard objects, including wood and whatever else is handy.
Even while asleep, it keeps moving
