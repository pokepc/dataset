## Alpha Pokémon

In mythology, Arceus is said to have existed before the universe and emerged from an egg in a place
of nothingness before shaping the world. Legends of Sinnoh and Hisui describe it as the creator of
all things, said to have shaped the universe with its 1,000 arms.

As the heavenly source of light across Hisui, it is believed to guide and protect all Pokémon. This
Ground Type form changes its type to match a held Plate through Multitype.
