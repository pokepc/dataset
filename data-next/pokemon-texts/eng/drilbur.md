## Mole Pokémon

Drilbur spins its body or brings its claws together to whirl at high speed, letting it bore straight
through the ground at about 30 mph. Its speed underground is so great that it could even give a car
on the surface a run for its money.

It is a skilled digger that moves swiftly through soil with its claws, and it may rush toward prey
after building up speed. Because it burrows through fields, it often causes damage to vegetable
crops and is not welcomed by many farmers.
