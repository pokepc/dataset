## Key Ring Pokémon

Klefki constantly collects keys, and it seems to like them so much that even its horned head
resembles one. It can slip its horn into tiny gaps in metal to absorb metal ions, though the reason
it gathers keys is not fully clear.

It is even-tempered, but will jingle its keys fiercely to threaten attackers. Some people trust
Klefki with important keys, since it holds onto a key it likes and protects it no matter what. It
especially seems to favor master keys.
