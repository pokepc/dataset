## Sea Weasel Pokémon

Buizel keeps its head above water by inflating the flotation sac around its neck like an inflatable
collar, letting it watch for movement and see what is going on around it. When it dives, the sac
collapses again.

It propels itself through the water by spinning its two tails like a screw, or a forked tail in a
way that resembles a steamboat screw by coincidence. The tails also help slice away clinging
seaweed.
