## Emperor Pokémon

Empoleon is revered as the Master of the Waves in Hisui, where its powerful presence has long earned
respect. The three horns on its beak signal its strength, and the leader is said to have the largest
horns.

It swims as fast as a jet boat, using sharp wings that can slice through drifting ice and even ice
floes. Though it avoids needless conflict, it will mercilessly strike back if its pride is
threatened.
