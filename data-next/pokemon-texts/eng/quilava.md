## Volcano Pokémon

Quilava is fully covered in nonflammable fur, letting it withstand even fierce fire attacks. Its
flames can grow more intense as it prepares for battle, and it uses the heat to intimidate foes.

It keeps opponents at bay with blazing fire and gusts of superheated air, while its quick nimbleness
helps it dodge attacks even as it scorches them. If it turns its back, it is warning that a powerful
attack from the fire on its back is coming.
