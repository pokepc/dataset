## Elder Tree Pokémon

Trevenant controls the trees of the forest through roots that reach from its feet, using them almost
like a nervous system. It is feared as a ghostly guardian that will attack those who try to cut down
or ravage the forest, and legends say it can trap such intruders so they never leave.
