## Hermit Crab Pokémon

Slowbro lives lazily by the sea, often spacing out as it gazes at the water. A Shellder clings to
its tail and feeds on the sweet fluids there, and that bite is what transformed Slowpoke into
Slowbro.

With Shellder’s poison flowing through its body, Slowbro loses its sense of pain and may even be
briefly inspired when the shell bites down, though it quickly forgets. If Shellder is thrown off or
breaks away, Slowbro reverts to Slowpoke.
