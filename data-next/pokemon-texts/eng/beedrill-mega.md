## Poison Bee Pokémon

Mega Beedrill flies at violent speed, striking with the large venomous stingers on its forelegs and
tail. Its best attack is to dart around, jab repeatedly, and then pull away before the foe can
react.

It has three poisonous stingers, with the tail barb said to hold its most powerful poison. This form
is extremely territorial and may appear in swarms, attacking furiously if anyone comes near its
nest.
