## Pumpkin Pokémon

On dark new-moon nights, Gourgeist roams town streets and even comes knocking at doors. Those who
hear its eerie singing or answer its knock are said to be cursed or carried off to the afterlife.

It gives off ghostly cries that sound like the wails of suffering spirits. Gourgeist binds prey with
its hairlike arms, then sings cheerfully while watching it struggle. Its size is fixed to the form
it evolved from and cannot be changed.
