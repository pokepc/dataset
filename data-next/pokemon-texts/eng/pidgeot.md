## Bird Pokémon

Pidgeot has dazzling, glossy plumage and striking head feathers that many Trainers find beautiful.
It also spreads its broad wings to intimidate enemies while racing through the skies at Mach 2
speed.

Its powerful chest muscles let it whip up fierce gusts with only a few flaps, even strong enough to
bend tall trees. With outstanding vision, it can spot splashing prey from high above and skim the
water’s surface to seize unwary Magikarp.

Its large talons make it a feared predator as it hunts at high speed.
