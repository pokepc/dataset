## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is almost always found stuck to walls, its strange shape seeming
to carry some kind of meaning. Its form resembles ancient writing or hieroglyphs, and researchers
still cannot tell whether the symbols or Unown came first.

Different Unown forms are said to have different abilities, and when several are together, an odd
power may emerge. They also seem to communicate telepathically with one another, adding to the
mystery surrounding these unusual living characters.
