## Garden Pokémon

Florges makes exquisite flower gardens its territory and draws power from the energy given off by
flowering plants. In times long past, castle governors would invite it to create gardens that would
beautify their domains.

It controls the flowers it grows, and the petal blizzards it unleashes are said to be both beautiful
and overwhelming. Florges can live for several hundred years and is believed to devote its life to
protecting gardens.
