## Gleam Eyes Pokémon

Luxray can see through solid objects, instantly spotting prey hidden behind walls or other cover.
When its eyes shimmer gold, it is said to see through anything, and this talent also helps it scout
for danger.

It uses this power to track down prey and even find its lost young. Because seeing through solid
objects drains a lot of electricity, Luxray sleeps for long periods to build up energy. It forms
packs led by a single male.
