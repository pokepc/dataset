## Psi Pokémon

Abra sleeps for 18 hours a day, and its psychic strength is so taxing that it must rest that long to
keep its powers. Even while asleep, it can use its extrasensory abilities, sensing minds and danger
around it.

When threatened, Abra will teleport to safety before an attack can land, sometimes even while still
sleeping. It may also teleport at random, seeming to leave copies of itself behind as an illusion.
