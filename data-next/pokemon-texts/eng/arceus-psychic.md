## Alpha Pokémon

In mythology, Arceus is said to have been born before the universe existed, emerging from an egg in
a place where there was nothing and then shaping the world. Legends also describe it as the being
that shaped the universe with its 1,000 arms.

In Hisui, it is revered as the heavenly source of light that shines across the land, guiding and
protecting all Pokémon. As Psychic Type, its form changes with a held Plate through Multitype.
