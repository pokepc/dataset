## Cavern Pokémon

Terrakion is a legendary Pokémon spoken of in Unovan legend for its phenomenal power and fierce
resolve. It came to the defense of Pokémon that had lost their homes in a war among humans, and
battled against humans to protect other Pokémon.

Its charging attacks are powerful enough to break through a giant castle wall in one blow. It
mercilessly crushes anyone or anything that bullies smaller Pokémon.
