## Ogre Scorpion Pokémon

Drapion has a brutish, ferocious temperament and a sturdy shell that makes it hard to wound; even
swords will bounce off its body. Its head can rotate 180 degrees, helping it avoid blind spots, and
its clawed arms are strong enough to crush a car.
