## Frigatebird Pokémon

Kilowattrel inflates its throat sac to amplify the electricity it produces, and it stores the
current generated by its wings there. By riding the wind, it can travel more than 430 miles in a
day.

Its feathers contain very little oil, making it a poor swimmer. This Pokémon is built for the air,
where it can soar far and fast with remarkable efficiency.
