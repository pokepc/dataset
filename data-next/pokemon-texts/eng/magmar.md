## Spitfire Pokémon

Magmar was born in or near a volcano, and its body is constantly cloaked in flames with an orange
glow that can blend into fire. Its fiery surface shimmers like the sun, and when it breathes deeply,
heat waves rise around it and make it hard to see clearly.

It dislikes cold places and seeks hot surroundings, often moving through warm areas or heating its
environment with scorching blasts. In battle, it releases intensely hot fire from all over its body
to intimidate foes, sometimes igniting grass and trees
