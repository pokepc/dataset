## Darkness Pokémon

Sableye lives quietly deep in caves, where it lurks in darkness and is seen only rarely. It uses its
sharp claws to dig through the ground in search of rocks and raw gems, which it eats to survive.

The gemstones it feeds on can crystallize and rise to the surface of its body, giving each Sableye
unique patterns. In the dark, its gemstone eyes glitter, and when they shine with a sinister glow,
it is feared as a spirit thief.
