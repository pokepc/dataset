## Screech Pokémon

Misdreavus hides in darkness and waits for the middle of the night to startle people with sudden
screams, shrieks, or a creepy, sobbing cry. It enjoys mischievous tricks like sneaking up behind
people, biting and yanking their hair, and watching their shocked reactions.

Its red orbs absorb the fear it causes, turning those fearful feelings into nourishment and energy.
It is said to practice frightening others constantly, and the eerie light of its orbs often
accompanies its childlike weeping.
