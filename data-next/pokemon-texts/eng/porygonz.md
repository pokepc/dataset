## Virtual Pokémon

Porygon-Z was altered with additional software in hopes of making it a more advanced Pokémon, or
even enabling it to move through alien dimensions. The update appears to have had flaws, and its
behavior became strangely unstable.

Its offensive capabilities increased greatly, but the oddness of its movements and actions grew just
as much. Because of this defective modification, some even question whether Porygon-Z is truly an
evolution.
