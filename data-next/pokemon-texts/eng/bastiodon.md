## Shield Pokémon

Bastiodon is a docile Pokémon that feeds on grass and berries. Its huge, rock-hard face acts like a
shield, repelling frontal attacks and giving it a very stable defense; the face is said to be as
hard as steel, even stronger than diamond.

When threatened, Bastiodon and its kind can line up side by side to form an almost unbreakable wall,
often sheltering their young. However, this defense leaves them vulnerable to attacks from behind,
and much about their natural life remains unknown.
