## Cocoon Pokémon

Silcoon tethers itself to tree branches with silk and hangs quietly while it awaits evolution.
Wrapped in a cocoon of thin threads, it conserves energy by moving as little as possible, though its
eyes keep a sharp watch on its surroundings.

It was once thought to endure without eating, but it is now believed to drink rainwater or dew that
collects on its silk. The hard cocoon helps protect it as it stores the energy it gathered as a
Wurmple.
