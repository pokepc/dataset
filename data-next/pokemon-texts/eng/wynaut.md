## Bright Pokémon

Wynaut is almost always seen wearing a big, cheerful smile. Its tail reveals its mood: when it
becomes angry, it slaps the ground with it.

Wynaut tends to live and move in packs, clustering closely together to sleep in caves or to squeeze
against one another on moonlit nights. This jostling toughens its spirit and endurance, helping it
learn to deliver powerful counterattacks.

It also loves sweet fruit, using its earlike arms to pick it, and is often drawn to fruit gardens by
the scent.
