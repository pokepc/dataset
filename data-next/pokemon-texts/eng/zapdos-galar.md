## Electric Pokémon

Zapdos is a legendary bird Pokémon that lives in thunderclouds and is said to appear when the sky
grows dark or when a cloud splits apart. It freely controls electricity, and its wingbeats and
feather rubbing can bring down lightning, sometimes setting off fierce summer storms.

It is often described as nesting in the deepest, darkest thunderclouds, where crackling sounds
follow it as it flies. Zapdos is also said to grow stronger when struck by lightning itself.
