## Fang Scorpion Pokémon

Gliscor hangs upside down from branches to watch for prey, then swoops down when the moment is
right. It moves through the sky in complete silence, gliding on dark wings and seeming to dance as
it closes in on its target.

It can catch even the faintest breeze and ride it for great distances without a single flap. When it
attacks, it uses its long tail to seize prey and its sharp fangs to finish the job, taking on a
satisfied look afterward.
