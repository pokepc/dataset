## Wicked Pokémon

Shiftry is a mysterious Pokémon said to live atop towering trees that have stood for over a thousand
years, though it also chooses to remain quietly deep in forests where people rarely venture. Feared
long ago as a forest guardian, it is said to arrive on chilly winter winds.

By flapping its broad leafy fans, Shiftry can whip up violent gusts at nearly 100 feet per second,
powerful enough to blow things away and even level houses. It is also said to read an opponent’s
mind and act before the foe can move.
