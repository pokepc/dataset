## Lava Pokémon

Slugma’s body is made of magma, with intensely hot magma flowing through it instead of blood to
carry nutrients and oxygen. Because of this, it must keep moving and never sleeps; if it stops or
grows cold, its body hardens and can become brittle.

It is commonly seen in volcanic areas, slowly slithering about in search of warm places. To avoid
cooling off, it stays near heat and may gather with others in hotter-than-normal spots.
