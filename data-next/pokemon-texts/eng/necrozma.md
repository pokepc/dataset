## Prism Pokémon

Necrozma is thought to have come from another world long ago and may have lain asleep underground
for ages. Its body darkens when impurities build up, and it must absorb light to survive and stay
active.

Driven by that need, it rages in search of light and appears to be in pain as it does so. It has an
extraordinarily vicious disposition and constantly fires laser beams that can cut through almost
anything.
