## Rock Pokémon

Geodude is commonly found in fields, on roads, and along mountain trails, where it looks so much
like an ordinary boulder that people often step on or trip over it by accident. Many remain half
buried in the ground while watching climbers, and if disturbed, it can fly into a rage and swing its
fists.

It climbs steep, rugged paths with its arms and can use both hands to scale precipitous cliffs.
