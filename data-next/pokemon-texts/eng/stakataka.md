## Rampart Pokémon

Stakataka is a mysterious life-form that appeared from an Ultra Wormhole, and it seems to be made up
of many life-forms stacked one on top of another. Its strange, brick-like body gives it the
appearance of a moving stone wall.

When walls of stone begin to move and attack, Stakataka is revealed as the brute behind the
disturbance. Its uncanny presence evokes an Ultra Beast, adding to its air of mystery.
