## Ghost Dog Pokémon

It is said that Greavard is a dog Pokémon reborn from one that died in the wild without ever
interacting with a human. Its existence is tied to loneliness and the wilderness, giving it a
haunting yet gentle air.

Despite that origin, Greavard is very friendly and dislikes being alone. Even a little attention is
enough to make it latch onto someone and follow them devotedly.
