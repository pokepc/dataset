## Whisper Pokémon

Whismur usually murmurs so softly that it is barely audible, but when it senses danger or feels
frightened, it lets out cries as loud as a jet plane. The sound can be startlingly painful to anyone
nearby, and its own cry can frighten it into crying even harder.

This Pokémon is very timid and often works itself into exhaustion once it starts wailing. After
crying until it can hardly go on, Whismur settles down and falls asleep. Its ear canals let it keep
crying without needing to catch its breath.
