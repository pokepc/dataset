## Alpha Pokémon

In mythology, Arceus is said to have existed before the universe and to have emerged from an egg in
a place where nothing existed. Legends tell that it shaped the world and all there is within it.

It is also described as the heavenly source of light across Hisui, with a radiance that guides and
protects Pokémon. This Fighting Type form changes with a held Plate to match its type.
