## Small Fry Pokémon

When Wishiwashi School Form senses danger, its eyes tear up and begin to shine. The light from its
tears can be seen from far away and draws other Wishiwashi together, letting them gather into a
school to face enemies that would overwhelm them alone.

Individually, Wishiwashi are very weak and easy prey, even though they are also considered tasty.
When they unite, though, they can stand together against bullies and give attackers a painful
lesson.
