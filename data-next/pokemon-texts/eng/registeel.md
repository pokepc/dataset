## Iron Pokémon

Registeel’s body is harder than any metal, yet it is also hollow and made of a strange, mysterious
material that can stretch and shrink. Studies say its body cannot be scratched, and it seems to have
been tempered by immense pressure deep underground over tens of thousands of years.

Long ago, people imprisoned this Pokémon in ancient times. Some even say it was born deep in the
planet’s mantle and emerged to the surface about 10,000 years ago. No one knows what it eats.
