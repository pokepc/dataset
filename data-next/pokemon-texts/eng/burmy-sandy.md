## Bagworm Pokémon

Burmy’s Sandy Cloak is a shelter of twigs, leaves, and other nearby materials that helps protect its
scrawny body from cold, wintry winds. If the cloak is damaged, it quickly patches it up with
whatever is at hand, and it may keep remaking the covering even when suitable materials are scarce.

In warm weather, the cloak becomes thinner, but it is still worn for protection. Burmy’s cloak can
also reflect its surroundings, with sandy or rocky places encouraging this form.
