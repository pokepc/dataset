## Apple Nectar Pokémon

Appletun evolved after eating a sweet apple, and its body gives off a nectar-like aroma that
attracts bug Pokémon it feeds on. Its sweet scent seems to spread from all over its body, making it
easy to draw prey close.

Its body is coated in sweet nectar, and the skin on its back is especially delicious. In the past,
children even treated it as a snack.
