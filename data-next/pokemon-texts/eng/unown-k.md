## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a mysterious Pokémon shaped like ancient writing or hieroglyphs, and researchers still do
not know whether human language or Unown came first. Its flat, thin body is usually found stuck to
walls, and its form appears to carry some kind of meaning.

Unown are said to communicate telepathically, and when two or more are present, an odd power may
emerge. Its many forms only change appearance, but each one deepens the mystery of these strangely
shaped Pokémon.
