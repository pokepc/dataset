## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is usually found stuck to walls, its shape seeming to carry some
meaning. It resembles ancient writing or hieroglyphs, and researchers still do not know whether the
markings inspired Unown or the other way around.

These strange forms are thought to be linked in some way, and they may communicate telepathically
with one another. Alone, nothing happens, but when two or more are present, an odd power is said to
emerge.
