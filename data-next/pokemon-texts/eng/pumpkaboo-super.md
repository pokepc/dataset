## Pumpkin Pokémon

A trapped spirit inhabits the pumpkin body of Pumpkaboo. At sunset, it grows restless and active,
and its eerie light can hypnotize and control those who see it.

Pumpkaboo is said to gather wandering spirits into its body and carry them on to where they belong.
Its size is fixed when it is encountered, and the Super Size form has its own distinct stats.
