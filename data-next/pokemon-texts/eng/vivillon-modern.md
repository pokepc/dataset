## Scale Pokémon

Vivillon’s wing patterns vary around the world, shaped by the climate and topography of its habitat.
This form’s pattern is fixed and differs only in appearance from other Vivillon patterns, while it
continues to scatter colorful scales.

Modern Pattern Vivillon cannot change its pattern once set. The pattern depends on the real-world
region linked to its original Trainer, and trading a Scatterbug, Spewpa, or egg does not alter the
form it will become.
