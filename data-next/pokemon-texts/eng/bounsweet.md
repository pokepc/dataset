## Fruit Pokémon

Bounsweet gives off a sweet, fruity aroma that is especially tempting to bird Pokémon. Its body is
so appealing that it is often swallowed whole, and when threatened it only seems to draw in even
more enemies.

To defend itself, Bounsweet spins the sepals on its head in an attempt to keep bird Pokémon away,
but this usually does little good. Its sweat is too sugary for people to drink as-is, though it can
be diluted into a pleasant juice.
