## Blazing Pokémon

When injured in fierce battle, Darmanitan hardens into a stone-like form and meditates, sharpening
its mind to keep fighting on. In this state, its power is tied to the heat of its internal fire,
which can reach 2,500 degrees Fahrenheit.

That intense fire gives it tremendous force, and its thick arms can deliver punches powerful enough
to obliterate a dump truck.
