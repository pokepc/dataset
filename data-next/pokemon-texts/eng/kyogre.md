## Sea Basin Pokémon

Kyogre is said to be the personification of the sea itself, a mythical Pokémon that has expanded the
oceans by bringing heavy rains and towering tidal waves. It can control water, summon rain clouds
that fill the sky, and even save people from drought.

Legends tell of many clashes with Groudon, after which Kyogre fell into a long sleep in the depths
of the sea or a marine trench. Through Primal Reversion, it can return to its true form and call
storms that raise sea levels.
