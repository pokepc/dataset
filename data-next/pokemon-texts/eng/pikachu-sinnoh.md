## Mouse Pokémon

Pikachu stores electricity in its cheek sacs, which charge while it sleeps and can discharge
suddenly if it is threatened, startled, or still drowsy after waking. Its stored power can equal a
lightning bolt, and when many Pikachu gather, their combined electricity may even trigger storms.
