## Gear Pokémon

Klang’s body is made of a big gear and a minigear. By changing the direction of its rotation, it
communicates its feelings, and when it is angry, it spins faster.

It can fire its minigear at opponents at high speed, and if the gear does not return, Klang will
die. When it goes all out, the minigear fits perfectly into the outer gear and its rotation speed
rises sharply. In Galar, it is seen as a symbol of industrial technology and is often used as a
logo.
