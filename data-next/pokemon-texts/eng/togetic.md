## Happiness Pokémon

Togetic is said to bring good fortune, appearing before kindhearted or pure-hearted people to share
its happiness and shower them with joy dust. It is drawn to caring people and grows dispirited if it
is not with them.

Although it barely flaps its wings, Togetic can float in midair and even stay aloft while following
its trainer. Reports also suggest it is not seen in the wild and may evolve under the loving care of
a trusted human companion.
