## Floating Pokémon

Jellicent is mostly seawater, and it moves by expelling the water it has absorbed. It is said to
make sunken ships its lair, with a castle of wrecks resting on the seafloor where it lives.

It lurks near fancy cruise ships and tankers, waiting to drag prey away. Fishermen fear that it can
pull them into the sea and steal their life energy, and ships and crews that enter its habitat are
said to be lost without a trace. On full-moon nights, schools of Jellicent gather near the surface
to hunt.
