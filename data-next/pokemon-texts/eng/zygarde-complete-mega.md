## Order Pokémon

When the Kalos region’s ecosystem falls into disarray, Mega Zygarde (Complete Forme) appears and
reveals its hidden power. It is said to watch over the land from deep within its cave, monitoring
those who would harm the ecosystem.

This form is formed when about half of its cells have assembled, and it shows no mercy to those who
oppose it. Some believe it can still change into an even more powerful state when fighting threats
to the ecosystem.
