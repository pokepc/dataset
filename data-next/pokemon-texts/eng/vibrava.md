## Vibration Pokémon

Vibrava’s wings are still growing, so it uses them less for long-distance flight and more for
producing powerful ultrasonic waves. By vigorously vibrating or rubbing its wings together, it can
make prey faint and cause severe headaches in people, so trainers nearby may need earplugs.

After prey is stunned, Vibrava may melt it with acid or bury it in the sand to preserve it. It also
feeds heavily on prey each day, dissolving it in digestive juices to help its wings develop.
