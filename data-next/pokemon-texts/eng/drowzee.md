## Hypnosis Pokémon

Drowzee puts its prey to sleep and eats their dreams, using its big nose to sniff out what people
are dreaming. It remembers every dream it consumes, and its favorite meals are fun, pleasant dreams,
especially those of children.

If a good dream is forgotten, Drowzee may have eaten it. It can sometimes show the dreams it has
eaten before, and it is said that an itchy nose while sleeping means one is hovering nearby above
your pillow. Bad dreams taste sour to it, and too many can make it sick.
