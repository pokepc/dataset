## Patient Pokémon

Wobbuffet lives quietly in darkness, often in caves and at night, to keep its pitch-black tail
hidden. It is never the first to attack and generally endures blows without striking back.

When threatened, especially by light, shock, or an attack on its tail, it inflates its body to
strengthen its counterstrike and may try to drag its foe down with Destiny Bond. If several
Wobbuffet meet, they become competitive and test each other’s endurance, sometimes even going
without food.
