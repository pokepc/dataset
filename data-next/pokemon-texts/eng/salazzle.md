## Toxic Lizard Pokémon

Only female Salazzle have been found, and they use pheromones in a gas they emit to captivate male
Salandit. Deep in caves, they gather groups of Salandit to serve them, and competition among
Salazzle is often judged by how many male followers each has.

Their poisonous gas can be diluted and used to make fragrant perfumes. A Salazzle that is not
brought enough food may punish its attendants with a fierce slap from its flame-spewing palm.
