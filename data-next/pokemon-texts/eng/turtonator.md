## Blast Turtle Pokémon

Turtonator lives in volcanoes, where it feeds on sulfur and other minerals. The materials it eats
help form the explosive shell on its back, which is coated in unstable substances and can detonate
violently if struck. Its weak point is the hole in its stomach.

It can gush fire and poisonous gases from its nostrils, and even its droppings are dangerously
explosive. Because moisture weakens its shell, it takes quiet refuge in caves on rainy days.
