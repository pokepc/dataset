## Mouse Pokémon

Raticate keeps its balance with its whiskers, and if they are cut off it is said to slow down. Its
sturdy, constantly growing fangs never stop working, so it gnaws on hard objects like rocks, logs,
and even walls to wear them down.

Its webbed hind feet help it swim across rivers, and in some places it is known to travel from
island to island. Despite its appearance, it can be surprisingly violent, and it may bite if
disturbed, especially around its whiskers.
