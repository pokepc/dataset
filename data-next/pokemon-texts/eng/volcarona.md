## Sun Pokémon

Volcarona is said to have appeared like an embodiment of the sun, bringing warmth to people and
Pokémon during bitter winters and even when volcanic ash dimmed the sky. Legends tell of it hatching
from a cocoon of raging flames, and ancient murals depict it as a fire deity.

As it flies, Volcarona scatters ember scales from its six wings, creating a sea of fire around its
battles. Some feared its burning scales as a bringer of disaster, while others believed it used them
to aid those suffering in the cold.
