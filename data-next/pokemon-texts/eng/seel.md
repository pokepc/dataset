## Sea Lion Pokémon

Seel lives amid icebergs and frigid seas, where the colder it gets, the better it feels. Its light
blue fur and thick, tough body help protect it from bitter cold, even around -40 degrees Fahrenheit,
and it relishes swimming in icy water nearly filled with floating ice.
