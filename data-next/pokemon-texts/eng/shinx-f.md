## Flash Pokémon

Shinx generates electricity by contracting and extending its muscles, and its fur shines when it is
in trouble or senses danger. When threatened, its whole coat can dazzle attackers, letting it flee
while they are momentarily blinded.

Its excited trembling can indicate that it is building up a great amount of electricity. It also
signals to others of its kind by shaking the tip of its tail while the tail tip shines brightly.
Though small, it has an aggressive nature and must be handled patiently.
