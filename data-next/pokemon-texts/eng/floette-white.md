## Single Bloom Pokémon

Floette flutters through flower fields, tending to blossoms that are beginning to wilt and giving
them its own power. It is deeply devoted to flowers and will not forgive anyone who damages a flower
bed.

When a well-kept bed comes into bloom, Floette appears to celebrate with an elegant dance. It also
draws out the hidden power of flowers to battle, and the more beautiful the blossom, the stronger
the power it can hold.
