## Long Body Pokémon

Furret has a very slim, long body and short legs, yet it is quick, nimble, and fleet. It can slip
through narrow spaces to escape danger, and its tail is so long that it is hard to tell where it
begins.

It makes nests and burrows that fit its body so well that other Pokémon cannot enter, and these
underground homes can become maze-like. Furret also uses its speed to corner foes and to hunt or
chase Rattata.

It will curl around its young to help them sleep.
