## Flower Pokémon

Vileplume has the world’s largest petals, and its heavy head can be hard to hold up. As it walks,
its broad flower petals shake loose clouds of toxic pollen, often turning the air yellow.

The pollen it scatters is highly allergenic and can cause severe attacks or even paralysis. In some
accounts, it uses those huge petals to lure prey, then douses them with poisonous spores before
catching and devouring them.
