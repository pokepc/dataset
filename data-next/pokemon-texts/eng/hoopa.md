## Mischief Pokémon

Hoopa is a mischievous troublemaker that uses its loop to warp space and send anything it wants far
away. It gathers things it likes and passes them through the ring to teleport them to a secret
place.

Its true form is said to hold tremendous power, with legends telling that its greed once led it to
carry off an entire castle in search of treasure. It can seize what it desires with six rings and
six huge arms, but when its power is sealed, it becomes much smaller.
