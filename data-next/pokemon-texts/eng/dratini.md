## Dragon Pokémon

Long thought to be a myth, Dratini was only recently confirmed after a fisherman caught one and a
small colony was found living underwater. It is called the Mirage Pokémon because so few have seen
it, though its shed skin has sometimes been found.

Dratini is born large and keeps growing by shedding its skin again and again as life energy builds
within its body. Its skin is soft after a molt, and the tiny bump on its forehead is actually an
undeveloped horn.

It is still weak, so it lives quietly on the sea
