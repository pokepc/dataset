## Blossom Pokémon

As a bud, Cherrim barely moves, sitting placidly until strong sunlight appears. Its folded petals
are tough enough to shrug off pecks, and they give off a scent that draws other Pokémon close.

When the sun shines strongly, Cherrim opens its petals and becomes cheerful and very active. In
bloom, it seems to bask in the light with its whole body, as if making up for the stillness it
endured before flowering.
