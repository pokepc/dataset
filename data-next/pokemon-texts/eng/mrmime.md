## Barrier Pokémon

Mr. Mime is a master of pantomime, always practicing its act and using its gestures to make others
believe something unseen is there. Its skill can be so convincing that imagined objects seem to
become real.

From its fingertips, it emits a strange force that hardens the air into invisible walls, which can
block attacks. If someone interrupts its performance or fails to show appreciation, it may answer
with a sharp flurry of slaps from its broad hands.
