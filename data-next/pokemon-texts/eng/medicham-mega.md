## Meditate Pokémon

Through meditation and harsh yoga training, Mega Medicham sharpens its sixth sense and hones its
spiritual power. It can sense what others are thinking, foresee an opponent’s next move, and even
see the aura of its foes.

Its movements are elegant and dance-like, letting it avoid attacks and strike back in the same
motion. It is also known to meditate for a month without eating, and it can hide its presence by
blending with fields and mountains.
