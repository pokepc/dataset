## Dragon Pokémon

Mega Salamence’s long-held wish to fly was realized when its body’s cells suddenly changed and
magnificent wings grew in. In its joy, it soars and wheels across the sky, often breathing flames as
it flies.

When it becomes angry, that happiness disappears and it turns into an uncontrollable menace. It
lashes out with shredding claws and fire, scorching fields and mountains and destroying everything
around it until it wears itself out.
