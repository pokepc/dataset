## Mouse Pokémon

Rattata is cautious and hardy, able to make its nest almost anywhere and live wherever food is
available. It constantly listens for danger even while asleep, using its ears to stay alert.

This small, quick Pokémon will chew on almost anything, and its sharp fangs grow throughout its
life, so it gnaws on hard objects to keep them worn down. It scavenges constantly, eating whatever
it can find.

Rattata also reproduces rapidly, so one sighting often means many more are nearby.
