## Numb Pokémon

Numel’s body holds magma at about 2,200 degrees Fahrenheit, with its humped back serving as the main
store of that heat. The blazing magma gives it great strength, but when it gets wet or the weather
turns rainy, the magma cools and hardens, making its body heavy and its movements slow.

It is hardy enough to carry heavy loads and has long served humans at work. Though it seems very
dull-witted and may not even notice being hit, it cannot endure hunger for long.
