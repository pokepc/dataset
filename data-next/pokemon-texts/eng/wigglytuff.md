## Balloon Pokémon

Wigglytuff has a soft, rubbery body that can draw in air and keep inflating itself seemingly without
limit. When angry, it may swell to an enormous size, and its bouncing, balloon-like body can be used
to intimidate opponents.

Its fine, dense fur is especially prized for how luxurious and pleasant it feels. People are drawn
to touch it, and sleeping beside Wigglytuff is said to be wonderfully comfortable. Its large
saucerlike eyes are kept clear by a thin layer of tears, which quickly wash away dust.
