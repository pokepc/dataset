## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally’s true power awakens through a deep bond of trust with its Trainer. Once its control mask
is broken and it is freed from the burden it once carried, its speed increases greatly and its
hidden strength emerges.

In this form, Silvally can change its type at will, a flexibility that is especially clear in
battle. The bond that released its power also strengthened its will, allowing it to become the
Pokémon it was meant to be.
