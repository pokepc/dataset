## Digging Pokémon

With its powerful ears, Diggersby can lift boulders weighing a ton or more and dig through dense
bedrock as if it were an excavator. Its strength makes it a great help at construction sites and
during tunnel work.

After digging, it tends to lounge around lazily. The fur on its belly holds heat especially well,
and people once used the shed fur to make heavy winter clothing.
