## Thorn Pokémon

Roselia’s beautiful flowers release a calming fragrance, and the stronger its aroma, the healthier
it is. Its scent can soothe living things and make foes careless, while plants raised on clean,
nutrient-rich springwater may bloom in rare or unusual colors.

Though lovely to look at, Roselia is dangerous to handle. Its arms bear toxic thorns, its head
thorns hold a vicious poison, and it can shoot sharp thorns at anyone who tries to steal its
flowers.

It uses different poisons in each hand when it attacks.
