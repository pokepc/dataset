## Black Tea Pokémon

Polteageist lives in antique teapots, and the antique form is especially notable for being tied to
rare, authentic vessels rather than the more common forgeries. It is said that a genuine pot is an
uncommon find among them.

If leftover black tea is left unattended, this Pokémon may appear and pour itself into it,
transforming the tea into a new Polteageist.
