## Cottonweed Pokémon

Skiploom’s flower opens and closes with changes in temperature, blossoming when the weather warms
above 64 degrees Fahrenheit. Because the amount it opens reflects the heat, it is sometimes used as
a thermometer.

It spreads its petals to soak up sunlight and floats in the air to get closer to the sun. When rain
begins, it shuts its flower and hides in the shade of a tree to avoid getting wet.
