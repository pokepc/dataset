## Sea Basin Pokémon

Kyogre is said to be the personification of the sea itself, a mythical Pokémon that widened the
oceans by bringing heavy rains and towering tidal waves. Legends tell of many clashes with Groudon
as each sought the power of nature, after which Kyogre fell asleep in the depths of the sea or a
marine trench.
