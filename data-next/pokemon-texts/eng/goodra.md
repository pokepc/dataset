## Dragon Pokémon

Goodra is an amazingly friendly Dragon-type Pokémon that becomes deeply attached to those close to
it. It will happily hug its beloved Trainer with its sticky, slime-covered body, though that
affection can leave them coated in goo. If left alone, it grows lonely and may even shed gooey
tears.
