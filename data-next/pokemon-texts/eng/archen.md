## First Bird Pokémon

Archen is an ancient bird Pokémon restored from a fossil. It lived in treetops, where it ate berries
and bug Pokémon, and moved about by hopping from branch to branch. Though it has wings, it cannot
truly fly, and instead is known for its strong jumping ability.

It was long thought to be the ancestor of all bird Pokémon, though later research cast some doubt on
that claim. Even so, it can glide down from tall treetops to catch prey.
