## Symbol Pokémon

Unown U is a flat, thin Pokémon that is usually found stuck to walls, where its shape seems to carry
some kind of meaning. Its form resembles ancient writing or hieroglyphs, leaving it a mystery
whether human language or Unown came first.

Unown are thought to communicate telepathically, and when several are together, an unusual power is
said to emerge. By itself, however, it does nothing.
