## Royal Sword Pokémon

Aegislash is said to have attended generations of kings, using its spectral power to influence both
people and Pokémon. It can also sense natural leadership, and legend says the one it chooses is
destined to become king.

In its defensive stance, Aegislash relies on its steel body and a force field of spectral power to
lessen damage. Its powers are so strong that it once forced people and Pokémon to build a kingdom to
its liking.
