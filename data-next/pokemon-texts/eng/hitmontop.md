## Handstand Pokémon

Hitmontop fights while spinning like a top, using smooth, dance-like kicks that can be both graceful
and hard to approach. By doing a handstand and then whirling at high speed, it throws off an
opponent’s timing and can travel faster than it does by walking.

Its spinning kicks combine offense and defense in one motion, and the centrifugal force makes its
attacks more powerful. If it spins especially fast, it may even bore its way into the ground.
