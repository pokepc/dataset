## Jellyfish Pokémon

Tentacruel keeps its 80 tentacles short when idle, but on the hunt it can lengthen them at will to
form an inescapable poisonous net. It wraps prey, stings with venomous tips, and weakens its catch
with a harsh toxin, even trapping many victims at once.
