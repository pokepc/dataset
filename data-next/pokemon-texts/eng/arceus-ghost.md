## Alpha Pokémon

In Sinnoh and Hisui legends, Arceus is said to have emerged from an egg in a place where nothing
existed, then shaped the world and all that is within it. Myth also says it was born before the
universe itself and wielded 1,000 arms to shape the cosmos.

As the heavenly source of light over Hisui, it is believed to guide and protect all Pokémon. This
Ghost Type form changes along with its type to match a held Plate through Multitype.
