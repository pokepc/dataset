## Season Pokémon

Deerling’s fur changes color and scent with the seasons, and even temperature and humidity can
subtly influence its appearance. In its Winter Form, these changes help it blend in with its
surroundings, and people sometimes use Deerling to tell the seasons apart.

It also has a habit of hiding in grass when it senses hostility. Like all of its forms, this change
in appearance is seasonal only; Deerling switches forms to match the current season.
