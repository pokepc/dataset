## Spirit Pokémon

Yamask is said to be the spirit of a person from a bygone age, still carrying the mask that was once
its human face. It wanders through ruins at night, searching for someone who knows its face, and it
keeps memories of its former life.

Sometimes it looks at the mask and cries, as if recalling the person it used to be. Its lonely
rambling through old grave sites and ruins gives it a haunting, wistful air.
