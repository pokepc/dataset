## Martial Arts Pokémon

Mienshao uses the long fur on its arms like whips, striking with such speed that its attacks are
hard to see. It can unleash relentless combo attacks that overwhelm opponents before it builds up
power for a finishing blow.

When it faces a truly tough opponent, Mienshao may bite off some of the fur on its arms to lighten
itself. Its bizarre wail warns of a flurry of blinding kicks and chops, and those kicks can shatter
massive boulders.
