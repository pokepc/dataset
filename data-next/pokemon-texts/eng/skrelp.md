## Mock Kelp Pokémon

Skrelp resembles rotten seaweed or kelp, letting it blend in with drifting or rooted seaweed and
avoid detection. It hides motionless among the plants, and because it is not a strong swimmer, it
often clings to seaweed for safety.

When prey or other Pokémon come close, Skrelp can spray liquid poison before finishing them off. It
also uses its disguise to stay hidden while it stores up power for evolution, and rotten seaweed is
its main food source.
