## Mime Pokémon

Mime Jr. mimics the movements and expressions of those it sees, especially people and foes, in an
effort to understand them. It likes busy places such as marketplaces and often copies others much
like a child, watching their reactions with bright eyes.

It is not very skilled at imitation, but it tries its best to confuse opponents before slipping
away. When it catches a glimpse of a graceful Mr. Rime, it carefully studies and copies its dance
steps as practice.
