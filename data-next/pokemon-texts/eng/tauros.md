## Wild Bull Pokémon

Tauros is a rowdy, high-stamina Pokémon that keeps rampaging until it crashes into something. Once
it fixes on a target, it rushes forward in a headlong charge, whipping its body or tails to build up
even more force before tackling at full speed.
