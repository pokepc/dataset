## Armor Bird Pokémon

Skarmory’s body is wrapped in hard steel armor, yet its hollow, light build lets it soar at great
speed. Its wings have swordlike edges, and the feathers it sheds are so sharp that people once
fashioned swords and knives from them.

Though sturdy, its metal body rusts easily, so it tends to stay in its nest on rainy days. Its wings
are battered by repeated battles, but they regrow fully once a year, restoring their keen cutting
edges.
