## Poodle Pokémon

With its fur trimmed into an elegant style, this Furfrou moves more swiftly and is admired for its
refined appearance. In Kalos, it was once entrusted as a guardian of the king, and nobles even
competed to create the most exquisite trim.

Its fur keeps growing unless someone it trusts trims it, and this form can be changed at any time in
Lumiose City. If left alone, it will return to its natural form after five days or when placed in
the PC.
