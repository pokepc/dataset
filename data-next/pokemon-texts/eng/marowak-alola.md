## Bone Keeper Pokémon

Marowak overcame its sorrow to evolve a sturdier body, and its spirit is said to be tempered and
hard to break. Once weak and timid, it became far more fierce after learning to wield bones as
weapons.

The bone it carries is its key weapon, and it can throw it with great skill like a boomerang.
Marowak also uses bone-thumping rhythms to communicate, and it has been seen pounding rocks to tap
out messages to others.
