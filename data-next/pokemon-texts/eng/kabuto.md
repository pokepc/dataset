## Shellfish Pokémon

Kabuto is a Pokémon regenerated from fossils, often recovered from ancient remains found on the
ocean floor. Though believed to be almost entirely extinct, living examples have been reported in a
few places, and it is said to have changed little in 300 million years.

It is thought to have lived on beaches and hidden on the sea floor, using the eyes on its back while
staying concealed. Kabuto protects itself with a hard, stiff shell, and its shell becomes harder as
it molts every three days.
