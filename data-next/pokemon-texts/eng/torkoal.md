## Coal Pokémon

Torkoal digs through mountains and abandoned coal mines in search of coal, then packs it into the
hollow spaces of its shell and burns it for energy. It grows weaker as the fire inside fades, and if
it runs out of coal, it can no longer move.

When threatened, Torkoal blows out thick black smoke or soot to drive foes away. Its smoke can
reveal its mood, and the force of the smoke is said to reflect its health. When preparing for
battle, it burns extra coal to strengthen itself.
