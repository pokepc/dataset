## Frost Tree Pokémon

Mega Abomasnow lives quietly in mountains buried in snow, where it hides itself by whipping up
blizzards and blanketing wide areas in white. It is known as the Ice Monster and as a Pokémon that
brings blizzards.

A shake of its massive body can create whiteout conditions, and its great strength can split huge
boulders with ease. Though it prefers solitude deep in the mountains, it will drive off packs of
Darumaka that threaten Snover.
