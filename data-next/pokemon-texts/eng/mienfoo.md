## Martial Arts Pokémon

Mienfoo is a fierce fighter that overwhelms opponents with flowing, continuous attacks. Though
small, it prides itself on speed, trading raw power for a greater number of precise moves, and its
sharp claws and graceful strikes can cut down careless foes.

It often lives and trains in packs in the mountains, where each group develops its own style of
kicks and chops. By concentrating, Mienfoo sharpens its elegant combinations, and a well-trained one
can chop with its arms more than 100 times in a minute.
