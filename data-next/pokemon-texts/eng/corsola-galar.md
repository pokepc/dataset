## Coral Pokémon

Corsola lives in warm southern seas, where its coral branches catch the light in brilliant colors
and are treasured for their beauty. The branches grow and shed continuously, and if one breaks off,
it can quickly grow back again.

It prefers clean, unpolluted water and its coral can discolor or crumble in dirty seas. Groups of
Corsola may gather in warm waters and even serve as hiding places for smaller Pokémon, while people
in some southern regions have built communities around them.
