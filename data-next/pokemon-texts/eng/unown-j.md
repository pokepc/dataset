## Symbol Pokémon

Unown are flat, thin Pokémon that are always found stuck to walls, and their shapes seem to carry
some meaning. Their many forms resemble ancient writing or hieroglyphs, leaving it a mystery whether
human language or Unown came first.

They are said to communicate telepathically, and when several are together, an unusual power may
emerge. Their different forms are thought to hint at varied abilities, though their form only
changes appearance and cannot be altered.
