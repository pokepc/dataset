## Spiral Pokémon

Omastar is a prehistoric Pokémon that is thought to have died out when its spiral shell grew too
large and heavy. Weighed down by it, Omastar could move only slowly and reach nearby food, which
likely made it unable to catch prey.

It uses its well-developed tentacles to seize prey and can wrap them tightly to immobilize them.
Once it has a target, it bites hard with sharp fangs and beak-like mouth parts, and its fangs were
even said to be strong enough to crush rock.
