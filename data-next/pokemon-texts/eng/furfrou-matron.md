## Poodle Pokémon

Trimming Furfrou’s fluffy fur makes it look more elegant and also helps it move more swiftly. In
Kalos, these Pokémon were once chosen as guardians of the king, and aristocrats even vied to give
them the most exquisite styles.

Its fur keeps growing if left alone, but it will only let someone it trusts trim it. Its trim is
only a change in appearance and can be altered again at any time; if not maintained, it returns to
its Natural Form after five days or when placed in the PC.
