## Volcano Pokémon

Typhlosion cloaks itself in a shimmering heat haze made by its intensely hot flames, making it hard
to see. It attacks with blasts of fire that can reduce almost anything to cinders.

When its rage peaks, its body becomes so hot that anything that touches it may ignite at once. It is
also said to rub its blazing fur together to trigger devastating explosions.
