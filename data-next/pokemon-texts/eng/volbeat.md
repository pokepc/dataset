## Firefly Pokémon

Volbeat lives around clean ponds, and when night falls its rear begins to glow. It communicates with
others by flashing and adjusting the brightness of its light, and its tail can shine like a
lightbulb.

In groups, Volbeat can fly through the night sky and trace geometric patterns with their lights. It
is also drawn to the sweet aroma given off by Illumise.
