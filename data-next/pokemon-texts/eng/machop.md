## Superpower Pokémon

Machop is small in stature, but its body is made almost entirely of muscle, giving it strength far
beyond its size. It trains constantly and is never satisfied, even after a hard day of exercise.

It builds power by lifting boulders, carrying rocks, or even hefting Graveler like weights. A
dedicated martial artist, Machop studies many styles and may travel in search of stronger training,
growing tougher and more confident all the while.
