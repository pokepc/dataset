## Big Horn Pokémon

Stantler’s curved, strangely shaped antlers can distort the flow of air, creating a bizarre space
that unsettles anyone who looks at them. Those who stare too long may feel their senses slip away,
as if they are being pulled toward the antlers’ centers.

Its magnificent antlers were once prized as works of art, which led to Stantler being hunted close
to extinction. The black orbs that fall from its antlers can be ground into a powder used as an
effective remedy for insomnia.
