## Fox Pokémon

Fennekin fills itself with energy by chewing on a twig as it walks. Its roomy ears release air
hotter than 390 degrees Fahrenheit, and it uses blasts of hot air to intimidate opponents.

The twig it carries serves as more than a snack, giving it strength as it moves along. When
threatened, it can puff heat from its ears to startle and warn off foes.
