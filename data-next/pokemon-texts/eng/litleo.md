## Lion Cub Pokémon

Litleo is a hot-blooded, curious Pokémon that is quick to fight when angered. Its short mane grows
hot during battles, and the stronger its opponent is, the more heat surges from its mane and the
more power flows through its body.

Young Litleo live with a pride, but once they can hunt on their own, they are driven out to make
their way alone. They leave to become stronger by living independently and facing tough opponents.
