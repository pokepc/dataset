## Ball Pokémon

Voltorb is usually found in power plants, where its uncanny resemblance to a Poké Ball has led many
to mistake it for one. It can discharge the electricity stored in its body, and people who touch it
risk being shocked or even exploded.

It rolls to move, but even a small jolt or uneven ground can trigger a self-destruction. Extremely
sensitive and mysterious, Voltorb is said to have some connection to Poké Balls, and its origin
remains unexplained.
