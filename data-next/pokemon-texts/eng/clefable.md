## Fairy Pokémon

Clefable is a timid, wary Fairy Pokémon that is rarely seen and quickly hides when it senses people.
It is said to protect its own world and often lives in quiet, remote mountains or other secluded
places, sometimes in packs.

Its hearing is extremely sharp, sensitive enough to catch very faint sounds from far away, so it
avoids noisy areas. On calm, moonlit nights, it may wander to deserted lakes, skipping lightly as if
flying and even walking across the water.
