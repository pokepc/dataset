## Meteorite Pokémon

Solrock draws its power from sunlight and solar energy, making it especially strong in the daytime.
It absorbs that light during the day, and on sunny days groups of them have been seen lining up to
face the sun.

Always expressionless, Solrock moves silently and floats in the air. By rotating and spinning, it
gives off intense heat and a brilliant sunlike light that can blind foes, while also sensing their
emotions or thoughts.
