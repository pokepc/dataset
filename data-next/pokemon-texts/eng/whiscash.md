## Whiskers Pokémon

Whiscash is extremely territorial, claiming a large pond or swamp as its own and driving off
intruders with violent tremors. When threatened, it thrashes about so fiercely that it can trigger
massive quakes, and sightings of it leaping from the water are often taken as a sign of an
earthquake.

It lives at the bottom of swamps, where it lies in wait for prey and eats anything alive. Because
its vision is poor, it senses movement through vibrations in the water with its whiskers, then
swallows prey whole.
