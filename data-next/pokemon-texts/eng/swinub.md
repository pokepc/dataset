## Pig Pokémon

Swinub searches for food by rubbing and rooting its snout into the ground, and its nose is tough
enough to dig through frozen earth and buried snow. With a very sensitive sense of smell, it can
track down mushrooms, berries, or anything else that seems enticing, and it may dash headlong toward
a scent it likes.
