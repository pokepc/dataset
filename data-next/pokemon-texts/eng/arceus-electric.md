## Alpha Pokémon

In mythology, Arceus is said to have come forth from an egg when nothing yet existed, then shaped
the universe and all that is in it. Legends also tell that it was born before the universe itself,
with a thousand arms behind its creation of the world.

As the heavenly source of light in Hisui, its radiance is said to guide and protect all Pokémon.
This Electric Type form changes with a held Plate to match its type through Multitype.
