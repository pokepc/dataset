## Toxic Mouth Pokémon

Toxicroak’s knuckle claws secrete a vicious toxin, and even a scratch can be enough to fell an
opponent. Its crimson claws contain poison so potent that it can be turned into a tonic only by
diluting it with wild grasses and boiling it for days.

Before battle, it croaks to churn the poison stored in a throat sac, making it even more potent. It
then sways, dodges, and bounces in close before lunging with poisonous claws, and it lets out a
victory croak when its prey goes down.
