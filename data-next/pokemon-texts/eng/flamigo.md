## Synchronize Pokémon

Flamigo is said to tie the base of its neck into a knot so the energy stored in its belly does not
leak out through its beak. This unusual trait helps explain its tightly controlled, disciplined
presence.

It is also known for a behavior called synchronizing, which lets whole flocks move and attack at the
exact same moment. When they act together, their coordination is remarkably precise and perfectly in
step.
