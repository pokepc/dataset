## Mole Pokémon

Dugtrio is a set of triplets that emerged from one body, so all three think alike and work together
as they burrow endlessly. Their teamwork lets them drive through even hard ground, sometimes
reaching depths of 60 miles or more and causing the earth to shake as they go.

Its three heads move in turn to loosen soil and push it forward, and it can strike from an
unexpected direction in battle. The heads are sleek, smooth, and extremely hard, letting Dugtrio dig
with powerful headbutts.
