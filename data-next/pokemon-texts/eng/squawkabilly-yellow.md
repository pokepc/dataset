## Parrot Pokémon

Squawkabilly with yellow plumage is a city-dwelling Pokémon that gathers in flocks divided by
feather color. These groups are highly territorial, and they often clash with one another over
control of their space.

When the flocks head out to search for food in the morning and evening, the commotion becomes
especially loud. Among the color-based groups, the green-feathered flocks are said to hold the most
influence.
