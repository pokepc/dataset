## Fox Pokémon

At birth, Vulpix has a single white tail, but as it grows the tail splits into six and may sprout
still more. Its fur and tails are prized for their beauty, becoming more lustrous and elegantly
curled, and the Pokémon feels slightly warm when held.

A fire burns steadily inside its body, and it can spit that flame as fireballs or guide them freely
through the air. In hot daytime weather, it releases flames from its mouth to keep from overheating.
If threatened by a stronger foe, it may feign injury to slip.
