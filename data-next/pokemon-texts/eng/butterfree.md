## Butterfly Pokémon

Butterfree flits from flower to flower, drawn to nectar and honey, and can locate even faint flower
patches or blossoms miles from its nest. It gathers honey every day, carrying it back on the hairs
of its legs, and its wings are dusted with a powder that helps repel water so it can fly even in
rain.

When threatened, it beats its wings rapidly to scatter highly toxic dust and scales into the air.
Its large eyes are made up of many tiny eyes, giving it keen sight as it searches for flowers and
keeps watch.
