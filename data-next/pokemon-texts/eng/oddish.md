## Weed Pokémon

Oddish spends the day buried in soil or tucked underground, often leaving only its leaves exposed.
It seeks fertile ground, absorbs nutrients through its whole body, and may even take on a weedlike
appearance; if disturbed, it lets out a harsh shriek.

At night, moonlight wakes it, and it roams about on its rootlike feet to scatter its seeds. It is
said to grow by bathing in moonlight, and richer soil makes its leaves glossier.
