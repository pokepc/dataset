## Beaver Pokémon

Bibarel is an industrious worker that builds its nest by damming streams and rivers with bark, mud,
branches, and roots, cutting the materials with its sharp incisors. The dams it creates help keep
nearby rivers from overflowing, which people often appreciate.

Its fur repels water and also helps retain heat. Though it moves sluggishly on land, Bibarel can
swim with great speed.
