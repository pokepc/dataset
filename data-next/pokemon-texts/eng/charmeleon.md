## Flame Pokémon

Charmeleon is hotheaded and constantly seeks out opponents, calming down only after it wins. In
battle, it swings its burning tail to raise the surrounding temperature and whips it around to knock
foes down before tearing at them with sharp claws.

When it faces a strong enemy or gets excited in a tough fight, it becomes agitated and its tail
flame flares bluish-white. It may also breathe or spout intense flames, scorching even the area
around it. In rocky mountains, its tail can shine at night like a star.
