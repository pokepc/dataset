## Gratitude Pokémon

Shaymin lives in flower patches and hides by curling up to look like a blossoming plant. It can
dissolve toxins in the air and quickly turn ruined land into a field of flowers, appearing when
winter ends and people give thanks.

If it is lovingly hugged and senses gratitude, the flowers on its body bloom. The blooming of
Gracidea flowers grants it the power of flight, and it is said to deliver the message of gratitude.
