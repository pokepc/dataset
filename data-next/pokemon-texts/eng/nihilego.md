## Parasite Pokémon

Nihilego is one of the mysterious Ultra Beasts that came into this world through an Ultra Wormhole.
It is thought to produce a powerful neurotoxin, and it appears to live as a parasite by feeding on
people and Pokémon.

Its nature is unclear, and it may not even be fully sentient. At times, it has been seen acting in a
way that resembles a young girl, while those it infests are reported to become suddenly violent.
