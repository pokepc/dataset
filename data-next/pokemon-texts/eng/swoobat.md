## Courting Pokémon

Swoobat emits sound waves of many frequencies from its nose, and some are powerful enough to shatter
rocks or reduce concrete to rubble. It shakes its tail vigorously when releasing these ultrasonic
waves, but doing so leaves it tired and unable to fly for a while.

A courting male’s ultrasonic waves can lift the mood of anyone who hears them. Its nose shape is
also viewed in some regions as an omen of good luck.
