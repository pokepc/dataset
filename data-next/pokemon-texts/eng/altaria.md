## Humming Pokémon

Altaria glides through the sky with a calm, graceful motion, its cotton-like wings and fluffy body
letting it ride warm updrafts and blend into white clouds. On sunny days, it seems almost like part
of the sky itself.

Its crystal-clear soprano humming and melodies can fill listeners with a dreamy wonder. When it
bonds with a person, it may gently wrap them in its soft wings and hum, though it can answer
provocation with shrill cries before striking without mercy.
