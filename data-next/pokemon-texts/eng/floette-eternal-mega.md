## Single Bloom Pokémon

Mega Floette flutters through fields of flowers, tending to blossoms that are beginning to wilt and
giving them its own power with heartfelt care. It is especially drawn to well-tended flower beds,
where it appears when the flowers bloom and celebrates with an elegant dance.

It raises flowers and uses them as weapons, drawing out their hidden power in battle. The more
beautiful the blossom, the greater the power it holds, and it does not forgive anyone who tramples a
flower bed.
