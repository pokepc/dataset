## Big Voice Pokémon

Loudred uses its round ears as speakers to unleash an overwhelming voice. By inhaling deeply,
stamping its feet, and powering its cries with strong stomach muscles, it can send out shock waves
and air pressure strong enough to blow away opponents and even damage or topple houses and trucks.

It often bellows while stomping to build up power, and its voice is used to punish foes. After
shouting, Loudred may be unable to hear for a while, which is considered one of its weak points.
