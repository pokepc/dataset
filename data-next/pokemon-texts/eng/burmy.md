## Bagworm Pokémon

Burmy covers itself with a cloak of twigs, leaves, or other nearby materials to shelter from cold
winds. Its cloaked body is scrawny and vulnerable, and if the covering is even slightly damaged in
battle, it quickly repairs it with whatever is at hand.

Even when born where there are no cocooning materials, Burmy somehow ends up with a cloak. Its cloak
is thinner in hot weather, and it can change to suit the terrain after battle, taking on plant,
sandy, or trash-like coverings.
