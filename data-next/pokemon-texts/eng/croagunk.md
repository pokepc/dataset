## Toxic Mouth Pokémon

Croagunk keeps poison sacs in its cheeks and uses them to make eerie, blubbering cries that can
unsettle foes. It relies on surprise, catching opponents off guard before jabbing them with its
toxic fingers or striking when they flinch.

It is known for fighting unfairly only as a matter of survival, and its peculiar call and comic
appearance have made it a popular mascot. Though poisonous, fluid from its fingers can be diluted
and used in remedies for lower-back pain.
