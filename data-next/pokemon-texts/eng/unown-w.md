## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is usually found stuck to walls, and its shape seems to carry
some kind of meaning. Each form resembles ancient writing or hieroglyphs, leaving it a mystery
whether the symbols inspired Unown or the other way around.

Unown are thought to communicate telepathically, and when several are together, an odd power is said
to emerge. Their many forms change only their appearance, not their nature, and the W form is one of
these strange letterlike shapes.
