## Sludge Pokémon

Born from polluted sludge, Alolan Grimer gathers in filthy places and feeds on wastewater and other
industrial waste. Its sludgy, rubbery body can squeeze through even tiny openings, and it often
crawls into sewer pipes to drink foul runoff.

It constantly leaks germ-infested fluid, leaving an overpowering stench wherever it moves. Bits of
its body can break off and become new Grimer, spreading the filth and germs that can keep plants
from growing.
