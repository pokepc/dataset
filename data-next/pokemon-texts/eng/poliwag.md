## Tadpole Pokémon

Poliwag has very thin, slick, flexible skin, so its spiral innards can be seen through its belly,
and even sharp fangs bounce off it. The direction of that swirl varies by area, and it may look
clearer after eating; if it turns whitish, it can signal illness.
