## Iron Leg Pokémon

Metagross is the fusion of two Metang, and its four linked brains work together so efficiently that
it can perform calculations faster than a supercomputer. It uses this sharp intelligence to analyze
opponents with great accuracy and back them into a corner.

It can float by tucking in its four legs, and it also has great physical strength. When hunting, it
pins prey with its claws and heavy body before chewing it with the mouth on its stomach.
