## King Pokémon

Calyrex (Ice Rider) is remembered as a merciful ruler from ancient times in Galar, where it was said
to reign in legend as a king. Its presence was associated with healing and blessings, inspiring
reverence across the region.

It is also said to mend hearts and bring plants to life with renewed growth. These tales portray
Calyrex as a gentle Pokémon whose power was tied not only to rule, but to restoration and abundance.
