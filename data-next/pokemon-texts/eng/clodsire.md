## Spiny Fish Pokémon

Clodsire lives at the bottom of ponds and swamps, where it spends its time in muddy waters. It is
known to carry Wooper on its back and ferry them safely across from one shore to another.

When threatened or attacked, it retaliates by thrusting thick spines from its body. This is a
dangerous defense, but Clodsire uses it without hesitation, putting everything on the line.
