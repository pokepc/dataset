## Skunk Pokémon

Stunky protects itself by spraying a foul, noxious fluid from its rear. The stench is powerful
enough to drive away Pokémon, make people feel queasy, and even linger for 24 hours after the spray.

If it lifts its tail and points its rear toward a target, it is about to attack. It can aim the
spray at an opponent’s face from over 16 feet away, and its odor is so revolting that one whiff may
even cause fainting or memory loss.
