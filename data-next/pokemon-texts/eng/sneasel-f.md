## Sharp Claw Pokémon

Sneasel is a cunning yet savage Pokémon that blends into the dark of night to ambush prey. It hides
sharp claws in its paws, then suddenly extends them to startle foes or strike vulnerable spots.

It scales trees and even steep cliffs with its hooked claws, often slipping into nests after the
parents leave. There it steals eggs, sometimes with another Sneasel helping to lure adults away, and
it may drive off bird Pokémon to feed on what remains.
