## Restraint Pokémon

Espurr’s expression never changes, but behind that blank stare it is struggling to contain
devastating psychic power. Its intense psychic energy is emitted from an organ sheltered by its
ears, which helps keep the power from leaking out.

Although it has enough power to send a wrestler flying or blast everything within 300 feet, Espurr
cannot control it and finds that strength troublesome.
