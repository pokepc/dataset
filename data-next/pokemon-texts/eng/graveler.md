## Rock Pokémon

Graveler is a slow-footed Pokémon that moves by curling up and rolling instead of walking. It is
often seen tumbling down mountain trails and steep slopes, barreling over obstacles without slowing,
and once it builds momentum, it can be hard to stop.

It lives in holes dug into sheer cliff faces and climbs mountain paths toward the summit before
rolling back down. Graveler freely endures chips and broken pieces as it goes, showing little
concern for anything in its path.
