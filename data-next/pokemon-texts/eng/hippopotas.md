## Hippo Pokémon

Hippopotas lives in arid places and is usually hard to spot because it burrows into the ground or
hides beneath the sand. It seals its nostrils and moves through dunes as if walking, often gathering
in small colonies.

It protects itself by covering its body with sand and dislikes getting wet. Instead of sweating, it
expels grainy sand, and when agitated it can blow sand from its nostrils. Active by day, it spends
cold desert nights tucked safely underground.
