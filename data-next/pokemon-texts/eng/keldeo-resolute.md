## Colt Pokémon

By blasting water from its hooves, Keldeo can glide over the surface of oceans and rivers, and it is
often seen at scenic waterfronts. It excels at fighting with its legs, dashing across the world to
seek more chances to train.

When Keldeo is resolute, power fills its body and its movements become swifter, with jumps so fast
they are hard to follow. Some say its true power awakens only after harsh battles and the horn on
its forehead is fully developed.
