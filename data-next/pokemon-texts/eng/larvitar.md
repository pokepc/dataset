## Rock Skin Pokémon

Born deep underground, Larvitar eats its way through the soil above to reach the surface, where it
can finally see its parents. It feeds on dirt and soil, and does not emerge until it has consumed
the ground around it.

After eating roughly a mountain’s worth of earth, Larvitar becomes a pupa and then falls asleep so
it can grow. The bright light at the surface can be hard for it to handle.
