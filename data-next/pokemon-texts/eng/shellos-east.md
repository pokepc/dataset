## Sea Slug Pokémon

Shellos lives along seashores and other bodies of water, where its shape and coloration develop to
fit its habitat. In the warm waters around eastern Sinnoh, it is often found in abundance, and it
can even crawl toward people with surprising friendliness.

Its squishy body can secrete a mysterious purple fluid when it is startled or pressed hard. The
liquid is harmless but very sticky, and it can be troublesome to wash off. Shellos can function on
land for a time, though its skin must not dry out.
