## Pumpkin Pokémon

A spirit trapped inside its pumpkin body gives this Pokémon an eerie restlessness, especially after
sunset. It is said to roam at night, guiding wandering spirits toward where they belong so they can
pass on.

Light shining from the holes in its pumpkin can hypnotize and even control those who see it. Its
size is fixed when it is encountered, and the Small Size form cannot be changed.
