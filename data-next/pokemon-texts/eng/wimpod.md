## Turn Tail Pokémon

Wimpod is an astonishingly cowardly Pokémon that lives on beaches and even seabeds. It gathers in
swarms and stays constantly alert for danger, scattering at the first sign of an enemy. When it
flees, its many legs whirl so quickly that it leaves a sparkling clean trail behind.
