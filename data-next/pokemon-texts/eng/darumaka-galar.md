## Zen Charm Pokémon

Darumaka keeps a fire burning inside its body, and when that flame is strong it becomes restless and
runs about. If the fire dwindles, it quickly falls asleep, folding its limbs into its body as it
rests.

Even in sleep, it stays balanced and never topples over, no matter how it is pushed or pulled. Its
droppings are hot, and people once used them in their clothes to stay warm, making it a symbol of
good fortune.
