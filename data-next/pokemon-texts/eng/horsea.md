## Dragon Pokémon

Horsea lives in oceans with gentle currents, often nesting in the shade of corals. It eats small
insects and moss from rocks, and if the current grows strong, it anchors itself by wrapping its tail
around rocks or coral.

When danger approaches, it sprays water or thick black ink from its mouth and flees. It moves with
quick, dance-like motions, using its fins to swim in any direction while still facing forward, and
it can even shoot down flying bugs with precise blasts of ink from the water’s surface.
