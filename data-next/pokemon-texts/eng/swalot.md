## Poison Bag Pokémon

Swalot has no teeth, so it swallows anything whole, its cavernous mouth opening wide enough to fit
an automobile tire. Once prey is spotted, it sprays a hideously toxic fluid from its pores or
follicles to weaken the target before gulping it down.

Its powerful stomach acid and special enzymes can dissolve almost anything it eats. The only thing
it cannot digest is its own stomach.
