## Seed Pokémon

Sunkern is so weak that it tries to move as little as possible, conserving the nutrients it has
stored for evolution. It does not eat; instead, it survives on morning dew and dewdrops collected
from under plant leaves.

It may suddenly fall or plummet from the sky in the morning. When threatened, even by a Spearow, its
only defense is to shake its leaves desperately. After a cold summer, its population is said to
increase dramatically.
