## Automaton Pokémon

Mega Golurk flies through the sky at astonishing speed, its internal energy surging when the seal on
its chest is disturbed. Ancient people are said to have created it to protect people and Pokémon,
and some accounts describe it faithfully carrying out its master’s orders or serving as a laborer.

Its mysterious power is sometimes thought to come from a limitless engine inside its body, though
that has never been proven. When its seal is removed, it can lose control and wreak widespread
destruction.
