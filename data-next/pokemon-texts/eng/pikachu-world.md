## Mouse Pokémon

Pikachu stores electricity in its cheek pouches and can release it in sudden crackling shocks,
sometimes with enough power to match a lightning bolt. If it is startled or meets something new, it
may strike at once, and it can also pass electricity to another weakened Pikachu.
