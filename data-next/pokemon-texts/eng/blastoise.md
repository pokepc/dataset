## Shellfish Pokémon

Blastoise is a brutal Pokémon with pressurized water jets on its shell, and it uses them for
high-speed tackles. It deliberately makes itself heavy so it can withstand the recoil, and it can
also crush foes with its weight before withdrawing into its shell in a pinch.

Its shell spouts are highly accurate, letting it aim water shots with great precision from far away.
Once it locks on to a target, it can blast water with even more force than a fire hose, strong
enough to punch holes through thick steel.
