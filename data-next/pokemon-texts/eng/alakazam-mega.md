## Psi Pokémon

Mega Alakazam is famed for a brain and memory that seem to surpass a supercomputer, with an IQ said
to reach 5,000. Its brain continues to grow throughout its life, and its vast psychic power is what
keeps its heavy head lifted while it moves its weak body.

It can use many psychic abilities to their fullest, especially by closing its eyes to sharpen its
other senses. The spoons it carries are said to have been created by its own psychic power, and it
may even share one with someone it trusts deeply.
