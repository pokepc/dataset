## Willpower Pokémon

Known as the Being of Willpower, Azelf is said to sleep at the bottom of a lake to help keep the
world in balance. Legends say it emerged from the same egg as Uxie and Mesprit, linking it to the
other beings of the lake trio.

When Azelf flew, people gained the determination to do things, and it was said to have given humans
the strength to face life’s hardships. Some stories also describe it as a feared being that can
manipulate an opponent’s will.
