## Water Bird Pokémon

Pelipper is a messenger of the skies, using its huge bill to carry small Pokémon and eggs to safety.
Its beak is so spacious that it can even hold food for others, and young males are said to gather
and bring back supplies to the nest.

It nests on steep sea cliffs and skims low over the waves to find food. By dipping its bill into the
water, it scoops up prey and water together, then swallows its catch in one big gulp. On calm days,
it may bob on the waves to rest its wings.
