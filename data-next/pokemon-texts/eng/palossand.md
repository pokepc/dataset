## Sand Castle Pokémon

Palossand is known as the Beach Nightmare, a castle-like Pokémon that lives on beaches but cannot
keep its shape if drenched by heavy rain. It can control the sand itself, using a sandstorm to stop
opponents before pulling prey down and draining away their vitality or even their souls.

Each grain of sand seems to have a will of its own, and the mound has become a grim fortress. Its
power to curse grew stronger as it evolved, and its buried depths are said to hold the dried remains
of those it has weakened.
