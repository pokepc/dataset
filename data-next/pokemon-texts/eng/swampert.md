## Mud Fish Pokémon

Swampert is immensely strong, able to drag boulders weighing more than a ton and even tow a large
ship through the water. Its thick arms are as hard as rock, and a single swing can smash foes or
break boulders apart.

It has keen vision that works even in murky water, and it senses approaching storms by reading
subtle changes in wave sounds and tidal winds with its fins. When rough weather threatens, it piles
up boulders to shield its seaside nest.
