## Symbol Pokémon

Unown is a flat, thin Pokémon that is usually found stuck to walls, and its shape appears to carry
some kind of meaning. Its many forms resemble ancient writing, hieroglyphs, or text characters,
leaving researchers uncertain whether human writing or Unown came first.

Some believe the different forms may reflect different abilities, and Unown seem to communicate
telepathically with one another. When one is alone, nothing happens, but if two or more gather, an
odd power is said to emerge.
