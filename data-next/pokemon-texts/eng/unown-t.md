## Symbol Pokémon

Unown (T) is a strangely shaped Pokémon that resembles ancient writing or hieroglyphs, leaving it a
mystery whether language inspired Unown or the other way around. Its thin body is usually found
stuck to walls, where its form appears to carry meaning.

Different Unown forms are believed to have different abilities, and they may communicate
telepathically with one another. When several are together, an odd power is said to emerge, while a
lone Unown does nothing unusual.
