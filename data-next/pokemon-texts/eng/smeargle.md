## Painter Pokémon

Smeargle marks its territory by using the fluid that oozes from the tip of its tail like a
paintbrush. This secreted fluid has a predetermined color for each individual, and its many unique
symbols and marks have been found in over 5,000 forms.

Its creations can cover walls in places where many Smeargle live, and some of its symbols are prized
by fans. As it grows up, it may even let its comrades leave footprints on its back.
