## Wild Duck Pokémon

Farfetch’d is almost always seen carrying a plant stalk, and it treats that stalk as both weapon and
lifeline. It uses it like a sword, and different individuals have been observed handling it in
distinct ways. Some are even known to quarrel over especially good stalks.

It lives where reedy plants grow and is rarely seen, so its numbers are thought to be declining.
