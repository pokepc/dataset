## Iron Ball Pokémon

Beldum’s body is made of magnetic material, and magnetism flows through it instead of blood. It uses
controlled magnetic pulses or waves to communicate with others of its kind, and swarms of Beldum can
move in perfect unison.

Its own magnetic force repels the ground’s natural magnetism, allowing it to float. When it sleeps,
it anchors itself to a cliff with the hooks on its rear. It can also use magnetic traction to draw
opponents in close and strike them with its sharp rear claws.
