## Revolving Pokémon

Inkay flashes the light-emitting spots on its body to daze opponents and drain their will to fight.
While foes are distracted, it can scuttle away and hide, or seize prey by drawing it close with its
blinking lights and wrapping it in its long tentacles.

Its luminescent spots also serve as a means of communication with others of its kind. By flashing
them in complex patterns, sometimes while spinning, Inkay can exchange information through different
rhythms of light.
