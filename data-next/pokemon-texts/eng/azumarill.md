## Aqua Rabbit Pokémon

Azumarill spends most of its life in water, where its body color and bubble-like stomach pattern
help hide it from foes. On sunny days, it may float on the surface to sunbathe, and it can stay
submerged for long periods by storing a large volume of air.

Its long ears are highly sensitive and let it notice the movements of living things, even in
fast-running rivers or along riverbeds. When it plays in the water, it rolls up its ears to keep
them dry.
