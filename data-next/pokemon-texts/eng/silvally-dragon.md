## Synthetic Pokémon

Silvally's hidden strength awakens through a deep bond of trust with its Trainer. Freed from the
heavy control mask that once restrained it, it evolves into a faster form and gains the power to
change its type at will, a trait that is especially striking in battle.

Once known as Type: Null, it was given its present name after it awakened and evolved. In this form,
the trust it shares with its Trainer is the final key to releasing its true power.
