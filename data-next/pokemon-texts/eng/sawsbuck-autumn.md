## Season Pokémon

Sawsbuck migrates with the seasons, and its horns reveal what time of year it is. The plants growing
on its horns change as the seasons turn, and the leaders of the herd are said to have especially
magnificent horns.

Because it follows the seasons, some people even call Sawsbuck a harbinger of spring. Its form
changes with the season, altering its appearance to match the time of year.
