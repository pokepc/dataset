## Order Pokémon

When the ecosystem of Kalos falls into disorder, Zygarde emerges from deep within its cave and
reveals its hidden power. It is said to watch over the region, silently monitoring those who would
destroy the environment.

When enough of its cells have gathered, Zygarde takes on a form that acts as the ecosystem’s
guardian. It is believed to hold still greater power within it, and to change into an even stronger
form when confronting threats to nature.
