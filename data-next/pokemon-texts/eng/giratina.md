## Renegade Pokémon

Giratina is said to live in a world on the reverse side of ours, where common knowledge is distorted
and strange. It was banished for its violence, and from the Distortion World it silently gazed upon
the old world.

Some accounts place it in an ancient cemetery, while a Hisuian verse describes it as the deep shadow
cast by a powerful light. Giratina can change between Altered Forme and Origin Forme, transforming
in the Distortion World or when holding a Griseous Orb.
